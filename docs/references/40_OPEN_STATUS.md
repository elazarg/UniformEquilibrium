# Open status in the external literature

This page records the durable headline from the project's detailed source
audit. It concerns the external mathematical literature, not what this
repository has proved.

The primary-source audit last verified the following boundary in August 2026:

| Players | Absorbing games | General finite stochastic games |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two, zero-sum | Settled | Settled |
| Two, non-zero-sum | Settled | Settled |
| Three | Settled | Open |
| Four or more | Open, including quitting games | Open |

The general multiplayer question and the finite-quitting specialization are
distinct. A positive result for quitting games would not by itself settle the
general problem; a genuine quitting counterexample would refute both universal
claims.
