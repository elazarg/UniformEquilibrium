## Exact statement

now have the checked subset recorded above.  The remaining subsequence,
host-compression, and source-facing ledger compositions remain ordinary
mathematics.  The actualization gives a genuine retraction to an enlarged

## 1. Abstract saturated point

Write, for a decoration \(X\),

\[
  D(X)=\text{whole total debt},\qquad
  M(X)=\text{marked stage mass},\qquad
  G(X)=\text{actual marked gain}.
\]

Let \(D_*>0\) be the global minimum debt and let \(Q\) be a point of the compact
prefix carrier such that

\[
  D(Q)>D_*,\qquad D(\operatorname{tail}Q)=D_*.
  \tag{1}
\]

Assume that \(Q\) lies in the normalized slice with positive densities
\(m,g>0\):

\[
  mD(Q)\le M(Q),\qquad gD(Q)\le G(Q).
  \tag{2}
\]

In the saturated arm these inequalities are equalities:

\[
  M(Q)=mD(Q),\qquad G(Q)=gD(Q).
  \tag{3}
\]

For the single-density toll construction one has \(g=\Delta m\), where
\(\Delta>0\) is the retained reward-gap label.  If \(Q\) is the canonical
half-density saturation of an incoming point \(P\), then

\[
  m=\frac{\rho(P)}2,qquad
  \rho(Q):=\frac{M(Q)}{D(Q)}=m=\frac{\rho(P)}2.
  \tag{4}
\]

Equation (4) is an exact natural transition, not an estimate.

## 2. Off-minimum carrier actualizer

The equality \(D(Q)=D_*\) used by the currently named minimum-return
actualizer is not needed for its compactness construction.

Because \(Q\) belongs to the closure of the raw prefix orbit, choose raw
decorations \(X_n\to Q\).  Every \(X_n\) is represented by one literal source
rank and one literal finite common-prefix word.  Continuity gives

\[
  D(X_n)\to D(Q),\quad M(X_n)\to M(Q),\quad G(X_n)\to G(Q),
  \tag{5}
\]

as well as convergence of the complete whole and tail semantic/law points.
All exact raw decoration identities hold termwise.

Define

\[
  \lambda_Q:=\frac{mD_*}{2}>0,qquad
  \gamma_Q:=\frac{gD_*}{2}>0.
  \tag{6}
\]

Global minimality and (2) imply

\[
  M(Q)\ge mD(Q)\ge mD_*=2\lambda_Q,
  \tag{7}
\]

and similarly

\[
  G(Q)\ge gD(Q)\ge gD_*=2\gamma_Q.
  \tag{8}

After deleting a finite prefix of the sequence,

\[
  M(X_n)\ge\lambda_Q,qquad G(X_n)\ge\gamma_Q

for every \(n\).  Reindexing the retained raw descendants gives an actual

Thus the saturated off-minimum point is not merely an abstract carrier
endpoint.  It retracts to an executable, source-attached decorated family.
For the single-density toll, (6) reads

\[
  \gamma_Q=\Delta\lambda_Q.
  \tag{10}

## 3. Exact obstruction to retaining the old absolute floor

Let the incoming source carry an absolute marked-mass floor

\[
  \lambda\le M(P),\qquad \lambda>0.
  \tag{11}
\]

Suppose one asks that the retracted family \(Y_n\to Q\) retain this *same*
floor:

\[
  \lambda\le M(Y_n)\quad\hbox{for every }n.
  \tag{12}
\]

Continuity forces the necessary condition

\[
  \lambda\le M(Q).
  \tag{13}
\]

For the canonical saturated half-density point, slice minimality gives

\[
  D(Q)\le D(P),
\]

and (3)--(4) give

\[
  M(Q)
   =\frac{M(P)}{2D(P)}D(Q)
   \le\frac{M(P)}2.
  \tag{14}
\]

Using the equality in (14), preservation of the old floor requires the exact
limit inequality

\[
  \boxed{
    \lambda\le M(Q)
      =\frac{M(P)D(Q)}{2D(P)}
  }
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \boxed{2\lambda D(P)\le M(P)D(Q).}
  \tag{15}
\]

For every strict inequality \(\lambda'<M(Q)\), continuity is sufficient:
one shifts the raw approximating sequence until every marked mass exceeds
\(\lambda'\).  Equality \(\lambda=M(Q)\) is only necessary and needs separate
one-sided control on the chosen approximants; convergence alone may approach
the limit from below.  Thus the robust executable criterion is the strict
version of (15).

The simpler factor-two condition

\[
  2\lambda\le M(P)
  \tag{16}
\]

is necessary because \(D(Q)\le D(P)\), but it is not sufficient: the ratio
\(D(Q)/D(P)\) may be strictly below one.  A source-independent sufficient
condition using a uniform debt upper bound \(D^{\max}\) is

\[
  M(P)>2\lambda\frac{D^{\max}}{D_*},
  \tag{17}
\]

because \(D(Q)\ge D_*\) and \(D(P)\le D^{\max}\).  Directly assuming
\(\lambda<M(Q)\) is sharpest.

The incoming packet supplies only \(\lambda\le M(P)\).  Therefore the old
absolute resolution cannot be recovered from the existing hypotheses.  For
example, if

\[
  \lambda\le M(P)<2\lambda,
  \tag{18}
\]

then a family converging to the saturated point and satisfying the old floor
does not exist.  This is a topological impossibility, independent of how the
raw approximants are selected.

The same argument applies to an old absolute gain floor.  In the
single-density chamber it is enough to track mass because \(G=\Delta M\).

There are two obvious but incompatible repairs:

* stripping the literal common prefix returns to the old base row and its old
  mass floor, but resets the density and loses (4);
* keeping the descendant point preserves exact half-density, but its natural
  guaranteed floor is the new \(\lambda_Q=mD_*/2\), not the old \(\lambda\).

The missing producer must reconcile these two operations or provide an
independent renewal floor.

## 4. Why selectable input resolution does not remove the obstruction

The Fin4 source theorem permits choosing a small input resolution below the
minimum-law atom scale.  This does not preserve the originally requested floor
through saturation.  Before the packet is selected, the theorem only returns

\[
  M(P_\lambda)\ge\lambda.
\]

It gives no uniform ratio even for the necessary condition

\[
  M(P_\lambda)/\lambda\ge2.
\]

After observing \(P\) and \(Q\), one may harmlessly *downgrade* the declared
floor on the same eventual raw family to any

\[
  0<\lambda'<M(Q).
\]

This gives one-step executable slack without reselecting the profiles.  It
does not give a renewable absolute floor: the admissible downgrade depends on
the newly selected \(Q\), and may decrease at every saturated transition.  A
useful strengthening would instead select the same packet with the uniform
sufficient slack (17), while all labels and normalized-return data remain
fixed.

### Finite pre-funding from the arbitrary-resolution source

There is nevertheless a useful stronger finite statement.  Fix a desired
output floor \(\lambda_0>0\).  If the original minimum-law atom has mass
\(\mu\), choose a source resolution \(\Lambda\) satisfying

\[
  2\lambda_0\frac{D^{\max}}{D_*}<\Lambda<\mu.
  \tag{19}
\]

Run the fixed-\(\Lambda\) source construction.  Its selected limit \(P\)
satisfies \(M(P)\ge\Lambda\).  At a saturated successor \(Q\),

\[
  M(Q)=\frac{M(P)D(Q)}{2D(P)}
       \ge\frac{\Lambda D_*}{2D^{\max}}
       >\lambda_0.
  \tag{20}
\]

Therefore the off-minimum actualizer can retain the *prescribed*
\(\lambda_0\) floor for this transition.  This is a genuine one-step repair
using the existing arbitrary-resolution quantifier.

More generally, after \(N\) consecutive exact saturated halvings the mass
ratios telescope:

\[
  M(P_N)=\frac{M(P_0)D(P_N)}{2^N D(P_0)}
    \ge \frac{\Lambda D_*}{2^N D^{\max}}.
  \tag{21}
\]

Thus any fixed finite \(N\) can be pre-funded at floor \(\lambda_0\) by
choosing

\[
  2^N\lambda_0\frac{D^{\max}}{D_*}<\Lambda<\mu.
  \tag{22}
\]

This is substantially stronger than independent per-step lower bounds: only
one debt ratio remains after telescoping.  It still does not fund an
unbounded chain, since \(\Lambda<\mu\le1\) while the required left side grows
as \(2^N\).

## 5. Quantitative loss under repeated saturated retraction

Let \(D^{\max}\) be a checked uniform upper bound on total terminal semantic
debt.  If the incoming actual family has marked-mass floor \(\lambda_n\), then

\[
  \rho_n=\frac{M(P_n)}{D(P_n)}
  \ge\frac{\lambda_n}{D^{\max}}.
  \tag{23}
\]

After canonical saturation and the actualization above,

\[
  \rho_{n+1}=\frac{\rho_n}{2},
  \tag{24}
\]

and one may take

\[
  \lambda_{n+1}
   =\frac{\rho_{n+1}D_*}{2}
   \ge \lambda_n\frac{D_*}{4D^{\max}}.
  \tag{25}
\]

Thus every finite iteration remains executable and carries a positive
resolution, but the guaranteed floors may decay geometrically.  Equations
(24)--(25) do not yield a finite natural-valued rank.  This exactly matches the
abstract Zeno obstruction: strict density progress without an absolute renewal
floor can continue indefinitely.

## 6. Full actual provenance reduces infinite renewal to screening

The source-faithful actualizers above permit one further non-scalar
classification.  For each literal descendant, flatten its arbitrary prefix
and its originating premark roots into one finite word.  Let

\[
  S_i=\Pr(i\text{ Continues through the word}),
\]

and let

\[
  H_i=\prod_{j\ne i}S_j
\]

be the reach probability after deleting player \(i\)'s prescribed clock.  At
the pure pair mark,

\[
  M=\prod_iS_i.
\]

For \(i\ne j\), the division-free identity is

\[
  H_iH_j=M\prod_{k\ne i,j}S_k\le M.
  \tag{26}
\]

Along any infinite saturated retraction, choose one literal raw actualizer per
stage close enough that \(M_n\to0\).  Compactness of \([0,1]^4\) and (26) give,
after a subsequence, exactly two cases.

### One visible host

For one fixed \(h\), \(H_{h,n}\ge\eta>0\).  Force \(h\) to Continue through
the retained premark word and let it choose its better Boolean endpoint at the
pure pair mark.  The modified actual profile:

* reaches that mark with probability \(H_{h,n}\ge\eta\);
* has a nonempty pure marked coalition because the old coalition was a pair;
* has marked \(h\)-defect exactly zero; and
* preserves the complete postmark behavioral tail literally.

After freezing the Boolean endpoint on a finite-label subsequence, this is a
source-attached concentrated endpoint with one fixed absolute mass floor.  It
exits the vanishing-resolution Zeno arm.  It does not automatically preserve
whole-profile near-minimality, so its final atlas consumer remains a separate
question.

### Full screening

For every player,

\[
  H_{i,n}\longrightarrow0.
  \tag{27}
\]

If target and comparison siblings differ only at the remote marked endpoint,
their prescribed payoffs and laws differ by \(O(M_n)\).  The gain mover's cap
is equal by own-strategy invariance.  For every other player \(i\), coupling
under an arbitrary behavioral deviation gives

\[
  |B_i(\mathrm{target}_n)-B_i(\mathrm{comparison}_n)|
    \le2R H_{i,n}\longrightarrow0.
  \tag{28}
\]

This includes Never and arbitrarily late stopping.  Thus the fixed pair,
historical paid sibling, complete law, payoff vector, and every unrestricted
cap coordinate all become invisible behind the prefix.  The common postmark
tail remains literal but unreachable under every unilateral deletion in the
limit.

The companion actual regression in
[the actual Zeno deleted-survival packet](FIN4_ACTUAL_ZENO_DELETED_SURVIVAL_HOST_OR_FULL_SCREENING.md) realizes full
screening in a rational Fin4 quitting table with one fixed decorated base row,
literal common prefix words, fixed pair and comparison labels, exact gain
\(G_n=M_n>0\), zero marked-owner defect, a fixed positive-debt postmark tail,
complete semantic/law coalescence, and unique all-Continue at the limiting
cap.  Its global minimum is zero because all-Never is an exact equilibrium.
An independent calculation in
[its independent review](../feedback/CODEX_AMPERE__ACTUAL_ZENO_DELETED_SURVIVAL_PASSPORT__BY_CODEX_CURIE.md)
checks the full behavioral-cap bound and the regression.

Therefore the full actual Fin4 fields improve the scalar boundary to

\[
  \boxed{
    \text{fixed-resolution host compression}
    \quad\lor\quad
    \text{fully screened prefix escape}.
  }
  \tag{29}
\]

Every listed actual law/cap/source field except positive global-minimum
provenance is compatible with the second arm.  A final elimination of full
screening must use \(D_*>0\) quantitatively, not merely retain it as an
external pointer.

The exact remaining producer can therefore be asked without another scalar
passport:

> Let a fixed positive-minimum Fin4 forced-pair source admit arbitrarily long
> source-faithful saturated retractions whose flattened literal prefix words
> satisfy \(H_i\to0\) for every player.  Using the global-minimum inequality
> on those same descendants, produce a terminal approximation, a charged
> source return, or a renewable finite-rank regeneration; alternatively build
> an actual positive-gap table realizing this fully screened machine.

There is an exact quantitative remnant.  Retain the literal source provenance
of one descendant.  Thus \(P_n\) is its original selected base decoration,
whose marked stage mass satisfies

\[
  \Lambda\le M(P_n)
  \tag{30}
\]

for one fixed packet floor \(\Lambda>0\), and \(X_n\) is obtained from
\(P_n\) by prefixing the finite product-root word

\[
  q_{n,0},q_{n,1},\ldots,q_{n,L_n-1}.
\]

The order here is chronological: \(q_{n,0}\) is played first, and the suffix
after \(q_{n,L_n-1}\) is the actual base profile represented by \(P_n\).
Put

\[
  c_{n,t}:=\operatorname{Cont}(q_{n,t}),\qquad
  c_{n,<t}:=\prod_{s<t}c_{n,s},\qquad
  c_n:=\prod_{t<L_n}c_{n,t},
  \tag{31}
\]

where \(\operatorname{Cont}(q)\) is the joint all-Continue probability of
the product root \(q\).  Literal marked-mass transport through the common
prefix gives

\[
  M(X_n)=c_nM(P_n).
  \tag{32}
\]

In the fully screened arm \(M(X_n)=:M_n\to0\), so (30)--(32) imply

\[
  c_n=\frac{M_n}{M(P_n)}
      \le\frac{M_n}{\Lambda}\longrightarrow0.
  \tag{33}
\]

For \(0\le t<L_n\), let \(Z_{n,t+1}\) be the complete terminal semantic pair
of the literal suffix immediately after root \(q_{n,t}\), and define the
total cap-root defect

\[
  R_{n,t}:=
  \operatorname{RootDefect}\!\left(q_{n,t};Z_{n,t+1}^{B}\right)
  \ge0.
  \tag{34}
\]

Here \(Z_{n,t+1}^{B}\) is the unrestricted behavioral cap vector of that
actual suffix.  More explicitly, let \(Q_i(q_{-i})\) be player \(i\)'s
pure-Quit endpoint value and let \(C_i(q_{-i};b_i)\) be its pure-Continue
endpoint value when the continuation cap is \(b_i\).  Then

\[
  \operatorname{RootDefect}(q;b)
  :=\sum_i\left(
    \max\{Q_i(q_{-i}),C_i(q_{-i};b_i)\}
    -q_iQ_i(q_{-i})-(1-q_i)C_i(q_{-i};b_i)
  \right).
\]

This is nonnegative and is exactly the sum of the four one-row product-root
Nash defects against continuation cap vector \(b\).  The exact one-root debt
identity is

\[
  D(Z_{n,t})=R_{n,t}+c_{n,t}D(Z_{n,t+1}).
  \tag{35}
\]

Iterating (35) in the displayed chronological order yields the fully
source-matched ledger

\[
  D(X_n)=c_nD(P_n)+
    \sum_{t<L_n}c_{n,<t}R_{n,t}.
  \tag{36}
\]

Every \(X_n\) is an actual behavioral profile descended from the retained
forced-pair source.  Hence global minimality gives \(D(X_n)\ge D_*>0\).
Since \(D(P_n)\le D^{\max}\) and \(c_n\to0\), equations (33) and (36) imply,
eventually,

\[
  \boxed{
    \sum_{t<L_n}c_{n,<t}R_{n,t}\ge\frac{D_*}{2}.
  }
  \tag{37}
\]

Thus a fully screened word carries order-one **aggregate cap-defect charge**
on the same literal source word.  This is not yet an executable paid-edge
ledger.  Each \(R_{n,t}\) is computed against an unrestricted suffix cap
vector, need not be realized by the prescribed action at that row, and the
sum in (37) may diffuse over arbitrarily many non-Nash roots.  Backward
exactifying those roots instead collapses toward the all-Continue
minimum-tail stack.  The open bridge is precisely the source-faithful
conversion of the aggregate cap charge (37) into a literal paid return or a
renewable finite-rank regeneration.

## 7. Scope of the source-faithful result

This result is stronger than a scalar \((D,M,G)\) countermodel in the following
sense.  The retracted family consists of actual behavioral profiles and
retains:

* complete whole and tail payoff/cap vectors;
* complete whole and tail terminal laws;
* literal comparison and target profiles;
* literal finite prefix words and source ranks;
* exact post-mark spine equality;
* exact zero marked-owner defect; and
* fixed terminal, owner, and mover labels.

The compact carrier point itself does not contain the comparison source's
whole law/cap point as a separate coordinate, but the raw actualizer restores
that data at every finite rank.  Hence source provenance is not the present
failure.

What remains unavailable is one of:

1. the exact floor inequality (15) at every renewal;
2. a new absolute floor independent of the halving density;
3. a consumer of the zero-density/Zeno limit using its complete law/cap
   provenance; or
4. a different finite rank which decreases under saturated actualization even
   when the resolution floor shrinks.

## Verdict

The saturated arm admits a genuine source-faithful retraction, and the exact
half-density transition survives it.  Therefore the earlier concern that a
carrier minimizer might be nonexecutable can be removed.

The requested stronger retraction preserving the incoming absolute resolution
does not follow and can be impossible.  Its exact limit requirement is

\[
  2\lambda D(P)\le M(P)D(Q),
\]

whereas the current forced-pair producer supplies only
\(\lambda\le M(P)\).  Even factor-two input slack is merely necessary, not
sufficient when debt falls.  Consequently the remaining normalized-inert
obstruction is a real renewable-resolution problem, not a missing compactness
or source adapter.  With all actual product-word data retained, that problem
has the sharper form (29): only fully screened prefix escape can support an
indefinite Zeno chain without producing a fixed-resolution endpoint.


## Adapter and consumer

The adapter starts with the checked normalized prefix-orbit carrier and its literal raw decorations. The generic actualizer selects raw descendants converging to the off-minimum saturated point and retains the full decoration. The deleted-survival vector is computed from each flattened literal word. A visible host is immediately converted to a fixed-resolution concentrated endpoint; full screening yields the ledger (37).

No checked consumer currently converts (37) to paid chronology. A successful consumer must preserve the source and actual suffixes while producing terminal approximants, an admissible positive return, or a renewable minimum-source rank descent.

Factor the existing minimum-return actualizer through a generic carrier actualizer whose only debt assumption is \(D_*\le D(Q)\). ## Scope and nonclaims

- Equality of a requested floor with the limiting mass is not sufficient without one-sided approximation.
- Finite-depth pre-funding is not infinite renewable resolution.
- Host compression need not preserve whole-profile near-minimality.
- The fully screened cap-defect ledger is not yet an admissible payoff return.
- No positive-gap table or terminal approximation is produced.
