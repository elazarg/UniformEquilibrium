# Finite cone certificates produce alternating-pair uniform equilibria

This is an ordinary mathematical proof of a finite raw-data producer for
alternating-pair uniform equilibria. No new Lean verification or general
Fin4 closure is claimed. The compact angular/radial Brouwer argument removes
inverse-sign and premium-sign restrictions. The strategy architecture remains
alternating pairs; the result enlarges its complete raw-data coverage.

## 1. Exact finite raw criterion

There are four players I={0,1,2,3}. A nonempty first-quitter coalition S
absorbs at a finite original reward vector r(S). Live dates, including the
selecting date, and perpetual Continue pay zero. Randomization is private
and independent. A unilateral replacement can be any behavioral strategy,
with arbitrary memory, private randomness, stopping dates, and Never.

Choose a partition into two pairs, let m be its mate involution, and write
O(i)=I minus{i,m(i)}={j,k}. From the sixty original rewards define

    s_i=r_i({i}), Z_i(S)=r_i(S)-s_i for nonempty S,
    Gamma_ii=0, Gamma_i,l=Z_i({l}) for l!=i,
    p_i=Z_i({i,m(i)}), c_i=p_i-Gamma_i,m(i),
    P_i=Z_i({j,k}).

Assume only that Gamma is invertible and each c_i>0. Put B=Gamma inverse.
There is NO entrywise sign condition on B, and p_i and P_i may be arbitrary
signed finite numbers. Own levels s_i may also have either sign.

Choose a real four-by-four proof-weight matrix V such that

    V_a,l>0 for every a,l, det V!=0,
    sum_a V_a,l=1 for each l.                           (1)

This is finite nonstrategic certificate data. It is not a hazard vector,
continuation value, root, or equilibrium. For a positive x define

    D(x)=product_l(1+x_l),
    T_i(x)=c_i x_m(i)(x_j+x_k+x_jx_k)
           +p_i x_m(i)^2/(1+x_m(i))-P_i x_jx_k.

The product N_i(x)=D(x)T_i(x) is the literal polynomial

    N_i(x)=[c_i x_m(i)(x_j+x_k+x_jx_k)-P_i x_jx_k]D(x)
            +p_i x_m(i)^2 product_(l!=m(i))(1+x_l).

Compute the four polynomials in y=(y0,y1,y2,y3)

    G(y)=V inverse B N(Vy).                             (2)

They have total degrees between2 and7. Require EVERY monomial coefficient
of each G_a to be nonnegative. There are exactly325 possible monomials of
total degree2 through7, hence1300 finite coefficient inequalities. Missing
coefficients are zero. No behavior at an unknown strategic point is an input.

Finally compute

    R_a,b=max_l V_a,l/V_b,l,
    kappa_i=c_i/(R_j,m(i)+R_k,m(i))>0,                  (3)

and require the twelve raw joining inequalities

    Z_i({i,j}), Z_i({i,k}), Z_i({i,j,k})<=kappa_i.       (4)

There are no additional passive bounds or restrictions on the twenty-four
unmentioned nonsingleton coordinates. All three pair partitions are allowed.
The class is an explicit finite semialgebraic certificate class in the raw
rewards and proof weights V; exact rational inputs and weights permit exact
rational checking. Existence of a strategy is not part of its definition.
Column normalization imposes no loss: replacing an arbitrary positive
invertible V by V S inverse, with S its positive diagonal column sums,
changes G(y) to S G(S inverse y). This preserves every coefficient sign
and every ratio in (3).

**Theorem.** Every table satisfying these tests has a proper period-two exact

## 2. The coefficient certificate forces a positive invariant cone

Let Delta={z>=0:sum_l z_l=1}. The positive normalized cone section is
C=V Delta. Every v in C has positive coordinates and sum one. More generally,

    x_a<=R_a,b x_b.                                    (5)

This follows columnwise from V_a,l<=R_a,b V_b,l, then summing against y_l.
It is valid on the whole cone, not only its generators.

Write G_d for G's degree-d homogeneous part. Its top part has the exact form

    G_7(y)=V inverse B [product_l x_l] C_3(x),
    x=Vy, (C_3(x))_i=c_i x_m(i)x_jx_k.                 (6)

For every nonzero y>=0, x>>0 and C_3(x)>>0. Since V and B are invertible,

    G(y)>=G_7(y)>=0, G(y)!=0.                           (7)

    B T(Vy)=V G(y)/D(Vy)>>0,

## 3. Explicit radial bounds and a product Brouwer construction

For each coordinate vector e_l, (6) implies G_7(e_l)>=0 and nonzero. Thus

                clip_[r,R](t+1-h(z,t)) ).             (12)

G(tz)=(sum_b G_b(tz))z=t D(tVz)z. Put x=tVz. Then x>>0 and

    B T(x)=V G(tz)/D(x)=tVz=x,
