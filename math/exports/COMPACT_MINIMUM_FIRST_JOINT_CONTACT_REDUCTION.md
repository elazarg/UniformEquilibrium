# A compact all-END minimum has a paid first joint cap contact

## Conjecture-facing change and strategic inputs

This is ordinary mathematics, not a Lean-checked theorem. It removes the
ACTUAL-calendar requirement for the first joint cap-contact normal form.
At the same fixed table, weights and positive full-SUM minimum, it constructs
a proper joint product row, a current finite cap contact, and a genuine
compact suffix. The selected source itself is preserved when it minimizes
original Never among the compact all-END minima. This applies even when no
actual law realizes the source or suffix.

The inputs are finite native rewards with a positive absorbing debt gap,
positive recipient weights and raise, and a nonempty all-END subset of the
true compact global-minimum set under the stated strict gap bound. The
prior universal all-END source construction already supplies such fixed
weights and sufficiently small raises from arbitrary native positive-gap
data. That source construction is credited as old; it is not reproduced or
counted as a new raw reward-table UE criterion here. No head, maximizing
finite deadline, actual calendar, minimizing tail, prescribed Nash root,
root selector, common debt vector or favorable rate is supplied.

The previously surviving source mode was a compact all-END global minimum
whose cap could be attained only at native Never or an accumulation clock,
with no produced current finite contact. The theorem excludes that mode at
the selected least-original-Never minimum by explicit finite-prefix
comparisons. It does not realize the suffix, consume the paid contact,
produce a UE target or solve Fin4. The source and its original Never
coordinates are retained throughout.

## Exact fixed-source statement

Let I have four owners. A native quitting table Z has finite rewards, own
singleton, live and literal Never rewards zero. Owners use independent
complete laws on natural dates plus a separate Never point; every whole-law
unilateral deviation is allowed. Retain the tuple(U,B,e,N) of prescribed
payoffs, complete caps, Never offers and ORIGINAL marginal Never masses.
Let C be the compact Euclidean closure of actual tuples. Set

    D_Z=sum_i(B_i-U_i), nu=product_i N_i,
    A=inf{D_Z(P): P actual, nu(P)=0}>0.

Fix recipient weights theta_i>0 and t>0, write Theta=sum theta_i,rho=tTheta,
and form the ONE fixed original table

    R_i(S)=theta_i[Z_i(S)+t], live/Never still zero.

On C the exact original full-SUM objective is

    F=sum_i theta_i(B_i-U_i)+rho nu.

Let mu=min_C F and suppose there is at least one global minimum with B=e.
Assume rho<theta_min*A, theta_min=min theta_i. These are the fixed all-END
source hypotheses; no additional strategic witness is used below. The old
positive-gap all-END source construction produces such fixed weights and
raises at every native A>0 table. The theorem does not require a new source
cube, actual attainment or a choice of weights depending on a minimum.

**Theorem.** Among the compact all-END global minima choose X minimizing
the ORIGINAL nu. There are a proper product row q in[0,1)^4 and a point
Y in C, with every N_i(Y)>0 and B(Y)=e(Y), such that

    X=prefix(q,Y), at least two q_i are positive,
    Q_i^Z(q)=B_i^Z(X)=e_i^Z(X) for some owner i.       (CONTACT)

Here Q_i^Z(q) is the native current forced-Quit offer. Thus a finite date0
query attains the native cap at the FIRST prescribed joint product row,
over a possibly NONREALIZED compact suffix. The row is not asserted Nash.
Every owner has strictly positive original raised debt. If all off-diagonal
joining increments Z_i({i,j})-Z_i({j}) are nonzero, the contact has a
positive-probability retained kernel event distinguishing current Quit from
native Never. The suffix need not be a minimum or an actual infinite law.

For any given compact all-END global minimum, without the least-nu tie
break, the construction instead produces such a contact minimum at the
SAME table, weights and global value, with no increase of nu.

## Continuous full-cap prefix maps and original source correspondence

For any product row q, let Q_i be its forced-Quit offer, P_i the passive
nonempty-opponent contribution, H_i=product_(j!=i)(1-q_j), h=product_j(1-q_j).
The exact maps are

    U'_i=q_iQ_i+(1-q_i)P_i+hU_i,
    B'_i=max(Q_i,P_i+H_iB_i),
    e'_i=P_i+H_i e_i, N'_i=(1-q_i)N_i.             (1)

They hold at EVERY point of C by prefixing actual realizing sequences.
They do not require actual attainment of a suffix. In particular every
finite variation of a displayed prefix stays in the SAME compact carrier.

Every finite deadline guarantees absorption. Its raised reward is its
native reward plus t, multiplied by theta_i. Late native finite replies
converge to e_i because native own singleton is zero. Hence, on C,

    B_i^R=theta_i(B_i+t),
    U_i^R=theta_i[U_i+t(1-nu)],
    e_i^R=theta_i[e_i+t(1-H_i^N)], H_i^N=product_(j!=i)N_j.

The image is the full original raised payoff/cap carrier, so F is its TRUE
unweighted SUM objective. In particular every actual conditional suffix has
raised sum debt at least mu. No observer or fixed-payoff fibre is used.

If M>=1 bounds native rewards, moving a smallest ORIGINAL Never mass to a
finite date gives the exact repair bound

    A<=D_Z+14M nu^(1/4)                             (2)

on actual profiles and, by continuity, on C. Moving the mass changes four
prescribed payoffs and three caps by at most twice M times that mass; the
mover's cap depends only on opponents and is unchanged. Thus nu=0 implies
D_Z>=A even at a nonrealized point. Since F(AllNever)=rho, every global
minimum has mu<=rho<theta_min*A and consequently nu>0. Compactness and(2)
also give mu>0: a zero minimum would have D_Z=nu=0 and contradict A>0.

Let M_R>=1 bound raised rewards, and put

    eta=mu²/(8M_R)>0, s_i=R_i({i})=theta_i t>0.

The existing checked four-player quadratic cap-margin theorem gives, at
EVERY original raised global minimum,

    B_i^R-s_i>=mu+eta.                              (3)

Therefore native B_i>0. The all-END global-minimum set is nonempty, compact
and closed. Selecting its least ORIGINAL nu is legitimate before any head
or contact is chosen. No prescribed law for that point is required.

## A finite first macro row over a compact suffix

We give the needed macro argument explicitly, reusing the strict-margin
and early-collision bookkeeping rather than assuming a marked calendar.
Choose finite actual profiles P_n converging to X in all(U,B,e,N). They
exist by first taking actual realizers and then censoring sufficiently
small late FINITE mass to Never; choose that moved mass tending to zero.
Uniform full-cap coupling and the explicit N coordinates preserve the
same limit. All their original Never masses are positive for large n.

Write epsilon_n=D_R(P_n)-mu>=0, epsilon_n->0. By(3), take n large enough
that every raised cap margin at P_n is at least mu+3eta/4. Set

    delta=min(1/8,mu/(8M_R),eta/(8M_R))>0,
    epsilon_n<min(eta/8,eta²/(128M_R)).

Delete leading rows only while their total activity ell=sum q_i is less
than delta. The following estimates make the deletion uniform in its
possibly unbounded number of dates. Suppose previous deleted activity is
at most eta/(8M_R), so current cap margins remain at least mu+eta/2.
For the next small row, own-singleton comparison gives

    Q_i(q)<=s_i+2M_R ell,
    B_i(current)-Q_i(q)>=mu+eta/4>0.

Thus its full cap MUST use Continue in(1). In particular

    |B_i(tail)-B_i(current)|<=2M_R ell.

Exact full debt accounting, with epsilon=D_R(current)-mu and
epsilon_tail=D_R(tail)-mu>=0, now gives

    epsilon=h epsilon_tail+sum_i q_i[B_i(current)-Q_i]-(1-h)mu
           >=h epsilon_tail+(eta/4)ell.              (4)

Since 1-h<=ell and epsilon<=eta/8, deleting the row decreases excess:

    epsilon_tail<=epsilon-(eta/8)ell.

Summing over ANY number of deleted rows bounds their total activity by
8epsilon_n/eta<eta/(16M_R). Their cap changes are at most twice M_R times
this activity, so all current margins stay above mu+eta/2. The induction
closes inside the original collar; no per-date error is accumulated.

The finite calendar cannot consist entirely of small rows: after its last
finite atom all owners Never, and the raised cap is s_i, contradicting that
same positive margin collar. Hence there is a first remaining row q^n with
sum q_i^n>=delta. The removed activity tends to zero. The changes of
prescribed payoffs, Never offers and original N are O(epsilon_n), and so
are all raised caps by the exact Continue identities. The continuous
native/raised correspondence therefore shows that the shifted head tuple
STILL converges to the SAME X in all native(U,B,e,N).

Let Y_n be the actual conditional tuple after this row. Extract subsequences
q^n->q and Y_n->Y in C. Formula(1) gives X=prefix(q,Y), and sum q_i>=delta.
Since N_i(X)=(1-q_i)N_i(Y)>0, every q_i<1 and N_i(Y)>0. All-END pulls
back: if B_i(Y)>e_i(Y), the Continue cap in(1) would exceed e_i(X).
Thus Y is all-END. No actual realization of Y was inferred.

Finally q cannot have only one positive coordinate, say q_h=a>0. Formula(3)
and the one-solo-row complete cap ledger give

    mu>=a(B_h^R(X)-s_h)+(1-a)D_R(Y)
       >a mu+(1-a)mu=mu.

Here B_h^R(X)=B_h^R(Y) because(3) places it above s_h. This uses only the
GLOBAL full-SUM floor D_R(Y)>=mu. Therefore at least two q_i are positive.
Properness and independence give positive literal nonsingleton root mass.
This is a finite first JOINT product row, without an actual infinite calendar.

## The compact finite-head contact ray

If a current native offer Q_i(q) already equals B_i(X), the theorem follows.
Otherwise every native gap e_i(X)-Q_i(q) is strictly positive. Choose any
existing positive q_j, and increase it from z0=q_j toward1 with Y fixed.
Write X_z=prefix(q(z),Y). Every X_z is in C. Since Y is all-END, its
complete prefix caps are exactly

    B_i(X_z)=max(Q_i(q(z)),e_i(X_z)).

Each of the FOUR gaps e_i(X_z)-Q_i(q(z)) is affine in z. Let z* be its
first zero after z0, or1 if there is no earlier zero. Throughout[z0,z*]
the profile is all-END. Every U_i,e_i and ORIGINAL nu is affine in this
single hazard, so F(X_z) is affine. There is a two-sided all-END neighborhood
of z0 because 0<z0<1 and all four initial gaps are strict. Global minimality
therefore makes this affine function CONSTANT, with value mu.

If z*=1, then nu(X_1)=0, so(2) gives

    F(X_1)>=theta_min*A>mu,

contradicting constancy. Thus z*<1. X_(z*) is another compact all-END global
minimum, with a current finite contact. Its original Never is STRICTLY less:

    nu(X_(z*))=nu(X)*(1-z*)/(1-z0)<nu(X).

The recipient j's own cap and Never offer ignore its own law, so its current
gap never changes; the newly contacting owner is different from j. Every
originally positive root hazard remains positive, so the root is still joint.
This constructs a contact minimum from any given all-END minimum.
At the selected least-nu X it is a contradiction. Hence its ORIGINAL
displayed first joint row already satisfies(CONTACT).

No compact tail is played after an infinite prefix. The only comparisons
are finite-head continuous maps on the actual closure; all original N are
retained. This is the precise removal of the actual-attainment requirement
for the first-joint-contact normal form, not for its suffix or later calendar.

## Paid contact and a retained kernel difference

At X every raised debt equals theta_i[d_i^Z+t nu]>=theta_i t nu>0.
The finite root contact is therefore PAID. Native current Quit and native
Never both have value B_i(X). Raised current Quit attains B_i^R(X), whereas
raised literal Never is smaller by theta_i t product_(k!=i)N_k(X)>0.

If every off-diagonal joining increment is nonzero, choose a positive root
hazard of an opponent j of a contact owner i; at least one exists because
the root is joint. Along finite realizers of the displayed head and suffix,

limiting probability q_j product_(k!=i,j)N_k(X)>0. Current Quit pays
Z_i({i,j}) there and native Never pays Z_i({j}); their difference is nonzero.
This is a retained finite-head kernel witness over a possibly nonrealized
suffix, not an asserted actual infinite law or an admissible descent.

## Exact boundaries and falsification controls

* The original singleton rewards are strictly positive after the raise:
  s_i=theta_i t. Native own-singleton zero is used to identify late finite
  caps and native Never; raised literal Never remains a separate response.
  At the produced contact its deficit from the raised cap is exactly
  theta_i t product_(j!=i)N_j>0. No finite/Never alias is used.
* If Z is identically zero, then A=0 and the compact full-SUM minimum is0,
  achieved by absorbing profiles; a proper least-Never source need not
  exist. Thus the positive-gap and strict weighted absorbing-bound
  hypotheses cannot be dropped from the source argument.
* The macro proof deletes rows only when sum q_i<delta; equality is kept
  as a macro row. Zero rows contribute zero to its charge and cause no
  error even if arbitrarily many precede the retained row. Every deleted
  NONZERO row has a strict Continue cap, justifying the exact equality in(4).
* The source-to-suffix identity N_i(X)=(1-q_i)N_i(Y)>0 excludes a sure-Quit
  retained row. Conversely the variation endpoint q_j=1 is genuinely
  absorbing even over a nonrealized Y: prefix every actual realizer of Y
  by that sure row. Its compact limit satisfies the same absorbing floor.
* The newly binding current contact belongs to an owner different from
  the varied owner, because a response menu and native Never offer depend
  only on opponents. It may belong to an owner whose prescribed q_i=0;
  no positive-own-mass cap-contact assertion is made.
* A lone positive root hazard contradicts the exact solo ledger using
  D_R(Y)>=mu. The inequality is a global floor, not a tail-minimum claim.
  At least two positive proper hazards give positive literal nonsingleton
  root mass by product independence, with no public lottery.
* At mu=theta_min A the sure-Quit endpoint would no longer contradict the
  gap bound. The proof retains the strict inequality rather than silently
  adding its boundary. A contact exactly at hazard1 is excluded by that
  same strict bound and continuity of the full-cap prefix map.
