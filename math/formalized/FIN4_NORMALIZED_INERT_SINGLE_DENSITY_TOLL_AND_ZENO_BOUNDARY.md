## Exact statement

Consider the strict normalized-inert arm produced from an actual Fin4 forced
singleton-to-pair source.  Fix its singleton owner `j`, forced outsider `o`,
and the positive table gap

\[
\Delta=r_o(\{j,o\})-r_o(\{j\})>0.
\tag{1}
\]

For a decorated source or prefix descendant `X`, write

\[
D(X)=\text{whole terminal semantic debt},\qquad
M(X)=\text{marked pair mass},\qquad
G(X)=\text{actual paid sibling gain}.
\]

Let `C` be the closed arbitrary-prefix carrier generated by this literal
source family.  The following results hold.

### Theorem A: one density and the arbitrary-root tent toll

Every `X in C` satisfies the exact source identity

\[
\boxed{G(X)=\Delta M(X).}
\tag{2}
\]

Consequently the two normalized passport inequalities are one inequality.  If
the selected passport `P` has positive debt and mass and the canonical
densities are

\[
m=\frac{M(P)}{2D(P)},\qquad
g=\frac{G(P)}{2D(P)},
\tag{3}
\]

then `g=Delta m`, and on `C`

\[
gD(X)\le G(X)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad mD(X)\le M(X).
\tag{4}
\]

Let `Q` minimize `D` on that single-density slice, and put

\[
D=D(Q),\qquad M=M(Q),\qquad s=M-mD\ge0.
\tag{5}
\]

For an arbitrary product root `q`, let

\[
c=\operatorname{Cont}(q),\qquad a=1-c,
\qquad R=R(q;B(Q)),
\tag{6}
\]

where `R` is its total one-root Nash defect against the full behavioral cap
of `Q`.  Prefixing has the exact ledger

\[
D(q*Q)=cD+R,\qquad M(q*Q)=cM.
\tag{7}
\]

The prefixed point remains in the normalized slice exactly when

\[
\boxed{mR\le cs.}
\tag{8}
\]

Whenever (8) holds, slice minimality gives `R>=aD`.  Splitting according to
(8), every product root therefore satisfies the global tent bound

\[
\boxed{
R(q;B(Q))\ge
\min\left\{
D(Q)\operatorname{Abs}(q),
\frac{\operatorname{Cont}(q)[M(Q)-mD(Q)]}{m}
\right\}.}
\tag{9}
\]

In particular, exactly one of the following local arms holds:

\[
\boxed{M(Q)=mD(Q)}
\tag{10}
\]

or, for every root with

\[
\operatorname{Abs}(q)\le\frac{M(Q)-mD(Q)}{M(Q)},
\]

one has the linear absorption toll

\[
\boxed{R(q;B(Q))\ge D(Q)\operatorname{Abs}(q).}
\tag{11}
\]

In the canonical saturated arm (10), mass, gain, and debt have the exact
proportionality

\[
\boxed{
\frac{M(Q)}{M(P)}
=\frac{G(Q)}{G(P)}
=\frac{D(Q)}{2D(P)}.}
\tag{12}
\]

Since `D(Q)<=D(P)`, saturation loses at least half of the source mass and
gain.

### Theorem B: the density-to-zero boundary

Let

\[
r_0=\frac{M(P)}{D(P)},\qquad
m_n=\frac{r_0}{n+2},\qquad g_n=\Delta m_n,
\tag{13}
\]

and select a debt minimizer `Q_n` on each corresponding slice.  After a
subsequence, one of the following holds:

1. a cluster has `D=D_*` and positive marked mass, and so re-enters the
   positive-density minimum-return actualizer;
2. the limiting marked mass and gain both vanish; or
3. a cluster `Q_infty` satisfies the global fixed-cap barrier

   \[
   \boxed{\forall q,\quad
   R(q;B(Q_\infty))\ge
   D(Q_\infty)\operatorname{Abs}(q).}
   \tag{14}
   \]

The quantifiers in (14) are literal: one common subsequence and limit are
chosen first, and then the inequality holds for every product root, including
full-absorption roots.

### Theorem C: the scalar interface admits an exact Zeno chain

The data in Theorems A--B do not imply a terminal output or a well-founded
rank.  More precisely, for arbitrary constants

\[
0<D_*<D_0,\qquad M_0>0,\qquad\Delta>0,
\]

there is a compact scalar prefix system

\[
\mathcal C_0=\{X_\mu:0\le\mu\le M_0\}
\]

with

\[
D(X_\mu)=D_0,\qquad D_{\rm tail}(X_\mu)=D_*,\qquad
M(X_\mu)=\mu,\qquad G(X_\mu)=\Delta\mu.
\tag{15}
\]

For every `a in [0,1]`, define `c=1-a` and

\[
q_a*X_\mu=X_{c\mu},\qquad R(q_a;X_\mu)=aD_0.
\tag{16}
\]

This action is continuous and composition-coherent; it satisfies all the
prefix ledgers and the all-root barrier with equality.  Its only zero-defect
root is all-Continue.  Nevertheless

\[
P_n=X_{2^{-n}M_0}
\tag{17}
\]

is an infinite chain in which every `P_(n+1)` is a canonical saturated slice
minimizer for `P_n`, while

\[
D(P_n)=D_0>D_*,\qquad M(P_n)>0,\qquad G(P_n)>0
\]

at every finite stage.  Thus neither the tent toll nor geometric passport
loss is itself a terminal consumer or a natural-valued rank.

### Theorem D: a renewable absolute density floor is sufficient

Let an executable source class carry

\[
\rho(P)=\frac{M(P)}{D(P)}
\]

and a fixed `rho_min>0`.  Suppose every regenerated source has
`rho(P)>=rho_min`, and every saturated transition regenerates a source in the
same class satisfying

\[
\rho(P')\le\frac12\rho(P).
\tag{18}
\]

Then saturated transitions terminate after finitely many steps.  An explicit
strict natural-valued rank is

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}(P)
=\min\{N\in\mathbb N:2^{-N}\rho(P)<\rho_{\min}\}.}
\tag{19}
\]

## Proof

At a literal forced singleton-to-pair row, both endpoint profiles absorb at
the marked date.  If `L` is its reached live mass, then

\[
M=L,\qquad G=L\Delta.
\]

A common prefix of survival `c` multiplies both quantities by `c`.  Hence
`G=Delta M` holds on every raw prefix descendant.  The zero set of the
continuous map `X |-> G(X)-Delta M(X)` is closed, so the identity extends to
the complete carrier.  This proves (2)--(4).

The exact prefix formulas give (7).  Substitution into the single slice
inequality gives

\[
m(cD+R)\le cM
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
mR\le c(M-mD)=cs,
\]

which is (8).  When feasible, minimality yields

\[
D\le cD+R,
\]

and hence `R>=aD`.  When infeasible, `R>cs/m`.  This proves (9).  If `s>0`
and `a<=s/M`, the inequality `aM<=s` gives

\[
maD\le(1-a)s=cs.
\]

Thus `R<aD` would make the prefix feasible and strictly lower debt, a
contradiction.  This proves (10)--(11).  Equation (12) follows by substituting
the canonical value of `m` into `M(Q)=mD(Q)` and using `G=Delta M`.

For Theorem B, put

\[
u_n=\frac{m_nD(Q_n)}{M(Q_n)}\in(0,1].
\]

If `u_n` is bounded below, compact boundedness of debt and `m_n -> 0` force
`M(Q_n)->0`, hence also `G(Q_n)->0`.  Otherwise pass to `u_n->0`; the radius
in (11) is `1-u_n->1`.  For any fixed root of absorption below one, (11)
eventually applies and passes to the limit by continuity.  Full-absorption
roots follow by adding a vanishing Continue tremble to every marginal and
taking a second limit.  If instead a cluster has `D=D_*` and `M>0`, it is a
minimizer of the positive-density slice with density `M/(2D_*)`, giving the
first arm.

For Theorem C, equations (15)--(16) give directly

\[
D(q_a*X)=cD(X)+R(q_a;X),\quad
(M,G)(q_a*X)=c(M,G)(X),\quad
R=D\operatorname{Abs}.
\]

Composition uses \(a\mathbin\oplus b=1-(1-a)(1-b)\) and

\[
aD_0+(1-a)bD_0=(a\mathbin\oplus b)D_0.
\]

The normalized slice based at `P_n` is exactly

\[
\{X_\mu:M(P_n)/2\le\mu\le M(P_n)\}.
\]

Debt is constant on the slice, so `P_(n+1)` is a saturated minimizer.  This
proves the Zeno chain.

Finally, after `n` transitions satisfying (18),

\[
\rho(P_n)\le2^{-n}\rho(P_0).
\]

For the rank in (19), if `N=rank(P)` then

\[
2^{-(N-1)}\rho(P')\le2^{-N}\rho(P)<\rho_{\min},
\]

so `rank(P')<=N-1`.  Since every regenerated source has density at least
`rho_min`, its rank is positive.  This proves strict finite descent.

## Conjecture-facing change

This strictly narrows the live Fin4 `NormalizedInert` obligation.

The actual forced-pair provenance collapses the generic two-density passport
to one scalar density and gives an exact arbitrary-root tent toll.  The
remaining scalar limits are exhausted by positive-density minimum return,
vanishing passport, and a global fixed-cap barrier.

The Zeno system proves that no argument using only these scalar passport
coordinates, prefix ledgers, compactness, slice minimality, and positive named
tail debt can finish the branch.  A successful proof must use additional
executable provenance.  One exact sufficient target is now isolated:

\[
\boxed{
\text{source-faithful saturated regeneration preserving the half-density}
\; + \;
\text{a uniform absolute density floor}.}
\]

For the existing forced-pair sources the incoming mass floor `lambda>0` and a
compact debt upper bound `D_max` would give

\[
\rho_{\min}=\lambda/D_{\max}.
\]

What is absent is a source-faithful retraction of a saturated off-minimum
carrier minimizer into that same source class while retaining the halved
density.

## Probability and strategy-class audit

Equation (2) compares two actual behavioral profiles that differ only at one
reached date.  A sure quitter is present under both endpoint actions, so no
continuation or cap-attainment assumption enters the gain calculation.
The whole-debt prefix ledger and `R(q;B(Q))` use unrestricted behavioral caps.
The root `q` in (6)--(14) is an arbitrary product root, not a stationary
strategy or a supplied exact Nash root.

The Zeno construction is intentionally an abstract scalar prefix system.  It
is not asserted to be a behavioral quitting-game carrier, and therefore is
used only to refute deductions from the displayed scalar interface.

## Boundary tests

- At saturation `s=0`, no positive toll radius is claimed.
- At zero absorption, (11) reduces to nonnegativity of root defect.
- Full absorption is not covered by the small-radius hypothesis, but is
  covered by the global tent bound without a spurious positive lower bound.
- The full-absorption limit in Theorem B is obtained by product Continue
  trembles, not by exchanging quantifiers.
- The actual Fin4 table `r_i(S)=-1` if `i in S` and zero otherwise realizes
  the global barrier at a positive-debt semantic point: at the pure `{0}`
  profile, `D=1`, `R(q)=sum_i x_i>=Abs(q)`, and all-Continue is the unique
  exact root.  Its global minimum is nevertheless zero, so it is a boundary
  regression rather than a counterexample.
- The scalar Zeno system realizes saturation and the toll with equality at
  every scale while keeping positive passport at every finite stage.
