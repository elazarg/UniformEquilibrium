## Exact statement

Let $I=\operatorname{Fin}4$, let

\[
r:\{S\subseteq I:S\ne\varnothing\}\longrightarrow\mathbb R^I
\]

be a finite quitting-game reward table, and let $D$ denote total terminal
debt against unrestricted unilateral behavioral deviations.  Assume that
the compact terminal-semantic carrier has positive global minimum

\[
D_*:=\min D>0.
\tag{1}
\]

Suppose the canonical forced-pair construction is in its strict killed-mover
endpoint arm.  Thus it supplies actual behavioral profiles $E_n$, marked
dates $s_n$, one fixed marked owner $p$, and, after one finite-label
subsequence, one fixed routed nonempty coalition $T$, with the following
literal data.

1. At the marked row of $E_n$, player $p$'s root-coordinate Nash defect
   against the actual post-mark prescribed payoff is zero.
2. The unconditional stage mass of $T$ has a fixed positive lower bound.
3. The complete post-mark semantic/law tails converge to a point whose
   semantic debt is $D_*$.
4. The whole endpoint semantic/law points converge to a point of debt
   $L>D_*$.
5. The endpoint keeps its literal pre-endpoint sibling as historical
   provenance.  Prefixing both profiles by the same word transports their
   positive source-to-endpoint payoff difference by the word's joint
   Continue factor.  Let $g_n>0$ denote this transported marked passport
   gain.  After choosing constants

   \[
   0<\theta<\frac{m}{L},\qquad
   0<\psi<\frac{g}{L},
   \tag{2}
   \]

   its limiting marked mass $m$ and historical gain $g$ have positive
   densities relative to endpoint whole debt.  The gain is passport data; it
   is not a claim that the already improved endpoint has another profitable
   toggle in the same direction.

Close every $E_n$ under every finite literal word of product roots, shifting
the marked date by the word length and retaining the whole joint point,
post-mark joint tail, transported stage mass, and transported gain.  Take the
closure $\mathcal K$ of these decorated actual descendants and the compact
normalized slice

\[
\mathcal K_{\theta,\psi}
=\left\{x\in\mathcal K:
D(\operatorname{tail}(x))=D_*,\quad
m(x)\ge\theta D(\operatorname{whole}(x)),\quad
g(x)\ge\psi D(\operatorname{whole}(x))\right\}.
\tag{3}
\]

Let $\bar x$ minimize whole debt on this slice.  Then exactly the following
exhaustive reduction holds.

### A. Off-minimum inert passport

If

\[
D(\operatorname{whole}(\bar x))>D_*,
\]

then every exact cap--Nash product root at the cap of
$\operatorname{whole}(\bar x)$ is the all-Continue root:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Nash}(B(\operatorname{whole}(\bar x)))
=\{\mathbf C\}.}
\tag{4}
\]

The complete normalized passport remains attached to this off-minimum joint
point.  This is the maintained unique-all-Continue inert atlas node.

### B. Actual minimum-return concentrated packet

If

\[
D(\operatorname{whole}(\bar x))=D_*,
\]

then there are actual raw descendants $V_k$, shifted marked dates $t_k$,
and a constant $\rho>0$ such that

\[
D(V_k)\longrightarrow D_*,
\qquad
D(\operatorname{tail}_{t_k}(V_k))\longrightarrow D_*,
\tag{5}
\]

\[
\rho\le
\operatorname{StageMass}(V_k,t_k,T),
\qquad
\operatorname{Def}_p(V_k,t_k)=0
\quad(k\in\mathbb N).
\tag{6}
\]

The same actualizer retains comparison profiles $S_k$, one fixed gain mover
$a$, and the source-matched whole-profile payoff floor

\[
0<\frac{\psi D_*}{2}
\le U_a(V_k)-U_a(S_k).
\tag{6a}
\]

This is a genuine active passport attached to the actual endpoint sequence,
but it is still producer data.  It is not a terminal-equilibrium, return, or
rank conclusion by itself.

After deleting finitely many indices, define

\[
\begin{aligned}
\texttt{profiles}(k)&:=V_k,\\
\texttt{subseq}(k)&:=k,\\
\texttt{mark}(k)&:=t_k,\\
\texttt{cutoff}(k)&:=t_k+1,\\
\texttt{scale}(k)&:=1/(k+1).
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]

These data form a literal
`QuittingReprojectionConcentratedPacket` with owner $p$, terminal $T$,
and resolution $\rho$.  In particular, the normalized owner-defect numerator
is identically zero; no copied source sibling and no cap--Nash exactness on a
copied sibling are required.

Choose any fixed $o\in T\setminus\{p\}$.  The checked concentrated-packet
consumer gives exactly

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\texttt{HasQuittingConcentratedSingletonStrategicDispatch}
   (\texttt{witness},\texttt{packet},o)\\
&\quad\lor\quad
\operatorname{Nonempty}
  (\texttt{QuittingConcentratedCollisionMinimumResidual}
    (r,\texttt{minimum},p,T,\texttt{packet})).
\end{aligned}}
\tag{8}
\]

The post-mark convergence in (5) additionally forces every selected collision
tail cluster to have debt $D_*$.  Hence in the nonsingleton case the fixed
positive tail-escape alternative of the existing collision compiler is
eventually false, and the packet reaches its maintained three-role
transfer/limit-chord output.

Equation (8) is a residual output, not a terminal or descent conclusion.  The
theorem does not claim that either the strategic-singleton output, the
collision-minimum output, or the unique-all-Continue passport is already
consumed.

## Conjecture-facing change

The canonical support-rank handoff left the strict endpoint excursion

\[
D(E_n)\to L>D_*
\]

open.  A tempting but invalid repair was to exactify $E_n$, copy the same
root word back to its source sibling, and require that copied sibling also
return to the minimum fibre.  A horizontal endpoint change can alter other
players' caps, so the copied word need not remain cap--Nash.

The present reduction removes that unnecessary premise.  If normalized
minimization reaches $D_*$, the endpoint actualizers $V_k$ themselves
supply the concentrated packet and enter the existing maintained consumer.
The copied sibling is irrelevant to this entrance.  If normalized
minimization stays above $D_*$, prefix-invariant minimization produces the
already named unique-all-Continue inert passport.

Thus the strict killed-mover endpoint is no longer an additional atlas leaf:

\[
\boxed{
\text{strict endpoint}
\Longrightarrow
\text{off-minimum inert passport}
\ \lor\
\text{minimum-return strategic/collision packet output}.}
\tag{9}
\]

This is a strict named narrowing of the off-minimum endpoint obligation.  It
does not reduce the number of still-unconsumed terminal consumer classes:
the minimum-return three-role output and the off-minimum inert passport remain
the live conjecture-facing tasks.

## Definitions, probability, and strategy class

Profiles are arbitrary behavioral strategies in the discrete-time quitting
game.  At each live history each player independently randomizes between Quit
and Continue.  A unilateral deviator may use any behavioral strategy,
including Never, arbitrarily late stopping, randomized hazards, and arbitrary
dependence on the observed live history.

For a profile $\sigma$,

\[
B_i(\sigma)=\sup_{\tau_i}U_i(\sigma[i\leftarrow\tau_i]),
\qquad d_i(\sigma)=B_i(\sigma)-U_i(\sigma),
\qquad D(\sigma)=\sum_i d_i(\sigma).
\]

The complete terminal law includes every finite nonempty terminal coalition
and all-Never.  Stage mass in (6) is unconditional: it already includes the
probability of surviving every earlier root.

For a finite root word $W$, let

\[
c(W)=\prod_{q\in W}\Pr_q(\text{all Continue}).
\]

Literal prefixing transports both marked mass and the marked passport gain by
the same factor $c(W)$, while leaving the post-mark tail unchanged.  No Nash
condition is imposed on the raw words used to define $\mathcal K$.

## Proof

### 1. Compact normalized minimization

The joint semantic/law carrier is compact.  Whole and tail joint points,
marked mass, and marked gain therefore live in a compact product.  The closure
$\mathcal K$ of the raw finite-prefix orbit is compact.  The empty prefix
places the strict endpoint limit in $\mathcal K_{\theta,\psi}$, so the slice
is nonempty; its defining conditions are closed, so it is compact.  Hence
whole debt has a minimizer $\bar x$.

### 2. Exact roots at the minimizer are all Continue

Prefixing by a fixed root is continuous and maps the raw orbit into itself;
therefore it maps $\mathcal K$ into itself.  If $q$ is exact cap--Nash at
the whole semantic point of $\bar x$, with joint Continue mass $c$, exact
debt scaling gives

\[
D(T_q\operatorname{whole}(\bar x))
=cD(\operatorname{whole}(\bar x)).
\tag{10}
\]

Marked mass and gain also become $cm$ and $cg$, while the tail is
unchanged.  Thus the normalized density inequalities remain exact and the
prefixed point stays in the slice.  Minimality gives

\[
D(\operatorname{whole}(\bar x))
\le cD(\operatorname{whole}(\bar x))
\le D(\operatorname{whole}(\bar x)).
\]

Because this debt is at least $D_*>0$, $c=1$.  A product root has joint
Continue mass one only when every player Continues surely.  Conversely,
all Continue is exact against a behavioral cap because each cap coordinate
dominates the immediate singleton-Quit value.  This proves (4).

### 3. Minimum-slice actualizers form the packet

Assume the slice minimum is $D_*$.  Choose raw decorated descendants
converging to $\bar x$, and call their actual profiles and shifted marks
$(V_k,t_k)$.  Continuity gives both debt convergences in (5).  The density
condition and $D(V_k)\to D_*>0$ give eventually

\[
\operatorname{StageMass}(V_k,t_k,T)
\ge \frac{\theta D_*}{2}.
\]

Set $\rho=\theta D_*/2$ after deleting the finite exceptional prefix.
Every raw descendant is formed by prefixing before the marked row.  Hence its
marked root and literal post-mark tail are unchanged except for the date
shift.  The killed-mover best endpoint has zero marked coordinate defect, so
the second statement in (6) is exact.

The identity subsequence is strictly monotone, $t_k<t_k+1$, and
`scale k = 1/(k+1)` is positive and tends to zero.  Positive stage mass and
the literal profile spine give carrier membership, exact semantic prefixing,
and positive root incidence.  Since the owner-defect numerator is zero, its
quotient by `scale k` tends to zero.  These are precisely all fields of
`QuittingReprojectionConcentratedPacket`.

The gain-density inequality similarly gives the positive floor
$\psi D_*/2$.  The raw decorated family stores both the endpoint descendant
and the commonly prefixed comparison descendant, so this scalar is the
literal payoff difference in (6a), not an unattached annotation.

The routed geometry ensures $T\setminus\{p\}\ne\varnothing$: leaving a
pair leaves the other member, while joining a pair retains both original
members.  Thus a fixed $o\ne p$ belongs to $T$, and the checked theorem
named below gives (8).

Finally, the residual's `tail_tendsto` selects a subsequence of the same
literal post-mark tails.  Their debts converge to $D_*$, and debt is
continuous, so the residual cluster has debt $D_*$.  This excludes its
strict tail-escape arm and leaves its minimum-tail other-defect/three-role
arm.

## Boundary tests

### Positive minimum is essential

If $D_*=0$, the minimizer argument does not force $c=1$.  In the all-zero
reward table every root has zero debt, including absorbing exact roots.

### The copied sibling need not be exact

The finite four-player regression in
`CODEX_ATLAS_GATEKEEPER__MONODROMY_VERTICAL_REPAIR.md` has an exact prefix
over one deep source and a one-player endpoint sibling for which another
player's cap changes by one; the copied prefix acquires defect $1/2$.  Its
global minimum is zero, so it is not a positive-minimum counterexample, but it
decisively refutes any proof which transports cap--Nash exactness through the
horizontal endpoint update using only common past, common tail, and marked
mass.

### Raw descendants need not satisfy the normalized density inequality

If a finite raw prefix has joint Continue mass $c$ and positive root defect
$e$, then whole debt becomes $cD+e$ while mass and gain become $cm,cg$.
The density ratio may decrease.  This is why prefix invariance is asserted for
exact roots at points of the closed normalized slice, not for every finite raw
descendant.  Raw descendants are used only as actualizers of a slice point.

### Fixed positive mass is indispensable

Without $m\ge\theta D$, a minimum-slice realizing sequence may have marked
mass tending to zero and cannot supply a positive `resolution` field.  A
positive limiting tail-law atom alone is not fresh root absorption.
