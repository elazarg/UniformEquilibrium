## Exact statement

Let \(I=\operatorname{Fin}4\), let \(r\) be a bounded quitting reward table,
and let Never pay zero.  Behavioral randomizations are independent across
players and dates conditional on public survival, and one deviator may replace
their complete behavioral strategy.  Suppose the game has a terminal
exploitability witness of gap

\[
 \Gamma>0.
\]

Take the full vanishing-survival alternative B of
[`FIN4_FIRST_EXACT_ROOT_SURVIVAL_OR_UNIQUE_SURE_RESET.md`](FIN4_FIRST_EXACT_ROOT_SURVIVAL_OR_UNIQUE_SURE_RESET.md).
Thus it supplies actual stationary structured sources \(\tau_n\), semantic
pairs

\[
 X_n=(u_n,B_n)=\operatorname{Sem}(\tau_n),
\]

exact independent product Nash roots \(q_n\) against \(u_n\), and literal
prefixed profiles

\[
 \rho_n=q_n::\tau_n,
 \qquad Y_n=\operatorname{Sem}(\rho_n).
\]

After a subsequence, \(q_n\to q\), where \(q\) has one unique sure quitter
\(k\):

\[
 q_k=1,\qquad q_i<1\quad(i\ne k).
\]

For every sufficiently large \(n\),

\[
 d_k(Y_n)\ge D_*/2>0.                                      \tag{1}
\]

Alternative B additionally supplies an attained complete cap response
\(\beta_{n,k}\) at the literal finite profile \(\rho_n\).  It forces
Continue at the newly prefixed root and, conditional on all opponents
Continuing, uses an attained Quit0-or-Never cap response in the stationary
tail \(\tau_n\).  Define

\[
 \chi_n=\rho_n[k\leftarrow\beta_{n,k}].                    \tag{2}
\]

Then, for every sufficiently large \(n\), all of the following hold.

1. The edge \(\rho_n\to\chi_n\) is an actual complete behavioral cap update:

   \[
    U_k(\chi_n)-U_k(\rho_n)=d_k(Y_n)\ge D_*/2,
    \qquad d_k(\chi_n)=0.                                  \tag{3}
   \]

2. There is a player \(j_n\ne k\) with

   \[
    d_{j_n}(\chi_n)\ge\Gamma.                              \tag{4}
   \]

   After a subsequence, the label is one fixed \(j\ne k\).  At the exact
   source \(\chi_n\), this player has a literal paid first-disagreement row
   of gain \(\Gamma\).

3. If \(M>0\) bounds all reward coordinates, the actual-reach refinement at
   the same source supplies a possibly different paid row for the same
   player, of gain \(\Gamma/4\), whose source witness is in the prescribed
   stopping-law support and which satisfies

   \[
    \Gamma\le4M\,\operatorname{OwnSurvival},
    \qquad
    \Gamma\le8M\,\operatorname{OpponentLiveMass}.           \tag{5}
   \]

4. The finite child has the exact floor split

   \[
   \begin{split}
    &U_i(\chi_n)\ge \operatorname{Pun}_i\quad\hbox{for all }i,
      \quad\hbox{or}\\
    &U_{i_n}(\chi_n)<\operatorname{Pun}_{i_n}
      \quad\hbox{for some displayed }i_n\ne k.
   \end{split}                                               \tag{6}
   \]

   In the all-floor arm, the actual child and either paid row form a checked
   floor-safe paid-row source.  The checked marked exact-prefix construction
   starts literally at \(\chi_n\).  Under the terminal witness, its
   absorption masses are summable, its terminal-semantic profiles converge
   to an all-Continue port, and the unchanged paid suffix has a strictly
   positive limiting reach along that one orbit.  This reach need not be
   bounded below uniformly as \(n\) varies.

There is also a robust finite-root statement.  Let

\[
 \alpha_n=1-q_{n,k}\longrightarrow0,
\]

force \(k\) to Quit surely in \(q_n\), and retain the other three marginals.
Those free marginals form a \(4M\alpha_n\)-Nash point of the induced
singleton-base binary game.  Thus every compact limit is an exact point of
that induced game.  This auxiliary fact is not needed for the literal cap
update (2)--(5).

## Definitions and assumptions

For an actual profile \(\sigma\), write

\[
 d_i(\sigma)=B_i(\sigma)-U_i(\sigma),
\]

where \(B_i\) is the supremum over all unilateral behavioral strategies,
including calendar-dependent randomization, every finite pure stopping time,
and literal Never.  The response \(\beta_{n,k}\) is an actual attained
maximizer, not merely an exact endpoint of the newly prefixed one-stage game.

For a product root \(x\) and continuation payoff \(v\), let

\[
 E_i(x;v)=Q_i(x_{-i})-C_i(x_{-i};v_i)
\]

be Quit payoff minus Continue payoff.  The induced singleton-base game fixes
\(k\) as a sure quitter, lets the other three players choose Quit or Continue,
and has no continuation payoff because the row absorbs surely.

The full Alternative-B provenance is essential.  Arbitrary actual profiles,
exact roots, root convergence, and (1) alone do not imply that a complete
tail cap is attained.  Here attainment follows from stationarity of the
literal tails and the checked fact that their unrestricted pure-time cap is
the better of Quit0 and Never.

## Proof

### The approximate singleton-base point

Let \(\widetilde q_n\) be obtained from \(q_n\) by forcing \(k\) to Quit
surely.  Couple the two root laws so that only \(k\)'s action changes.  Fix a
free player \(i\ne k\).  On the event that \(k\) Quits, the endpoint
difference for \(i\) agrees exactly with the endpoint difference in the
singleton-base game and the continuation is never reached.  The complementary
event has probability \(\alpha_n\).  Both endpoint differences lie in
\([-2M,2M]\).  Therefore

\[
 \left|E_i(\widetilde q_n;0)-E_i(q_n;u_n)\right|
 \le4M\alpha_n.                                             \tag{7}
\]

The own marginal of \(i\) is unchanged.  Exact root complementarity for
\(q_n\) gives

\[
 (1-q_{n,i})E_i(\widetilde q_n;0)\le4M\alpha_n,
 \qquad
 -q_{n,i}E_i(\widetilde q_n;0)\le4M\alpha_n.                \tag{8}
\]

These are exactly the Continue and Quit regrets of the two-action induced
game.  Thus the free marginals are \(4M\alpha_n\)-Nash.  Since
\(\alpha_n\to0\), compactness and continuity put every limit in the exact
induced Nash set.

### The finite complete-cap update

By Alternative B.2, \(\beta_{n,k}\) attains the complete cap of \(k\) at
\(\rho_n\).  Replacing only \(k\)'s strategy does not change \(k\)'s
opponents, so

\[
 B_k(\chi_n)=B_k(\rho_n).
\]

Attainment gives \(U_k(\chi_n)=B_k(\rho_n)\).  Subtracting
\(U_k(\rho_n)\) and using (1) proves

\[
 U_k(\chi_n)-U_k(\rho_n)=d_k(Y_n)\ge D_*/2.
\]

The same two equalities give \(d_k(\chi_n)=0\).  This proves (3) with
complete behavioral caps, not merely one-stage root payoffs.

### The distinct debtor and paid rows

Apply the terminal exploitability witness at the literal profile \(\chi_n\).
It selects \(j_n\) with \(d_{j_n}(\chi_n)\ge\Gamma\).  The zero identity in
(3) forces \(j_n\ne k\).  Finiteness of \(I\) fixes one label \(j\ne k\)
after a subsequence.

Choose the actual behavioral deviation supplied by terminal exploitability;
its gain at \(\chi_n\) is at least \(\Gamma\).  Both this deviation and the
prescribed strategy induce probability laws on pure stopping times, and each
payoff is exactly the expectation of the corresponding pure-time payoff.
Support-pair averaging therefore selects one receiving atom from the
deviation law and one source atom from the prescribed law whose payoff
difference is at least the difference of the two expectations, hence at least
\(\Gamma\).  The first-disagreement decomposition of those two literal atoms
gives a paid row of gain \(\Gamma\).  This is the expectation argument
underlying `exists_paidFirstDisagreementRow_at`, with the observer and both
pure-time witnesses returned in one package; it does not approximate or
assume attainment of the complete cap.

Applying `positiveDebt_exists_actualReach_paidRow_withSupport` to the same
debt at scale \(\Gamma\) gives a possibly different row of gain
\(\Gamma/4\) and exactly the two bounds in (5).  The two rows are not
identified.

### The floor split and marked orbit

For every actual opponent profile,

\[
 \operatorname{Pun}_i\le B_i.
\]

At \(\chi_n\), equation (3) gives \(U_k=B_k\), hence
\(U_k(\chi_n)\ge\operatorname{Pun}_k\).  Therefore either every player is
above the punishment floor or a displayed underfloor player is distinct from
\(k\), proving (6).

In the all-floor arm, package \(\chi_n\), the paid observer and row, and the
floor inequalities as a `QuittingPaidRowFloorSafeSource`.  The checked
marked-orbit constructor produces literal profiles by repeatedly prefixing
exact roots to this same child.  Under the terminal witness, the
cumulative-charge alternative cannot return a uniform payoff, so the selected
orbit has summable absorption.  Its roots therefore have absorption tending
to zero.  The product of their positive survival probabilities has one
strictly positive limit, giving positive limiting reach of the unchanged paid
suffix.  This limit is attached to the selected orbit and may degenerate
across the external index \(n\).

## Boundary tests

### Cap attainment cannot be omitted

If \(\beta_{n,k}\) is only an \(\varepsilon_n\)-best response, the same
calculation yields only

\[
 d_k(\chi_n)\le\varepsilon_n,
\]

not the exact zero in (3).  Root exactness does not repair this: it compares
the two current root actions against the prescribed tail payoff and does not
attain the unrestricted cap of an arbitrary nonstationary tail.

### Finite induced membership is only approximate

At finite \(n\), \(q_{n,k}<1\) in general.  On the event that \(k\)
Continues, the old continuation payoff enters a free player's endpoint
comparison.  Thus forcing \(k\) sure can change the comparison by order
\(\alpha_n\); exact induced Nash membership is not justified before the
limit.  Equations (7)--(8) are the sharp statement supplied by this coupling.

### The cap update can reactivate outsiders

Changing \(k\)'s entire strategy may alter every other player's complete cap.
The theorem deliberately selects a new debtor \(j\ne k\); it does not claim
that old zero-debt coordinates remain zero or that total debt decreases.

### Positive suffix reach is not recurrence

Within one marked exact orbit, summable absorption leaves the original paid
suffix with positive limiting reach.  This does not identify an endpoint with
the initial semantic source, preserve the tropical cap pin, or give a lower
bound uniform over \(n\).  It therefore does not create a charged return.

## Scope and nonclaims

singleton-base handoff, or another tropical source.  It does not preserve the
cap pin for \(j\), prevent cross-coordinate cap leakage, repair an underfloor

stated above.  The remaining obstruction is still the generic floor-safe
