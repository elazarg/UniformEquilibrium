Two questions have to be separated:

1. Are the accumulated constraints mutually inconsistent?
2. If not, do they force a finite-dimensional mechanism that can actually be written down?

The second question now has a sharp answer. The full-support ballistic branch collapses to a **positive stable trajectory of one fixed \(4\times4\) matrix pencil**. Moreover, there is an explicit rational quadratic-coalition table realizing that local mechanism. Thus the constraints are not locally contradictory; they isolate a very specific **Zeno eigenmachine coupled to a terminal-law bubble**.

This goes beyond the consolidated picture, where the normalized ballistic chain was still treated as lacking an absolute interpretation. 

## 1. Ballistic matrix-pencil reduction

Let

$$
M_{ij}=r_i(\{j\})-r_i(\{i\})
$$

be the normalized singleton matrix, and let

$$
C_{ij}=r_i(\{i,j\})-r_i(\{j\}), \qquad C_{ii}=0,
$$

be the collision-insertion matrix.

The existing strict-ray analysis produces normalized states

$$
(c_n,\tau_n,\rho_n),
$$

where:

* \(c_n\in\Delta_4\) is the current marginal-hazard direction;
* \(\tau_n\in\Delta_4\) is the remaining-tail hazard barycenter;
* \(\rho_n\in(0,1]\) is current hazard divided by remaining hazard.

They satisfy the exact renewal identity

$$
\tau_n=\rho_n c_n+(1-\rho_n)\tau_{n+1}.
\tag{1}
$$

The current omega-chain implementation retains only

$$
W_n:=M\tau_n+\rho_n Cc_n\le 0,
\qquad
(c_n)_i(W_n)_i=0.
\tag{2}
$$

It explicitly records that no absolute lift or terminal consumer has yet been obtained.  The underlying finite-level normalized cap-flow and endpoint decomposition are already exact.

There is, however, a missing equality.

### Full finite support forces \(W_n=0\)

Assume the same actual source ray has eventually positive current hazard at every player:

$$
(c_t)_i>0
\qquad\text{for every \(i\) and all sufficiently large \(t\)}.
\tag{3}
$$

The exact endpoint complementarity at finite \(t\) says

$$
(c_t)_i\,s_{t,i}=0,
$$

where \(s_{t,i}\ge0\) is the normalized endpoint slack. Therefore (3) gives

$$
s_{t,i}=0
\qquad\text{for every \(i\)}
\tag{4}
$$

at every sufficiently late actual date. Passing (4), together with the complete-source-window convergence, through the normalized endpoint decomposition gives

$$
\boxed{M\tau_n+\rho_n Cc_n=0}
\qquad\text{for every \(n\in\mathbb Z\)}.
\tag{5}
$$

So the full-support ballistic omega chain satisfies equality, not merely complementary inequality.

This is an ordinary-mathematics strengthening of the current Research theorem; I have not yet compiled it in Lean. The formal proof should be short because all ingredients—finite complementarity, source-coordinate convergence, tail-normalized cap flow, and collision-error convergence—are already present.

### Eliminate the current direction

Substitute

$$
\rho_n c_n=\tau_n-(1-\rho_n)\tau_{n+1}
$$

from (1) into (5). One obtains the descriptor recurrence

$$
\boxed{(M+C)\tau_n=(1-\rho_n)C\tau_{n+1}.}
\tag{6}
$$

This is the main reduction.

The apparently infinite-dimensional ballistic behavior is governed by the fixed matrix pencil

$$
P(z)=M+C-zC=M+(1-z)C.
\tag{7}
$$

If the ballistic floor is \(\rho_n\ge\eta>0\), then

$$
0\le 1-\rho_n\le 1-\eta<1.
\tag{8}
$$

Hence, writing \(a_{n+1}=(1-\rho_n)a_n\) and \(x_n=a_n\tau_n\),

$$
Cx_{n+1}=(M+C)x_n,
\qquad
\|x_{n+1}\|_1\le(1-\eta)\|x_n\|_1.
\tag{9}
$$

Thus it is literally a positive exponentially contracting descriptor trajectory.

If \(C\) is invertible, set

$$
T=C^{-1}(M+C).
$$

Then

$$
T\tau_n=(1-\rho_n)\tau_{n+1}.
\tag{10}
$$

Let \(K\) be the closed cone generated by the omega orbit. Equation (10) makes \(K\) \(T\)-invariant, and on \(K\),

$$
\|Tx\|_1\le(1-\eta)\|x\|_1.
$$

Intersecting \(K\) with the simplex and applying the elementary normalized fixed-point argument gives a nonzero \(x\ge0\) and

$$
\lambda\in[0,1-\eta]
$$

such that

$$
Tx=\lambda x.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\boxed{\bigl(M+(1-\lambda)C\bigr)x=0.}
\tag{11}
$$

Consequently, every full-support ballistic residual with invertible \(C\) contains a **positive stable pencil mode**. If \(x\) has proper support, the behavior has already collapsed to a face of cardinality at most three. If \(x>0\), it is a genuinely four-active mode.

Combined with the existing strict-ray dispatch—positive limiting root and return/descent, or ballistic, omitted player, or a binding face of cardinality three—this yields a much tighter terminal classification.

## 2. An explicit rational Zeno eigenmachine

The local mechanism is nonempty. Consider the checked paired singleton matrix

$$
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&3&-1&-1\\
3&0&-1&-1\\
-1&-1&0&3\\
-1&-1&3&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{12}
$$

This matrix has full normal core, is standard \(Q\), has no homogeneous simplex solution, and lies in the nonprojective residual-hard chamber.

Now take

$$
C=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-\frac32&-\frac32&1\\
1&0&-\frac32&-\frac32\\
-\frac32&1&0&-\frac32\\
-\frac32&-\frac32&1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{13}
$$

These are exact rational facts:

$$
\det C=17,
\tag{14}
$$

every row of \(C\) sums to \(-2\), and, with

$$
x=\frac14(1,1,1,1)^{\mathsf T},
$$

we have

$$
Mx=x,\qquad Cx=-2x.
\tag{15}
$$

Therefore

$$
\left(M+\frac12 C\right)x=0,
\qquad
C^{-1}(M+C)x=\frac12x.
\tag{16}
$$

So \(x\) is a strictly positive pencil mode with contraction eigenvalue

$$
\lambda=\frac12.
$$

Moreover, every singleton owner has a literal gain-one collider:

$$
C_{1,0}=C_{2,1}=C_{3,2}=C_{0,3}=1.
\tag{17}
$$

### The full reward table

For a baseline vector \(s\in\mathbb R^4\), define, for every nonempty quitting coalition \(S\),

$$
r_i(S)
=
s_i+\sum_{j\in S}M_{ij}
+\mathbf 1_{\{i\in S\}}
   \sum_{j\in S\setminus\{i\}}C_{ij}.
\tag{18}
$$

This is a rational quadratic-coalition table whenever \(s\) is rational. It has:

$$
r_i(\{i\})=s_i,
$$

$$
r_i(\{j\})-r_i(\{i\})=M_{ij},
$$

and

$$
r_i(\{i,j\})-r_i(\{j\})=C_{ij}.
$$

For a product root with quit vector \(q\), against cap \(s+b\), the exact Quit-minus-Continue endpoint difference is

$$
G_i(q,b)
=
(Cq)_i-b_i\prod_{k\ne i}(1-q_k).
\tag{19}
$$

The exact successor cap relative to \(s\) is

$$
F_i(s+b,q)-s_i
=
\left(\prod_k(1-q_k)\right)b_i
+(Mq)_i
+q_i(Cq)_i.
\tag{20}
$$

These formulas contain no approximation.

### Exact fully mixed Zeno orbit

For \(0<a<1\), let

$$
q_i(a)=\frac a4
$$

for all four players and put

$$
b(a)=-\frac{a}{2(1-a/4)^3}.
\tag{21}
$$

Because \(Cq(a)=-\frac a2\mathbf1\), equation (19) gives

$$
G_i(q(a),b(a)\mathbf1)=0
$$

for every player. Thus \(q(a)\) is an exact fully mixed one-stage Nash root against cap

$$
s+b(a)\mathbf1.
$$

Using (20), the exact successor cap is

$$
F(s+b(a)\mathbf1,q(a))
=
s-\frac a4\mathbf1.
\tag{22}
$$

Define \(a_{t+1}\) by

$$
b(a_{t+1})=-\frac{a_t}{4},
$$

or equivalently,

$$
\boxed{
a_t=\frac{2a_{t+1}}{(1-a_{t+1}/4)^3}.
}
\tag{23}
$$

For every \(a_t\in(0,1)\), there is a unique \(a_{t+1}\in(0,1)\), and

$$
a_{t+1}<\frac{a_t}{2}.
\tag{24}
$$

Hence

$$
\sum_t a_t<\infty.
\tag{25}
$$

We have therefore constructed an exact product-root cap recursion with all of the following properties simultaneously:

* all four players quit with positive probability at every finite date;
* every player’s total clock is summable;
* total exact absorption is Zeno;
* the cap converges to \(s\);
* the normalized hazard direction is always \(x\);
* \(a_{t+1}/a_t\to\frac12\);
* the renewal ratio tends to \(\frac12\);
* the normalized omega limit is precisely the positive pencil eigenstate in (16).

This is the root/cap side of the proposed counterexample geometry in closed rational form. It is not yet a source-attached terminal-semantic minimum construction.

### The limiting cap has a unique exact root

At the limiting cap \(s\), the endpoint vector is simply

$$
G(q,0)=Cq.
$$

For any exact Nash root, complementarity implies

$$
q_i(Cq)_i\ge0
$$

for every \(i\), and hence

$$
q^{\mathsf T}Cq\ge0.
\tag{26}
$$

But every symmetric off-diagonal pair of \(C\) is strictly negative:

$$
C_{ij}+C_{ji}<0 \qquad(i\ne j).
$$

Therefore, whenever at least two coordinates of \(q\) are positive,

$$
q^{\mathsf T}Cq
=
\sum_{i<j}(C_{ij}+C_{ji})q_iq_j
<0,
$$

contradicting (26).

If exactly one coordinate \(q_j\) is positive, then its designated collider \(j+1\bmod4\) has

$$
(Cq)_{j+1}=q_j>0
$$

while continuing surely, which also violates one-stage Nash. Consequently,

$$
\boxed{q=0}
$$

is the unique exact root at the limit cap. The limit is therefore a literal unique-all-Continue inert port.

## 3. What this settles—and what it does not

The construction is deliberately **not** a counterexample. With \(s=0\), all Never is an exact behavioral equilibrium. Taking \(s_i>0\) removes that particular equilibrium while preserving all the local formulas above, but it does not prove a positive unrestricted terminal gap or exclude other equilibria.

What it does establish is more diagnostic:

> The hard singleton chamber, full collider geometry, four-active summable Zeno clocks, uniformly ballistic normalized renewal, a positive stable pencil mode, and a unique all-Continue limiting cap are jointly consistent and have an explicit rational quadratic realization.

So the long list of requirements is not contradictory merely at the root/cap level.

But it now points to a sharply defined global object. A genuine counterexample would have to attach to this finite-dimensional Zeno eigenmachine a positive-minimum joint-law object satisfying all of the following:

1. its positive finite atom remains in a remote relative-timing bubble;
2. that bubble supplies the limiting cap \(s\) without supplying an actual diagonal continuation seed there;
3. every paid replacement changes the law or leaks debt sideways;
4. no reset regenerates a positive radial scale;
5. no proper-support pencil mode becomes a renewable three-player descent;
6. no source-compatible finite window closes into a charged return.

The canonical approximate absolute lift also becomes transparent. Given a normalized chain satisfying (1) and (5), choose a radial scale \(A_n\) with

$$
A_{n+1}=(1-\rho_n)A_n,
\qquad
\varepsilon_n=\rho_nA_n,
$$

put

$$
q_{n,i}=\varepsilon_n(c_n)_i,
\qquad
v_n=s-A_nM\tau_n.
$$

Then renewal gives the exact first-order identity

$$
v_{n+1}-v_n=\varepsilon_nMc_n.
$$

The product-law Bellman and Nash defects are only \(O(A_n^2)\). Since \(A_n\) decays geometrically in the ballistic branch, those defects are summable. What is missing is not a Nash–Bellman chronology; it is the **terminal-semantic seed that identifies its limiting baseline with one actual source-attached continuation law**.

That isolates the remaining theorem:

$$
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c}
\text{A positive-minimum causal terminal-law bubble cannot ride a}\\
\text{positive stable Fin4 pencil mode without producing either}\\
\text{a terminal approximation, a charged reset, or a proper-face regeneration.}
\end{array}}
$$

The full-support inert branch is therefore no longer an amorphous behavioral residual. Its cap chronology is a stable positive mode of the rational pencil \(M+(1-\lambda)C\), and that mechanism can be demonstrated exactly. The unresolved content is precisely the coupling between that mode and the remote positive-minimum atom.
