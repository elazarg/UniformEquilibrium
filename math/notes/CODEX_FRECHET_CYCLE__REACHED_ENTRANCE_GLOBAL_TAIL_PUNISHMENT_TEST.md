## 2. Full changed-tail objective

Write C for the compact full semantic carrier, with coordinates (u,b).
For a product root q set c=∏ᵢ(1−q_i), α_i=∏_(j≠i)(1−q_j), and let
Q_i be the immediate Quit endpoint, A_i the zero-continuation Continue
contribution, and R_i the prescribed terminal contribution at the root.
The full prefix map is

    U_i(T_qz)=R_i+c u_i,
    B_i(T_qz)=max(Q_i,A_i+α_i b_i).

It retains every later pure stopping date, Never, and arbitrary randomized
complete responses. Thus the changed-tail objective is

    Ψ_q(u,b)=Σ_i max(Q_i,A_i+α_i b_i)−Σ_i R_i−cΣ_i u_i.

For every actual tail τ, q::τ is an actual independent product profile,
so Ψ_q(U(τ),B(τ))≥D*. Continuity extends this to every z∈C. The entrance
tail attains equality in this transformed objective:

    Ψ_q(z⁺)=D* = min_[z∈C] Ψ_q(z).

This is not the statement that z⁺ minimizes its own unprefixed SUM debt.
It is precisely the extra universal changed-tail family absent from a
fixed-tail root optimization. I tested its simplest nontrivial screening
boundary, rather than trying to temporalize any response arrow.

## 3. One sure owner: exact nonlocal competitor

Assume q has exactly one sure quitter k. Then c=0, α_k=α>0, and
α_j=0 for every j≠k. Every prescribed payoff and every nonowner cap is
independent of the entire continuation. Only the owner's cap can change.
Since U_k=Q_k, put

    H=Σ_(j≠k) [B_j(T_qz)−U_j(T_qz)],
    θ=(Q_k−A_k)/α.

The quantity H is independent of z, and the exact full debt is

    Ψ_q(z)=H+α(b_k−θ)₊.                         (1)

Let P_k be the infimum of player k's unrestricted cap over all independent
opponent laws. Every carrier point has b_k≥P_k. For each η>0, the
definition of the infimum gives three independent opponent laws whose
cap against player k is at most P_k+η. Choose any own tail law for k.
This is one actual tail τ_η, and q::τ_η is an actual global competitor.
No public correlation is used. The three opponent tails may change
simultaneously; their own incentives in this auxiliary choice are not
asserted or required.

Equation (1) implies

    inf_[actual τ] D(q::τ)=H+α(P_k−θ)₊.          (2)

Punishment attainment is NOT assumed. The finite η choices approximate
the right-hand side from above with error at most αη.

For an entrance tail with cap b_k⁺, the maximum possible reduction by
this changed-tail construction is

    Δ=α[(b_k⁺−θ)₊−(P_k−θ)₊]
      =min(d_k(z*), α(b_k⁺−P_k)).               (3)

If Δ>0, choose η<Δ/(2α). The actual competitor q::τ_η then has
D(q::τ_η)<D*−Δ/2, contradicting the defining infimum. This is the exact
condition under which the remaining max channel genuinely decreases.

At a global minimum, however, (3) forces only the following exhaustive
alternative:

    d_k(z*)=0, equivalently b_k⁺≤θ;
    or b_k⁺=P_k>θ, with d_k(z*)>0.              (4)

In the first arm, decreasing the tail cap is hidden by the immediate-Quit
maximum. In the second arm, the active cap channel is already minimized
over ALL possible opponent tail laws. There is no further reduction
available by this operation. For an actual near-minimum one-sure source
with D≤D*+ε, the same argument gives the exact quantitative necessary
condition min(d_k,α(b_k−P_k))≤ε.

The carrier entrance causes no actualization gap in this comparison:
the comparison profile uses the limiting root q itself, which is a valid
product root, and actual τ_η. It never attempts to play the possibly
unattained tail z⁺. If desired, replacing the actual source roots q_n by
q changes their prefixed U and B by a uniformly vanishing amount because
all tail semantic coordinates lie in the fixed reward box.

## 4. Why this does not consume the entrance

Punishment normality P_k≤s_k does not contradict the second arm of (4).
The surviving head values there are

    U_k(z*)=Q_k,
    B_k(z*)=A_k+αP_k,
    d_k(z*)=A_k+αP_k−Q_k>0.

The root's other players can absorb immediately and contribute through
A_k. Consequently a low minimized TAIL cap does not say that the HEAD
cap is low, or that the sure owner's head payoff equals its singleton.
The strict minimum payoff/cap singleton margins apply to z*, not to the
possibly off-minimum tail z⁺. Transferring them to b_k⁺ would be invalid.

Changing the sure owner's complete law to realize its cap may kill
its own debt, but then prescribed reach of the tail becomes positive
and the other three unrestricted caps/payoffs can all change. Global
minimality allows that lost owner debt to reappear on the others. No
bound preventing that leakage was obtained here. This is the precise
missing implication, not a proof that no other joint-law move works.

With at least two sure quitters, every α_i is zero and tail replacement
is completely invisible. With no sure quitter, c>0 and changes to all
tail payoffs enter Ψ_q; independently chosen coordinate punishments
cannot be installed as a single shared product tail without proving a
joint realization. Those other branches were not investigated here.

Here is the precise correspondence, including the scope difference.
Choose for each player j a finite portfolio of ARBITRARY actual complete
stopping laws τ_j^a, with private independent portfolio weights π_j.
Let τ^a be a product vertex, w(a)=∏_j π_j(a_j), and let τ̄ be the product
of the playerwise mixed stopping laws. Mixing laws means mixing their
probability distributions on finite times and Never, then taking the
canonical behavioral realization; it does not mean averaging hazards
pointwise or adding a public randomizing device.

The fixed-root embedding of each player's stopping law is

    E_(q,j)(μ)=q_j δ_0+(1−q_j) Shift(μ).

This affine map commutes with that player's mixture. Consequently q::τ̄
is exactly the independent mixture of the complete HEAD-law portfolios
E_(q,j)(τ_j^a). Full prescribed payoff is multilinear in these laws, and
every fixed pure-time response payoff is multilinear in its opponents.

Write H_i(a)=max(Q_i,A_i+α_i B_i(τ^a)), the full head cap, and define

    κ_i^q = Σ_a w(a) H_i(a) − B_i(q::τ̄) ≥ 0.

Nonnegativity follows by moving the supremum over a COMMON complete
response outside the average. More exactly, if V_i^q(s,a) is the payoff
of the full pure-time response s against (q::τ^a)_−i, then

    κ_i^q = inf_s Σ_a w(a)[H_i(a)−V_i^q(s,a)].

The root-zero response has value Q_i. Every later response, including
Never, has value A_i+α_i V_i(t,τ^a_−i) for the corresponding tail time t.
Thus these are the unrestricted head caps, not only a chosen finite
response menu. Exact linearity of the prescribed payoff now gives

    Ψ_q(τ̄) = Σ_a w(a) Ψ_q(τ^a) − Σ_i κ_i^q.       (5)

Here Ψ_q(τ) abbreviates Ψ_q(U(τ),B(τ)). Formula (5) is precisely the
prior note's cap-Jensen identity applied AFTER the fixed-root embedding.
