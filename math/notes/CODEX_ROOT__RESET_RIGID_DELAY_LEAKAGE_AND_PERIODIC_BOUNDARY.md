# Reset-rigid delay, leakage, and periodic boundary

## Status

## Exact useful content

At a reset-rigid minimum point, equal total debt and annihilation of the reset
owner's debt force exact aggregate transfer of that debt to the other players.
The usual owner premium and aggregate transfer inequality therefore contain no
additional slack.

Two exact regressions delimit what can be recovered from terminal semantic and
law data alone.

1. Prepending any finite deterministic all-Continue delay to a profile leaves
   its terminal law and prescribed payoff unchanged, while player (i)'s cap
   becomes
   \[
   \max\{r_i(\{i\}),B_i(\sigma)\}.
   \]
   At a positive-minimum point the strict singleton moat makes this the same
   cap. Thus the same displayed pair and law can hide the relevant response at
   arbitrarily late dates. This is a representation-level no-go; the delayed
   realizers need not refine a retained literal source chronology.

2. A finite two-date construction gives two profiles with the same initial
   semantic pair and law and the same returned terminal law after the reset
   owner's response, but with arbitrarily different caps for a nonmover. Thus
   source/target payoff-law data plus the mover's exact response identity do
   not control cross-coordinate cap leakage. The example has zero global
   minimum debt and is not a reset-rigid counterexample.

There is also a correct supplied-block consumer. For a finite exact
Nash--Bellman word, let (c) be joint survival through the word and (c_{-i})
opponent-deleted survival for player (i). If (b^0,b^L) are its endpoint
caps, periodic repetition has complete behavioral cap error bounded by

\[
 \frac{c_{-i}}{1-c_{-i}}|b_i^0-b_i^L|
 +
 \frac{c}{1-c}|b_i^0-b_i^L|.
\]

Hence a family whose endpoint cap mismatch is small relative to both joint
and opponent-deleted absorption yields terminal approximants, and therefore a
uniform-equilibrium payoff through the existing compiler. This overlaps the
known finite-word cap-friction and periodic-return estimates.

## Remaining producer

either:

- a literal source-matched finite block whose cap endpoint displacement is
  negligible relative to joint and every relevant opponent-deleted
  absorption; or
- a source-faithful response/regeneration theorem that preserves previously
  zero debt coordinates.

The reviewed material therefore proves the precise negative implication

\[
 \text{reset-rigid numerical and law data alone}
 \not\Longrightarrow
 \text{a cap-controlled temporal return}.
\]
