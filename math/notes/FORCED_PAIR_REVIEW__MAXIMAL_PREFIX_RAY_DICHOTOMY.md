# Maximal exact prefixes of a forced pair form one scalar ray

Author: `FORCED_PAIR_REVIEW`

## Current status

Ordinary mathematics, with most scalar identities already checked in the
generic maximal-cap-prefix development.  The new specialization is the
tail-independent pure-pair family and its exact consequence for the remaining
Fin4 whole-source-return seam.

The result closes the maximal-prefix route if its scalar debt limit is the
global minimum.  If the limit is strictly larger, it gives an exact no-go for
continuing to use this maximal-prefix architecture: normalized debt and
positive-debt support never change, and the total charge available after a
deep restart tends to zero.  The retained pair defect remains uniformly paid,
but it is a horizontal marked-row update, not an operation inside the exact
prefix ray.

Thus maximal prefixing has no third hidden output.  It reduces the remaining
producer to eliminating one quantitatively explicit pair-marked ray stall by
a genuinely non-prefix operation.

## 1. Question

Fix a Fin4 positive-minimum source with

\[
D_* >0.
\]

Suppose the forced-pair reduction has supplied fixed distinct labels \(j,o\),
a fixed pair \(C=\{j,o\}\), actual tails \(\sigma_n\) with

\[
D(\operatorname{Sem}(\sigma_n))\longrightarrow D_*,
\tag{1}
\]

and a fixed player \(p\ne o\) whose pure-pair root defect is

\[
\delta_p\ge \delta_0>0.
\tag{2}
\]

The forced-pair packet also supplies that the marked owner `o` has zero
coordinate root defect at the pure pair.  These two incentive fields are part
of the source data; they do not follow from arbitrary tails and labels alone.

In the reviewed forced-pair packet one may take

\[
\delta_0=\lambda D_*/6.
\tag{3}
\]

Start an actual profile \(P_n^0\) at date zero with exactly \(C\) Quitting
surely and attach \(\sigma_n\) after the counterfactual all-Continue outcome.
Recursively prefix a maximal-absorption exact cap--Nash root:

\[
P_n^{k+1}=q_k\triangleright P_n^k.
\tag{4}
\]

Does this produce whole-source return to \(D_*\), or can the retained pair
defect consume an off-minimum maximal-root stall?

## 2. Pure-pair semantics is exactly tail-independent

For every tail \(\sigma\), the semantic pair of the date-zero pure-pair
profile is one fixed table-level pair \(Z_C=(U^C,B^C)\).  Explicitly,

\[
U_i^C=r_i(C),
\tag{5}
\]

and

\[
B_i^C=
\begin{cases}
\max\{r_i(C),r_i(C\setminus\{i\})\},&i\in C,\\
\max\{r_i(C),r_i(C\cup\{i\})\},&i\notin C.
\end{cases}
\tag{6}
\]

Indeed, after any unilateral behavioral deviation at least one member of
\(C\) still Quits surely at date zero.  The tail is unreachable under
prescribed play and under every unilateral deviation, including Never,
arbitrarily late stopping, and privately randomized or history-dependent
behavior.  Only the deviator's date-zero membership in \(C\) can matter.

Consequently all \(P_n^0\) have the same complete terminal-semantic pair
\(Z_C\), even though their literal counterfactual tails are different.  Put

\[
D_0:=D(Z_C).
\tag{7}
\]

Global positive minimality gives \(D_0\ge D_*>0\).

The canonical maximal-root selector depends only on the current semantic cap.
It follows inductively that the roots \(q_k\) and the semantic pairs

\[
Z_k:=\operatorname{Sem}(P_n^k)
\tag{8}
\]

are independent of \(n\).  The actual profiles retain their different tails,
but the entire maximal-prefix semantic orbit is one table-and-pair object.

## 3. Exact ray identities

Write

\[
c_k:=\Pr_{q_k}(\text{all Continue}),
\qquad
a_k:=1-c_k,
\qquad
\alpha_k:=\prod_{h<k}c_h.
\tag{9}
\]

Every \(q_k\) is exact Nash against the cap of \(Z_k\).  Exact playerwise
cap-prefix debt scaling gives, for every player \(i\),

\[
d_i(Z_k)=\alpha_k d_i(Z_C).
\tag{10}
\]

Summing,

\[
D_k:=D(Z_k)=\alpha_kD_0.
\tag{11}
\]

Positive global minimality implies

\[
\alpha_k\ge D_*/D_0>0.
\tag{12}
\]

Therefore positive-debt support and normalized debt are exactly invariant:

\[
\operatorname{supp}^+d(Z_k)=\operatorname{supp}^+d(Z_C),
\qquad
\frac{d_i(Z_k)}{D_k}=\frac{d_i(Z_C)}{D_0}.
\tag{13}
\]

The pure pair occurs at shifted date \(k\).  Its stage mass is

\[
m_k=\alpha_k,
\tag{14}
\]

because its conditional root mass is one.  Its local root and literal
post-pair tail are unchanged.  Hence the same best-endpoint update of \(p\)
has actual whole-profile gain

\[
g_k=m_k\delta_p
    =\alpha_k\delta_p
    =\frac{D_k}{D_0}\delta_p
    \ge \frac{D_*}{D_0}\delta_0>0.
\tag{15}
\]

Thus debt, pair mass, and the paid pair edge share the same scalar survival
factor.  Maximal prefixing neither loses the causal packet nor turns it into a
new strategic direction.

## 4. The scalar limit dichotomy

The sequence \(D_k\) decreases and is bounded below by \(D_*\).  Let

\[
L:=\lim_kD_k\ge D_*.
\tag{16}
\]

This one scalar, which depends only on the reward table and pair \(C\), gives
the exhaustive answer.

### 4.1 Return arm: \(L=D_*\)

Choose the actual profile \(P_n^n\): attach the \(n\)-th retained tail
\(\sigma_n\) behind the pure pair and place the first \(n\) maximal exact
roots above it.  Then

\[
D(\operatorname{Sem}(P_n^n))=D_n\longrightarrow D_*;
\tag{17}
\]

\[
\Pr_{P_n^n}(C\text{ at date }n)
=\alpha_n\longrightarrow D_*/D_0>0;
\tag{18}
\]

the post-pair spine is literally \(\sigma_n\); the marked owner defect remains
zero; and the fixed paid mover gain satisfies

\[
g_n\ge (D_*/D_0)\delta_0.
\tag{19}
\]

This supplies the formerly missing whole-source-return field `(SR)` while
retaining the fixed pair atom and the minimum-return tail.  It therefore feeds
the checked `ConcentratedCollisionFourRole` packet compiler.  The compiler's
fixed positive tail-escape alternative is eventually impossible by (1), so
the checked three-role transfer/limit-chord output is obtained.

Explicitly, take the diagonal packet

```text
profiles n := P_n^n
subseq      := id
mark n      := n
cutoff n    := n + 1
scale n     := 1 / (n + 1)
resolution := D_* / D_0.
```

The semantic-prefix equation is the literal decomposition at the pure-pair
row; its all-Continue spine is `sigma_n`; pair mass is at least the displayed
resolution; the normalized marked-owner defect is identically zero; and the
whole source debt is (17).  The preselected `p` is a paid mover.  The compiler
selects any distinct debt recipient only after its near-minimum transfer
argument.

This is a genuine closure of one arm, not a debt-only near-return claim.

### 4.2 Ray-stall arm: \(L>D_*\)

Put \(\eta=(L-D_*)/2>0\).  Every actual profile generated by this maximal
prefix orbit satisfies

\[
D(Z_k)\ge L>D_*+\eta.
\tag{20}
\]

Therefore no choice of finite horizons in this orbit can supply `(SR)`.
Equations (13) show more: no finite prefix changes positive-debt support or
normalized debt, so the architecture cannot produce support-rank descent.

The exact one-step telescope is

\[
D_ka_k=D_k-D_{k+1}.
\tag{21}
\]

Hence

\[
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}D_ka_k=D_0-L,
\tag{22}
\]

and, since \(D_k\ge D_*\),

\[
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}a_k
\le\frac{D_0-L}{D_*}<\infty.
\tag{23}
\]

In particular

\[
a_k\to0,
\qquad
\sup_{H\ge N}\sum_{k=N}^{H}a_k\longrightarrow0.
\tag{24}
\]

Every finite maximal-prefix segment is a checked exact punishment-floor
prefix, and its charge is the corresponding sum of \(a_k\).  Equation (24)
says that an arbitrarily deep restart of this same architecture has no fixed
positive renewable charge.  The total historical budget is finite and has
already been paid by the scalar debt drop.

At the same time, (14)--(15) give the nonvanishing limits

\[
m_k\longrightarrow L/D_0>0,
\qquad
g_k\longrightarrow (L/D_0)\delta_p>0.
\tag{25}
\]

Thus the stall is quantitative: fresh exact cap roots become inert while a
literal pair atom and paid same-stage endpoint remain uniformly present ever
deeper in the suffix.

## 5. Compact stall normal form

Take a convergent subsequence of the joint terminal semantic/law points of
\(P_n^k\) along any cofinal diagonal in the stall arm.  Exact atom transport
and (25) give a limiting law with pair coordinate at least \(L/D_0>0\).
The maximal-root absorption tends to zero, so the roots tend coordinatewise
to all Continue.  Closedness of the endpoint-Nash graph gives

\[
\text{all Continue is exact Nash against the limiting cap}.
\tag{26}
\]

The limiting semantic debt is \(L>D_*\).  Maximality need not pass to the
limit: the exact correspondence there may be uniquely all Continue, or may
acquire a positive-absorption root by support entry.  This is precisely the
checked changed-state residual in
`exists_offMinimum_retainedLaw_allContinue_or_supportEntry`.

The retained pair defect does not contradict (26).  It is computed at the
shifted pure pair against its prescribed continuation.  Equation (26) is a
Nash statement at the fresh outer root against the current unrestricted cap.
Identifying these two rows would conflate prescribed payoff with cap and a
suffix atom with fresh root absorption.

## 6. Exact architectural no-go in the stall arm

The allowed operation in the proposed architecture is: choose another exact
cap--Nash root at the current outer cap and prefix it.  Within the canonical
maximal choice, every such continuation remains on the scalar ray (10)--(13).
Consequently, when \(L>D_*\), further maximal prefixing can produce none of:

1. whole-source debt convergence to \(D_*\);
2. strict positive-debt-support descent;
3. a fixed positive charge renewable after arbitrarily deep restarts; or
4. conversion of the retained pair edge into a fresh exact cap-root charge.

Items 1--3 are direct consequences of (20), (13), and (24).  For item 4,
the pair edge is a unilateral replacement at the shifted absorbing row.  It
is not one of the outer roots selected against the current cap.  Applying it
changes the suffix semantic pair and generally invalidates the cap--Nash
certificate of the copied outer word.  Recomputing a new exact word after
that horizontal update is a new architecture, not another step on this ray.

Thus the fixed pair defect cannot consume the ray stall **using exact maximal
prefixing alone**.  It remains valuable data for a new cross-coordinate
repair, but it is not hidden absorption or capacity in the maximal-root word.

This is stronger than merely naming descent versus stall: the stall's entire
finite orbit, debt rank, marked mass, paid gain, and remaining capacity are
computed exactly.  Any successful next theorem must leave the ray by changing
the marked past/root and then reestablishing cap exactness, or must consume the
off-minimum joint limit through its support-entry branch.

## 7. Exact local regression

The pointwise inert possibility is real even with the forced-pair packet's
zero marked-owner defect.  Put `C={0,1}`.  For players `0,2,3`, define

\[
r_i(S)=
\begin{cases}
2,&i\notin S,\\
1,&S=\{i\},\\
0,&i\in S\text{ and }|S|\ge2.
\end{cases}
\tag{27}
\]

For player `1`, define

\[
r_1(S)=
\begin{cases}
1,&S=\{1\},\\
2,&\{0,1\}\subseteq S,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\tag{28}
\]

At the pure pair,

\[
U=(0,2,2,2),\qquad B=(2,2,2,2),\qquad d=(2,0,0,0).
\]

Thus player `1` is a zero-defect marked owner and player `0` has a fixed
positive leave gain.  All Continue is the unique exact cap root: Continue is
strictly dominant for players `0,2,3`, and once player `0` Continues, player
`1` receives `2` from Continue and `1` from Quit.  Hence the maximal-prefix
orbit is exactly inert and the paid pair edge shifts without loss.

The pure singleton `{1}` is a terminal Nash profile, so the global minimum is
zero.  This is not a counterexample to the quitting-game conjecture.  It tests
all local pair, owner, paid-row, and ray fields and proves that positive global
minimum provenance must be used through an operation not present in the scalar
prefix ray.

## 9. Scope and next question

No uniform-equilibrium payoff, terminal approximation, cumulative near-return,
or support descent is claimed in the ray-stall arm.  The local regression has
global minimum zero and is used only to test pointwise implications.

The next legitimate question is narrower than whole-source return:

> Starting from the quantitative ray stall (20), (24), and (25), can one
> apply one marked pair endpoint update and re-exactify the changed suffix
> while controlling the other coordinates' cap leakage, so that the new ray
> either reaches \(D_*\) or strictly lowers a finite source rank?

That re-exactification step, not further maximal prefixing of the unchanged
pair source, is the remaining producer.
