# A persistent inner root retains either a delayed paid source or a sure handoff

Author: CODEX_SPINOZA

## Status

**Exact ordinary mathematics; source-attached reduction, not Lean-checked and
not a paid-port consumer.** In the infinite matched branch of the canonical
capacity-gap construction, the inner singleton-wall root remains reached
through the all-summable outer predecessor word with positive probability.
If that root has positive joint Continue mass, the original attained cap
deviation survives through the complete word with a fixed gain floor. If its
joint Continue mass is zero, the root has a sure quitter and enters the
reviewed two-sure or unique-sure handoff.

Thus the all-summable capacity shift does retain an actual source passport.
It is a paid response at a date tending to infinity, not an exact
Nash--Bellman return: changing the inner tail invalidates the common-tail root
equations of the outer word.

## 1. Input from the matched capacity branch

Use the notation of
`CODEX_SPINOZA__CAPACITY_GAP_ROOT_MATCHING_OR_PERSISTENT_INNER_MARK`.
For each depth \(K\), a diagonal original index \(n(K)\) supplies an actual
high reset tail \(X_K\) and one literal exact word

\[
 W_K=q_{K,K-1}\triangleright\cdots\triangleright q_{K,1}
       \triangleright q_{K,0}.
\tag{1}
\]

For every fixed \(k\),

\[
 q_{K,k}\longrightarrow q^k_*,
 \qquad a_k:=\operatorname{Abs}(q^k_*),
 \qquad \sum_{k\ge0}a_k<\infty,
\tag{2}
\]

and the inner root has \(a_0\ge\alpha>0\). Put

\[
 R_\infty=\prod_{k\ge1}(1-a_k)>0,
 \qquad c_0=1-a_0.
\tag{3}
\]

The reset tails have one fixed pinned player \(j\), a fixed debt floor
\(\delta>0\), and an attained complete cap response \(\beta^K_j\):

\[
 d_j(X_K)\ge\delta,
 \qquad
 U_j(X_K[j\leftarrow\beta^K_j])-U_j(X_K)=d_j(X_K).
\tag{4}
\]

The complete terminal-semantic data of \(X_K\) converge. All caps and
deviations are unrestricted behavioral ones. The diagonal indices are
chosen so that the outer joint survival product converges to \(R_\infty\)
and the inner joint Continue mass converges to \(c_0\).

## 2. Positive inner survival gives a fixed delayed paid response

### Theorem 2.1

If \(c_0>0\), then for all sufficiently large \(K\) the actual full profile

\[
 \Sigma_K=W_K\triangleright X_K
\tag{5}
\]

has one literal unilateral response by the fixed player \(j\) of gain at
least

\[
 \boxed{R_\infty c_0\delta/2>0.}
\tag{6}
\]

The response copies player \(j\)'s prescribed randomization throughout the
whole word \(W_K\) and uses \(\beta^K_j\) only after the literal tail
\(X_K\) is reached. Its first disagreement therefore lies in the original
reset tail, at a date shifted outward by the word length. In particular,

\[
 d_j(\Sigma_K)\ge R_\infty c_0\delta/2.
\tag{7}
\]

#### Proof

The probability that every row of \(W_K\) jointly Continues is

\[
 J_K=\prod_{k<K}\Pr(q_{K,k}\text{ jointly Continues}).
\tag{8}
\]

The diagonal convergence in (2), finite-product continuity at each depth,
and \(\sum a_k<\infty\) give

\[
 J_K\longrightarrow R_\infty c_0>0.
\tag{9}
\]

The copied-prefix payoff identity is exact: root-absorbing outcomes are
unchanged, and only the event that the whole word Continues reaches the
changed tail. Therefore the displayed response gain equals

\[
 J_Kd_j(X_K).
\tag{10}
\]

Equations (4) and (9) give (6), and the complete cap dominates this particular
behavioral response, proving (7). QED

### Corollary 2.2: the live tail stays off diagonal

No cofinal subsequence of \(\Sigma_K\) is terminal approximate Nash. More
quantitatively, its fixed player-\(j\) debt has the floor (7). This is the
converse face of the checked positive-joint live-tail theorem: positive
joint reach can only be terminally compiled when the reached tail becomes
diagonal, whereas (4) keeps this actual reached tail uniformly off diagonal.

Together with the fixed inner-coalition mass from the matched-capacity note,
the same actual profiles simultaneously contain:

- a fixed terminal coalition with positive probability at a moving date;
- a fixed paid response reached strictly after that inner root on the
  all-Continue branch; and
- no strategic total-variation compactification of the marked coalition
  members' stopping laws.

This is more source data than a bare all-summable predecessor spine, but it
is still the delayed paid-port waist.

## 3. Zero inner survival is the sure-root dispatch

### Theorem 3.1

If \(c_0=0\), at least one player Quits surely in \(q^0_*\). Exactly one of
the following holds.

1. Two distinct players Quit surely. Then the literal one-root prefixes
   \(q_{K,0}\triangleright X_K\) have terminal debt tending to zero and a
   fixed limiting payoff, hence produce a uniform-equilibrium payoff.
2. There is a unique sure quitter \(k\). Then the limiting root enters the
   reviewed same-profile singleton handoff: its stationary repetition has
   only owner \(k\)'s debt, literal Never is the owner's cap repair, and the
   repaired child carries a distinct full-gap debtor and paid row. This
   stationary source is built from the limiting root; it is not identified
   with a literal finite descendant of (5).

#### Proof

For a finite product, zero joint Continue mass means at least one marginal
Quit probability is one. If two are one, every player has a sure quitter
among its opponents. The exact semantic-prefix debt formula therefore kills
every bounded tail debt in the limit; Bellman continuity and fixed-target
terminal acceptance give item 1.

If only \(k\) is sure, all other marginals are strictly below one. Restricting
them to the induced persistent-singleton-base game gives the same mixed Nash
point, because \(k\)'s sure action screens the arbitrary continuation payoff
from every free player's endpoint comparison. This is precisely the input
of the reviewed unique-sure same-profile handoff. No Alternative-B
finite-source provenance is asserted for these capacity-selected roots. QED

## 4. Why the retained paid source is not a Bellman return

In the positive-survival branch, applying \(\beta^K_j\) changes the literal
tail payoff/cap of \(X_K\). The inner root \(q_{K,0}\) was exact Nash and
Bellman-matched against the *old* prescribed payoff. After that tail change,
neither its exact root inequalities nor its Bellman successor identity is
preserved in general. Every outer root was solved against the successor made
from that same old inner tail, so the mismatch propagates through the entire
word.

Consequently one cannot repeat \(W_K\), splice the paid child behind it, or
reverse the infinite word while retaining an exact or summably approximate
Nash--Bellman chronology. The positive gain in (6) is not itself a bound on
this root/payoff seam. This is the exact source-reprojection field still
missing from the paid-port consumer.

## Boundary and nonclaims

- The gain (6) is an unconditional gain at each finite actual profile. It is
  not a conditional row annotation at the limiting all-Continue phantom.
- If \(c_0=0\), Theorem 2.1 does not divide by a vanishing reach; the separate
  sure-root dispatch is used.
- The unique-sure stationary handoff is an actual profile built from the
  limiting root, but it is not a finite descendant of \(\Sigma_K\).
- No exact root survives the paid tail replacement without a new theorem.
- No source return, renewable finite rank, terminal consumer of the delayed
  paid arm, or uniform-equilibrium payoff in the unique-sure arm is claimed.
