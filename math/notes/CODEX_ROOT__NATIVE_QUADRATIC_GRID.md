# A finite computation for the AllNever obstruction

The [quadratic cost theorem](../exports/QUADRATIC_COST_AND_ALLNEVER_SOURCE_EXCLUSION.md) defines the coefficient used below. The [exact rational implementation](../experiments/CODEX_ROOT__NATIVE_QUADRATIC_GRID.py) also computes the example in the proof.

Keep the definitions of the quadratic theorem. Let κ₊(w) be its same infimum and limit with the additional constraint Γz≥−a at tolerance a, where z is the vector of total owner masses of the unit word. It is finite because a diffuse word in the fixed direction y with Γy≫0 is feasible at every positive tolerance by choosing it sufficiently long. Always κ₊≥κ. A native positive-gap AllNever-only table requires equality for every strictly positive w: the native optimized words produced above achieve κ in the limit with Γz≥0 at their limiting total. This condition can be tested across every time ordering and every word length with a certified, computable accuracy. In particular any strict gap at any fixed weight is detected at a finite resolution. By bounded slopes in w, a strict gap at any real positive weight is also detected at some rational positive weight. This does not say that equality implies existence or nonexistence of the game-theoretic source.

Let M≥1 bound the entries of Γ,J in absolute value, let W=∑wᵢ, and fix y∈Δ with h=minᵢ(Γy)ᵢ>0. Let N≥1 and ε_N=(1+16M)/N. For the free endpoint and the constrained endpoint run this same directed acyclic shortest-path recursion. States are n∈ℕ⁴ with sum n≤N, starting at 0. A nonzero increment k∈ℕ⁴ carries n−k to n whenever 0≤k≤n coordinatewise and

    Γ n−J k≥−N ε_N               coordinatewise.

Its cost is N⁻² ∑ᵢ wᵢ kᵢ(Γ n−J k)ᵢ. At sum n=N take the least path cost over all states for b_N, or only over Γ n≥−N ε_N for b_N⁺. The direction of construction is backwards in the physical calendar: n/N is the tail including the newly prepended k/N. Zero rows may be removed, because doing so leaves all costs and all residuals at retained dates unchanged. Conversely every path implements the indicated finite unit word on consecutive dates. Thus negative edge costs cause no difficulty and every allowed predecessor is processed before its successor. There are at most binom(N+4,4) states and binom(N+8,8) predecessor pairs, by representing each pair as two nonnegative four-vectors (n−k,k) of total at most N. The computation uses rational arithmetic for rational input.

For ε_N≤h, the following two-sided bounds apply with the same constants to (b_N,κ) and (b_N⁺,κ₊), written here as (b,v):

    b−21MW/N ≤ v ≤ b+7MW ε_N/h.                                  (G)

In particular there is a polynomial-time algorithm in N for fixed player count, with error O(1/N) independent of all higher-coalition rewards and of every candidate word's length. The following argument proves the stated constants.

Take any finite unit word x and its four cumulative masses Fᵢ(t)=∑_{s≤t}xᵢ(s). Round each NFᵢ(t) to floor(NFᵢ(t)+θᵢ), using a fixed 0≤θᵢ<1 per owner. At its last date choose exactly N−∑ᵢ floor(Nzᵢ) owners with nonzero remainder to be rounded up, taking θᵢ=1−frac(Nzᵢ) for these and zero for the others. This makes the new total exactly N and all cumulative discrepancies in units of physical mass at most 1/N. Every new atom discrepancy is at most 2/N in each component, as is every tail discrepancy. Thus residuals g change by at most 16M/N at every old date. Γz changes by at most 4M/N per component. A rounded zero row can be discarded. For the cost discrepancy for each owner, changing g in its integral against the new nonnegative marginal costs at most 16M/N. For the remaining term ∑(xᵢ'−xᵢ)gᵢ, use summation by parts against the cumulative discrepancies. The bound is (sup|gᵢ|+TV(gᵢ))/N≤5M/N: the tail term ΓR has variation at most M, and the atom term Jx has variation at most 2M because ∑_{i,t}xᵢ(t)=1. We obtain the total weighted bound 21MW/N. In defining v choose an arbitrarily near-optimal word of tolerance at most 1/N (and Γz≥−1/N in the constrained case). The rounded word is admitted by the recursion, proving the left inequality in (G). There is no assumption of a minimizing word of any length.

Conversely let a unit word be allowed at tolerance a=ε_N. Put s=a/h≤1. Append the geometric tail s(1−r)rⁿ y for n=0,1,…, with r sufficiently close to one so (Γ−(1−r)J)y≥0, then renormalize by 1+s. Every old guard gains sΓy and every tail guard is nonnegative before normalization. In the endpoint-constrained case the total passive vector Γz gains the same sΓy. Hence the repaired unit word is exactly feasible in the respective infinite-word problem. Finite truncations and normalization approach its cost and satisfy both constraints to vanishing tolerance, so its cost is an upper bound for v. Before normalization the old cost changes by at most MWs in the old rows and the tail contributes at most 2MWs². Scaling the old cost of magnitude at most 2MW by (1+s)⁻² changes it by at most 4MWs. The total cost difference is at most 7MWs. This proves (G).
