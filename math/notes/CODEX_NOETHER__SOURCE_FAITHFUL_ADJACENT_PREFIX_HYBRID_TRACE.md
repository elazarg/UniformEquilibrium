## 1. Exact question

Fix a finite quitting game on `Fin 4`, a reward bound

\[
 |r_i(S)|\le R,
\]

and a positive global minimum debt `D_*>0`.  Consider one equality-arm
minimum response endpoint sequence

\[
 E_n=B_n[o\leftarrow Q_{q_n}],
 \qquad q_n\in\mathbb N\cup\{\infty\},                 \tag{1.1}
\]

for one fixed old observer `o`.  Thus `E_n` literally prescribes the pure-time
response `Q_{q_n}` to `o`.  Assume the response endpoint is regenerated by the
source-faithful construction, so its causal chronology uses the literal
profiles `E_n`, rather than a newly selected realizing sequence.

Let `W_n` be the resulting exact cap--Nash word and put

\[
 P_n=W_n*E_n,
 \qquad a_n=|W_n|,
 \qquad \widehat q_n=\operatorname{shift}_{a_n}(q_n). \tag{1.2}
\]

The source-faithful theorem gives

\[
 c(W_n)\longrightarrow1                                \tag{1.3}
\]

and hence the installed-response estimate

\[
 \Phi_o(P_n,\widehat q_n):=
 U_o(P_n[o\leftarrow Q_{\widehat q_n}])-U_o(P_n)
 \longrightarrow0.                                    \tag{1.4}
\]

Follow the actual singleton branch through:

1. anchored owner compression on the retained chronology;
2. cross-tail closure;
3. pure singleton normalization;
4. the fixed hard-residual outsider join, producing a pure pair `F_n` at a
   marked date `t_n` with a fixed mass floor `lambda>0`;
5. normalized-passport actualization by an arbitrary product-root word `R_n`;
6. live-root-idempotent pure-pair normalization; and
7. the final endpoint-origin mover update, producing `H_n`.

The question is whether the old response can be carried to `H_n`, or whether
its first failure can be turned into actual paid data with a uniform floor.

## 3. The pre-passport part preserves the old installation or is already paid

Let the singleton owner be `j`, and let `f != j` be the fixed forced outsider.
The forced singleton-to-pair update has the checked actual payoff floor

\[
 g_{\mathrm{join}}
 :=\lambda\,\gamma>0,                                 \tag{3.1}
\]

where `gamma` is the hard-residual terminal gap.

If `o=f`, this update is already a source-attached paid own-strategy edge for
the old observer, and there is nothing to install farther.

Assume henceforth that `o != f`.  Then

\[
 \boxed{\Phi_o(F_n,\widehat q_n)\longrightarrow0.}     \tag{3.2}
\]

Here `F_n` is the literal pure-pair profile after owner compression,
cross-tail closure, singleton pureification, and the forced outsider join.

### Proof of (3.2)

The argument is about actual live histories, not raw off-live strategy
values.

If `o=j`, positive anchored singleton mass and the fact that the supplied
suffix literally prescribes `Q_{q_n}` imply that the least supported owner
date selected by anchored compression is exactly `widehat q_n`.  `Never`
cannot occur in this subcase, because a Never owner has zero singleton mass.
Thus owner compression and singleton pureification reapply the already
prescribed Quit action of `o` at that date.

If `o!=j`, positive singleton mass at the selected date implies that the pure
clock `Q_{widehat q_n}` Continues there.  Otherwise `o` would quit before or
simultaneously with `j`, and the singleton `{j}` would have zero mass.
Therefore singleton pureification re-applies the old response's Continue
action to `o`.

In either case the final pair `{j,f}` contains a sure quitter other than `o`,
unless `o=j`, in which case `o` itself follows exactly `Q_{widehat q_n}` at
the pair date.  Hence both the prescribed profile and the old-response
counterfactual absorb no later than the marked pair row.  The restarted tail
of the cross-tail closure is inaccessible to this comparison.

Before the source-faithful word ends, the two profiles can differ; after the
word and through the marked row their `o` actions agree.  Coupling the prefix
therefore gives the same bound as at the source-faithful entrance:

\[
 |\Phi_o(F_n,\widehat q_n)|
 \le 2R(1-c(W_n))\longrightarrow0.                    \tag{3.3}
\]

This also explains why the cross-tail restart is not the first genuine
response seam.  It is nonlocal as a raw profile operation, but the named
comparison is screened before its restarted tail can matter.

## 4. Four marginal prefix hybrids

Let

\[
 R_n=(r_{n,0},\ldots,r_{n,L_n-1})
\]

be the arbitrary actualizer word, where `L_n=|R_n|`.  This length has no
uniform bound.  Let `C^{L_n}` be the all-Continue word of the same length and
define the twice-shifted old response

\[
 \overline q_n
 :=\operatorname{shift}_{L_n}(\widehat q_n)
 =\operatorname{shift}_{a_n+L_n}(q_n).                \tag{4.1}
\]

The equality includes `Never`, since every finite shift fixes `Never`.

Choose once and for all an ordering

\[
 i_1,i_2,i_3,i_4
\]

of `Fin 4`.  For `s=0,...,4`, define a hybrid word `R_n^s` by using player
`i_h`'s complete marginal root word from `R_n` when `h<=s`, and using
all-Continue for every other player.  Put

\[
 Z_{n,s}:=R_n^s*F_n.                                  \tag{4.2}
\]

Then:

1. `Z_{n,0}=C^{L_n}*F_n` is the literal delay of `F_n`;
2. `Z_{n,4}=R_n*F_n` is the actualizer's raw profile;
3. `Z_{n,s+1}` differs from `Z_{n,s}` by one player's complete behavioral
   strategy and no opponent coordinate of that player changes;
4. every hybrid has the same pure pair at the shifted mark
   `L_n+t_n`; and
5. every hybrid has the same complete post-mark all-Continue spine.

Most importantly, every hybrid retains the actualizer's fixed marked-mass
floor.  Write `c_n^s` for the joint survival of `R_n^s`.  Each omitted
Continue probability is replaced by one, so

\[
 c_n^s\ge c_n^4.                                      \tag{4.3}
\]

If `m_n` is the marked-pair mass in `F_n`, exact literal-prefix transport
gives

\[
 \Pr_{Z_{n,s}}(\text{marked pair at }L_n+t_n)
 =c_n^s m_n
 \ge c_n^4m_n
 \ge\rho,                                             \tag{4.4}
\]

where `rho=actualizer.resolution>0`.

Define

\[
 \phi_{n,s}:=
 U_o(Z_{n,s}[o\leftarrow Q_{\overline q_n}])
 -U_o(Z_{n,s}).                                       \tag{4.5}
\]

All-Continue delay is exact, so (3.2) gives

\[
 \phi_{n,0}\longrightarrow0.                         \tag{4.6}
\]

The unbounded temporal word has now been reduced to four literal unilateral
strategy edits while preserving its full product provenance, ties, Never,
marked mass, and tail.

 g_{\mathrm{final}}

## 6. Exact telescope and exhaustive trace output

Every `phi_{n,5}` lies in `[-2R,2R]`.  Pass to a subsequence on which

\[
 \phi_{n,5}\longrightarrow\ell.                      \tag{6.1}
\]

There are three cases.

### Installed arm: `ell=0`

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 |U_o(H_n[o\leftarrow Q_{\overline q_n}])-U_o(H_n)|
 \longrightarrow0.}                                  \tag{6.2}
\]

The old response is installed at the next endpoint with the exact combined
clock offset (4.1).

### Direct paid-response arm: `ell>0`

Eventually

\[
 U_o(H_n[o\leftarrow Q_{\overline q_n}])-U_o(H_n)
 \ge \ell/2.                                          \tag{6.3}
\]

This is a literal source-attached profitable unilateral behavioral deviation
at the next endpoint.  It covers finite clocks and `Never` without an
attainment assumption on the unrestricted cap.

### Negative-reentry arm: `ell<0`

For all large `n`, (4.6) gives

\[
 \phi_{n,0}-\phi_{n,5}\ge |\ell|/2.                  \tag{6.4}
\]

Telescoping through the five edits gives some `s in {0,...,4}` with

\[
 \phi_{n,s}-\phi_{n,s+1}\ge |\ell|/10.               \tag{6.5}
\]

There are only five positions and four player labels, so a further finite
subsequence fixes both.

If that step changes `o`, replacing `o` by the common response erases the
difference between the two response columns.  Hence (6.5) is exactly

\[
 U_o(Z_{n,s+1})-U_o(Z_{n,s})\ge |\ell|/10.            \tag{6.6}
\]

This is an actual paid own-strategy edge.

If the step changes `p!=o`, (6.5) is exactly the positive response square

\[
\begin{aligned}
 |\ell|/10\le{}&
 \bigl[U_o(Z_{n,s}[o\leftarrow Q_{\overline q_n}])
       -U_o(Z_{n,s})\bigr]\\
 &-\bigl[U_o(Z_{n,s+1}[o\leftarrow Q_{\overline q_n}])
       -U_o(Z_{n,s+1})\bigr].                         \tag{6.7}
\end{aligned}
\]

Orienting `Z_{n,s+1}` as the source and `Z_{n,s}` as the endpoint makes
(6.7) the usual positive four-corner convention.  The mover is the fixed
player `p`, the response is the fixed old observer `o`, and `p!=o`.

All four profiles in (6.7) are actual behavioral profiles.  No stationary,
finite-support, or best-response restriction is used.

### Terminal-label extraction

Let

\[
 N=|\{\text{terminal outcomes, including Never}\}|.
\]

Expanding (6.7) against the finite terminal outcome laws, the Never term has
reward zero.  Hence some nonempty coalition `A` satisfies the signed
four-law atom inequality

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \square\Pr(A):={}&
   \Pr_{Z_{n,s}[o\leftarrow Q_{\overline q_n}]}(A)
   -\Pr_{Z_{n,s}}(A)\\
 &-\Pr_{Z_{n,s+1}[o\leftarrow Q_{\overline q_n}]}(A)
   +\Pr_{Z_{n,s+1}}(A),
\end{aligned}
\]

and

\[
 \frac{|\ell|}{10N}
 \le r_o(A)\,
 \square\Pr(A).                                       \tag{6.8}
\]

A finite subsequence freezes `A`.  Thus the negative-reentry arm produces a
literal source-attached response rectangle with a fixed terminal label, not
merely an anonymous payoff difference.

The common role-touch floor can be recorded as

\[
 g_{\mathrm{role}}
 :=\min\left\{\lambda\gamma,
               \frac{\rho^2D_*}{8}\right\}>0.        \tag{6.9}
\]

and the compact nonzero-reentry branches have their own fixed floors
`ell/2` or `|ell|/10` after subsequence selection.

## 7. What this says about the actual constructor chain

There are two different answers depending on the meaning of “bounded literal
edit path.”

### One-date edits: no uniform bound

The actualizer stores

```text

```

with no length bound.  Indeed, all-Continue padding leaves every decoration
coordinate unchanged while increasing the literal word length arbitrarily.
Thus no bound on the number of one-date root edits can follow from the
normalized-passport fields.

### Unilateral complete-strategy edits: at most five

The four marginal hybrids (4.2), followed by the endpoint-origin move, give a
uniform bound of five strategically nontrivial edits.  The live-root
idempotent pure normalization can be omitted semantically or expanded into
four zero-effect one-date edits.  Hence the complete raw trace has at most
nine player edits, only five of which can change the response functional.

This is the strongest honest trace.  The arbitrary prefix is not a missing
reselection once the actualizer is retained: its exact root word, underlying
source rank, shifted mark, mass, gain, and post-mark spine are all public at
that level.  It becomes a provenance loss only when one projects to the
flattened endpoint-origin record.

## 8. Why this does not yet produce the existing minimum rectangle

The response-square arm (6.7)--(6.8) is source-faithful and retains:

* the old observer and pure-time label;
* one fixed mover distinct from the observer;
* one fixed positive square floor;
* one fixed terminal label;
* a common pure nonsingleton marked row of mass at least `rho`; and
* the exact common post-mark tail.

But a prefix-hybrid mover changes one player's complete finite-prefix
strategy, not only its Boolean action at the marked row.  More importantly,
the response profile `Z[o<-Q]` is not known to have vanishing observer debt or
to converge to the global minimum fiber.  The old response was cap-attaining
before the arbitrary passport prefix; that property does not automatically
survive the prefix hybrids.

Therefore (6.7) is not yet a
`FinFourMinimumResponseRectanglePacket`.  It is a nonlocal, source-attached
response rectangle carrying exactly the information which was previously
missing from the first-touch audit.  Its next consumer must either:

1. decode the fixed square atom without assuming target response debt;
2. prove that one of its two response columns reprojects to the same minimum
   fiber; or
3. turn the paid prefix participant into an admissible return or renewable
   support descent.

Claiming that the current response-chord compiler applies directly would
silently assume this remaining cap-attainment/minimum-fiber step.

## 9. Boundary regression

The installed arm alone is false, even while a marked pair survives with
fixed positive mass.

Take a profile whose delayed tail absorbs at a pure pair and let the old
observer prescribe Continue throughout the prefix.  At one new prefix date,
let only the observer Quit with probability `r in (0,1)`.  Give the observer
payoff one on its solo quit and zero on the later pair.  The old pure response
Continues, so after this prefix

\[
 \Phi=-r,
\]

while the later marked-pair mass is `1-r>0`.  The negative gap is exactly the
paid observer-prefix edit recovered by (6.6).

Embedding two passive zero-payoff players gives the same example on `Fin 4`.
Its global minimum is zero, so it is not a counterexample to a positive-minimum
theorem.  It is an exact regression against deleting the paid/square arms or
claiming that a positive marked-mass floor forces response installation.

All-Continue padding gives the complementary regression: word length can tend
to infinity while every semantic, law, mass, gain, and response-installation
quantity remains unchanged.  Thus temporal length itself is not the correct
obstruction; playerwise prefix effect is.
