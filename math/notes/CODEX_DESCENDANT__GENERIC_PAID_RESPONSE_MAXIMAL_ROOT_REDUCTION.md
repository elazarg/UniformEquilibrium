## 1. Input

Let \(P_n\) and \(Y_n\) be actual Fin4 behavioral profiles.  Suppose:

1. \(Y_n\) differs from \(P_n\) only in one fixed player's complete strategy,
   denoted \(p\);
2. \(D(P_n)\to D_*>0\), where \(D_*\) is the global minimum of terminal
   semantic debt;
3. for one fixed \(G>0\), eventually

   \[
   U_p(Y_n)-U_p(P_n)\ge G;
   \tag{1.1}
   \]

4. \(d_p(Y_n)\to0\); and
5. one Fin4 quantitative hard residual is retained.

The silently padded postmark paid splice supplies this input: choose response
tolerances tending to zero and reduce the limiting positive gain slightly to
obtain (1.1).

## 2. A fixed signed terminal atom is automatic

Let \(\eta_n\) and \(\xi_n\) be the terminal laws of \(Y_n\) and \(P_n\).
Since prescribed payoff is the reward moment of the terminal law and Never
has reward zero,

\[
 U_p(Y_n)-U_p(P_n)
 =\sum_{\varnothing\ne T\subseteq\operatorname{Fin}4}
   (\eta_n(T)-\xi_n(T))r_p(T).
 \tag{2.1}
\]

There are fifteen finite terminal labels.  Hence some \(T_n\) satisfies

\[
 (\eta_n(T_n)-\xi_n(T_n))r_p(T_n)\ge G/15.
 \tag{2.2}
\]

After a finite-label subsequence, one fixed nonempty coalition \(T\) works
at every rank.  The weaker denominator sixteen may be used to match the
existing rectangle interface:

\[
 (\eta_n(T)-\xi_n(T))r_p(T)\ge G/16.
 \tag{2.3}
\]

Joint compactness, without changing either actual sequence, gives a common
strict subsequence and joint limits

\[
 (Z(Y_n),\eta_n)\to z,
 \qquad
 (Z(P_n),\xi_n)\to q.
 \tag{2.4}
\]

Continuity gives

\[
 d_p(z)=0,
 \qquad D(q)=D_*,
 \tag{2.5}
\]

and the limiting signed atom still satisfies (2.3).

## 3. Literal maximal-root ledger

At the actual cap \(B(Y_n)\), choose an exact product root \(x_n\) of
maximum absorption.  Write

\[
 a_n=\Pr_{x_n}(\text{absorb}),
 \qquad s_n=1-a_n,
 \qquad D_n=D(Y_n).
 \tag{3.1}
\]

Prefix the same root to both profiles:

\[
 \widehat Y_n=x_n\star Y_n,
 \qquad
 \widehat P_n=x_n\star P_n.
 \tag{3.2}
\]

Only the first prefix is asserted exact cap--Nash.  Exact debt scaling and
global minimality give

\[
 D(\widehat Y_n)=s_nD_n,
 \qquad
 D_*\le s_nD_n,
 \qquad
 a_nD_n\le D_n-D_*.
 \tag{3.3}
\]

If \(|r_i(S)|\le M\), then every terminal payoff and cap lies in
\([-M,M]\), so \(D_n\le8M\).  Therefore

\[
 s_n\ge {D_*\over D_n}\ge {D_*\over8M}>0.
 \tag{3.4}
\]

The common prefix scales the signed suffix atom exactly:

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\bigl(\Pr_{\widehat Y_n}(T)-\Pr_{\widehat P_n}(T)\bigr)r_p(T)\\
 &\qquad=s_n(\eta_n(T)-\xi_n(T))r_p(T)
 \ge {GD_*\over128M}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{3.5}
\]

Also \(d_p(\widehat Y_n)=s_nd_p(Y_n)\to0\).

## 4. Exhaustive three-way reduction

After a strict subsequence, exactly one of the following ordered alternatives
is obtained.

### 4.1 Uniformly charged literal exact root

There exists \(\alpha>0\) such that

\[
 a_n\ge\alpha,
 \qquad
 a_nD_n\ge\alpha D_*>0.
 \tag{4.1}
\]

The literal prefix \(x_n\star Y_n\) retains the fixed signed atom with the
uniform bound (3.5).

This branch is automatically a quantitatively off-minimum actual paid port.
Indeed global minimality of the exact prefix gives

\[
 D(Y_n)\ge {D_*\over1-a_n}
 \ge {D_*\over1-\alpha},
 \tag{4.2}
\]

where necessarily \(\alpha<1\).  Hence

\[
 D(Y_n)-D_*\ge {\alpha D_*\over1-\alpha}.
 \tag{4.3}
\]

The literal edge \(P_n\to Y_n\), its gain \(G\), killed mover debt, and the
source ancestry are already supplied.  If the maintained paid-port interface
requires an outgoing response from the off-minimum profile rather than this
incoming edge, the hard terminal gap selects one directly at \(Y_n\), with a
fixed gain and a literal first-disagreement fork.  Thus the charged
alternative is not a fourth terminal component: it lies in the actual
off-minimum paid-port waist, with the extra exact absorbing root and
signed-law passport recorded here.

This arm has a further quantitative consequence which is absent from the
rectangle maximal-root record.  For any fixed product root \(x\), the
one-coordinate root Nash defect is one-Lipschitz in the corresponding tail
cap coordinate:

\[
 \left|\delta_i(b,x)-\delta_i(b',x)\right|
 \le |b_i-b'_i|.
 \tag{4.4}
\]

Indeed, the Quit endpoint is independent of the tail cap, while the Continue
endpoint changes by the opponent-all-Continue probability times
\(b_i-b'_i\).  The formula expressing \(\delta_i\) as the probabilities of
the two played actions times the positive parts of the two endpoint gaps,
together with one-Lipschitzness of positive part, proves (4.2).  Therefore,
because \(x_n\) is exact Nash against \(B(Y_n)\),

\[
 R(B(P_n),x_n)
 \le \sum_i |B_i(P_n)-B_i(Y_n)|,
 \tag{4.5}
\]

where \(R\) is total root cap defect.  The arbitrary-root minimum budget
applied to the near-minimum parent \(P_n\) gives

\[
 a_nD(P_n)
 \le D(P_n)-D_*+R(B(P_n),x_n).
 \tag{4.6}
\]

The mover's own cap is unchanged exactly, since \(P_n\) and \(Y_n\) differ
only in that player's strategy:

\[
 B_p(P_n)=B_p(Y_n).
 \tag{4.7}
\]

Consequently, on a subsequence with \(a_n\ge\alpha>0\), eventually

\[
 \sum_{i\ne p}|B_i(P_n)-B_i(Y_n)|\ge {\alpha D_*\over2}.
 \tag{4.8}
\]

After one finite-label and sign extraction there is a fixed nonmover
\(j\ne p\) and one orientation with

\[
 |B_j(P_n)-B_j(Y_n)|\ge {\alpha D_*\over6}.
 \tag{4.9}
\]

This is a fixed cross-coordinate cap-envelope displacement caused by the
same actual paid response.  Approximate the larger cap by one pure stopping
time.  In the corresponding orientation this gives one actual fixed-response
comparison between the two literal profiles, with payoff difference at least
\(\alpha D_*/12\).  Thus the charged arm is not an unstructured absorption
leaf: it yields a four-profile response chord carrying both the original
\(p\)-gain and an order-one effect on one fixed \(j\)-response.

This does not yet consume the arm.  The selected pure time can escape to
infinity, and the cross effect is an externality of changing \(p\)'s
strategy, not itself a profitable unilateral move by \(p\).  Moreover a cap
displacement need not be cap-switching curvature: all of player \(j\)'s
response values can translate by the same amount.  For a finite regression,
let \(P\) make \(p\) Never Quit and let \(Y\) make \(p\) Quit at date zero.
Give \(p\) payoff one exactly when \(p\) belongs to the terminal coalition,
and give \(j\) payoff one whenever \(p\) belongs to it, independently of
\(j\)'s action; set all other rewards and Never payoff to zero.  Then the
\(p\)-edge is paid and kills \(p\)'s debt, while \(B_j\) rises from zero to
one.  But every \(j\)-response value rises together, so every difference of
two \(j\)-response values has zero cross-effect.  Therefore (4.7) alone does
not enter the checked normalized-gap full-chord theorem; an additional
non-affine response-gap or chronological datum is genuinely necessary.

### 4.2 Minimum-fibre reset-rigid entry

The maximal absorptions tend to zero and

\[
 D(z)=D_*,
 \qquad d_p(z)=0.
 \tag{4.10}
\]

The hard-residual finite-atom theorem and the singleton/Never cap-tightness
argument imply that the limiting law has positive opponent incidence for

\(D_*\le B_p(z)-r_p(\{p\})\).  Thus the checked law-tight reset dispatcher
produces a reset-rigid chamber at this exact minimum joint-law point.
