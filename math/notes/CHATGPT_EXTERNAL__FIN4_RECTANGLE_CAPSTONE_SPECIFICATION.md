## Proposed well-founded target

Let \(H\) be a maintained Fin4 quantitative full-support hard residual, let
\(F\) be a positive-minimum tangent family based at \(z\), and let \(P\) be an
actual rectangle packet attached to that same source data. Write

\[
A(z)=\{i:d_i(z)>0\},\qquad \rho(z)=|A(z)|.
\]

The desired producer must return either:

1. a source-matched exact punishment-floor chronology which reaches the
   cumulative-charge near-return consumer; or
2. a new positive-minimum tangent family based at \(z'\) with

   \[
   D(z')=D_*,\qquad A(z')\subsetneq A(z).
   \]

Because \(1\le\rho(z)\le4\), the second arm is a natural-valued rank decrease
and can occur at most three times. The output must include actual profiles
realizing the new tangent family, not just the carrier point \(z'\).

## Reset-face split

Let \(G\) be the unrestricted observer-reset minimizer and \(F_\mu\) the
minimizer constrained to retain the rectangle endpoint law.

### Strict law premium

If

\[
\Delta_\mu=D(F_\mu)-D(G)>0,
\]

the missing theorem must turn this premium, the retained atom, and the literal
reached reset law into a charged exact punishment-floor chronology on the same
source or one of its actual tails. A newly selected edge elsewhere in the
game is insufficient.

The useful output is not one isolated positive edge. In the counterexample
regime such an edge can have a permanent payoff-escape radius. The theorem
must therefore return a cumulative near-return, or an edge together with a
source-faithful regeneration/descent mechanism which consumes the escape arm.

### Aligned minimizers

If

\[
D(F_\mu)=D(G),
\]

the desired rank conclusion needs all of

\[
D(G)=D_*,\qquad d_o(G)=0,\qquad o\in A(z),
\]

and the no-new-debtor property

\[
d_j(G)>0\Longrightarrow j\in A(z)\setminus\{o\}.
\]

Then \(A(G)\subsetneq A(z)\). The existing transfer identity only preserves
the debt sum and allows debt to enter a previously zero coordinate, so it does
not prove this. One needs either an adapter to the checked full-replacement
support-drop theorem or a direct no-new-support theorem for the aligned
double-reset limit.

## Source-provenance gate

Any source-attachment hypothesis must carry only data already produced by
named declarations. It must not assume the exact current/tail payoff
equalities, reached chronology, or punishment-floor edge whose construction is
the theorem's purpose.

The hard residual is a same-table invariant and survives regeneration. What
must be regenerated is its attachment to actual profiles, complete stopping
laws, reached suffixes, and the new minimum tangent family.
