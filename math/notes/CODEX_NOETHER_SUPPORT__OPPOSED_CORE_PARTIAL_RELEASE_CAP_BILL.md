## 1. The finite operation and EVERY response cap

Start with three sure date-zero owners j,k,h and optional owner o. Release
j partially: it Quits at zero with probability y and otherwise plays
Never. Reselect o's Quit probability p independently. Retain k,h surely
at zero. No date is inserted and at least one sure opponent remains under
EVERY unilateral intervention. Hence the entire response menu still
reduces to Quit at zero and Continue; every later finite date and Never
has the Continue value, at either original or final table.

For j let L_j(p) be its Continue-minus-Quit gap against k,h and o. For
i∈{k,h}, let L_i^on(p), L_i^off(p) be its corresponding gap conditional
on j Quitting or Continuing. These are affine functions of p. For o let
C_o(y) be its Quit-minus-Continue gap, affine in y. The exact FULL debts
are

    d_j=max(y L_j(p), −(1−y)L_j(p)),
    d_i=max(0, y L_i^on(p)+(1−y)L_i^off(p)),   i∈{k,h},
    d_o=max((1−p) C_o(y), −p C_o(y)).                     (1)

In particular the optional best action may flip after release; both
branches in (1) are kept. These are actual independent profiles, not
deleted-child Nash laws or correlated root mixtures. The three-sure
source fixes the on-row contacts, but does not fix L_i^off or C_o(0).
