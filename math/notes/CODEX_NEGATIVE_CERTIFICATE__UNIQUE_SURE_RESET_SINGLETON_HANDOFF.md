## Exact question

Let \(I=\operatorname{Fin}4\), let \(r\) be a quitting reward table, and let
\(\mathcal W\) be a terminal exploitability witness with gap

\[
 \Gamma=\mathcal W.\operatorname{terminalGap}>0.
\]

Let \(u\in\mathbb R^I\), let \(q\) be an exact independent product Nash root
of the one-stage quitting game with all-Continue payoff \(u\), and suppose a
fixed player \(k\) is the unique sure quitter:

\[
 q_k=1,\qquad q_i<1\quad(i\ne k).                              \tag{1}
\]

Let \(\widehat q\) denote stationary repetition of \(q\), and let

\[
 \widehat q^{-k}
 =\widehat q[k\leftarrow\operatorname{Never}]
\]

be the literal behavioral update. Does this exact source enter an existing
consumer without reselecting a different induced Nash point?

## Theorem

Put \(F=I\setminus\{k\}\). There is a point

\[
 z\in\operatorname{quittingPersistentBaseNashSet}
       (r,\{k\},F)                                             \tag{2}
\]

whose persistent-base root is exactly \(q\). Consequently the prescribed
owner theorem supplies one \(\delta_k>0\) and a
QuittingSingletonBaseStationaryHandoff at the same point \(z\), with the
following literal identifications and conclusions.

1. Its source profile is exactly \(\widehat q\).
2. Every \(i\ne k\) has zero complete behavioral debt at \(\widehat q\), and

   \[
   d_k(\widehat q)\ge\delta_k.                                 \tag{3}
   \]

3. Its owner repair is exactly the update
   \(\widehat q\to\widehat q^{-k}\). That update attains \(k\)'s complete
   behavioral cap, gains at least \(\delta_k\), and leaves

   \[
   d_k(\widehat q^{-k})=0.                                    \tag{4}
   \]

4. There is a player \(j\ne k\) such that

   \[
   d_j(\widehat q^{-k})\ge\Gamma,                              \tag{5}
   \]

   and \(\widehat q^{-k}\) carries a literal
   QuittingPaidFirstDisagreementRow for \(j\) of gain
   \(\Gamma\).
5. For every \(M>0\) satisfying
   \(\lvert r_i(S)\rvert\le M\) for all \(i,S\), the same repaired profile
   carries a QuittingPaidEndpointAtom for \(j\), with the quantitative mass
   and reward fields of the checked endpoint-atom theorem.
6. Exactly one of the checked floor alternatives holds:

   - every payoff coordinate of \(\widehat q^{-k}\) is at least its
     punishment value; or
   - a displayed player \(i\ne k\) is strictly below punishment there.

In the first arm, \(\widehat q^{-k}\) is the literal initial source of the
checked infinite exact-prefix orbit. Along that orbit every root is exact,
every semantic successor is the literal prefix of its predecessor, and

\[
 \operatorname{Abs}(q_t)\longrightarrow0.                    \tag{6}
\]

Moreover, for every fixed positive absorption threshold, the checked
charged-payoff-recurrence premise is false on this orbit.

Applied to alternative B of the reviewed survival theorem, this construction
uses its limiting \(q\) and the exact same stationary profile
\(\widehat q\). It does not introduce a separately selected singleton-base
Nash source.

## Proof

### 1. The free coordinates are an induced singleton-base Nash profile

Every marginal \(q_i\) for \(i\in F\) is a mixed distribution on the two
actions Quit and Continue. Encode these marginals as the mixed-polytope point
\(z\). At coordinate \(k\), equation (1) identifies \(q_k\) with pure Quit.
There are no players outside \(\{k\}\cup F\). Therefore

\[
 \operatorname{quittingPersistentBaseRoot}(\{k\},F,z)=q.      \tag{7}
\]

Fix \(i\in F\). If player \(i\) replaces its current action while every other
marginal is fixed, player \(k\) remains a sure quitter. Hence the current row
absorbs regardless of \(i\)'s action. The all-Continue tail payoff \(u_i\)
is never used. Both pure endpoint payoffs are exactly the expected utilities
in the finite binary game induced by the persistent base \(\{k\}\).

Since \(q\) is an exact one-stage Nash root against \(u\), both pure endpoint
payoffs for \(i\) are at most its prescribed root payoff. This holds for
every \(i\in F\), and binary mixed-deviation linearity makes it the complete
mixed Nash condition of the induced finite game. Thus (2) holds.

Equivalently, this is the hypothesis of the checked converse
mem_quittingPersistentBaseNashSet_of_free_endpointNash after screening the
irrelevant continuation payoff by the sure player \(k\).

### 2. Apply the prescribed-owner handoff at the same point

Apply
QuittingTerminalExploitabilityWitness.exists_prescribedOwner_stationaryHandoff
to \(\mathcal W\) and the preselected owner \(k\). The theorem returns one
\(\delta_k>0\), uniform over every point in the induced singleton-base Nash
set, and a QuittingSingletonBaseStationaryHandoff at each such point. Apply
its universal clause to the particular \(z\) from (2).

By (7), the handoff's source root is \(q\). Its stationary source is therefore
definitionally \(\widehat q\), rather than a new profile. The fields
source_free_semantics and source_owner_debt give zero debt for every free
coordinate and (3) for \(k\).

The checked identity
update_quittingSingletonBaseStationaryProfile_owner_alwaysContinue says that
the repaired profile is the literal unilateral replacement of \(k\) by
Always Continue. In a quitting game this strategy is literal Never. Hence the
repaired profile is exactly \(\widehat q^{-k}\).

The fields repaired_owner_payoff_eq_source_cap,
repaired_owner_cap_eq_payoff, and repaired_owner_gain now give (4) and the
gain assertion. The fields outsideDebtor_ne_owner, outside_debt, and paid_row
select \(j\ne k\), prove (5), and supply the source-matched paid row.

This reconstructs the stationary Never reset of the survival theorem through
the checked handoff. Its direct terminal-gap proof gives the sharper fact
that Never gains at least \(\Gamma\) at \(\widehat q\); the handoff only needs
its own positive uniform owner constant \(\delta_k\).

### 3. Endpoint atom and floor dispatch

Choose \(M>0\) bounding every reward coordinate. The checked theorem
QuittingSingletonBaseStationaryHandoff.paidEndpointAtom applies to the same
handoff and its repaired source \(\widehat q^{-k}\). It produces a
QuittingPaidEndpointAtom for \(j\), without changing the source, observer, or
opponent strategies. Its cap debt is unrestricted because the stationary
pure-time extremality theorem used by the handoff covers all behavioral
deviations.

The handoff field floor_dispatch is exactly the alternative in item 6. In
the negative arm it names a free coordinate, hence a player distinct from
\(k\), which lies strictly below punishment at the repaired source.

### 4. The floor-safe exact orbit

In the floor-safe arm, apply
QuittingSingletonBaseStationaryHandoff.repairedExactInfiniteOrbit. Its initial
semantic pair is the actual semantic pair of \(\widehat q^{-k}\), and each
successor is obtained by prefixing the selected exact root against the
literal preceding prescribed payoff. This is one executable semantic
chronology, not a list of independently selected stationary sources.

Under the same terminal exploitability witness, the checked theorem
QuittingTerminalExploitabilityWitness.repairedExactOrbit_absorption_tendsto_zero
gives (6). For each positive threshold, the checked theorem
not_repairedExactOrbit_chargedPayoffRecurrence rules out the maintained
fixed-charge payoff-recurrence premise. These are consequences of the
no-uniform branch, not new terminal consumers.

This completes the theorem.

## Exact ancestry and the remaining nonlocal seam

The survival theorem starts from actual tropical stationary sources
\(\tau_n\), prefixes arbitrary exact roots \(q_n\), and in the vanishing
joint-survival arm extracts a limiting root \(q\). The finite literal children
are

\[
 \rho_n=q_n::\tau_n.
\]

The stationary profile \(\widehat q\) is obtained by repeating the limiting
root. It is an actual behavioral profile, but in general

\[
 \widehat q\ne\rho_n,\qquad
 \widehat q\ne\tau_n,
\]

and there is no literal update edge from either finite sequence to
\(\widehat q\). The present theorem preserves the exact ancestry statement:
it does not retroactively turn compact convergence into chronology.

Starting at \(\widehat q\), however, the edge

\[
 \widehat q\longrightarrow\widehat q^{-k}
\]

is literal, source-matched, cap-attaining, and positively gaining. Its child
is the source of the full-gap paid row. The endpoint atom and floor dispatch
also live at that exact child. Thus the local unique-sure reset is consumed.
What remains is nonlocal:

1. enter \(\widehat q\) chronologically from the finite tropical/root-prefix
   sequence, or construct the same handoff at a finite source; and
2. in the floor-safe branch, consume the vanishing-absorption exact orbit
   without assuming a fixed charged payoff recurrence which is known to be
   impossible under the witness.

The first obstruction is a source-entry seam. The second is the established
all-Continue/inert exact-orbit waist. Neither is a missing local response at
the unique-sure profile.

## Boundary tests

### The owner root inequality is not needed for induced membership

Only free-player root inequalities enter (2). Once \(k\) is sure, every free
deviation is screened from the continuation payoff. The owner inequality is
needed in the survival theorem to identify its Never cap, but not to place
the free marginals in the persistent-base Nash set.

### Uniqueness of the sure quitter is not the consumer's source

The checked singleton-base handoff accepts arbitrary induced Nash points, even
ones with a free coordinate on a pure boundary. Uniqueness is inherited from
the survival classification and ensures \(O_k>0\) there; it is not silently
used to invoke the prescribed-owner theorem.

### Reactivation is exactly the paid child, not a contradiction

The matching-pennies regression in
FIRST_EXACT_ROOT_SURVIVAL_AND_UNIQUE_SURE_RESET has a unique sure owner whose
Never deletion gives another player a strict Quit0 gain. That behavior is
consistent with (5): the handoff is designed to select an outside debtor at
the repaired child. Reactivation falsifies renewal of the unique-sure form,
but it does not falsify the same-profile handoff.

### Floor safety is essential for the exact-orbit continuation

The repairedExactInfiniteOrbit construction requires every coordinate of the
repaired source to be above punishment. In the other floor-dispatch arm the
present theorem stops at the named under-punishment free coordinate. It does
not install a floor orbit anyway.

### A vanishing-absorption orbit is not a terminal equilibrium

Equation (6) occurs under a terminal exploitability witness. Every orbit
profile remains subject to the fixed terminal gap. Root absorption tending to
zero is therefore a boundary obstruction, not evidence that the profiles
become unrestricted terminal equilibria.

## Scope and nonclaims

- This note proves a same-profile consumer for the unique-sure stationary
  object; it does not make that object a finite descendant of the tropical
  source sequence.
- The Never edge from \(\widehat q\) to \(\widehat q^{-k}\) is literal and
  cap-attaining. No later return to a singleton-base source is proved.
- The outside debt, paid row, and endpoint atom are all co-realized at
  \(\widehat q^{-k}\), but they do not imply floor safety.
- In the floor-safe arm, the exact-prefix orbit is chronological. Its
  absorption tends to zero, and fixed charged payoff recurrence is excluded
  under the no-uniform witness.
- No source-entry interpolation, summable seam, renewable rank, terminal
  approximate Nash profile, or uniform-equilibrium payoff is proved.
