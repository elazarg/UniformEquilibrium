# Historical resolution: the Fin4 minimum-law singleton atlas leaf

## Resolution

The checked clock-compression theorem
`FinFourMinimumAtomProducer.exists_commonChronology_cofinal_ownerCompressedSingleton`
fixes one actual minimum-atom chronology before the resolution and depth and
produces a cofinally deep concentrated singleton endpoint for every positive
resolution below the retained singleton-law mass.  Its canonical
`mu^2 / 8` specialization is
`FinFourMinimumAtomProducer.nonempty_ownerCompressedSingletonProducer`.
Checked packetization then enters the forced-pair construction.

Thus singleton diffusion is no longer an atlas leaf.  The material below is a
stable historical record of the former question.

## Objective

Consume a positive singleton atom in the selected minimum joint law even when
its actual causal realizing profiles diffuse that mass through arbitrarily
late dates.

## Checked entrance

`FinFourProducerResidual.minimumSingleton` retains:

- the full quantitative hard residual and terminal exploitability witness;
- one selected minimum joint semantic/law point `(z_*, nu_*)`;
- `D(z_*) = D_* > 0` and global minimality;
- one singleton terminal coalition `{j}` with `nu_*({j}) > 0`; and
- its actual causal realizing sequence and arbitrarily deep source-matched
  suffix access.

The hard residual guarantees a positive finite atom but does not force its
mass to occur at one uniformly positive literal date in the realizing
profiles.

## Essential boundary

Nonsingleton anti-diffusion is unavailable.  A single player's complete
stopping law may spread fixed total finite mass over more and more dates while
its largest date atom tends to zero.  Therefore this leaf cannot be mapped to
the concentrated-singleton node without a new theorem.

Coordinatewise weak convergence of stopping laws is also insufficient: all
marginals may converge to Never while a relative-timing singleton or
coalition law remains visible only in the joint terminal limit.

## Exact remaining theorem

Prove one exhaustive implication:

```text
minimum-law singleton source
  -> recurrent concentrated-singleton atlas endpoint
     or terminal epsilon-Nash profiles for every epsilon > 0
     or positive cumulative admissible payoff near-return
     or source-preserving well-founded atlas descent.
```

The first arm must produce actual marked dates, a fixed positive stage-mass
floor, and literal source/tail provenance.  If concentration fails, the proof
must consume temporal diffusion itself rather than name a diffuse singleton
residual again.

## Possible legitimate methods

- occupation or Green accounts which remain uniform over arbitrary unilateral
  stopping laws;
- compactified stopping laws augmented by an exact singleton bubble or
  exchange packet;
- a source-matched punishment construction which controls an owner who can
  delay indefinitely;
- an exact finite-clock hierarchy followed by a proof that the escaping
  boundary is consumable; or
- a finite-rank support transition retaining the minimum-law source.

## Acceptable answers

1. prove concentration into the checked concentrated-singleton node;
2. compile the diffuse chronology directly to terminal approximants or a
   uniform payoff;
3. construct a source-preserving well-founded descent; or
4. construct an actual Fin4 positive-gap counterexample satisfying all the
   minimum-singleton source premises.

## Outputs that do not answer the question

- choosing one positive date whose lower bound depends on the profile index;
- a fixed finite-horizon witness;
- replacing the joint terminal law by its marginal weak limit;
- assuming tightness or attainment of the minimum semantic point;
- a static singleton-base profile selected elsewhere on the same table; or
- another diffuse/concentrated dichotomy with the diffuse arm unconsumed.
