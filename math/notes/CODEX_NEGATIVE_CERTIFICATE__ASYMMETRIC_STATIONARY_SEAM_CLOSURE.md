The genuinely multiscale version has a separate exact obstruction.  Consider
a finite deletion tree whose gate `g` has one distinguished owner `a_g` and

```text

```

while every other coordinate at that gate has a limit strictly below one.
If `W_i(t)` is the sum of the weights of the gates owned by `i` before a
leaf `t`, and `W(t)=sum_i W_i(t)`, then the policy and deleted-player reach
exponents are

```text
E_policy(t)=W(t),       E_i(t)=W(t)-W_i(t).       (N23)
```

Thus one common leaf can in principle correct four debts of respective
orders `h^(kappa_i)`: the exponent equations are

```text
W-W_i=kappa_i,
W=(kappa_0+kappa_1+kappa_2+kappa_3)/3,
W_i=W-kappa_i.                                  (N24)
```

They have nonnegative weights exactly when
`3 kappa_i <= sum_j kappa_j` for every `i`; for a common order `kappa`, take
all four owner weights equal to `kappa/3`.  In contrast, distinct sequential
leaves cannot be uniquely exposed to distinct owners at the same order.  If
an `i`-leaf has `sum_(j != i) W_j=kappa` and `W_i>W_k`, then every later
`k`-leaf has exponent at least
`kappa+(W_i-W_k)>kappa`.  Hence the tropical exponent bookkeeping permits a
shared leaf but not the desired sequence of four same-order private leaves.

### Global interpolation audit: what the positive minimum actually adds

This subsection tests whether compact interpolation can attach the stationary
paid port to the **global** minimum fibre.  It produces one exact signed

Let `S_n` be actual profiles realizing a minimum semantic/cap point `z`, and
let `P_n` be actual stationary-port profiles converging to its port point `y`.
Fix the collar coordinate `b`, and suppose the genuine global hypothesis gives

```text

```

The cap `B_b` ignores player `b`'s prescribed strategy.  Order the other three
players as `a_1,a_2,a_3`, and let `O_n^r` be the opponent profile obtained by
replacing the first `r` of these coordinates from `S_n` by those from `P_n`.
For any choice of player `b`'s own law, write `H_n^r` for the resulting actual

```text

```

`Delta/3-o(1)`.  Choose an `eta_n`-optimal unrestricted response `alpha_n` at


```text

```

then

```text
G_n^r-G_n^(r+1)
 = [U_b(alpha_n,O_n^r)-U_b(alpha_n,O_n^(r+1))]
```
