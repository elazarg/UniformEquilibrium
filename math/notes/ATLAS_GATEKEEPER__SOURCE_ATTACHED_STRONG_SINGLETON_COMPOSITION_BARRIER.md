## Non-composition theorem

The following four proposed compositions exhaust the useful overlaps of the
named notes, and every one fails at a literal profile boundary.

### 1. Leakage squares cannot start unconditionally

Changing (j) preserves (j)'s cap, so for

\[
 g_n=U_j(\tau_n)-U_j(\sigma_n)
\]

global minimality and (1) give the exact leakage account

\[
 \sum_{i\ne j}
   \bigl(d_i(\tau_n)-d_i(\sigma_n)\bigr)
 \ge g_n-o(1).
 \tag{4}
\]

If (g_n\) has a fixed positive lower bound, a fixed recipient and a near-cap
response do give the actual same-past/same-tail response square recorded in
`ATLAS_GATEKEEPER__SOURCE_ATTACHED_SINGLETON_ENDPOINT.md`.

But the owner compression may be the identity: the source owner may already
Quit surely at its selected least positive post-anchor date.  The checked
mass bound controls the target singleton mass, not the update amplitude.
Thus (g_n=0), every difference in (4) is zero, and no positive response
square follows.  The strong packet's separate (o)-update does not repair
this: its exact-best endpoint may be Continue, and even in Quit mode its
actual gain has no source-independent positive lower bound.

Hence the source-attached packet does not unconditionally produce the
positive premise of the leakage-square route.

### 2. Pure-toggle regeneration preserves the tail, not minimum semantics

`ATLAS_FALSIFIER__CONCENTRATED_SINGLETON_FULL_GAP_TOGGLE_CYCLE.md` may replace
the marked root by any pure nonempty coalition while preserving the literal
past, marked reach, and post-date tail.  The terminal witness then supplies a
full-gap horizontal toggle at every pure nonempty root.

This replacement has no smallness parameter.  It changes the absorbing
payoff at the marked row by an order-one table amount and may change all four
unrestricted caps.  Therefore (1) does not pass from \(\sigma_n\) to any pure
sibling, and (3) is not retained after further toggles.  The exact
same-response rectangle extracted from a horizontal cycle likewise gives no
target near-cap field: the explicit (H\)-regression in Section 7 of that
note makes its selected response arbitrarily far below the cap while keeping
the paid row, marked atom, and response cross-difference fixed.

Thus the horizontal adapter genuinely preserves source *syntax* (past and
tail), but not the whole-profile minimum or cap data required by a vertical
consumer.

### 3. Pure-singleton re-equilibration requires discarding the past

At a first-stage root where (j) Quits surely, choosing a Nash equilibrium
of the other three players' induced date-zero game makes their unrestricted
behavioral debts zero.  The owner's Continue option must be evaluated using
the literal tail cap (B_j(Z)).  This is the valid theorem in
`ATLAS_GATEKEEPER__PURE_SINGLETON_REEQUILIBRATION.md`.

It cannot be applied to the whole source profile while retaining its past.
If the sure-(j) row occurs at (t_n>0), an outsider may deviate before
(t_n); date-(t_n) re-equilibration says nothing about that payoff.  To get
the all-behavior zero-debt conclusion one must restart at the marked row (or
construct a new root-then-tail profile).  That operation discards the
cap-prefixed reference whose debt is controlled by (1).

Starting instead with a minimum carrier tail (Z) and a fresh sure-owner
root gives a legitimate one-active carrier, but it is no longer the atlas
target \(\tau_n\) or \(\rho_n\).  At the table level it is dominated by the
already checked persistent singleton-base construction, which for every
owner supplies a literal stationary one-debtor source.  The maintained atlas
question expressly excludes such an independently selected singleton-base
profile as a source-preserving consumer.

## Exact conclusion

There is no current combination of the five named note families which
narrows the source-attached strong concentrated-singleton obligation.  The
conflict is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c}
\text{near-minimum/cap-stack provenance is certified on the reference,}\\
\text{marked zero defect and collision geometry are certified on targets,}\\
\text{unique-debtor/floor recycling is certified only after a new first-row}
\\[-1mm]\text{re-equilibration or at a separately selected carrier.}
\end{array}}
\tag{6}
\]

None of the reviewed operations transports the first line through the second
to the third.  Conversely, every operation that supplies the third line
forgets the actual packet data required to regenerate the first two.

This conclusion does **not** assert that such a source-preserving theorem is
false under the full hard residual; an actual counterexample would decide
far more.  It says precisely that the existing note hypotheses do not
co-realize on one profile, and the known local regressions rule out filling
the mismatch by cap continuity, endpoint affinity, punishment normality, or
tail replacement alone.
