## Proposed theorem

Let \(F\) be a positive-minimum tangent family with base \(z_*\), positive-debt
support \(A\), and tangent matrix \(T\). Assume

\[
\sum_jT_{m,j}=0\quad\text{for every mover }m
\tag{1}
\]

and that there is no flat entry into a coordinate outside \(A\).

Fix \(m\in A\) and a compact full-replacement endpoint \(e\) for mover \(m\).
Then global minimality gives

\[
D(z_*)\le D(e).
\]

Exactly one of the following holds.

### Minimum-fiber support drop

If \(D(e)=D(z_*)\), flat minimum-fiber affinity identifies the debt change
coordinatewise with the tangent column \(T_{m,\bullet}\). The mover's endpoint
debt vanishes. Absence of flat support entry prevents any coordinate outside
\(A\) from becoming positive. Hence

\[
\operatorname{supp}_+d(e)\subsetneq A.
\]

The base-parameterized tangent-family extraction should then give a fresh
positive-minimum tangent family whose base is exactly \(e\) and whose support
rank is strictly smaller.

### Off-minimum paid-row exit

If \(D(z_*)<D(e)\), the endpoint curvature relative to the flat tangent column
is coordinatewise nonnegative, vanishes at the mover, and has positive total
sum

\[
D(e)-D(z_*)>0.
\]

Some nonmover therefore has positive curvature. The normalized curvature
decoder should produce, along the endpoint's exact source/full-replacement
subsequence, a fixed positive-gain paid first-disagreement row.

## Consequence if the composition is valid

The four tangent-family alternatives

1. positive total slope;
2. flat support entry;
3. flat charged circulation; and
4. flat potential co-decrease

would reduce by strong induction to only three terminal exits:

1. positive total slope;
2. entry into an initially inactive debt coordinate; or
3. an eventually paid first-disagreement row.

Flat charged circulation and flat co-decrease would no longer require a
conditioned-packet reprojection consumer: each full-replacement endpoint
either lowers the finite support rank and restarts exactly at its cluster, or
exits through the paid-row route.

## Why source chronology is not claimed

The support-rank argument is an existence recursion on exact minimum-fiber
bases. It does not splice the replacement endpoint into the original
behavioral continuation. In the strict case it retains the endpoint's own
literal subsequence only to decode the paid row.

Thus the proposal does not construct:

- a conditioned successor equal to a later source;
- a Nash--Bellman path through the endpoint;
- an all-behavior implementation relation to an artificial anchor; or
- a paid-row consumer.
