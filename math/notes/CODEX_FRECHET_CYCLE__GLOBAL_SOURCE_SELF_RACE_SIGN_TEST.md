dated outcome, union S∪T on a finite tie, and Never only when both are
Never. A finite outcome precedes Never. If μ is the source's dated

    μ⁻=R_*(μ⊗μ),
    ν⁻_(i,τ)=R_*(ν_(i,τ)⊗ν_(i,τ)).                  (1)

The second identity fixes i's clock at τ in BOTH replicas. The minimum
of those identical fixed clocks is τ, and the three opponents use their
independent min-copy laws. It therefore computes the actual deviating

p_i(t)[Pr(T_i≥t)+Pr(T_i>t)], and its Never mass is p_i(Never)².

## Exact nonlinear comparison and source test

For signed measures α,β on the finite dated-outcome space define the
symmetric bilinear expression H_i(α,β)=∫r_i(R(e,f))dα(e)dβ(f), with
Never reward zero. Put δ_(i,τ)=ν_(i,τ)−μ. Then the full new gain is

    g⁻_(i,τ)=2H_i(μ,δ_(i,τ))+H_i(δ_(i,τ),δ_(i,τ)). (2)

The full exploitability is the maximum of ALL rows (2). Neither term
is the original gain ∫r_i dδ in general. The quadratic term includes
simultaneous unions and signed early/late differences. A shared nonlinear
map in (1) does not satisfy the linear closure hypothesis from the
finite-observable residual-transport proposal.

There is a useful check on what the source already controls. Let p_i⁺
be the law of max(T_i,T'_i). For every atom, including Never,

    p_i⁻+p_i⁺=2p_i.

Thus opposite min-copy and max-copy directions are both admissible in
the original source face. Let A_i be the SAME λ-weighted first derivative
along p_i⁻−p_i. Applying the complete directional bound to all 16
independent choices of min or max endpoints gives

    Σ_i |A_i|≤R.                                      (3)

Indeed choose the direction sign opposite to A_i in each coordinate;
the derivative of that allowed simultaneous chord is −Σ_i|A_i|.
This uses one λ throughout. It is not a new sign for a finite joint
move: the higher-order profile terms remain. Equation (2) is a different
expansion, in the dated signed response measure, and its bilinear term
must not be identified with these profile derivatives without proof.

The bounded test has obtained no source-forced sign for those remaining
