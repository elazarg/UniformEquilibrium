## What repetition of the same reset can and cannot change

Use the constants c_i,c_j,g_p,g_k,K_p,K_k of the positive-participant
reset in the [two-zero reductions](../exports/TWO_ZERO_CONTINUATION_REDUCTIONS.md).
Put C=c_i+c_j, D=c_i c_j, W=1+C. An admissible reset of amplitude
t in (0,1) transforms the positive observer cap b and original Never by

    b'=a(t)b+[1-a(t)] beta(t), nu'=a(t)nu,
    a(t)=(1-t)/(1+Ct+Dt^2), beta(t)=(g_k+tK_k)/(W+Dt).

For m nonzero resets, b_m=A_m b_0+sum w_s beta(t_s), where
A_m=product a(t_s), w_s=(1-a(t_s)) product_(h>s)a(t_h)>0 and
sum w_s=1-A_m. Thus an exact return b_m=b_0 makes b_0 a positive weighted
average of beta(t_s). Since

    beta'(t)=(WK_k-Dg_k)/(W+Dt)^2,

beta is monotone or constant. A return therefore requires some t_star in
(0,1) with beta(t_star)=b_0. If K_k-Db_0!=0, necessarily

    t_star=(Wb_0-g_k)/(K_k-Db_0).

If K_k=Db_0, an algebraic return parameter exists exactly when g_k=Wb_0.
This proves that an
unavailable return parameter cannot be repaired by any finite sequence of
this same reset type. It does not prove that the single t_star passes its
full-cap guards: a composite path can change the pivot cap before returning.

For infinitely many resets with positive limiting Never, A_infinity>0 and
the same expansion has weights
w_s=(1-a(t_s))A_infinity/A_s>0, summing to1-A_infinity. All beta values
lie strictly on the same side of b_0 if no interior return exists; their
positive weighted average cannot be b_0. The other case A_infinity=0
cannot descend in the subcritical source: any observer-feasible limit then
has zero Never and J_r>=A>mu by the absorbing SUM-gap inequality. These
claims concern compact limits of finite compositions, not play after an
infinite calendar.

There is a stronger exact invariant. Set V=U_p-r nu and assume
Delta=WK_k-Dg_k!=0. Define

    lambda=(WK_p-Dg_p)/Delta,
    kappa=(K_k g_p-K_p g_k)/Delta, ell(b)=lambda b+kappa.

The pivot value update is
V'=a(t)V+[t g_p+t^2 K_p]/[1+Ct+Dt^2]. The two coefficient identities
g_p-lambda g_k=W kappa and K_p-lambda K_k=D kappa yield

    V'-ell(b')=a(t)[V-ell(b)].

Therefore (V-ell(b))/nu is invariant whenever the full-cap guards make the
operation admissible. A returning composition with survival factor rho has

    V_new-V=(1-rho)[ell(b)-V].

If V>=ell(b), no such return increases adjusted pivot value. In particular,
if the inherited pivot obstacle equals its old cap, the head's pivot cap
cannot decrease, and no returning composition of this type can improve the
global objective. This is a limitation of one reset family, not a surviving
counterexample class or an obstruction to a different reset.

## Why the global composition step remains open

Suppose the selected source is Phi_P(X) with an ACTUAL infinite head P
and X as in (5). The identity Phi_P(Phi_q(T)) is valid semantically.
It does not provide an actual head obtained by playing q after P: that

followed by q have original Never tending to N(P)(1-q), while their

Allowing arbitrary compact limits of prefix operators loses the same
information. Every augmented carrier point is already a limit of finite
prefixes applied to AllNever. Maximizing a tail Never coordinate over
that larger factorization class can terminate at AllNever while all
nonrealization remains in the head. That does not contradict the actual-
head source theorem. A valid new selection must preserve its realization
requirement or supply a separate global improvement consumer.

The cap-box result below identifies exactly which endpoint replacements
compose to observer-feasible carrier points and retain a fixed improvement.
Producing such a replacement, or changing the head to release its pinned
caps, remains open.

    alpha_n->1, rho_n->1, t_n->0, z_n->0.                 (8)

This argument does not assume realization of Q^* or execution after an

## Two-owner source heads require both participant premiums

This is an ordinary mathematical exclusion of an entire inherited-head

### Statement and global source

    Y_r=Phi_(P_r)(Q_r), Y_r,Q_r ALL-END, Q_r in C_p,
    N(P_r),N(Q_r)>0, B(Q_r)>=0,
    some observer cap of Q_r is zero,

where P_r is an ACTUAL independent head. These are the canonical selected
source properties; Q_r is not assumed minimizing. The strict inverse pivot
column and positive absorbing gap supply them in the [boundary reset source theorem](../exports/NONPOSITIVE_PAIR_BOUNDARY_RESET_REDUCTION.md).

For all sufficiently small r>0, a head with finite activity by at most TWO
owners must have exactly two active owners h,k and satisfy

    Z_h({h,k})>max(0,Gamma_hk),
    Z_k({h,k})>max(0,Gamma_kh).                         (T)

The pair need not contain p. Choose its labels so k!=p, hence k is an exact
observer. Beyond the positive-cap collar below, the head calculation uses
joining-genericity only to exclude equality of a positive singleton
coefficient and its participant reward; the strict
inequality failures are excluded by the finite debt floor proved below.
All calendars and ties are unrestricted; no head limit or guard slack is
assumed. The zero endpoint observer may be one of the two head owners.

### The uniform positive-cap collar is produced

A point of E attaining alpha lies in C_p and has J_r=r alpha. Thus

    mu_r<=r alpha, d_p(Y_r)<=r alpha, nu(Y_r)<=alpha.   (1)

Every cluster of Y_r as r decreases to zero belongs to E and, by(1), has

    min_l B_l(Y_r)>=epsilon.                          (2)

This holds uniformly over every choice of minimum. No actual realization
of a limiting minimum is used. It is a collar on the WHOLE global source,
not a hypothesis imposed on the inherited endpoint.

### Exact two-owner moment ledger

Suppress r. Let f,g be the finite head masses of h,k and n=1-f,m=1-g.

    epsilon<=B_l(Y)<=M(f+g).                          (5)

    epsilon<=y<=M f.                                 (6)

    delta_h>=epsilon^2/M.                            (7)

Thus the one-owner cases are already impossible when r is sufficiently small.

### A bilinear debt floor excludes every deficient premium

Assume g>0 and put Kstar=max(D,K,0). A used finite k head deadline attains
y. Such a response is D times a cumulative h mass plus K times its same-date

    y<=Kstar f, g y<=Kstar(W+C0).                     (8)

    delta_h>=f x>=xy/Kstar.                           (9)

          >=(y/Kstar)(mq+Ag)=xy/Kstar.                (10)

    delta_h>=rho[fmq+A(W+C0)]>=rho xy/Kstar.           (11)

Thus every deficient h premium, other than J=A>0, creates a fixed positive

For the other owner, suppose D<=0 and K<=0. Every finite k head deadline

Again g=0 uses(7). These cases exclude the symmetric deficient premiums.

Since x,y>=epsilon and delta_h<=r alpha, (9)--(12) contradict every listed

involved.

## A complete raw guard-transfer test excludes pair entries

Use any incoming root(19) whose support is exactly {h,k}. Impose explicitly

    A=Gamma_hk, D=Gamma_kh, J=Z_h({h,k}), K=Z_k({h,k}),
    J>max(0,A), K>max(0,D), rho_h=(J-A)/J, rho_k=(K-D)/K. (23)

An incoming root alone supplies J,K>0; it does NOT supply the HIGH
inequalities against A,D. Those follow separately for an entire two-owner
head, but are explicit raw assumptions here when other owners act later.

For each quiet l outside {h,k}, define

    G_h=Gamma_lh, G_k=Gamma_lk, H_l=Z_l({h,k}),
    L_h=Z_l({l,h})-G_h, L_k=Z_l({l,k})-G_k,
    T_l=Z_l({l,h,k})-H_l,
    F=(L_h/rho_k, L_k/rho_h,
                  L_h/rho_k+L_k/rho_h+T_l/(rho_h rho_k)),
    C=(G_h+L_h(1-1/rho_k), G_k+L_k(1-1/rho_h),
             H_l-L_h/rho_k-L_k/rho_h+T_l(1-1/(rho_h rho_k))).

The complete raw test for this quiet row is

    F<=0 coordinatewise, OR
    some lambda>=0 has C-lambda F>=0 coordinatewise.   (24)

It is a finite arithmetic test. When F has a positive coordinate, require
C_i>=0 at every zero F_i and compute

    lower=max(0,max_(F_i<0) C_i/F_i),
    upper=min_(F_i>0) C_i/F_i.

The negative-index maximum is omitted when empty. The row passes exactly
when lower<=upper, with lambda=lower. If BOTH quiet rows pass, the incoming
pair is impossible. This includes qualifying first-observer pair entries
even when three or four owners act later in the head.

Write w=B(Y)=U(Y)=e(Y), x=w_h and y=w_k. Incoming active indifference gives

    O_l=L_h y/(K-D)+L_k x/(J-A)+T_l xy/((J-A)(K-D)).

Set u=x/J, v=y/K, X=(J-x)/x>0 and Z=(K-y)/y>0. Direct expansion gives

    O_l/(uv)=F dot(X,Z,1),
    (I_l-O_l)/(uv)=C dot(X,Z,1).                       (25)

If F<=0, then O_l<=0<w_l. Otherwise(24) gives I_l-O_l>=lambda O_l:
when O_l>0, I_l>=O_l; when O_l<=0, positivity of w_l suffices. Thus both
