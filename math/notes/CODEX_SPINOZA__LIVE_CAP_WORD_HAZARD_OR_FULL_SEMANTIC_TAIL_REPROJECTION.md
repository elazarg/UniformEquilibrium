# A live cap word has a charged two-cut or fully reprojects to its actual tail

Author: CODEX_SPINOZA

## Status

**Exact ordinary mathematics; source-attached reduction, not Lean-checked and
not a terminal consumer.**  In the vanishing-ledger arm of the live cap-band
target, measure the total marginal hazard of the modified word.  A fixed
hazard floor gives a literal positive-minimum unmarked two-cut block and hence
an off-minimum exit or a direct actual cap response at its entrance.  If the hazard vanishes,
the exact cap-debt telescope upgrades the evident payoff/law collapse to
convergence of the complete unrestricted cap as well.  Thus the whole target
semantic/law point reprojects to its literal actual tail.

The remaining obstruction is consequently not an uncontrolled target-to-tail
cap seam.  It is the source-to-target strategic ancestry: the original exact
source has a fixed moving inner atom, whereas the paid target deletes that
owner hazard from the whole word.  Semantic/law reprojection does not turn the
tail into a unilateral child of the source and does not restore the source
roots.

## 1. Input and notation

Let \(I=\operatorname{Fin}4\), let all quitting rewards have absolute value at
most \(R\), and assume the terminal-semantic carrier has positive minimum
total debt \(D_*>0\).

Use the live cap-band target and its actual tail from
`CODEX_SPINOZA__LIVE_CAP_TARGET_POSTTAIL_DISTINCT_DEBTOR`:

\[
 Y_n=(y_{n,0},\ldots,y_{n,m_n-1})\triangleright Z_n.
\tag{1}
\]

Put

\[
 h_n=\sum_{t<m_n}\sum_i y_{n,t,i},
 \qquad
 a_n=\prod_{t<m_n}\prod_i(1-y_{n,t,i}),
\tag{2}
\]

where \(y_{n,t,i}\) is player \(i\)'s marginal Quit probability.  Let

\[
 L_{n,i}=\sum_{t<m_n}A_{n,t}c_{n,t,i},
 \qquad L_n=\sum_iL_{n,i},
\tag{3}
\]

be the reached cap-root defect ledger of the actual word.  Work in the
vanishing-ledger arm

\[
 L_n\longrightarrow0.
\tag{4}
\]

The exact arbitrary-root debt telescope is

\[
 d_i(Y_n)=L_{n,i}+a_n d_i(Z_n).
\tag{5}
\]

The target owner \(k\) Continues surely throughout the word,
\(a_n\ge\eta>0\), \(d_k(Y_n)\to0\), and \(d_k(Z_n)\to0\).  After a
subsequence one fixed outsider \(j\ne k\) satisfies
\(d_j(Z_n)\ge\Gamma\), with a literal finite-time-or-Never response at
\(Z_n\) whose lift behind the word gains at least \(\eta\Gamma/2\).

## 2. Hazard-floor arm

### Theorem 2.1

If, along a subsequence, \(h_n\ge\chi>0\), then the actual root sequence of
\(Y_n\), with entry cut \(0\) and exit cut \(m_n\), is a supplied positive-
minimum **unmarked** two-cut block with hazard floor \(\chi\).
Put

\[
 K_\chi=(1-e^{-\chi})D_*,
 \qquad
 \delta_\chi={e^\chi-1\over2}D_*.
\tag{6}
\]

For every retained \(n\), the two-cut coercive identity and the Fin4
pigeonhole step give

\[
 D(Z_n)\ge D_*+\delta_\chi
\tag{7}
\]

or a player \(p_n\) satisfying

\[
 d_{p_n}(Y_n)>K_\chi/8.
\tag{7a}
\]

Choosing an unrestricted behavioral response within \(K_\chi/16\) of this
cap gives an actual unilateral child of \(Y_n\), with gain and exact decrease
of player \(p_n\)'s debt strictly larger than \(K_\chi/16\).

### Proof

All rows and both cuts are literal rows and suffixes of the actual profile
\(Y_n\).  Both endpoint semantic pairs therefore lie in the carrier.  Equation
(2) gives the required total marginal hazard.  The retained global minimizer
supplies the positive-minimum fields.  The unmarked two-cut telescope gives
total charge at least \(K_\chi/2\) unless (7) holds.  With four players, one
entry debt is then larger than \(K_\chi/8\).  Approximate its complete
behavioral cap within \(K_\chi/16\).  Since a one-player replacement leaves
that player's cap unchanged, its payoff gain equals the exact decrease of
the same debt coordinate and is larger than \(K_\chi/16\).  QED

This is a real consumer of the target word as an unmarked two-cut object, but
its direct cap response is another horizontal child.  Because the entry cut
is zero, no pre-entry marked row exists and no post-mark preservation is
claimed.  It does not return that child to the original exact source.

## 3. Vanishing hazard gives full semantic/law reprojection

### Theorem 3.1

If \(h_n\to0\), then

\[
 a_n\longrightarrow1,
 \qquad
 \|U(Y_n)-U(Z_n)\|_\infty\le2Rh_n\longrightarrow0,
\tag{8}
\]

and, coordinatewise,

\[
 |B_i(Y_n)-B_i(Z_n)|
 \le4Rh_n+L_{n,i}\longrightarrow0.
\tag{9}
\]

Consequently

\[
 \operatorname{Sem}(Y_n)-\operatorname{Sem}(Z_n)\longrightarrow0
\tag{10}
\]

in the full prescribed-payoff/unrestricted-cap norm.  Their complete terminal
outcome laws also satisfy

\[
 \|\operatorname{Law}(Y_n)-\operatorname{Law}(Z_n)\|_{\rm TV}
 \le1-a_n\le h_n\longrightarrow0.
\tag{11}
\]

Thus every joint semantic/law cluster point of the paid targets is literally
a cluster point of their actual tails, and conversely.

### Proof

The union bound for one product row and then for the finite word gives

\[
 1-a_n\le h_n.
\tag{12}
\]

On the event that the word absorbs, both the word reward and the comparison
tail payoff lie in \([-R,R]\); on joint continuation the continuation is
exactly \(Z_n\).  Hence

\[
 |U_i(Y_n)-U_i(Z_n)|\le2R(1-a_n)\le2Rh_n.
\tag{13}
\]

Every prescribed payoff lies in \([-R,R]\).  Never yields zero, so every cap
lies in \([0,R]\), and therefore every debt is at most \(2R\).  Subtracting
the tail debt from (5) gives

\[
 d_i(Y_n)-d_i(Z_n)
 =L_{n,i}-(1-a_n)d_i(Z_n).
\tag{14}
\]

Since \(B=U+d\), equations (12)--(14) imply (9).  This is the step that a
payoff-only estimate would miss: vanishing cap-root ledger is what controls
the unrestricted cap.

Finally, conditional on joint continuation of the word, the terminal
coalition law is exactly the law of \(Z_n\).  The other component has total
mass \(1-a_n\).  The elementary mixture bound gives (11).  QED

### Corollary 3.2: the second debt is at the reprojected point

In the vanishing-hazard arm, the fixed tail debtor is also a fixed debtor of
the target up to \(o(1)\):

\[
 d_j(Y_n)\ge\Gamma-o(1),
 \qquad d_k(Y_n),d_k(Z_n)\longrightarrow0.
\tag{15}
\]

Moreover, a suffix response gaining at least \(\Gamma/2\) lifts to a target
response of gain \(a_n\Gamma/2=\Gamma/2-o(1)\).  Thus the paid target and its
tail have the same limiting complete semantic obstruction, not merely the
same payoff.

This follows from (5), \(L_{n,j}\to0\), and \(a_n\to1\), together with the
exact common-prefix gain identity.

## 4. The surviving source seam is macroscopic and strategic

Let \(\Sigma_n\) be the original exact-word source and \(k\) the cap-band
mover.  The actual edge is literal:

\[
 Y_n=\Sigma_n[k\leftarrow \widehat s_{n,k}],
 \qquad
 U_k(Y_n)-U_k(\Sigma_n)\ge D_*/2-e_n.
\tag{16}
\]

In the vanishing-hazard arm, (8) gives eventually

\[
 U_k(Z_n)-U_k(\Sigma_n)\ge D_*/4.
\tag{17}
\]

This is a semantic comparison, not a unilateral edge: passing from \(Y_n\)
to \(Z_n\) deletes the whole calendar prefix for every player.  The law
version is nevertheless quantitative.  Since payoff is the expectation of
the bounded terminal reward, (16) selects, after a subsequence, one fixed
nonempty coalition \(S\) such that

\[
 \bigl(\Pr_{Y_n}(S)-\Pr_{\Sigma_n}(S)\bigr)r_k(S)
 \ge {D_*\over 60}
\tag{18}
\]

eventually; by (11), the same lower bound holds with \(Z_n\) in place of
\(Y_n\), up to \(o(1)\).  Indeed the sum of the fifteen signed coalition
contributions is the gain in (16), so one is at least one fifteenth of an
eventual \(D_*/4\) gain.

The source contains the original fixed moving inner coalition atom, whereas
the target owner Continues surely through the word.  Thus (18) does not imply
total-variation compactness of the source stopping strategy or identify the
tail as its unilateral child.  It records exactly the nonlocal law seam left
after the target-to-tail cap seam has vanished.

## 5. Exact exhaustive reduction

After a subsequence, the vanishing-ledger live target has one of two outputs:

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{array}{l}
 \text{a fixed-hazard literal unmarked two-cut block, hence an off-minimum exit or}\
 \text{a direct actual cap response at the entrance;}\\[2mm]
 \text{or}\quad
 \operatorname{SemLaw}(Y_n)-\operatorname{SemLaw}(Z_n)\to0,
 \text{ while the exact source--tail law seam remains fixed.}
 \end{array}}
\tag{19}
\]

The second arm proves that the cap/payoff mismatch inside the modified word
is not the final obstruction.  The sole surviving field is a source
component/ancestry equality across the moving inner clock.  Replacing that
field by semantic convergence would repeat the known moving-deadline
compactification error.

## Boundary and nonclaims

- The full cap convergence in (9) uses both vanishing hazard and the
  vanishing reached cap-defect ledger.  Payoff/law convergence alone does not
  control unrestricted caps.
- The unmarked two-cut cap response need not preserve any pre-entry mark, the
  first mover's payoff gain, or the original exact source.
- The tail \(Z_n\) is actual and literally reached, but it is not a unilateral
  child of \(\Sigma_n\).
- Equation (18) is a signed terminal-law coordinate, not a common stopping-law
  limit, an exact root, or a minimum-fibre tangent family.
- No source return, prescribed-payoff Nashification, renewable rank, terminal
  approximate Nash profile, or uniform-equilibrium payoff is proved.
