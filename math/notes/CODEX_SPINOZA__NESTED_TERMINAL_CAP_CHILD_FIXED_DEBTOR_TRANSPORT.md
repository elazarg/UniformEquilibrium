# Nested terminal cap children transport one fixed outsider debt

Author: CODEX_SPINOZA

## Status

**Exact ordinary mathematics; theorem-level progress, but no Nash--Bellman
consumer.** The positive-survival reverse-prefix construction has more
coherence than the individual terminal-child statement records. Its literal
cap children form one nested projective sequence. After choosing the
terminal-gap observer once at one finite child, the same behavioral deviation
can be copied through every later child and retains a fixed positive gain.
Thus the child sequence has one fixed outsider debtor in addition to the
fixed owner's escaping sure clock.

This does not yet produce an exact or summably approximate Nash--Bellman
spine. The nested child root is obtained by forcing the cap owner to Continue,
and the other players' root comparisons see the full prescribed-payoff
displacement caused by the owner cap update. That displacement can be order
one even though the removed owner root hazard is summable. The exact missing
field is therefore a cross-coordinate tail-payoff seam, not source ancestry
or tester-label coherence.

## Question

Does the far-end exact cap clock from the reviewed positive-survival recursion
give more than an unrelated terminal child at each depth? In particular, can
one retain one fixed terminal-gap observer along the same literal genealogy,
and does the resulting genealogy become an exact or approximate
Nash--Bellman spine?

## Input

Let \(I=\operatorname{Fin}4\). Let

\[
 \tau^{n+1}=q^n::\tau^n
\]

be the positive-survival recursive exact-root family of Section 10 of
CODEX_SPINOZA__RECURSIVE_LITERAL_U_ROOT_PREFIX_LEDGER_AND_STALL. Write

\[
 h_{n,i}=q_i^n,\qquad
 c_n=\prod_i(1-h_{n,i}),\qquad
 s_{n,b}=\prod_{i\ne b}(1-h_{n,i}).
\]

The reviewed theorem gives

\[
 \sum_n\sum_i h_{n,i}<\infty,\qquad
 C_\infty:=\prod_nc_n>0.                                    \tag{1}
\]

Player \(b\)'s exact complete cap at \(\tau^n\) is the deterministic clock
\(A^n=n\). Define the literal cap child

\[
 \zeta^n=\tau^n[b\leftarrow A^n].                            \tag{2}
\]

It is terminal by date \(n\), and

\[
 d_b(\zeta^n)=0.                                             \tag{3}
\]

Fix a terminal exploitability gap \(\Gamma>0\).

## 1. Exact nested-child identity

For each \(n\), let \(\bar q^n\) be obtained from \(q^n\) by forcing player
\(b\) to Continue and retaining every outsider marginal:

\[
 \bar q_b^n=0,\qquad \bar q_i^n=q_i^n\quad(i\ne b).           \tag{4}
\]

Then the cap children satisfy the literal profile identity

\[
 \boxed{\zeta^{n+1}=\bar q^n::\zeta^n.}                      \tag{5}
\]

Indeed, \(\tau^{n+1}\) begins with \(q^n\), while \(A^{n+1}\) forces \(b\)
to Continue at that new root and then uses \(A^n\). Replacing \(b\)'s entire
strategy therefore changes \(q_b^n\) to zero and changes the old tail
\(\tau^n\) to \(\zeta^n\), exactly as in (5). No semantic or limiting
identification is used.

The joint Continue probability of \(\bar q^n\) is

\[
 \bar c_n=\prod_{i\ne b}(1-h_{n,i})=s_{n,b}\ge c_n.          \tag{6}
\]

Consequently

\[
 \bar C_{R,\infty}:=\prod_{n\ge R}\bar c_n
 \ge\prod_{n\ge R}c_n\ge C_\infty>0.                        \tag{7}
\]

## 2. Fixed-observer transport theorem

Fix one depth \(R\), one outsider \(j\ne b\), and one complete behavioral
deviation \(\beta_j\) at \(\zeta^R\) whose payoff gain is \(g>0\).
For \(N\ge R\), define \(\beta_j^N\) recursively. At every newly prefixed root
\(\bar q^n\), it copies the prescribed marginal \(\bar q_j^n\); conditional
on joint Continue through all roots \(n=N-1,\ldots,R\), it uses
\(\beta_j\) in the unchanged suffix \(\zeta^R\).

Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
 &U_j(\zeta^N[j\leftarrow\beta_j^N])-U_j(\zeta^N)\\
 &\qquad =
 \left(\prod_{n=R}^{N-1}\bar c_n\right)
 \bigl(U_j(\zeta^R[j\leftarrow\beta_j])-U_j(\zeta^R)\bigr)\\
 &\qquad \ge \bar C_{R,\infty}g.                            \tag{8}
\end{aligned}
\]

The proof is literal. At every root-absorbing history, the prescribed and
deviating \(j\)-actions have the same distribution because the new strategy
copies the prescribed root marginal. Only joint Continue reaches a different
suffix strategy. One use of the root-then-continuation payoff identity
multiplies the old gain by \(\bar c_n\); induction proves (8).

The profile \(\zeta^R\) is terminal by date \(R\), because \(b\) Quits
surely there. Hence every outsider's complete cap is attained among the
finite set \(\{0,\ldots,R,\operatorname{Never}\}\). Apply the terminal gap
once at \(\zeta^R\). Equation (3) forces its observer to be some
\(j\ne b\), its debt is at least \(\Gamma\), and an attained pure-time cap
\(\beta_j\) has actual gain \(g=d_j(\zeta^R)\ge\Gamma\). Therefore the same
fixed player satisfies

\[
 d_j(\zeta^N)\ge \bar C_{R,\infty}\Gamma
 \ge C_\infty\Gamma
 \qquad(N\ge R).                                             \tag{9}
\]

Thus neither the debtor label nor its actual behavioral response must be
reselected at later depths. The sequence carries:

- the fixed owner \(b\), whose prescribed strategy is the sure clock \(N\)
  and whose complete debt is zero;
- one fixed outsider \(j\), with the uniform complete-debt floor (9); and
- the original finite child \(\zeta^R\) as a literal suffix reached with
  probability at least \(\bar C_{R,\infty}\).

## 3. Exact cap-rank refinement

Because \(b\) Quits surely by date \(n\), every outsider's complete response
problem at \(\zeta^n\) is attained among

\[
 \{0,1,\ldots,n,\operatorname{Never}\}.                      \tag{10}
\]

For a fixed outsider \(j\), choose a cap time \(T_{n,j}\) recursively. The
dynamic-programming identity at (5) says that a cap at depth \(n+1\) is
either:

1. Quit at the new date zero; or
2. Continue and use a cap at depth \(n\).

Thus one may select

\[
 T_{n+1,j}\in\{0,T_{n,j}+1\},                                \tag{11}
\]

with the convention \(\operatorname{Never}+1=\operatorname{Never}\).
For finite selected times define the last-reset coordinate

\[
 R_{n,j}=n-T_{n,j}.
\]

Along every finite-time run, (11) makes \(R_{n,j}\) either stay fixed or jump
to \(n+1\). Hence the exact cap clocks have a sharp dichotomy:

- after finitely many resets, one fixed old cap is shifted forever and remains
  attached to a fixed finite suffix; if that cap is Never, this is the
  permanent-Never subcase; or
- reset-to-Quit0 occurs at arbitrarily large front depths.

This is an exact clock classification. It is not yet the renewable decreasing
rank requested by the spine question: the reset coordinate is monotone
forward and may jump without terminating.

## 4. The cross-coordinate Nash seam

Let

\[
 W^n=U(\zeta^n),\qquad \Delta_i^n=W_i^n-U_i^n.
\]

The Bellman identity in (5) is exact:

\[
 W^{n+1}=F_{\bar q^n}(W^n).                                  \tag{12}
\]

Player \(b\)'s prescribed Continue branch is sequentially optimal because
\(A^{n+1}\) attains its complete cap. Thus the \(b\)-coordinate root
comparison is Nash. The outsider coordinates need not be.

Fix \(i\ne b\). Replacing \(q_b^n\) by zero changes every current
root-absorbing endpoint by at most a constant times \(M h_{n,b}\).
For the Continue endpoint there is, in addition, the exact tail term

\[
 \bar s_{n,i}\Delta_i^n,\qquad
 \bar s_{n,i}:=\prod_{\ell\ne i,b}(1-h_{n,\ell}).             \tag{13}
\]

More explicitly, if \(Q_i,H_i,s_i\) denote respectively the Quit endpoint,
the Continue absorbing numerator, and opponent Continue mass at \(q^n\), and
bars denote the same quantities at \(\bar q^n\), then

\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\bigl[\bar Q_i-(\bar H_i+\bar s_{n,i}W_i^n)\bigr]
   -\bigl[Q_i-(H_i+s_{n,i}U_i^n)\bigr]\\
 &\qquad =
   (\bar Q_i-Q_i)-(\bar H_i-H_i)
   -\bar s_{n,i}\Delta_i^n
   -( \bar s_{n,i}-s_{n,i})U_i^n.                            \tag{14}
\end{aligned}
\]

The first, second, and fourth terms are bounded by
\(O(Mh_{n,b})\), hence are summable over \(n\). The third term need not be
summable:
\(\bar s_{n,i}\to1\), while the source hypotheses impose no decay or sign on
\(\Delta_i^n\). Positive far-end reach permits \(\Delta_i^n\) to have a
nonzero limit.

Therefore (12) is a literal Bellman chain but not an exact or known
summably approximate Nash--Bellman chain. The precise missing adapter is

\[
 \sum_n|\Delta_i^n|<\infty
 \quad\text{or an equivalent signed cancellation/return identity}           \tag{15}
\]

for every outsider active at the root. Source reach, cap-clock exactness, and
fixed-observer transport prove none of (15).

## 5. Punishment-floor split adds no return equation

At any fixed child \(\zeta^R\), the terminal gap gives positive debt by (9).
If it is punishment-floor safe, the checked marked exact-orbit theorem starts
a literal exact prefix orbit and, under no uniform payoff, returns the
summable all-Continue port with positive reach of the unchanged paid suffix.

If it has a punishment-floor violation, choose any infinite exact
dynamic-debt prefix tail from that child. The checked theorem
summable_dynamicDebtTailAbsorptionCharge_of_floorViolation_of_positiveDebt
identifies one fixed violator and makes the whole joint absorption series
summable. Thus underfloor is not a third escape.

These exact tails are re-solved against their own successor payoffs. They do
not use the forced-owner roots \(\bar q^n\) in (5), and they supply no equality
between their front semantic value and an earlier \(\zeta^R\). Hence both
floor arms reach the same summable exact-tail waist but neither removes the
cross-coordinate term (13).

## Boundary tests

### Positive finite reach does not make the cross seam small

The inequality \(\prod_n\bar c_n>0\) means that changing the far-end strategy
of \(b\) remains visible with positive weight. It therefore supports, rather
than suppresses, an order-one value displacement \(\Delta_i^n\). Treating
far-end positive reach as a small re-projection error has the sign of the
compactness argument backwards.

### Sure prescribed termination does not give finite-clock semantics

Every \(\zeta^n\) terminates by date \(n\), but outsider stopping laws retain
the old unbounded tail on counterfactual histories. Those histories are
reached if \(b\) deviates from its sure clock, so erasing them can change
\(B_b\) by order one. This is the exact regression in
CODEX_HAHN__TERMINAL_DEPTH_CAP_CHILD_FINITE_CLOCK_ADAPTER_FAILURE.

### Fixed debtor transport is not a unilateral source return

The response in (8) is profitable against \(\zeta^N\). It cannot be
transported back to \(\tau^N\) without changing the opponent strategy of
\(b\). A deviator \(j\) cannot condition on the counterfactual event that
\(b\) first switches to its cap. Thus the two labels are co-realized only on
the cap-child genealogy, whose roots have the seam (14).

## Scope and nonclaims

This note proves the exact nested identity, fixed-observer debt transport, and
cap reset/shift classification. It does not prove that \(\bar q^n\) is root
Nash for outsiders, that its Nash defect is summable, or that the child chain
is an accepted spine.

It does not turn the fixed \(j\)-deviation into a deviation at \(\tau^N\),
identify a semantic return, or construct a decreasing rank. The
punishment-floor results make all subsequent exact tails summable but do not
identify them with the nested cap-child chain.
