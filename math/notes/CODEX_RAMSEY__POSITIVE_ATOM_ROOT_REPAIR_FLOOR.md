### Proposition 6K (split replacement can pay clocks and retain the atom)

The preceding regression is sharp: low absorption, two labelled clocks, and
same-orientation residual atom renewal are probabilistically compatible.  A
replacement must reserve some of its atom after the clock cutoff rather than
spend the entire orientation on the firing row.

Use players `m,n,a,o`, with all rewards zero except

```text
r_o({m})=-1.
```

The common source has `m,n,o` Never Quit and `a` Quit surely at date two.  A
full `m` replacement

- Quits at date zero with probability `c=1/4`;
- conditional on Continue, Quits at date one with probability `d=1/2`;
- conditional on surviving both rows, Never Quits.

The `n` replacement Quits at date zero with probability `c=1/4` and otherwise
Never Quits.  Mix each replacement independently into its source marginal
with complete-law outer weight `h`, where `0<h<=1/4`.  Use the one-row cutoff
immediately after date zero.

At the executed root, both reset marginals have Quit probability `h/4`.
Therefore the packet mesh and total joint absorption are bounded by

```text
mesh=h/4,
h/4 <= alpha <= h/2,                                  (6K.1)
```

and deleting either reset label leaves the other exact exposure `h/4`.
Deleting `a` or `o` leaves both.  Thus joint and every one-player-deleted
clock have exposure at least `h/4`, while total absorption is `O(h)`.

For the atom comparison, keep the same outer `n` law in both components and
compare source `m=Never` with the full `m` replacement.  The initial terminal
`{m}` difference is

```text
A_0(h)=(1-h/4)*(c+(1-c)d)
      =(1-h/4)*5/8.                                   (6K.2)
```

The sign is positive after multiplication by `r_o({m})=-1`, as in the earlier
prescribed orientation.

Conditioning the executed packet on both reset players Continuing at date
zero filters the early target branches.  The residual posterior target weight
of `m` is

```text
F=h*(1-c)/(1-h*c)=(3h/4)/(1-h/4) <= 1/5.             (6K.3)
```

The residual source mover is still Never, whereas the residual full target
Quits at date one with probability `d=1/2`.  The same terminal label and
observer orientation therefore have exact reached-versus-full residual atom

```text
A_res=(1-F)*d >= 2/5.                                 (6K.4)
```

In particular `A_res>=A_0(h)/2`, because `A_0(h)<=5/8`.  The `n` target
component that survives the cutoff is identical to its source `Never` law,
so it creates no hidden residual collision.  The literal conditional port is
again a two-component `m` ray, with the same complete-law provenance.

This is an exact rational construction for rational `h`; it also exhibits the
favorable signed cancellation hidden by the absolute sufficient bound
(6E.3).  The prefix spends some `{m}` atom and changes component survival by a
constant, but the later reserved branch leaves a fixed residual atom.

The construction is not positive-minimum data and does not solve exact entry:
its semantic head is the prefix-generated head, not an arbitrary supplied
reached source.  Its role is to exclude a false impossibility conclusion from
Propositions 6F and 6J.  The remaining frontier theorem would have to select,
from the supplied replacement law or a permitted refinement of it, a cutoff
with both

```text
source baseline absorption O(h),
fixed conditional atom remaining after an O(h) two-label clock prefix,
```

and then solve the exact reached-source fiber entry.  The existing atom
alternative stores the full terminal difference but no such temporal split.

### Proposition 6L (explicit split-port renewal criterion)

The exact sufficient theorem behind Proposition 6K is simple and has the
right `O(h)` modulus.  Return to the one-mover notation of Proposition 6E.
Assume, for constants `C,A_*>0`,

```text
0<=a<=h,
S>=1-C*h,
T>0,
(c_0-c_1)*r_C >= A_*>0,                              (6L.1)
```

and take `h` small enough that

```text
(1-h)(1-C*h)>h,
```

so in particular the denominator below is positive and `pi(h)<1`.  The third condition is a
fixed atom in the **conditional residual component pair**, not merely in the
unconditioned full laws.

The actual prefix absorption satisfies

```text
1-M=(1-a)(1-S)+a(1-T) <= C*h+h.                       (6L.2)
```

Moreover its residual target posterior obeys

```text
F=a*T/M
 <= h/((1-h)(1-C*h)) =: pi(h).                       (6L.3)
```

Therefore the same reached-versus-full residual orientation has

```text
A_res=(1-F)(c_0-c_1)r_C
     >= (1-pi(h))*A_*.                                (6L.4)
```

Here `pi(h)->0`.  Coupling the residual reached mixture to its source
conditional component also gives total variation at most `pi(h)`, hence
prescribed and cap seams at most `2*R*pi(h)` and debt seam at most
`4*R*pi(h)`.  For a subsequent word built on these two conditional
components, Proposition 6B reduces every-interval clock control to its direct
posterior modulus `Pi_L`.  No bound `Pi_L<=pi(h)` is asserted without fresh
reach/likelihood control along that next word.

Thus, on a supplied common component fiber, the combined clock/renewal port
problem has an explicit positive solution whenever:

```text
the source component has only O(h) prefix absorption,
the chosen target-survival branch retains a fixed conditional atom.
```

The target component may spend a constant amount of probability on the clock
prefix; no target survival close to one and no small `|S-T|` are needed.
This is why the absolute sufficient error `e_L` in (6E.3) is not necessary.

Proposition 6L still assumes exactly the fields that the checked frontier does
not store: a high-survival source cutoff and a fixed atom after conditioning
the full replacement on that cutoff.  It also starts only after the actual
reached source has been selected on the common entrance fiber.  It is
therefore a precise local target for a temporal-splitting selection theorem,
not the requested producer itself.

### Proposition 6M (an `O(h)` debt tube is not yet a sublinear availability budget)

There is a second quantitative gap after the missing absorption upper bound.
Corollary 6IA and Proposition 6J show that a literal cutoff with

```text
delta=o(h),
alpha=O(h)
```

places its residual terminal semantic pair only in a tube

```text
dist(z_tail, MinimumSet) <= mu(C*h+o(h)),             (6M.1)
```

where `mu(s)->0` is the compact sublevel modulus from Proposition 6I.  This is
enough for qualitative return to the **whole** minimum set.  It does not imply
the operationally sublinear availability loss required by
`QuittingBudgetStablePacketSystem`.

The missing inference already fails for elementary compact minimization.  On
`X=[-1,1]`, let the minimum set be `{0}` and let the excess function be
`E(x)=x^2`.  Its exact sublevel-distance modulus is

```text
mu(s)=min(1,sqrt(s)).                                (6M.2)
```

For every `C>0`,

```text
mu(C*h)/h = sqrt(C/h) -> +infinity.
```

Even the sharp linear-growth model `E(x)=|x|`, for which `mu(s)=min(1,s)`,
only gives `mu(C*h)/h -> C`, not zero.  Thus compactness, and even a linear
error bound, do not turn the first-order scalar debt budget in (6I.5) into a
sublinear radius loss.

This does not prove that the actual frontier residual moves by first order:
the inequality (6I.5) is only an upper bound, and balanced reset geometry may
produce additional cancellation.  It proves the narrower route audit:

```text
source baseline absorption O(h)
  + compact minimum-set transport
does not by itself instantiate cost_sublinear.        (6M.3)
```

To use minimum-set distance as the availability account, one needs either an
`o(h)` residual excess together with a quantitative error bound strong enough
to preserve `o(h)`, or a direct `o(h)` return estimate in the chosen
base/component fiber.  Alternatively one may use a different radius whose
loss cancels the first-order absorption term.  None of these follows from the
reviewed Proposition 6I modulus.

### Proposition 6N (the small-debt seed cannot itself be an actual minimum-carrier port)

Let

```text
D_*=quittingTerminalSemanticDebtSum(frontier.base)>0,
n=|I|.
```

The positive-debt support is nonempty, so `n>0`.  Every actual terminal
semantic carrier point `z` satisfies

```text
D_* <= sum_i debt_i(z)                               (6N.1)
```

by global minimality of `frontier.base`.  Consequently some coordinate obeys

```text
debt_i(z) >= D_*/n.                                  (6N.2)
```

Indeed, if every coordinate were strictly below `D_*/n`, summing over the
finite player set would contradict (6N.1).  In particular, at accuracy

```text
eta_0=D_*/(2*n)>0,                                   (6N.3)
```

there is no actual carrier point whose debt is coordinatewise at most
`eta_0`.

There is also a fixed **total-semantic seam** to every small-debt anchor.  Let
`w=(u',b')` have nonnegative candidate debt with

```text
debt_i(w)<=eta  for every i.
```

Choose `i` as in (6N.2).  The triangle inequality gives

```text
|z.U_i-w.U_i|+|z.B_i-w.B_i|
  >= |debt_i(z)-debt_i(w)|
  >= D_*/n-eta.                                     (6N.4)
```

Thus, for `eta<=D_*/(2*n)`, every direct total-coordinate seam from an actual
carrier point to a coordinatewise `eta`-debt annotation costs at least
`D_*/(2*n)`.  In particular this cost cannot be hidden in a modulus tending to
zero.  For a diagonal anchor the sharper lower bound in (6N.4) uses
`debt_i(w)=0`.

This has a precise consequence for the checked packet compiler.  A packet
system whose canonical annotation at every port is the literal terminal
semantic pair of an actual positive-minimum profile cannot satisfy the
arbitrarily-small seed field.  Nor is that repaired by putting a finite exact
actual-source prefix *before* an artificial anchor inside the flattened
certificate: the compiler's initial candidate remains the prefix head, so if
that head is the actual carrier point, (6N.2) is still its initial debt.

Therefore the seed clause and the actual-source entrance clause require two
logically distinct objects.  A successful construction cannot use a direct
vanishing endpoint seam as their adapter.  It must either

```text
- start the compiler at an artificial small-debt annotation and prove a
  separate source-to-anchor/payoff adapter which is not counted as its initial
  candidate; or
- discharge a solved-game/uniform-payoff disjunct before entering the
  positive-minimum packet system.
```

This is not a new obstruction to the conjecture; it is the exact quantitative
reason the question's artificial-anchor proviso is substantive.  In
particular, “the reached actual source is within `delta` of the frozen source”
cannot by itself serve as the required seed adapter, since both points remain
inside the same positive-minimum carrier barrier.

## Current result

In the positive-target collision arm of the vanishing-debt rectangle
dispatch, reached-row localization returns one fixed player with a uniformly
positive legal one-row gain.  At that row the pure-time observer Quits surely.
Consequently the selected player's one-row Nash defect is independent of the
attached continuation vector: the row always absorbs through the observer.

Therefore the literal atom row cannot be repaired by choosing a different
artificial Bellman tail.  More quantitatively, if rewards and both candidate
tails are bounded by `M`, then any product root satisfying one-row Nash defect
at most `eta` must be at marginal `l1` distance at least

```text
(gamma - eta) / (4*M)
```

from the literal reached root, where `gamma>0` is the fixed gain floor supplied
by reached-row localization.  The same conclusion applies to the normalized
condition

```text
g_i <= eta * (1-O_i),
```

because `1-O_i<=1`.  In particular, when `eta<gamma/2`, every such repair has
root displacement at least `gamma/(8*M)`, independently of reset mesh and
semantic-source distance.

This rules out a direct use of the positive-collision atom roots in the
opponent-absorption-normalized compiler.  It does not rule out a macroscopic
root-changing construction which retains two hazard labels and closes its
artificial Bellman seams.

There is also a complementary positive fact.  In the actual
positive-collision orientation, the same localized roots already have two
fixed persistent labels: the observer Quits surely, while any fixed other
member of the collision terminal Quits with probability at least `lower`.
Moreover, a word of these roots forgets every terminal annotation except the
observer's cap, and forgets that last coordinate at geometric rate
`(1-lower)^K`.  Thus neither clock production on these selected rows nor
artificial terminal-annotation stability is the remaining problem in this
orientation.  This does not supply conditioned behavioral-port provenance.
For the artificial word considered below, the obstruction is already at the
**head**:
every such root has candidate debt at least `gamma` for the selected other
player, for every artificial successor pair.  A small-initial-debt
construction would therefore need a separate bridge into this clock-rich
word.  The revised local question is earlier than that issue: it first asks

## 3. Sure-opponent tail invariance

Fix a product root `q`, distinct players `o` and `i`, and suppose

```text
q_o(Quit)=1.
```

Let `u,w` be arbitrary continuation payoff vectors.  Then

```text
Q_i(q,u)=Q_i(q,w),
C_i(q,u)=C_i(q,w),
Succ_i(q,u)=Succ_i(q,w),
g_i(q,u)=g_i(q,w).                                      (3.1)
```

Here `Q_i,C_i,Succ_i,g_i` denote the forced-Quit payoff, forced-Continue
payoff, prescribed mixed payoff, and coordinate Nash defect.

### Proof

Whether player `i` is forced to Quit, forced to Continue, or uses its
prescribed marginal, player `o` still Quits surely.  The current row therefore
absorbs with probability one.  The all-Continue continuation branch has
probability zero, so neither endpoint nor their prescribed mixture reads the
continuation vector.  Taking their maximum and subtracting the mixture proves
the last equality.  This argument uses only `i != o`.

Equivalently, the player-`i` opponent Continue coefficient is exactly

```text
O_i(q)=0.                                               (3.2)
```

Thus this is the binding boundary of the general tail-Lipschitz estimate, not
a continuity estimate with a loose constant.

## 4. Application to the positive-target reached row

Fix one rank returned by positive-target reached-row localization.  Let `q`
be its literal live root, `u` its literal next-tail prescribed payoff, and
`g_i(q,u)` the selected fixed player's conditional coordinate defect.

The exact reached-row identity gives

```text
globalGain_i = liveMass * g_i(q,u).
```

Since `0<=liveMass<=1` and `globalGain_i>=gamma>0`,

```text
g_i(q,u) >= gamma.                                     (4.1)
```

The pure-time observer Quits surely at its selected stopping date and the
selected player differs from that observer.  Hence (3.1) applies.  For every
artificial continuation `w`, without any closeness assumption,

```text
g_i(q,w)=g_i(q,u)>=gamma.                              (4.2)
```

### Consequence 4A: no tail-only normalized repair

At the literal root, `O_i(q)=0`, so the normalized target would require

```text
g_i(q,w) <= eta*(1-O_i(q)) = eta.
```

This is impossible for every `eta<gamma`.  In particular, arbitrary
nonsemantic Bellman annotations do not repair this atom row.  The root itself
must change.

This strengthens the unchanged-root/unchanged-tail obstruction stored in
`PureTimeReachedRowLocalization.lean`: on this sure-observer row the literal
tail is not the issue at all.

## 5. Quantitative root-displacement floor

Assume all terminal rewards and the two displayed continuation vectors are
coordinatewise bounded in absolute value by `M`.  Positivity of `gamma`
forces `M>0`.

For two product roots `q,qhat`, define marginal distance

```text
D(q,qhat) = sum_j |q_j(Quit)-qhat_j(Quit)|.
```

Let `q` have `q_o(Quit)=1`, let `i!=o`, and let `u,w` be bounded tails.  Then

```text
|g_i(q,u)-g_i(qhat,w)| <= 4*M*D(q,qhat).               (5.1)
```

### Proof

Couple each pair of Bernoulli marginals maximally and take the product
coupling.  The probability that some coordinate differs is at most `D` by
the union bound.

For either forced action of player `i`, couple only the opponents.  On the
event that no opponent coordinate differs, the new observer also Quits and
the same terminal coalition occurs on both sides.  The two tails are then
irrelevant.  On the disagreement event the two payoff variables lie in
`[-M,M]`.  Therefore each forced endpoint changes by at most `2*M*D`.

The same full-action coupling bounds the two prescribed successor payoffs by
`2*M*D`: when no coordinate differs, the old sure observer makes both sides
absorb at the same terminal; otherwise the payoff difference is at most
`2*M`.  The maximum of two numbers changes by no more than the larger endpoint
change.  Hence

```text
|max(Q,C)-max(Qhat,Chat)| <= 2*M*D,
|Succ-Succhat|            <= 2*M*D.
```

Adding these estimates proves (5.1).  Notice that arbitrary tail displacement
is already included: the repaired tail is read only on an event where the
new observer fails to match the old sure Quit, which belongs to the coupling
disagreement event.

### Consequence 5A: normalized repair is macroscopically root-changing

Suppose the literal positive-target row has (4.1), while a repaired root and
tail obey

```text
g_i(qhat,w) <= eta*(1-O_i(qhat)).
```

Since `1-O_i(qhat)<=1`, (5.1) gives

```text
gamma <= eta + 4*M*D(q,qhat),
```

and therefore

```text
D(q,qhat) >= (gamma-eta)/(4*M).                        (5.2)
```

For `eta<gamma/2`,

```text
D(q,qhat) >= gamma/(8*M).                              (5.3)
```

The lower bound is independent of the stopping-law reset scale, frozen mesh,
or terminal-semantic source error.

### Consequence 5B: the canonical own-marginal repair has a signed toll

Thin the localized subsequence once more so that the selected player's better
pure action is fixed.  Keep every opponent marginal and the artificial tail
fixed, and change only player `i`'s marginal toward that better action.  The
two pure endpoint payoffs and their maximum `B_i` do not change.  For every
own mixing probability,

```text
prescribed payoff_i = B_i - g_i.
```

If the old gap is at least `gamma` and the repaired gap is at most `eta`, the
repair therefore has the exact signed displacement

```text
Uhat_i-U_i = g_i-ghat_i >= gamma-eta,
Bhat_i-B_i = 0.                                      (5.4)
```

Thus the most direct root repair is not an unsigned perturbation: it makes a
fixed positive prescribed-payoff move in one fixed coordinate.  Any bounded
chronology using infinitely many such repairs needs compensating exact
Bellman motion or prescribed seams.

This repair preserves the sure observer.  It also preserves the fixed
collision-member clock introduced below unless the selected player is that
member and its better action is Continue.  In that exceptional dropout case,
if the endpoint action gap is `A>0`, the repaired Quit probability is at most
`eta/A`; because the old Nash defect is at least `gamma` and the old Quit
probability is at most one, `A>=gamma`, so the retained hazard is at most
`eta/gamma`.  Hence the canonical repair has the exact dichotomy:

```text
retain the two displayed labels and pay the signed move (5.4),
or remove the only displayed collision partner at vanishing scale.
```

This is a statement about the canonical own-coordinate endpoint repair.  A
simultaneous multi-player Nashification can change the endpoint payoffs and
is not covered by that exact identity.  It does satisfy the following weaker
dichotomy.

### Consequence 5C: arbitrary repair is cross-player or signed

For a repaired root `qhat`, define the selected player's opponent-marginal
displacement

```text
E_i(q,qhat) = sum_{j!=i} |q_j(Quit)-qhat_j(Quit)|.
```

Let the old and repaired diagonal one-row pairs in coordinate `i` be
`(U_i,B_i)` and `(Uhat_i,Bhat_i)`, and assume the repaired gap is at most
`eta<gamma`.  The same coupling as in Section 5, now coupling only the
opponents, gives

```text
|Bhat_i-B_i| <= 2*M*E_i(q,qhat).                       (5.5)
```

Arbitrary displacement of the repaired tail is still covered: the old sure
observer means that a tail can be read on the repaired side only on an
observer-mismatch event, already counted in `E_i`.

Every one-row semantic pair satisfies `U=B-g`.  Hence

```text
Uhat_i-U_i
 = (Bhat_i-B_i) + (g_i-ghat_i)
 >= gamma-eta-2*M*E_i(q,qhat).                         (5.6)
```

It follows that every simultaneous repair obeys the exact alternative

```text
E_i(q,qhat) >= (gamma-eta)/(4*M),

or

Uhat_i-U_i >= (gamma-eta)/2.                           (5.7)
```

Thus simultaneous Nashification can evade the signed prescribed toll only by
a fixed collective change in the selected player's **opponent** law.  The
remaining open issue is whether that cross-player move can retain fixed
fractions of both displayed clock labels and close a bounded artificial
Bellman return.

## 6. Two fixed clocks and a geometric tail-forgetting word

Now retain the actual positive-collision hypotheses.  Thus the fixed


At every localized rank let `q_n` be the literal root at the selected reached
row.  The stored stage mass factorizes as

```text
stageMass_n(T) = liveMass_n * rootMass(q_n,T) >= lower.
```

Since `liveMass_n<=1`,

```text
rootMass(q_n,T) >= lower.                            (6.1)
```

The pure-time observer satisfies `q_n,o(Quit)=1`.  Since the exact-terminal
event `T` is contained in the event that `b` Quits, (6.1) also gives

```text
q_n,b(Quit) >= lower.                               (6.2)
```

Consequently the sequence consisting of one such selected root from each
rank has two fixed persistent labels in a pointwise form stronger than
nonsummability.  Its joint survival is zero at every row.  After deleting
`o`, survival over `K` consecutive selected roots is at most
`(1-lower)^K`; after deleting any player other than `o`, the sure observer is
still present and survival is zero.  Hence joint and every one-player-deleted
survival vanish from every suffix.

Each selected root is literal at its own reached row.  Concatenating roots
from different ranks defines a new executable root sequence, but does not by
itself prove that this sequence is the conditioned residual profile of the
preceding reset packet.  The construction below concerns its exact artificial
prefix maps and clocks, not that missing behavioral-port identification.

The same rows have an exact tail-forgetting property.  Write `Phi_q` for the
terminal-semantic prefix map.  For arbitrary successor pairs `S,T` and a root
`q` with `q_o(Quit)=1`,

```text
(Phi_q(S)).U = (Phi_q(T)).U,
(Phi_q(S)).B_i = (Phi_q(T)).B_i       for i != o,
|(Phi_q(S)).B_o-(Phi_q(T)).B_o|
  <= O_o(q) * |S.B_o-T.B_o|.                         (6.3)
```

The first two statements hold because current absorption is certain for the
prescribed action and for either forced action of every `i!=o`.  For the
observer, only the event that every outsider Continues can read its successor
cap.  In the positive-collision row, the exact terminal event `T` includes an
outsider and has root mass at least `lower`; therefore

```text
O_o(q_n) <= 1-lower.                                 (6.4)
```

Iterating (6.3)--(6.4) through a word of `K` selected roots proves that its
head prescribed vector and all nonobserver cap coordinates are completely
independent of the terminal annotation, while the two possible head observer
caps differ by at most

```text
(1-lower)^K * |S.B_o-T.B_o|.                         (6.5)
```

This is an explicit source-tail modulus on the exact artificial prefix maps,
using the same literal roots which supply the two clocks.  It does not compare
two conditioned behavioral ports: no semantic-state-only identification is
being made.

There is a sharp head obstruction.  For the fixed player `i` selected by
reached-row localization, (3.1) and (4.1) show that **every** successor pair
`S` satisfies

```text
debt_i(Phi_q(S)) = g_i(q,S.U with its displayed cap) >= gamma. (6.6)
```

More directly, both the prescribed payoff and the two pure endpoints for
`i` are independent of `S`, so the candidate debt is exactly the same fixed
one-row defect as at the literal tail.  Hence no exact artificial block whose
first root is one of these selected rows can meet the chronological initial
debt bound when `eta<gamma`.

Equations (6.2)--(6.5) remove the non-survival and artificial endpoint-tail
sensitivity of the positive-collision word.  They do not identify that word
with the residual stopping-law port of a preceding packet.  Equation (6.6)
already isolates a necessary remaining producer as a **small-debt,
source-matched entry bridge** into the clock-rich word.  Such a bridge must
precede the first selected row, retain the required provenance, and pay its
artificial seam and direct-debt accounts within the requested suffix-uniform
budget.

## 6A. Exact ray-matched one-block reprojection

The local question does have a complete positive answer when the supplied
reached source lies on the same complete stopping-law reset ray as the frozen
packet.  This is narrower than semantic-pair closeness, but it isolates an
exact missing selection hypothesis rather than another Bellman estimate.

Fix a behavioral profile `sigma`, a mover `m`, and a replacement stopping law
`tau` for `m`.  For `a in [0,1]`, let

```text
sigma^a = update sigma m (complete-law mixture of sigma_m and tau at weight a).
```

Let `b` be the frozen ray coordinate and `a` the reached ray coordinate.
Assume

```text
0 <= a,b <= h <= 1/2.                                  (6A.1)
```

Let `delta` be a chosen coordinatewise bound on both coordinates of the
initial terminal semantic discrepancy:

```text
|x^a_0.U_i-x^b_0.U_i| <= delta,
|x^a_0.B_i-x^b_0.B_i| <= delta       for every i.
```

Fix a finite length `L`.  For each component law and cutoff `t<=L`, write
`S_t,T_t` for survival of the source and target component of mover `m`, and

```text
M_t(c)=(1-c)S_t+cT_t.
```

Assume the finite port condition number

```text
M_t(a)>=rho,   M_t(b)>=rho>0       for every t<=L.      (6A.2)
```

All opponents use exactly the same strategies on the two sides.

### Proposition 6A (ray-matched block)

Let `x^c_t` be the terminal semantic pair of the literal residual profile of
`sigma^c` after `t` all-Continue rows, and let `q^c_t` be that profile's
literal live root.  Then the block

```text
(x^a_0,q^a_0,x^a_1,...,q^a_{L-1},x^a_L)
```

has all of the following properties.

1. Its first pair is exactly the reached source `Sem(sigma^a)`.
2. Every internal identity is exact:

   ```text
   x^a_t = Phi_(q^a_t)(x^a_(t+1)).                     (6A.3)
   ```

3. Its terminal pair `x^a_L` is the semantic pair of an actual residual
   behavioral profile and hence belongs to the carrier.  The frozen/reached
   residual ports are mixtures of the same two **conditional** component
   laws.  Their posterior coordinates are `F_L(a),F_L(b)` and may be order
   one, so this does not prove that the same small-ray theorem can be restarted
   there or that the endpoint lies on the next canonical frozen ray.  Their
   direct posterior difference is nevertheless a valid finite-port parameter
   for Proposition 6B.
4. In the initial unconditioned comparison, the source and replacement
   component laws, mover, observer, pure-time label, terminal orientation,
   and complete-law provenance are unchanged.  This does not assert that the
   pure time or terminal orientation survives at the residual endpoint.
5. If all terminal rewards have absolute value at most `R`, then at the
   terminal seam

   ```text
   |x^a_L.U_i-x^b_L.U_i| <= 2*R*h/rho^2,
   |x^a_L.B_i-x^b_L.B_i| <= 2*R*h/rho^2,
   |debt_i(x^a_L)-debt_i(x^b_L)| <= 4*R*h/rho^2.       (6A.4)
   ```

   At the initial seam the given semantic error `delta` may be used instead.
6. On every interval `s<=u<=L`, joint survival and every one-player-deleted
   survival differ from the frozen `b`-block by at most

   ```text
   min(1, L*h/rho^2).                                  (6A.5)
   ```

Thus one unified valid modulus is

```text
omega(h,delta)=delta + max(4*R,L)*h/rho^2.             (6A.6)
```

For a family of packets this tends to zero under the explicit condition

```text
delta -> 0,       max(R,L(h))*h/rho(h)^2 -> 0.         (6A.7)
```

Here `R` is table-fixed; it is displayed in (6A.7) only to show the semantic
constant.

### Proof

Items 1--3 are literal profile facts.  Conditioning `sigma^a` on `t`
all-Continue outcomes produces its actual shifted residual profile, so the
terminal semantic prefix identity gives (6A.3).  No artificial semantic pair
or off-path root is introduced.

The posterior weight of the target component at cutoff `t` is

```text
F_t(c)=c*T_t/M_t(c).
```

Direct cancellation gives the two-prior identity

```text
F_t(a)-F_t(b)
 = S_t*T_t*(a-b)/(M_t(a)*M_t(b)).                       (6A.8)
```

Since `S_t,T_t<=1`, (6A.1)--(6A.2) imply

```text
|F_t(a)-F_t(b)| <= h/rho^2.                            (6A.9)
```

The conditional hazard is the convex combination of the two component
hazards with posterior weight `F_t(c)`.  Hence only mover `m` changes between
`q^a_t` and `q^b_t`, and its Quit-probability change is at most (6A.9).
Telescoping products over the rows in any interval proves (6A.5).  If the
deleted player is `m`, the two deleted clocks agree exactly; otherwise the
same one-coordinate telescope applies.  The joint clock is identical in
form.

At time `L`, the two actual residual profiles use the same conditional source
and target component laws with mixture weights `F_L(a),F_L(b)`.  Couple their
component labels.  The disagreement probability is at most (6A.9).  For any
fixed behavioral deviation of any player, the resulting payoff variables are
in `[-R,R]`, so their expectations differ by at most `2R*h/rho^2`.  This is
uniform in the deviation; taking the supremum proves the cap bound in (6A.4),
while the prescribed bound is the same coupling without a deviation.  The
debt bound follows by subtraction.

Finally, complete stopping-law mixtures are affine at the level of the full
terminal outcome law.  This is an **initial whole-profile** statement.
Suppose the original atom alternative at `sigma^0`
uses terminal label `C`, mover `m`, replacement `tau`, and either its
prescribed or rectangle orientation.  Replacing `sigma^0` by `sigma^a` while
keeping the full endpoint `sigma^1` multiplies the same signed terminal atom
by exactly `1-a`.  This remains true after applying the same observer
pure-time response in both rectangle profiles.  Thus, from `a<=1/2`, the same
terminal label and orientation retain at least half the original charge.  In
the rectangle arm the small endpoint-debt field is unchanged because its
full-replacement endpoint is still `sigma^1`.  This proves item 4 only at the
initial literal atom interface, without an argmax-stability assumption.  It
does not transport the atom through the conditioning cutoff.

### Proposition 6B (length-free all-interval sharpening)

The reviewed estimate (6A.5) is valid but not sharp.  Under the same
hypotheses, define the direct posterior-port distance

```text
Pi_L(a,b)=max_(0<=s<=L) |F_s(a)-F_s(b)|.
```

Every interval `s<=u<=L` has joint and one-player-deleted survival difference
at most `Pi_L(a,b)`.  In particular this is at most

```text
min(1,h/rho^2),                                        (6B.1)
```

with no factor `L`.  Consequently one may replace (6A.6)--(6A.7) by

```text
omega_port(delta,Pi)=delta + max(4*R,1)*Pi,
omega_sharp(h,delta)=delta + max(4*R,1)*h/rho^2,       (6B.2)

delta -> 0,              h/rho(h)^2 -> 0.              (6B.3)
```

This sharpening is new after the independent review of Proposition 6A and
has not yet been independently checked.

#### Proof

At suffix start `s`, the mover's **entire conditional residual stopping law**
under entrance weight `c` is

```text
(1-F_s(c))*mu^S_s + F_s(c)*mu^T_s.
```

For any event `E` determined by that residual law, including survival through
the interval `[s,u)`, its probability difference between `a` and `b` is

```text
(F_s(a)-F_s(b))*(P_muT_s(E)-P_muS_s(E)).
```

Its absolute value is therefore at most `|F_s(a)-F_s(b)|`, which is bounded
by (6A.9).  The opponents' interval-survival factor is common.  Multiplying
by that factor proves (6B.1) for joint survival and for deletion of any player
other than `m`; after deleting `m`, the two clocks agree exactly.  This is a
single complete-law coupling at the suffix entrance, rather than a rowwise
telescope, so packet length does not enter.

The same coupling already proves (6A.4), and debt is still the difference of
the cap and prescribed coordinates.  Taking the maximum of the clock
constant `1` and semantic constant `4*R` gives (6B.2)--(6B.3).

### Proposition 6C (posterior amplification pays a mover clock)

There is an exact local dichotomy at every cutoff.  If `0<a<=h` and, for some
`beta>0`,

```text
F_L(a) >= beta,
```

then the mover's actual survival through the prefix is at most

```text
M_L(a) <= h/beta.                                      (6C.1)
```

Hence actual joint survival and every player-deleted survival except deletion
of `m` are at most `h/beta`.  The deleted-`m` survival is exactly the frozen
deleted-`m` survival because all opponents use the same laws on both sides.
In particular, if that frozen deleted-`m` survival is at most `1-c`, then the
actual block has

```text
deleted m:       survival <= 1-c,
deleted i != m:  survival <= h/beta.                   (6C.2)
```

Indeed, the posterior identity `M_L(a)*F_L(a)=a*T_L` and `T_L<=1` give
`M_L(a)<=a/beta<=h/beta`.  Every relevant survival product contains the
mover factor, except when the mover itself is deleted.

Thus Bayesian amplification is not a loss of all clock information: it
converts the mover port into a nearly complete finite-prefix hazard while
leaving the frozen opponent-only clock unchanged.  This does **not** solve
reconnection.  The terminal conditional semantic pair is unweighted, its
posterior may be order one, and the initial terminal orientation may already
have disappeared.  Proposition 6C supplies a whole-prefix clock dichotomy,
not the required residual atom or a reusable next source.

### Proposition 6D (simultaneous radial-port version)

The length-free argument is not restricted to one mover.  For each player
`j`, let the frozen and reached laws be mixtures of the same two complete
component laws with entrance weights `b_j,a_j`.  At suffix `s`, write their
posterior component weights as `F_(j,s)(b_j),F_(j,s)(a_j)` and put

```text
Pi_s=sum_j |F_(j,s)(a_j)-F_(j,s)(b_j)|,
Pi_L=max_(s<=L) min(1,Pi_s).                            (6D.1)
```

Then at every suffix start `s`:

1. the total-variation distance between the two complete residual product
   profiles is at most `min(1,Pi_s)`;
2. every future joint-survival event and every one-player-deleted survival
   event differs by at most `min(1,Pi_s)`;
3. prescribed coordinates differ by at most `2*R*min(1,Pi_s)`; and
4. player `i`'s cap differs by at most
   `2*R*min(1,sum_(j != i)|F_(j,s)(a_j)-F_(j,s)(b_j)|)`, hence by
   `2*R*min(1,Pi_s)`.

The proof independently couples each player's latent component label at the
suffix and takes a union bound.  Conditional on all labels agreeing, the
entire residual product profile agrees.  For a fixed deviation by player `i`,
only opponent labels enter its cap experiment, which proves item 4 before
taking the supremum over deviations.

If all mixed suffix survivals on both sides are at least `rho` and
`sum_j|a_j-b_j|<=H`, then

```text
Pi_L <= min(1,H/rho^2).                                (6D.2)
```

Thus a simultaneous radial reset with total entrance-weight error `H=O(h)`
has a length-free finite-port modulus under `h/rho^2 -> 0`.  As in the
one-mover theorem, this is conditional on a shared component fiber and says
nothing by itself about residual atom orientation or the next canonical ray.

### Proposition 6E (exact residual atom-reconnection account)

Posterior amplification by itself does not destroy a fixed terminal
orientation.  The exact losses are instead the part of that terminal atom
already spent before the cutoff and the difference between the two component
survival ports.

Let `P^0,P^1` be the complete source and full-replacement profile laws used by
one prescribed atom, let `E_C` be its fixed terminal event, and let

```text
r_C=reward(C)_observer.
```

For the all-Continue cutoff event `H_L`, assume both component survival
probabilities are positive and write

```text
S=P^0(H_L),                  T=P^1(H_L),
c_0=P^0(E_C | H_L),         c_1=P^1(E_C | H_L),
A_0=(P^0(E_C)-P^1(E_C))*r_C,
A_pre=(P^0(E_C and not H_L)-P^1(E_C and not H_L))*r_C.
```

Let the actual reached entrance law be the complete-law mixture
`P^a=(1-a)P^0+aP^1`, let

```text
M=(1-a)S+aT,
F=aT/M,
```

and compare its residual law after `H_L` with the residual full endpoint
`P^1(.|H_L)`.  The same terminal label and prescribed orientation then have
the exact residual atom

```text
A_res
 = (1-F)*(c_0-c_1)*r_C
 = (1-a)/M * (A_0-A_pre-(S-T)*c_1*r_C).              (6E.1)
```

#### Proof

Splitting the terminal event before and after the cutoff gives

```text
A_0
 = A_pre + (S*c_0-T*c_1)*r_C
 = A_pre + S*(c_0-c_1)*r_C + (S-T)*c_1*r_C.          (6E.2)
```

Conditioning the entrance mixture gives target posterior `F`, so its residual
law is `(1-F)P^0(.|H_L)+F P^1(.|H_L)`.  Comparison with the full residual
endpoint multiplies the conditional component atom by `1-F`.  Substituting
`1-F=(1-a)S/M` into (6E.2) proves (6E.1).

If rewards are bounded by `R`, `0<=a<=h<1`, and

```text
e_L=|A_pre|+R*|S-T|,
```

then, whenever `A_0>e_L`, the same orientation obeys

```text
A_res >= (1-h)*(A_0-e_L),                             (6E.3)
```

because `0<M<=1`, `|c_1|<=1`, and `(1-a)/M>=1-a>=1-h`.
Thus a fixed positive atom is residually reusable with asymptotically full
charge provided

```text
h -> 0,
|A_pre|+R*|S-T| -> 0.                                 (6E.4)
```

In the project's prescribed-arm normalization, if

```text
K*A_0 >= q/2
```

for fixed `q>0`, then (6E.4) implies eventually

```text
K*A_res >= q/4.
```

Thus the same terminal label witnesses the prescribed alternative at halved
charge `q/2`: the output predicate at charge `q/2` requires exactly the
displayed normalized atom lower bound `q/4`.  More quantitatively, with
`e_L=|A_pre|+R|S-T|`, it is enough that

```text
h*(q/2)+(1-h)*K*e_L <= q/4,                           (6E.4a)
```

because (6E.3) then gives
`K*A_res >= (1-h)*(q/2-K*e_L) >= q/4`.  Thus `q/4` is
the allowable combined normalized loss from the original `q/2` threshold;
it is not a claim that the output charge is `q/4`.

For a rectangle atom, the original threshold is `K*A_0>=q/4` and the
charge-`q/2` output threshold is `K*A_res>=q/8`.  The analogous sufficient
condition is

```text
h*(q/4)+(1-h)*K*e_L <= q/8.                           (6E.4b)
```

This rectangle conclusion remains conditional on including the common
observer response in `P^0,P^1` and choosing a cutoff before that response
absorbs; no such cutoff or residual rectangle port is selected here.

No posterior floor appears in (6E.3): when source survival is small, the
conditional component atom is amplified by the reciprocal source survival,
exactly cancelling the small source posterior.  The Bayes counterexamples
escape through the `|S-T|` term (and, in other examples, may escape because
the fixed terminal atom has already occurred before the cutoff).

Equivalently, failure of residual orientation has the sharp finite-block
trichotomy.  If `A_0>=A_*>0`, `a<=h<1`, and

```text
A_res < (1-h)*A_*/2,
```

then at least one of

```text
|A_pre| >= A_*/4,
R*|S-T| >= A_*/4                                      (6E.5)
```

holds.  Otherwise (6E.3) gives the opposite inequality.  This isolates the
remaining selection problem: choose a positive-survival cutoff before the
fixed terminal orientation is spent and with source/replacement survival
mismatch `o(1)`.  Proposition 6E does not prove that the checked atom packet
has such a cutoff, nor does it place the residual law on the next canonical
frontier ray.  In the rectangle arm the same calculation applies only after
including the common observer response in both component laws and choosing a
cutoff before its pure-time absorption; that additional selection is not
claimed here.

### Proposition 6F (one-shot atom cannot both fire and reconnect on its own ray)

The two error terms in Proposition 6E are genuine.  There is a fixed rational
three-player game and a fixed prescribed atom for which every literal cutoff
before the atom row retains the atom but has no atom-generated mover clock,
whereas the first cutoff containing that row has mover hazard of order `h`
and residual atom exactly zero.  This is a sharp conditional impossibility for
**reusing the same ray**, not an instance of the positive-minimum frontier and
not a negative answer to the existential local question.

Let the players be `m,a,o`.  Give `m,a` reward zero everywhere.  Give observer
`o` reward `-1` at terminal `{m}` and zero at every other terminal.  In the
source component, `m` Never Quits.  In the target component, at date zero `m`
Quits with probability `1/2`; conditional on Continue it Never Quits.  Player
`a` Continues at date zero and Quits surely at date one; `o` Never Quits.

The source terminal is `{a}` surely.  The target terminal is `{m}` with
probability `1/2` and `{a}` with probability `1/2`.  Therefore terminal
`C={m}` has the positive prescribed orientation

```text
A_0=(0-1/2)*(-1)=1/2.                                 (6F.1)
```

For entrance target weight `h`, the literal mover Quit probability at date
zero is `h/2`.  Thus the one-row radial packet carries an order-`h` labelled
mover hazard.  Before date zero, the same source/target atom is still (6F.1)
but no positive-length part of this word has been executed.  Conditional on
all Continue at date zero, however, both component residual mover laws are
identically Never.  Their conditional terminal laws are both the point mass
on `{a}`, and hence

```text
A_res=0.                                               (6F.2)
```

In the notation of Proposition 6E, `A_pre=A_0`, while the conditional atom is
zero.  The atom was spent in the prefix rather than destabilized by semantic
source error or an ill-defined posterior.

Consequently, a useful `o(h)`-clock-loss theorem cannot obtain both current
clock progress and residual same-atom reuse merely by truncating the supplied
complete-law ray.  It needs a **fresh-atom reconnection theorem** at the
reached endpoint, or a different packet whose clock is not paid by exhausting
the retained terminal orientation.  A constructor free to choose a new
behavioral continuation is not ruled out, and no tangent family or positive
minimum is asserted.

### Exact limitation

The proposition does not infer `a` or the component port from the semantic
pair `x^a_0`.  Different complete stopping-law ports can have the same
terminal semantic pair, and conditioning can separate them by order one.
Consequently the hypothesis

```text
reached source = Sem(sigma^a) for the same component laws
```

is strictly stronger than `|reached source-frozen source|<=delta`.  Likewise,
the atom access does not provide (6A.2) with a rate compatible with a growing
`L`.  Removing the same-ray and finite-reach hypotheses is one unresolved
part of the one-block question.  A separate unresolved part is
**atom-terminal reusability**: conditioning at time `L` can pass the observer's
pure stopping time and can spend the fixed terminal atom in the prefix.
Proposition 6E shows that order-one posterior coordinates are not by
themselves an atom obstruction; the exact losses are prefix atom expenditure
and component-survival mismatch.  Actual carrier membership and semantic
endpoint proximity control neither field and do not place the residual port
on the next canonical packet ray.

### Proposition 6G (failed renewal is paid as an exact prefix-port displacement)

The failure trichotomy (6E.5) can be stated directly in terms of the actual
entrance law, rather than only the two frozen components.  This does not
produce a fresh atom, but it identifies an exact chronological statistic that
must move when the old atom is spent.

Retain the hypotheses and notation of Proposition 6E, assume `R>0`, and write

```text
E_pre = E_C and not H_L.
```

If `A_0>=A_*>0`, `a<=h<1`, and

```text
A_res < (1-h)*A_*/2,
```

then at least one of the two exact actual-source displacements obeys

```text
|P^a(E_pre)-P^0(E_pre)| >= a*A_*/(4*R),               (6G.1)
|P^a(H_L)-P^0(H_L)|   >= a*A_*/(4*R).                 (6G.2)
```

Indeed, Proposition 6E gives either `|A_pre|>=A_*/4` or
`R*|S-T|>=A_*/4`.  In the first case `A_pre` is nonzero, hence `r_C` is
nonzero, and

```text
|P^a(E_pre)-P^0(E_pre)|
  = a*|P^1(E_pre)-P^0(E_pre)|
  = a*|A_pre|/|r_C|
  >= a*A_*/(4*R).
```

In the second case,

```text
|P^a(H_L)-P^0(H_L)| = a*|T-S| >= a*A_*/(4*R).
```

Thus, if the radial coordinate also satisfies `a>=kappa*h` for a fixed
`kappa>0`, loss of the residual fixed-orientation atom forces an order-`h`
displacement in either the prefix terminal-event port or the cutoff-survival
port.  This is a **port displacement**, not an absorption or deleted-clock
lower bound: the sign may point in either direction, and (6G.1) concerns one
specified terminal event.  It is also compatible with the desired local
modulus, since an order-`h` displacement vanishes.  The proposition therefore
does not solve reconnection.  Its use is accounting: a proof cannot discard a
failed atom renewal as a purely semantic relabelling; the loss is visible in
one exact chronological statistic of the executed mixture.

### Proposition 6H (exact fixed-root entry criterion and boundary failure)

Semantic proximity does not make a fixed root prefix locally surjective.  The
precise obstruction can be read off one root.

Fix a product root `q`.  Let `0` denote the zero payoff vector and define, for
each player `i`,

```text
beta = product_j q_j(Continue),
O_i  = product_(j != i) q_j(Continue),
A_i  = Succ_i(q,0),
Q_i  = Quit_i(q,0),
C_i  = Continue_i(q,0).
```

For arbitrary real semantic pairs `x=(u,b)` and `z=(v,d)`, not yet requiring
carrier membership,

```text
x = Prefix_q(z)
```

holds if and only if, coordinatewise,

```text
u_i = A_i + beta*v_i,                                  (6H.1)
b_i = max(Q_i, C_i + O_i*d_i).                         (6H.2)
```

This is just the partition into nonempty root absorption and all Continue.
For the cap coordinate, forced Quit has no continuation branch, whereas
forced Continue reaches the tail cap exactly when every opponent Continues.

Consequently, if `beta>0`, the prescribed tail is uniquely forced:

```text
v_i=(u_i-A_i)/beta.                                    (6H.3)
```

For the cap coordinate, if `O_i>0`, exact entry requires `b_i>=Q_i` and

```text
b_i>Q_i  => d_i=(b_i-C_i)/O_i,
b_i=Q_i  => d_i<=(Q_i-C_i)/O_i.                        (6H.4)
```

If `O_i=0`, it instead requires the rigid equality

```text
b_i=max(Q_i,C_i),                                      (6H.5)
```

independently of the tail.  Carrier membership adds the nontrivial further
conditions that the selected `z` be an actual/limiting semantic pair and
that `d_i>=v_i`; (6H.1)--(6H.5) are only the algebraic prefix test.

This criterion exposes both condition numbers in the local question.  On a
continue-active branch, perturbing the current prescribed and cap coordinates
requires tail perturbations divided respectively by `beta` and `O_i`.
On a quit-active branch the current cap is pinned to `Q_i`, so no two-sided
neighborhood lies in the image of the fixed root at all.  Iterating a fixed
word replaces these coefficients by its joint and player-deleted survival
products.

The lack of local surjectivity already occurs between arbitrarily close
**actual** semantic pairs.  Take two players `i,j`.  Give player `i` reward
one only at terminal `{i,j}` and zero at every other terminal; give player
`j` reward zero everywhere.  In profile `sigma^p`, player `i` Never Quits,
while player `j` Quits at date zero with probability `p` and otherwise Never
Quits.  Then

```text
Sem(sigma^p) = ((0,0),(p,0)).                          (6H.6)
```

Indeed, player `i` earns zero as prescribed and can earn `p` by Quitting at
date zero; every later deviation earns zero on both branches.  At `p=1`, let
`q` be the literal first root.  Its forced-Quit value for player `i` is
`Q_i=1`.  Hence every pair in the image of `Prefix_q` has cap at least one,
by (6H.2), while `Sem(sigma^p)` has cap `p<1`.  Nevertheless its distance
from `Sem(sigma^1)` is `1-p`, which can be arbitrarily small.

This example is not positive-minimum atom/reset data and is not a negative
answer to the local question.  It proves the narrower entry fact: an exact
reprojector cannot keep the frozen first root solely by invoking semantic
continuity.  It must either select a compatible reached port, move the first
root (by exactly `1-p` in this example), or insert a separate bridge whose
own prefix image contains the reached source.

### Proposition 6I (an exact low-absorption block remains near the minimum set)

One part of terminal reusability follows from minimum debt without any atom
argument.  Let

```text
D_* = min_(z in X) D(z) > 0,
M_* = {z in X : D(z)=D_*},
n   = number of players,
```

where `D(z)` is total semantic debt and `X` is the compact terminal semantic
carrier.  Let an exact finite product-root word start at an actual semantic
pair `x`, end at an actual residual pair `z`, and have joint all-Continue
probability

```text
beta = product_(rows t) product_(players j) q_(t,j)(Continue) > 0.
```

If `x` is coordinatewise within `delta` of some minimum point `x_* in M_*`,
then

```text
D(z)-D_* <= [D_*(1-beta)+2*n*delta]/beta.              (6I.1)
```

Indeed, at every row and for every player, joint survival times the tail debt
is at most the current debt.  Iterating and then summing gives

```text
beta*D(z) <= D(x).                                     (6I.2)
```

Every debt coordinate is `cap-prescribed`, so semantic `delta`-closeness
gives

```text
D(x) <= D_*+2*n*delta.                                 (6I.3)
```

Combining (6I.2)--(6I.3) and subtracting `D_*` proves (6I.1).

There is a canonical game-dependent metric modulus converting this scalar
bound into distance from the whole minimum set.  In the coordinate sup
metric define, for `s>=0`,

```text
mu(s)=max { dist_infty(y,M_*) : y in X, D(y)<=D_*+s }.
```

Compactness makes the maximum well-defined, and

```text
mu(s) -> 0 as s downarrow 0.                           (6I.4)
```

For otherwise a sequence `s_k->0` and maximizers a fixed positive distance
from `M_*` would have a convergent carrier subsequence.  Continuity of `D`
would put its limit in `M_*`, contradicting continuity of distance to the
closed set `M_*`.

Writing `alpha=1-beta`, the actual terminal source therefore satisfies

```text
dist_infty(z,M_*)
 <= mu((D_* alpha+2*n*delta)/(1-alpha)).               (6I.5)
```

This tends to zero whenever the actual block absorption `alpha` and entry
error `delta` do.  More generally, if the actual joint survival differs from
a frozen survival by at most `omega_clock`, one may substitute any upper
bound on frozen absorption plus `omega_clock` for `alpha`.

The conclusion is intentionally distance to the **minimum set**, not to the
original base.  Multiple minimum points may carry different component fibers,
movers, observers, or terminal orientations.  Thus (6I.5) supplies a genuine
actual-terminal source and a quantitative minimum-debt tube, but it does not
make the old atom reusable or place the endpoint on the next canonical ray.
An isolated minimum, or atom/fiber access uniform over `M_*`, would be an
additional hypothesis under which this scalar part of reconnection closes.

#### Corollary 6IA (literal radial-packet specialization)

For a simultaneous frozen radial profile, let `a_j` be the effective outer
replacement weight of active marginal `j` and put `H=sum_j a_j`.  Couple all
component labels with the all-source profile.  The probability that any label
differs is at most `H`; hence, for rewards bounded by `R`, both prescribed and
cap coordinates differ from the all-source profile by at most `2*R*H`.
The cap bound holds uniformly over the deviating strategy before taking its
supremum.

If the all-source semantic pair is within `epsilon` of the frontier base, the
literal radial packet head is therefore within

```text
delta_head = epsilon+2*R*H
```

of that base.  Any positive-survival cutoff of the literal packet satisfies

```text
D(tail)-D_*
 <= [D_*(1-beta)+2*n*(epsilon+2*R*H)]/beta.            (6IA.1)
```

For the checked bounded radial weights, `H=O(h)` and `epsilon->0`.  Thus a
cutoff with `1-beta=O(h)` would return the literal residual source to the
minimum-set tube.  The existing frozen exposure theorem supplies the opposite
inequality—a lower bound of order `h` on absorption—but no matching upper
bound on total joint absorption at its selected cutoff.  Corollary 6IA must
therefore not be cited as a checked radial reconnection theorem; it isolates
one additional quantitative cutoff requirement.

### Proposition 6J (baseline absorption is an independent port datum)

The missing upper bound cannot be recovered from radial mesh, two labelled
clock lower bounds, and a fixed atom alone.

First, there is an exact general inequality.  Let a simultaneous complete-law
radial profile replace marginal `j` with effective component weight `a_j`,
and let `P^0` be the all-source profile.  Put

```text
ell = 1-product_j(1-a_j) <= H=sum_j a_j.
```

For every finite all-Continue cutoff event `H_L`, component-label coupling
gives

```text
|P^a(H_L)-P^0(H_L)| <= ell <= H.                      (6J.1)
```

Indeed, on the event that every component label is the source label, the two
profile samples agree exactly.  The complementary label event has probability
`ell`.  Hence, writing `alpha=1-P(H_L)` for prefix absorption,

```text
|alpha^a_L-alpha^0_L| <= H,
alpha^a_L >= alpha^0_L-H.                             (6J.2)
```

The analogous deleted-player estimate uses only nondeleted labels and is at
least as sharp.  Therefore an upper bound `alpha^a_L=O(h)` with `H=O(h)` is
equivalent, up to constants, to the **source baseline prefix absorption**
`alpha^0_L=O(h)`.  This is not a consequence of the radial label weights.

The necessity is witnessed by one fixed rational five-player reward table
and a sequence of arbitrarily fine exact packets.  Let the players be

```text
c, a, m, n, o.
```

All rewards are zero except

```text
r_o({m})=-1.
```

Fix an integer `N>=2` and put `h=1/N`.  In the common source profile:

- player `c` has Quit hazard `h/2` at dates `0,...,N-1`, and Never afterwards;
- player `a` Continues through date `N` and Quits surely at date `N+1`;
- `m,n,o` Never Quit.

For each reset label `m` and `n`, its full replacement Continues before date
`N`, Quits there with probability `1/2`, and Never Quits after surviving that
row.  Form the simultaneous outer packet by choosing each of these two full
replacement laws with independent complete-law weight `h`.

Let

```text
s_N=(1-1/(2*N))^N.
```

Every displayed marginal Quit probability through the cutoff after date `N`
is at most `h`: the `c` rows have hazard `h/2`, while the two reset marginals
have actual date-`N` hazard `h/2`.  Thus the literal packet mesh is `h->0`.

The source terminal law assigns mass `1-s_N` to `{c}` and mass `s_N` to
`{a}`.  The full `m` replacement instead assigns additional mass `s_N/2` to
`{m}`.  The union bound gives `s_N>=1-N/(2N)=1/2`, so the prescribed orientation
`C={m}` has the fixed atom

```text
A_0=(0-s_N/2)*(-1)=s_N/2 >= 1/4.                     (6J.3)
```

Both reset labels are nevertheless visible at order `h`.  After deleting
`c`, survival through the cutoff is `(1-h/2)^2`, so the deleted-`c` exposure
is

```text
1-(1-h/2)^2 = h-h^2/4 >= h/2.                        (6J.4)
```

After deleting either `m` or `n`, the other reset label remains and the
baseline `c` clock is also present.  After deleting `a` or `o`, both reset
labels and the baseline remain.  Also

```text
1/s_N=(1+1/(2*N-1))^N >= 1+N/(2*N-1) >= 3/2,
```

so `s_N<=2/3`.  Joint survival and every one-player-deleted survival except
the deleted-`c` value are therefore at most `2/3`.
Thus one common cutoff has joint and every-deleted exposure at least `h/2`,
with the two fixed reset labels `m,n`.

But the same cutoff has actual joint absorption

```text
alpha_N=1-s_N*(1-h/2)^2 >= 1-s_N >= 1/3.             (6J.5)
```

Conditioning on all Continue through the cutoff also filters every reset
target component that Quit at date `N`; surviving source and target mover
laws are all `Never`, and `a` Quits next.  Hence the residual fixed label
`{m}` has atom zero.  The fixed orientation was spent at the reset row rather
than transported to the residual port.

All probabilities and rewards in this construction are rational at each
`N`.  It is not a positive-minimum tangent family: in particular the reset
movers have zero debt, and the game has minimum debt zero.  Its exact scope is
the conditional obstruction requested here.  It proves that an `O(h)` upper
absorption cutoff and reusable orientation require additional
positive-minimum/source-fiber structure; neither follows from fine mesh,
two-label all-deleted exposure, and a fixed prescribed atom as finite packet
data.

### Proposition 6P (balanced debt return does not control semantic translation)

The Tier I availability problem cannot be reduced to balanced debt columns.
There is an exact two-player complete-law reset example whose two flat debt
directions cancel, whose simultaneous debt displacement is quadratic, but
whose full terminal semantic source moves linearly.

Let

```text
r({1})=(1,0),
r({2})=(0,1),
r({1,2})=(2,2).
```

The common source has both players Never Quit.  Its semantic pair is

```text
U^0=(0,0),       B^0=(1,1),       d^0=(1,1).         (6P.1)
```

For `0<=h<=1`, the player-1 radial reset mixes its source law `Never` with
Quit-surely-at-date-zero at complete-law weight `h`, leaving player 2 at
Never.  Direct pure-time optimization gives

```text
U^(1)=(h,0),
B^(1)=(1,1+h),
d^(1)=(1-h,1+h).                                     (6P.2)
```

Indeed, player 1 can guarantee its solo reward `1`.  Player 2 obtains
`1+h` by Quitting at date zero: it receives joint reward `2` on the
probability-`h` branch and solo reward `1` otherwise; waiting gives only
`1-h`.  Hence the exact normalized debt direction is `(-1,1)`.  By symmetry,
the player-2 reset has direction `(1,-1)`.  Both columns are flat and their
equal-weight sum is exactly zero.

Now apply both independent complete-law resets at the same weight `h`.  The
terminal law has each singleton with probability `h(1-h)`, the joint
coalition with probability `h^2`, and `Never` otherwise.  Therefore

```text
U^(12)=(h+h^2,h+h^2).
```

Against the other player's date-zero coin, Quitting at date zero gives
`(1-h)*1+h*2=1+h`, whereas every later finite quit gives `1-h` and Never gives
zero.  Thus

```text
B^(12)=(1+h,1+h),
d^(12)=(1-h^2,1-h^2).                                (6P.3)
```

Consequently

```text
d^(12)-d^0=(-h^2,-h^2)=o(h),
||Sem^(12)-Sem^0||_infinity=h+h^2.                   (6P.4)
```

The mesh is `h`, both fixed labels contribute hazard `h`, and total
absorption is `2h-h^2`; nevertheless the full semantic displacement divided
by `h` tends to one.  The surviving first-order motion is a simultaneous
translation of prescribed payoff and cap which debt balance cannot see.

This table has minimum total debt zero elsewhere (for example at sure joint
Quit), so it is not positive-minimum data and is not a negative answer to
Tier I.  Its exact scope is a route audit: frozen flat-circulation balance,
even together with quadratic debt return and the desired two labelled
hazards, does not itself imply an operationally sublinear availability loss
for any radius that controls distance to the chosen full semantic source.
An actual Tier I proof needs additional prescribed-payoff cancellation,
translation-invariant reusable port data, or a different stable radius.

### Proposition 6Q (the missing prescribed-circulation equation)

The preceding example is the two-coordinate instance of a general exact
first-order formula.  Let `J` be a finite set of movers, let `sigma` be a
common source profile, and let `tau_j` be one complete stopping-law
replacement for each `j in J`.  For `F subset J`, write `U_F` for the
prescribed payoff of the profile in which exactly the strategies in `F` are
fully replaced.  Independently mix replacement `j` into its source law with
weight `a_j in [0,1]`, and call the resulting prescribed payoff `U(a)`.

Complete-law mixture labels are independent, so exactly

```text
U(a)=sum_(F subset J)
       [product_(j in F) a_j * product_(j notin F)(1-a_j)] U_F.  (6Q.1)
```

If every terminal reward is bounded in absolute value by `R` and
`s=sum_j a_j`, then coordinatewise

```text
|U(a)-U_empty-sum_j a_j*(U_{ {j} }-U_empty)|
  <= 2*R*s^2.                                        (6Q.2)
```

To see the bound, sample the independent Bernoulli replacement set `F` and
put `K=|F|`.  Comparing (6Q.1) with the affine singleton expression produces
three nonnegative coefficient errors: the excess empty coefficient, the

```text
E[(K-1)1_(K>=2)]+E[K 1_(K>=2)]+Pr(K>=2)
 <= 2 E[K(K-1)]
 = 2 sum_(i!=j) a_i*a_j
 <= 2 s^2.
```

Multiplication by `R` proves (6Q.2).

For a frozen radial packet, take `a_j=h*w_j`, with `0<=w_j<=1`.  Then

```text

 = sum_j w_j*(U_{ {j} }-U_empty) + O(R*|J|^2*h).      (6Q.3)
```

Thus the balanced-debt equation

```text

```

does not by itself cancel the full semantic motion.  If the simultaneous debt
displacement is `o(h)`, then `B=U+d` and (6Q.3) show that prescribed payoff
and cap share the same remaining first-order translation

```text

```

Therefore a chosen-source Tier I radius can have `chi=o(h)` through the
literal radial construction only if this prescribed circulation also
vanishes asymptotically, or if the reusable port/radius is proved invariant
under the resulting translation.  The existing charged circulation stores
only the debt-tangent balance.  Proposition 6Q does not say the additional
equation is impossible for the positive-minimum family; it identifies the
extra finite-dimensional cancellation a successful selection must obtain.

### Proposition 6S (the frozen simultaneous face retains half the atom)

Corollary 6R by itself did not transport the atom inequality from the
original source through the other active radial resets.  That particular
gap can be closed by a direct terminal-law estimate.  This is still a
frozen-head statement; it does not address the conditioned successor.

Let `P0` be one frozen source, let `m` be the selected first mover, let `o`
be its atom observer, and let `T_j` be the full replacement of every active
mover `j`.  Let `h` be the frontier scale and `w_j in [0,1]` the fixed radial
weights.  Write

```text
a_j = h*w_j,
S   = sum_(j active) a_j,
n   = |I|.
```

Let `P` be the literal simultaneous radial packet profile.  Although its
behavioral marginal is written as a nested mixture, two applications of
`quittingTerminalOutcomeMass_stoppingLawMixture_eq` show that, for every
fixed environment and terminal outcome, it is terminal-law equivalent to
the single complete-law mixture

```text
(1-a_j)*(P0_j) + a_j*T_j.                           (6S.1)
```

This is only a terminal-law identity; no conditioned-hazard identity is
being asserted.

More generally, sequential application of the same affine identity gives
the following finite-face estimate.  If a profile is changed in finitely
many coordinates by complete-law mixtures of weights `a_j`, then for every
terminal outcome `C`,

```text
|Pr_new(C)-Pr_old(C)| <= sum_j a_j.                 (6S.2)
```

Indeed, one coordinate changes the outcome mass by
`a_j*(Pr_target(C)-Pr_source(C))`, whose absolute value is at most `a_j`;
then telescope over the coordinates.  If only opponents of player `o` are
changed and all rewards have absolute value at most `M`, the same one-switch
coupling, uniformly over every behavioral deviation of `o`, gives

```text
|U_new^o-U_old^o| <= 2*M*sum_j a_j,
|B_new^o-B_old^o| <= 2*M*sum_j a_j,
|d_new^o-d_old^o| <= 4*M*sum_j a_j.                 (6S.3)
```

The cap estimate follows by applying the payoff estimate to each fixed
deviation and then taking suprema.  These bounds concern unrestricted
behavioral deviations, not a finite controller class.

Put

```text
K  = |QuittingTerminalOutcome I|,
E0 = update P0 m T_m,
E  = update P  m T_m.
```

Assume the static alternative at this rank has charge `q>0` and error `e`.
For its prescribed branch, compare `P` with `P0` and `E` with `E0` in the
same displayed terminal atom.  For its rectangle branch, override `o` by
the same supplied pure-time response in all four profiles before making the
same comparison.  Equation (6S.2) yields in either branch

```text
K*A_new >= K*A_old - 2*K*M*S.                       (6S.4)
```

In the rectangle branch, (6S.3) also gives for the new full-replacement
endpoint

```text
d_new^o <= e + 4*M*S.                               (6S.5)
```

Here the observer override deletes any radial reset assigned to `o`, so the
profiles compared in (6S.5) differ only in opponent laws, as required.

Since `S<=n*h`, if

```text
2*K*M*n*h <= q/8,                                   (6S.6)
```

then the literal whole-packet profile `P`, with the same full target `T_m`,
satisfies

```text
HasQuittingStoppingLawVanishingDebtAtomAlternative
  reward P m o T_m (q/2) (e+4*M*n*h).               (6S.7)
```

For the prescribed arm, the old lower bound `q/2` loses at most `q/8`,
which is stronger than the new required bound `(q/2)/2=q/4`.  For the
rectangle arm, the old lower bound `q/4` loses at most `q/8`, exactly the new
required bound `(q/2)/4=q/8`.  The terminal and, in the rectangle arm, the
pure-time witness are the same witnesses supplied at that rank.

Take `M>0` to be any fixed reward bound.  Because `h` tends to zero, (6S.6)
holds eventually.  Since the decoder error `e` also tends to zero, the error
in (6S.7) vanishes.  Combining 6R and 6S therefore places a fixed positive
atom charge and one of the two persistent clock labels on the *same literal
frozen simultaneous packet*, without silently dropping the other active
resets.

This does not give the Tier-I restart.  The observer still need not equal the
second clock label, the rankwise terminal/pure-time witnesses need not
stabilize along one conditioned chronology, and after executing a cutoff the
actual residual port need not admit (6S.7) again.  Proposition 6S removes the
whole-face transport gap only at the initial frozen head.

### Proposition 6W (exact tail leakage in a finite atom splice)

One tempting next step is to retain a long enough prefix of the frozen source
and full-reset endpoint, attach the same reached continuation to both, and
declare the atom renewed.  The exact calculation shows why this is not yet a
valid restart theorem.

Fix two profiles `P,E` which differ only in the mover coordinate, an observer
`o`, and a nonempty terminal coalition `C`.  For a cutoff `L`, let

```text
a_P(L,C) = probability that P first stops as C before L,
a_E(L,C) = probability that E first stops as C before L,
beta_P(L) = probability P survives all L rows,
beta_E(L) = probability E survives all L rows.
```

Attach terminal profiles `Y_P,Y_E` after the respective first `L` root words.
Writing `p_Y(C)` for the terminal probability of `C` under a tail `Y`, direct
partition into absorption before `L` and all-Continue through `L` gives

```text
Pr[Prefix(P,L;Y_P)=C] - Pr[Prefix(E,L;Y_E)=C]
  = a_P(L,C)-a_E(L,C)
      + beta_P(L)*p_{Y_P}(C)-beta_E(L)*p_{Y_E}(C).       (6W.1)
```

Multiplication by `r_o(C)` is exactly the corresponding prescribed terminal
payoff atom.  In particular, attaching one common reached tail `Y` leaves the
term

```text
(beta_P(L)-beta_E(L))*p_Y(C)*r_o(C).                    (6W.2)
```

It does **not** cancel merely because the tail is common.  This differs from
`quittingTerminalPayoffDifferenceAtom_literalRootStack`: that checked theorem
places the *same root word* in front of two different tails, whereas the
reconnection attempt here compares the source word with the mover-reset word.

Because `C` is a finite terminal outcome,

```text
a_P(L,C) -> Pr_P(C),       a_E(L,C) -> Pr_E(C).         (6W.3)
```

Thus any fixed frozen atom can be captured, up to arbitrary loss, by one
finite pair of root words.  The clock cutoff from Proposition 6V may be
enlarged to this atom cutoff without losing either nonnegative hazard sum.
Nevertheless (6W.3) does not control (6W.2): `beta_P(L)` and `beta_E(L)` may
converge to different Never masses, and the reached tail may have positive
`C`-mass.  The full reset `E` is an order-one change even when the actual
radial packet `P` is only an `O(h)` perturbation of its frozen source.

Equation (6W.1) gives three honest ways a prescribed atom could survive a
reconnection:

```text
1. the reached tail has p_Y(C)=0;
2. the two retained words have beta_P(L)=beta_E(L);
3. separate source/endpoint tails are selected so that
   beta_P(L)*p_{Y_P}(C)-beta_E(L)*p_{Y_E}(C)
   has a controlled sign and size.                              (6W.4)
```

None of these is supplied by the current positive-minimum access.  The same
identity applies after the common pure-time override in the rectangle arm.
There is then a further, independent issue: even if the displayed terminal
atom is retained, the endpoint debt uses the observer's unrestricted
best-response cap.  A deviation may Continue past the retained prefix, so a
finite pure-time witness does not make that debt independent of the attached
opponent tail.

The finite-word holonomy estimates make the semantic part equally explicit.
Let `F_L` be the terminal-semantic prefix map of the source word.  Its
prescribed coordinate has Lipschitz coefficient `beta_P(L)` and player `i`'s
cap coordinate has coefficient at most the player-deleted survival
`beta_{P,-i}(L)`.  If the two labels of Proposition 6V each contribute at
least `kappa*h` in raw marginal hazard and `kappa*h<=1`, then

```text
beta_P(L), beta_{P,-i}(L) <= exp(-kappa*h) <= 1-kappa*h/2.       (6W.5)
```

Hence periodic repetition of this one source word has a unique literal
terminal semantic fixed point `z_per`, with exact all-behavior cap, and

```text
|z_per^U_i-z^U_i| <=
  |F_L(z)^U_i-z^U_i|/(kappa*h/2),
|z_per^B_i-z^B_i| <=
  |F_L(z)^B_i-z^B_i|/(kappa*h/2).                    (6W.6)
```

This gives an exact self-restarting actual profile and preserves both clock
labels, but it need not lie near the chosen frozen source and (6W.1) does not
give it the frozen atom.  If distance to the selected fiber is charged as
availability loss, then **using only** the uniform contraction gap
`kappa*h/2`, a residual-based proof of operationally sublinear loss needs an
`o(h^2)` upper estimate in (6W.6), not merely `o(h)`.  This is a sufficient
rate requirement for that argument, not a claim that every possible return
mechanism must satisfy it: the actual contraction gap could be larger, or a
different fiber-invariance argument could avoid dividing by `h`.
Proposition 6Q already identifies the missing first-order prescribed
circulation; it does not provide this stronger residual estimate.

I inspected the fixed-cutoff source-retaining results
`quittingFixedBlockResolvedHolonomyLift_isCompact` and
`quittingFixedCutoffResolvedHolonomyLift_isCompact` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Boundary/Holonomy/Compactness.lean`, together
with `QuittingBoundaryHolonomy.IsSelfSimilarAt` in
`Holonomy/SelfSimilarity.lean`.  They are consistent with (6W.5)--(6W.6),
but do not close this gate: the compact lifts concern fixed-cutoff exact
zero-boundary Nash--Bellman paths, while the frozen radial words are literal
behavioral prefixes and their cutoff may escape.  The self-similarity module
explicitly assumes, rather than produces, a strategically returning block.

Thus Proposition 6W is a reduction, not a Tier-I answer.  After Proposition
6V, the remaining local producer may be stated sharply as control of the
source/endpoint tail terms in (6W.1), the rectangle cap tail, and the
normalized source-fiber residual in (6W.6), on one forward-selectable family
of actual successors.

### Proposition 6X (one-row joint-reach equalization removes prescribed tail leakage)

There is a simple exact repair of the scalar leakage in (6W.1), although its
cost is not controlled by the present frontier.  It is useful because it
separates terminal-orientation renewal from availability.

Keep a prescribed-atom source/reset pair `P,E`, differing only in mover `m`,
and retain their first `L` root rows.  Assume their joint reach probabilities
`beta_P,beta_E` are positive and set

```text
beta_* = min(beta_P,beta_E),
c_P    = beta_*/beta_P,
c_E    = beta_*/beta_E.                              (6X.1)
```

Append one balance row.  Every player except `m` Continues, while `m` has
Continue probabilities `c_P,c_E` in the source and reset words respectively.
The two extended words then have the same joint reach `beta_*`.  Consequently
one arbitrary **common** reached tail contributes exactly the same amount to
every terminal coalition on the two sides and cancels from their prescribed
atom difference.

At the balance row, the only new terminal coalition is `{m}`.  Therefore, if
the retained terminal `C` is not `{m}`, its atom after the arbitrary common
tail is exactly

```text
(a_P(L,C)-a_E(L,C))*r_o(C).                         (6X.2)
```

If `C={m}`, use the distinct observer `o` as a common helper in the balance
row, with Continue probability `epsilon>0`.  The two extended reach
probabilities are then both `beta_* * epsilon`.  The new `{m}`-atom term is

```text
epsilon*(beta_P-beta_E)*r_o({m}),                   (6X.3)
```

so its absolute size is at most
`M*epsilon*|beta_P-beta_E|`.  Taking `epsilon` small suppresses this term;
taking `epsilon=1` incurs no helper hazard and is already adequate whenever
the reach mismatch is small.

The exact condition number of the repair is also transparent.  The only
nontrivial mover Quit probability inserted at the balance row is

```text
|beta_P-beta_E|/max(beta_P,beta_E)
  <= |beta_P-beta_E|/rho                           (6X.4)
```

when both reaches are at least `rho>0`.  In the singleton case the common
helper additionally has Quit probability `1-epsilon`.  Thus a low-cost
renewal follows from a reached-port survival match, while an uncontrolled
reach mismatch may require an order-one balance row.  This is the same
inverse-survival condition number that appears in the posterior estimates of
6A and 6L, now derived for the terminal-orientation seam itself.

By (6W.3), choose `L` so the truncated prescribed atom loses at most one
quarter of its fixed margin, while also extending beyond the clock cutoff of
6V.  Equations (6X.2)--(6X.3) then retain a fixed smaller charge after
attaching an arbitrary common actual successor, provided either `C!={m}` or
the singleton pollution in (6X.3) is kept below the remaining margin.  The
source and endpoint root words still differ only in mover `m`; the helper row
is common to both.  Hence this is a legal complete behavioral replacement
with literal prefix provenance.

This proposition concerns the **prescribed** atom arm.  In a rectangle arm,
the observer override changes the balance row and the endpoint-debt cap still
depends on unrestricted deviations into the attached tail.  A finite pure
Quit time localizes the terminal mass but not that best-response cap.  No
rectangle renewal is claimed here.

Nor does 6X solve Tier I.  The balance row is not part of the checked frozen
radial word, and (6X.4) is not known to be `o(h)` or even small.  Its source
head can therefore leave the chosen positive-minimum fiber by order one.  The
result says precisely that, in the prescribed branch, fixed terminal
orientation and arbitrary common-tail attachment are algebraically
compatible once one pays the survival-matching cost.  Producing that cost
within the availability budget and from the already reached source remains
open.

### Corollary 6Y (the exact prescribed-renewal dichotomy is Never-mass matching)

The cost in 6X has a clean asymptotic interpretation.  Let
`N_P,N_E` be the joint Never probabilities of the prescribed source and its
full mover-reset endpoint.  Then

```text
beta_P(L)-beta_E(L) -> N_P-N_E.                     (6Y.1)
```

Therefore, on any frozen-head subsequence for which
`|N_P-N_E|->0`, one may choose the atom/clock cutoff `L` far enough that

```text
K*|beta_P(L)-beta_E(L)|*|r_o(C)| -> 0               (6Y.2)
```

while the truncated prefix already retains an arbitrarily prescribed
fraction of the fixed terminal atom and both `kappa*h` hazard bounds.  Attach
the same arbitrary actual successor to the source and reset words.  Formula
(6W.1) and (6Y.2) then retain the prescribed atom with vanishing loss **with
no balance row at all**.  Thus equality of the two joint Never masses is a
sufficient tail-independent orientation adapter, even though the individual
conditioned ports may be far apart.

Conversely, suppose along a subsequence

```text
|N_P-N_E| >= nu > 0.                                (6Y.3)
```

At every sufficiently late atom-localizing cutoff,
`|beta_P(L)-beta_E(L)|>=nu/2`.  Any mover-only extension which makes the two
joint reaches exactly equal must remove at least `nu/2` unconditional reach
from the higher-survival word.  Conditionally at that word's entrance to the
repair, its cumulative added mover-Quit probability is at least

```text
|beta_P(L)-beta_E(L)|/max(beta_P(L),beta_E(L))
  >= nu/2.                                          (6Y.4)
```

The bound is achieved by the one-row repair in 6X and proves its optimality
among one-mover reach equalizers.  Alternatively, suppressing the common-tail term
uniformly over arbitrary terminal `C`-mass by a common helper requires making
the surviving reach small, which again costs order-one absorption when
(6Y.3) holds.  The only low-cost escape is then a restriction on the reached
tail itself, such as `p_Y(C)=o(1)`, or a more global replacement which does
not preserve this full-reset endpoint.

For the radial packet the source/reset Never mismatch is explicit.  If the
packet mover marginal is the effective complete-law mixture of source law
`S` and full target law `T` with weight `a=h*w`, then

```text
N_P-N_E
 = N_(-m) * ((1-a)*N_S+a*N_T-N_T)
 = N_(-m)*(1-a)*(N_S-N_T),                           (6Y.5)
```

where `N_(-m)` is the product of the other players' Never masses in the
common environment.  Thus the small radial weight does **not** make the
full-reset reach mismatch small; it converges to
`N_(-m)*(N_S-N_T)`.  Existing tangent/atom access controls no term in
(6Y.5).

Corollary 6Y is not a positive-minimum impossibility.  The mismatch may vanish
because the mover laws have equal Never mass or some opponent Quits almost
surely, and a reached successor may have negligible `C`-mass for independent
reasons.  It is the sharp prescribed-arm branch test suggested by 6W--6X:
Never-mass matching yields a literal common-tail atom adapter, while fixed
mismatch forces an order-one port repair unless the future orientation is
restricted.  Rectangle endpoint debt and chosen-fiber semantic return remain
separate.

This classification is consistent with the stronger checked finite-cap
interface in
`StoppingLaw/TerminalSemanticStoppingLawFiniteSplice.lean`.  There the exact
terminal cap error tends to

```text
NeverMass(moved law) * maximum pair-deleted survival limit,
```

and `tendsto_quittingFiniteSpliceError_zero_iff_zeroNever_pattern` in
`TerminalSemanticStoppingLawFiniteCapClock.lean` says that the universal
payoff/cap error vanishes exactly when the moved law has zero Never mass or
two distinct *other* coordinates have zero Never mass.  The 6V clock labels
are precisely mover and observer, so their presence alone does not kill the
pair-deleted clock needed for the rectangle cap.  Those declarations therefore
support, rather than remove, the distinction above: prescribed orientation
needs only the scalar joint-reach account (6Y), whereas unrestricted observer
debt after a finite splice sees a genuinely stronger pair-deleted port.

### Proposition 6Z (equal-reach words concatenate into an exact prescribed-atom chronology)

The scalar repair in 6X does more than permit one arbitrary common tail.  It
can be applied blockwise before **one** infinite tail is chosen, and hence
removes the purely logical objection that independently frozen source words
cannot be made into a literal chronology.  What remains uncontrolled is the
local carrier/scale geometry.

For each `n`, suppose we have finite product-root words `S_n,T_n` such that:

```text
* S_n and T_n differ only in mover m's marginal roots;
* their joint all-Continue reaches are the same number beta_n>0;
* absorption inside the two words carries, at one fixed observer o and
  terminal C, probability difference Delta_n whose reward-weighted value
  A_n=Delta_n*r_o(C) is at least q>0;
* the same fixed labels first,second have raw hazard sums at least
  kappa*h_n in S_n.
```

No relation between the semantic endpoints of different words is assumed.
Concatenate the source words and let `X_n` be the literal behavioral profile
whose successive blocks are

```text
S_n,S_(n+1),S_(n+2),... .                            (6Z.1)
```

At the port `X_n`, replace mover `m` during the first block by its roots from
`T_n`, and after that block use mover `m`'s actual component of `X_(n+1)`.
Every other player uses the source word and then its actual component of
`X_(n+1)`.  This is one legal complete behavioral replacement: the two
profiles differ in only mover `m`.

Partitioning at the end of the first block gives, exactly,

```text
Pr[X_n stops as C] - Pr[T_n ; X_(n+1) stops as C]
  = Delta_n + beta_n*p_(X_(n+1))(C)
          - beta_n*p_(X_(n+1))(C)
  = Delta_n.                                         (6Z.2)
```

Multiplying (6Z.2) by `r_o(C)` shows that every actual reached source `X_n`
has prescribed atom value `A_n>=q`, with the same orientation, mover, and
observer.  Since `beta_n>0`, its literal all-Continue successor `X_(n+1)` is
actually reached with positive probability.  The two labelled hazard
estimates concern the executed roots themselves.  Reward boundedness gives
one global bound on every actual semantic suffix.  Applying 6X separately to
each captured frozen word supplies the equal-reach hypothesis.  For singleton
`C={m}`, the helper contribution must first be included in `Delta_n`; the
resulting reward-weighted difference can then be bounded below after making
helper pollution smaller than a fixed fraction of the atom, before the
infinite concatenation is formed.

This closes a narrow but genuine issue: after equal reach has been arranged,
there is no further projective-consistency or infinite-profile obstruction to
literal prescribed-atom provenance.  The construction is **not** the Tier-I
kernel asked for in the question.  The balance row can have order-one hazard
by 6Y, and the semantic head generated by (6Z.1) need not lie near the frozen
positive-minimum source or in its availability fiber.  Moreover it gives a
packet only at the preselected scale `h_n`, not at every legal scale below a
port radius.  Finally, the rectangle endpoint cap is not covered.  Proposition
6Z therefore separates exact chronological assembly from the still-missing
small-cost, forward-selectable carrier entry.

### Proposition 6AA (same-word source matching pins the prescribed successor)

There is also an exact necessary condition on any attempted low-cost repair
which keeps a frozen root word literally unchanged.  Let `W` be a finite
product-root word, let

```text
beta(W) = product over all rows and players of Continue probability,
```

and attach two arbitrary actual tails `Y,Z`.  For every player `i`, partition
on absorption inside `W` versus all-Continue through `W`.  The prefix
absorption contribution is identical, so

```text
U_i(W;Y)-U_i(W;Z) = beta(W)*(U_i(Y)-U_i(Z)).          (6AA.1)
```

Consequently, if `beta(W)>0`, fixing both the literal word and its exact head
prescribed vector uniquely fixes the tail prescribed vector.  More
quantitatively, if a reached head `x` is within `delta` coordinatewise of the
frozen head `x_0=W;Z`, then every source-matched use of the same word satisfies

```text
|U_i(Y)-U_i(Z)| <= delta/beta(W).                    (6AA.2)
```

This yields the sharp one-block alternative:

```text
beta(W) small:       the word can forget a prescribed tail seam;
beta(W) >= rho > 0:  the successor must already lie in the rho-conditioned
                     forward prescribed fiber of the frozen suffix.       (6AA.3)
```

There is no analogous inverse statement for the cap without a stable active
branch: the cap is a maximum over prefix stopping and continuation branches.
The uniform forward estimate is instead governed by player-deleted survival;
an inverse factor is available only in a continuation-active cell, exactly as
in Proposition 6H.  For the rectangle atom, changes of the mover law and an
unrestricted observer deviation require the stronger pair-deleted account
recorded after 6Y.

Equations (6AA.1)--(6AA.3) show why an actual-source theorem cannot be obtained
from semantic head closeness plus frozen roots alone.  In the non-forgetting
regime, it must prove a forward viable-fiber statement for the *specific
frozen suffix*.  In the forgetting regime, it must simultaneously control the
deleted and pair-deleted clocks needed by `B`, not merely joint reach.  The
checked frozen packet currently supplies neither half of this alternative.

### Proposition 6AB (sharp persistent availability criterion on one fixed mixture fiber)

The conditioned-kernel theorem gives a positive posterior radius at one
cutoff.  For iteration on one fixed two-component fiber there is an exact
global characterization which separates posterior availability from clock
exhaustion.

Let two scalar stopping hazards have finite-prefix survivals `S_t,T_t`, let
`p in (0,1)`, and put

```text
M_t=(1-p)*S_t+p*T_t,
F_t=p*T_t/M_t.                                      (6AB.1)
```

Assume `M_t>0` for every finite `t`, so every displayed conditioned port is
actually reachable.  Then the following are equivalent:

```text
(a) there is epsilon>0 with epsilon<=F_t<=1-epsilon for every t;

(b) both S_t,T_t are positive and there are c,C>0 such that
        c*S_t <= T_t <= C*S_t
    for every t.                                    (6AB.2)
```

Indeed, with `r_t=T_t/S_t`, posterior odds satisfy

```text
F_t/(1-F_t) = (p/(1-p))*r_t.                         (6AB.3)
```

If `c<=r_t<=C`, one may take

```text
epsilon = min( p*c/(1-p+p*c),
               (1-p)/(1-p+p*C) ).                   (6AB.4)
```

Conversely, an `epsilon` in (a) forces

```text
((1-p)/p)*epsilon/(1-epsilon)
  <= r_t <=
((1-p)/p)*(1-epsilon)/epsilon.                       (6AB.5)
```

Write `S_infty,T_infty` for the two Never masses.  The conditional survival
of the actual mixed law from suffix `m` through `N` further rows is exactly

```text
M_(m+N)/M_m.                                        (6AB.6)
```

Therefore its survival tends to zero from **every** suffix if and only if

```text
(1-p)*S_infty+p*T_infty=0,
```

which, because the weights are strict and the Never masses nonnegative, is
equivalent to

```text
S_infty=T_infty=0.                                  (6AB.7)
```

Combining (6AB.2) and (6AB.7), a fixed two-component ray supports both a
uniformly interior posterior port and persistent all-suffix absorption exactly
when its component survival curves are uniformly comparable, stay positive
at every finite cutoff, and both tend to zero.  The positivity qualification
excludes a finite sure-death row, after which there is no reached successor
port to iterate.

For two distinct labelled coordinates satisfying this criterion, their
actual marginal hazard series both diverge.  Hence, for any prescribed
positive block targets `e_n`, one can recursively choose common finite
cutoffs so that each coordinate contributes hazard at least `e_n` in block
`n`: choose a cutoff for the first divergent nonnegative series, one for the
second, and take their maximum.  Setting `e_n=kappa*h_n` gives the two fixed
clock labels on the literal suffix chronology, while (6AB.4) supplies a
uniform positive component-availability radius at every successor.  Joint
and every one-player-deleted survival then follow from the two-label clock
argument.

This is a conditional Tier-I clock/availability kernel, not the requested
producer from positive-minimum atom data.  The checked frozen packet does not
state finite-prefix likelihood-ratio comparability, zero component Never
masses, or a reusable conditional atom/orientation.  Conversely, if one
component has positive Never mass, availability may remain excellent but its
fixed stopping law carries only a finite hazard budget; if both Never masses
vanish but `T_t/S_t` approaches zero or infinity, the clock persists while
the posterior port collapses.  These are the two exact failure modes which a
fresh-rank reconnection theorem must overcome.

The criterion is nonvacuous even with exact rational data and fixed
mover/observer labels.  Take two players `m,a`, let `a` Quit at every row with
probability `c=1/4`, and let mover component `S` have alternating hazards
`1/2,1/4,1/2,1/4,...`, while component `T` has the reversed alternation.  Mix
the two complete mover laws with prior `p=1/2`.  After every two-row block the
two component survivals are both `3/8`, so the posterior returns exactly to
`1/2`; after the first row it is `3/5`.  Both component Never masses vanish,
their survival ratio alternates between `1` and `3/2`, and the actual
two-row all-Continue successor is literally the same mixture port.

Give observer `a` reward `-1` at terminal `{m}` and zero elsewhere.  If
`u_S,u_T` are the probabilities that `m` is the exact first quitter under the
two mover components, the two-period recursion gives

```text
u_S-u_T
 = c*(1-c)*(1/2-1/4) /
     (1-(1-1/2)*(1-1/4)*(1-c)^2)
 = 6/101.                                           (6AB.8)
```

Comparing the actual half-mixture with full replacement by `S`, terminal
`{m}` therefore carries the positive prescribed atom `3/101`.  The common
two-row reach is `27/128`, so the tail cancels block after block.  The actual
mover hazards in the two rows are `3/8` and `2/5`, while observer `a`
contributes `1/4` in each row.  This is an exact self-restarting prescribed-
atom port with two persistent labels and uniform posterior radius.  Its game
has minimum debt zero, and its hazards are not a vanishing-mesh radial packet;
it is only a stress test showing that the fixed-fiber side of (6AB.2)--(6AB.7)
is mathematically inhabited.

The identities used here are the posterior-odds theorem
`ScalarHazard.posteriorTargetWeight_div_one_sub`, the shifted mixed-survival
formula `ScalarHazard.mixedSurvival_shift_posteriorTargetWeight`, and
`ScalarHazard.convexMix_neverMass` in
`MathUE/Probability/DiscreteHazardMixture.lean` and
`DiscreteHazardConditionalMixture.lean`.  I also checked
`frozenRadialLiteralPacket_twoLabel_available_or_sourceKilled`
(`UniformEquilibrium/Diagnostics/Quitting/Frozen/ConditionedRestartBarrier.lean`):
at one late frozen cutoff it reduces pointwise availability failure to a
killed original source component.  Proposition 6AB is the complementary
infinite-fiber classification; pointwise nonkilling alone does not give its
uniform likelihood-ratio bound or zero-Never clock condition.  The uniform
characterization and two-label block extraction were independently checked as

### Proposition 6AD (the nested radial posterior has only one collapse direction)

The actual frozen marginal has more structure than an arbitrary two-law
mixture.  Its outer target is itself the scale-`h` mixture of the source and
the unscaled replacement.  This makes the availability condition number
one-sided and gives a useful exact trichotomy.

At one cutoff let

```text
S = source-component survival > 0,
Q = unscaled-replacement survival >= 0,
r = Q/S,
T = (1-h)*S+h*Q,
w in (0,1) = strict outer circulation weight.
```

The reached outer target posterior is

```text
F = w*T/((1-w)*S+w*T)
  = w*(1-h+h*r)/(1-w*h+w*h*r).                       (6AD.1)
```

Direct subtraction gives

```text
1-F = (1-w)/(1-w*h+w*h*r),
F-w = w*(1-w)*h*(r-1)/(1-w*h+w*h*r).                 (6AD.2)
```

Thus the posterior can never collapse toward the source endpoint merely from
the nested reset:

```text
F >= w*(1-h)/(1-w*h).                                (6AD.3)
```

It can collapse toward the inner target only when the **scaled** replacement
likelihood `h*r=h*Q/S` is large.  More precisely, if `h*r<=K`, then

```text
1-F >= (1-w)/(1+w*K).                                (6AD.4)
```

For the convenient normalization `w<=1/4`, `h<=1/2`, there are three exact
branches:

```text
(i)  h*r>2:
       S < h*Q/2 <= h/2;                             (near-killed source)

(ii) r>=1 and h*r<=2:
       w<=F<=1/2, so min(F,1-F)>=w;                  (no radius loss)

(iii) r<1:
       F<w<=1/4 and
       0<=w-F<= (8/7)*w*h*(1-r).                    (6AD.5)
```

For (ii), (6AD.4) with `K=2` gives `1-F>=1/2`, while
the sign in (6AD.2) gives `F>=w`.  In (iii), the denominator in (6AD.2) is at
least `1-w*h>=7/8`.  No asymptotic argument is used.

This isolates the quantitative selection still missing from the frozen
packet.  An operationally sublinear radius loss follows in branch (iii) if a
cutoff can be selected with `1-Q/S=o(1)`.  Branch (ii) is better: a replacement
which survives at least as much as the source causes no loss at all until the
scaled likelihood enters branch (i).  Branch (i) says that any posterior
collapse occurs only after the original source component has reach of order
`h` or smaller.  The checked theorem
`frozenRadialLiteralPacket_twoLabel_available_or_sourceKilled` handles exact
zero source reach; (6AD.5) shows the quantitative near-zero boundary which its
strict positivity alternative does not control.

The same ratio is relevant to orientation renewal.  The survival-mismatch
term in 6E is small relative to source reach precisely when `Q/S` is near one;
therefore the favorable part of (iii) simultaneously stabilizes the outer
posterior and the fixed terminal atom.  What is not proved is the cutoff
selection which puts both retained labels in branches (ii) or the `o(1)` part
of (iii), nor any chosen-fiber match after shifting the two components.  The
formulas were independently checked as recorded in the review ledger above.

### Corollary 6AE (two near-killed labels make the literal word tail-forgetting)

The near-killed branch of 6AD has a direct semantic consumer.  Suppose the
aligned labels `first,second` both satisfy branch (i), with their outer weights
at most `1/4`.  The actual mixed survival of either labelled marginal through

```text
M=(1-w*h)*S+w*h*Q <= S+w*h <= 3*h/4.                 (6AE.1)
```

Therefore the joint survival of the frozen word and every one-player-deleted

remains.

Attach two arbitrary bounded actual continuation profiles `Y,Z` to the same

```text
|U_i(W;Y)-U_i(W;Z)| <= (3/2)*M_reward*h,
|B_i(W;Y)-B_i(W;Z)| <= (3/2)*M_reward*h,             (6AE.2)
```

where the second inequality is uniform over all behavioral deviations before

This is the complementary consumer to 6AD(ii)--(iii): either the conditioned

### Proposition 6AF (the exploitably killed branch yields a same-source paid row, not a solved exit)

There is one exact strategic output from the killed branch, but its type is
strictly weaker than any currently solved-game branch.  The following lemma
does not use the sure-Quit row.

```text
U_i(tau,sigma_-i)-U_i(sigma) >= a.                  (6AF.1)
```

Then there are two deterministic Quit-time-or-Never strategies `s_minus` and

```text
U_i(s_plus,sigma_-i)-U_i(s_minus,sigma_-i) >= a/2. (6AF.2)
```

`quittingTerminalPayoff_update_eq_expect_stoppingLaw_pureTime` gives

```text
U_i(sigma)=E_mu[V].                                 (6AF.3)
```

Hence some deterministic choice satisfies `V(s_minus)<=U_i(sigma)`.  If not,
every choice has value strictly above the expectation; choosing one positive-

`V-U_i(sigma)` strictly positive, contradicting (6AF.3).

Apply `exists_quittingPureTimeBehaviorStrategy_terminalPayoff_ge_sub` to

```text
          >= U_i(sigma)+a/2.                        (6AF.4)
```

Equations (6AF.3)--(6AF.4) prove (6AF.2).  Then


Consequently, `HasFrozenRadialTwoLabelExploitablyKilledSource` produces at

with observer equal to the exploitably killed mover.

### Proposition 6AG (unreachable-tail gauge on the killed component)

The requested raw later-rank **source-law** identification is not an invariant
statement on the killed branch.  This is stronger than saying that the current
interface lacks a proof.

Let a literal profile `sigma` have player `m` Quit surely at live date `t`.
Let `sigma^theta` agree with `sigma` for every player through date `t`, agree
with it for every player other than `m` at all dates, and assign an arbitrary
live hazard tail `theta` to `m` after date `t`.  Then

```text
Sem(sigma^theta)=Sem(sigma).                         (6AG.1)
```

For the prescribed payoff, play stops no later than `t`: either an opponent
stops earlier or `m` stops surely at `t`.  For `m`'s cap, changing its own
prescribed post-`t` strategy is irrelevant because the unrestricted cap
depends only on the opponents (`quittingContinuationBestResponseValue_update_self`).
For another player's cap, every unilateral deviation still faces `m`'s sure
Quit at `t`, so no post-`t` row is reached.  This proves (6AG.1) coordinate by
coordinate.

More is true.  Fix arbitrary replacement strategies `R_k` for every mover
`k`.  Then

```text
Sem(update sigma       k R_k)
  = Sem(update sigma^theta k R_k)                    (6AG.2)
```

for every `k`.  If `k=m`, the only changed coordinate is overwritten.  If
`k!=m`, the sure-`m` row persists and the preceding argument applies.  Thus a
rankwise post-sure-row gauge change preserves the source semantic pairs, all
full-replacement semantic pairs, and every replacement-debt bound.  It also
preserves the intermediate stopping-law reset rays used in every normalized
debt direction: when the reset mover is `m`, the two versions of `m` have the
same complete stopping law because all post-sure-row stopping masses are
zero; when the reset mover differs from `m`, the sure-`m` row persists.  Hence
it preserves every normalized debt direction stored by
`QuittingPositiveMinimumDebtTangentFamily`.  Propertywise, it also preserves
the reachable prefix through the sure row and the two named exposure lower
bounds.  If the selected cutoff extends past that row, it does **not** preserve
literal equality of every counterfactual raw post-sure root; those raw roots
are precisely the gauge being varied.

Nevertheless, the shifted source hazard after that cutoff is exactly the
arbitrary `theta`.  Hence no conclusion identifying that raw shifted source
law with the source law at a later frozen rank can follow invariantly from the
positive-minimum tangent-family fields on the killed branch.  Any valid
reconnection must instead do one of the following:

```text
* quotient the zero-posterior source component and reconnect only effective
  reached laws;
* impose a canonical unreachable-tail gauge; or
* build a fresh source-changing bridge which restores a strict two-sided
  mixture at the later port.
```

The first option alone does not restore Tier-I availability: after source
survival is zero its posterior is zero, while the reusable kernel requires a
strict two-sided radius.  Proposition 6AG therefore does not prove an
impossibility of fresh re-entry.  It rules out only the raw cross-rank source-
law identity as a consequence of the currently stored killed-source data.

### Proposition 6AH (Never-regularization removes the killed-source branch)

There is a direct way to select a different tangent-family source without
changing its limiting tangent.  This appears to remove the exploitable-killed
alternative completely; it does **not** solve later-rank reconnection.

Fix a reward bound `M>=1` and write `n=|I|`.  Start with a positive-minimum
tangent family with source `sigma_r`, scale `h_r`, and replacements `R_(r,m)`.
After discarding finitely many ranks, assume `h_r<1/2`.  Put

```text
epsilon_r = h_r^4,
s_r^2     = h_r^2+4*M*n*h_r^4.                       (6AH.1)
```

For every player `j`, replace the complete stopping law of `sigma_r(j)` by

```text
(1-epsilon_r)*Law(sigma_r(j))+epsilon_r*delta_Never. (6AH.2)
```

Let the resulting product profile be `sigma'_r`; retain the old replacement
strategies and use the new scale `s_r`.

#### Exact endpoint-debt field

Changing one player's complete stopping law by weight `epsilon_r` changes
every terminal-payoff coordinate and every unrestricted-cap coordinate by at
most `2*M*epsilon_r`.  The cap statement follows by applying the same bounded
payoff estimate to every fixed deviation and then taking suprema.  Hence every
debt coordinate changes by at most `4*M*epsilon_r` per smoothed player.

At the full replacement of mover `m`, smoothing `m` is overwritten, so only
the other `n-1` coordinates change.  The old endpoint bound therefore gives

```text
d_m(update sigma'_r m R_(r,m))
  <= h_r^2+4*M*(n-1)*h_r^4
  <= s_r^2.                                           (6AH.3)
```

Moreover `Sem(sigma'_r)->base`, while every active base debt is strictly
positive and `s_r^2->0`.  After one further finite truncation,

```text
s_r^2 <= d_m(Sem(sigma'_r))/2                         (6AH.4)
```

for every active mover.  Equations (6AH.3)--(6AH.4) give the exact `min`
field required in `replacement_moverDebt_le_tolerance`, not merely an
asymptotic substitute.

#### The tangent is unchanged

The new and old source semantic pairs differ coordinatewise by `O(n*epsilon_r)`.
Their total-debt sums differ by `O(M*n^2*epsilon_r)`, so

```text
(D(Sem(sigma'_r))-D(base))/s_r -> 0                 (6AH.5)
```

from the old excess-over-`h_r` limit, because `s_r/h_r->1` and
`epsilon_r/s_r=O(h_r^3)`.

For one mover, first compare the old and smoothed reset rays at the same
parameter `h_r`; sequential stopping-law coupling changes each debt
coordinate by `O(M*n*epsilon_r)`.  Then change the ray parameter from `h_r`
to `s_r`.  Since `h_r<1/2`, the larger point is a stopping-law mixture of the
smaller point and the same endpoint with relative weight at most
`2*|s_r-h_r|`; the `4*M*lambda` debt bound therefore costs
`O(M*|s_r-h_r|)`.  Consequently the normalized-direction difference is
bounded by a constant multiple of

```text
n*epsilon_r/s_r + |s_r-h_r|/s_r.                    (6AH.6)
```

Now

```text
s_r/h_r = sqrt(1+4*M*n*h_r^2) -> 1,
epsilon_r/s_r -> 0,
|s_r-h_r|/s_r -> 0.                                  (6AH.7)
```

Thus every new normalized debt direction has the same limit `tangent`.  All
remaining tangent-family fields either concern the unchanged base/tangent or
follow from (6AH.3)--(6AH.7).  Reindexing after the finite truncations makes
them hold at every new rank, and eventually `0<s_r<=1`.

#### Strict component survival

Equation (6AH.2) gives every smoothed source law Never mass at least
`epsilon_r>0`.  Hence its survival is positive at **every** finite cutoff.
The inner reset target at scale `s_r<1` retains at least its
`(1-s_r)*epsilon_r` source-Never mass, so its survival is also positive at
every cutoff.  Therefore every literal packet produced from the regularized
family lies in the positive-radius arm of
`frozenRadialLiteralPacket_twoLabel_availableConditionedKernel`; the
source-killed arm cannot occur.

This construction is compatible with the same flat charged circulation,
because its tangent and active base are unchanged.  It is a genuine
component-survival regularization, but not yet a full dispatch of the killed
branch: `epsilon_r=h_r^4` may give a tiny posterior radius and an order-one
loss from the prior radius.  It also does not identify the conditioned
source/replacement tails with any later regularized rank.  Those quantitative
availability-loss and cross-rank fiber problems remain open.

### Proposition 6AI (sharp radius barrier for tangent-preserving Never regularization)

The missing quantitative qualification in 6AH is not cosmetic.  Consider one
killed source component at the chosen cutoff, so its old survival is zero,
and suppose the unscaled replacement survival `Q_r` has
`liminf Q_r>0`.  Smooth the source with Never weight `epsilon_r`, use a reset
scale `s_r` with `s_r/h_r->1`, and keep a fixed strict outer weight
`w in (0,1)`.  Then the smoothed source and inner-target survivals are

```text
S'_r = epsilon_r,
T'_r = (1-s_r)*epsilon_r+s_r*Q_r.                    (6AI.1)
```

The reached outer posterior is therefore

```text
F_r = w*T'_r / ((1-w)*epsilon_r+w*T'_r).             (6AI.2)
```

Any generic perturbative preservation of the normalized tangent requires
`epsilon_r/h_r->0`; 6AH uses the much smaller `h_r^4`.  Under that rate and
the positive replacement-survival hypothesis, (6AI.1)--(6AI.2) give

```text
liminf (T'_r/h_r) >= liminf Q_r > 0,
epsilon_r/T'_r -> 0,
F_r -> 1.                                            (6AI.3)
```

Consequently

```text
min(F_r,1-F_r) -> 0,
|F_r-w| -> 1-w.                                      (6AI.4)
```

Thus an `o(h)` Never perturbation removes exact zero survival but cannot
provide operationally sublinear availability loss in this branch.  A full
dispatch must additionally show that replacement survival vanishes on the
same scale as the regularized source, change the source by a nonperturbative
but tangent-compatible construction, or leave this frozen ray through a
separate strategic compiler.  Proposition 6AI is conditional on the positive
replacement-survival subsequence; it is not a claim that every killed packet
has that behavior.

### Proposition 6AJ (exact prescribed-arm renewal by an infinite tail graft)

There is one branch in which literal later-rank reconnection does not require
identifying conditioned component laws.  Choose the future tail first and
graft it behind the already checked frozen root words.  This works for the
prescribed atom arm when both aligned labels are near-killed.  It does not
control the rectangle cap.

Fix a sequence of frozen packets `W_n` with lengths `L_n`, the same aligned
labels `first,second`, the same prescribed atom terminal `C`, and the same
positive atom charge `q`.  Let `P_n` be the frozen source profile of `W_n`
and let `E_n` be its full replacement of `first`.  Assume that, through
`L_n`, both labelled marginals of `P_n` have survival at most `epsilon_n`,
where `epsilon_n -> 0`.  In particular 6AE supplies
`epsilon_n=3*h_n/4` when both labels are in branch 6AD(i).

Concatenate the finite product-root words

```text
W_0 W_1 W_2 ...                                      (6AJ.1)
```

and call the behavioral profile represented by this live-root sequence
`X_0`.  Let `X_n` be its suffix beginning at `W_n`.  Then, literally,

```text
Residual_allC^{L_n}(X_n)=X_(n+1),                   (6AJ.2)
```

and the first `L_n` roots of `X_n` equal those of `P_n`.  Thus both clock
lower bounds in the frozen packet survive without error.

For every player `i`, an unrestricted deviation by `i` can see the changed
tail only if every opponent survives `W_n`.  After deleting `i`, at least one
of `first,second` remains.  Product independence therefore gives

```text
Reach_-i(W_n) <= epsilon_n.                          (6AJ.3)
```

With reward bound `M`, common-prefix coupling, uniformly before taking the
best-response supremum, gives

```text
|U_i(X_n)-U_i(P_n)| <= 2*M*epsilon_n,
|B_i(X_n)-B_i(P_n)| <= 2*M*epsilon_n,
|d_i(X_n)-d_i(P_n)| <= 4*M*epsilon_n.                (6AJ.4)
```

The source port is therefore an actual reached port tending to the same
frozen carrier, rather than a semantic annotation substituted after play.

The full replacement can be grafted compatibly as well.  Define a strategy
`R_n^graft` for `first` by using the original `E_n(first)` hazards before
`L_n` and the actual successor strategy `X_(n+1)(first)` afterward.  Updating
`X_n` in coordinate `first` by `R_n^graft` gives the original full-replacement
word through `L_n` and reaches exactly `X_(n+1)` after all Continue.  The
second label is unchanged by this replacement and has survival at most
`epsilon_n`.  Hence the source and full-replacement terminal laws each change
from their frozen versions only on events of probability at most
`epsilon_n`.  For the fixed prescribed atom,

```text
|A_C(X_n,R_n^graft)-A_C(P_n,E_n)| <= 2*M*epsilon_n. (6AJ.5)
```

Consequently the same terminal orientation and a fixed positive subcharge
survive eventually, with the exact output fraction chosen according to the
prescribed-branch decoder normalization.  The mover/observer labels and the
frozen source/replacement **prefix roots through `L_n`** are unchanged, and
(6AJ.2) supplies exact serial restart at the next grafted source port.  The
complete source and replacement stopping laws are deliberately changed after
the cutoff to the newly common actual suffix; this is not equality with the
original frozen complete laws.

This is an exact chronology, not merely pairwise compactness: defining the
single infinite word (6AJ.1) simultaneously fixes every future tail, so there
is no backwards infinite choice.  It closes the raw later-rank identity issue
on this conditional branch by replacing it with a literal suffix identity.

There are three strict limitations.

1. In the rectangle arm, the endpoint cap belongs to observer `second`.
   A deviation by `second` deletes the small second clock, while the
   full-replacement first coordinate may survive with order-one probability.
   Thus (6AJ.5) has no unrestricted-cap analogue without a third small label,
   a pair-deleted clock, or a separate endpoint-cap argument.
2. The hypothesis that both labels are near-killed at every selected rank is
   not supplied by the current cutoff theorem.  Mixed posterior branches
   6AD(ii)--(iii) remain outside this construction.
3. This constructs one exact selected chain.  It does not yet give the
   neighborhood-valued entrance rule for every sufficiently close reached
   port or prove the full availability-radius inequality of Tier I.
4. Relatedly, it supplies one preselected frozen scale at each grafted port,
   not a packet for every legal `0<scale<rho(port)` as required by
   `QuittingBudgetStablePacketSystem.packetAvailable`.

Within those limits, the prescribed near-killed branch has an exact selected
**source** chronology and prefix-level reset provenance: the future frozen
word is made the actual conditional tail, and atom loss is paid only once
through the small retained second-label reach.  Identifying the conditioned
replacement component itself with the later frozen replacement requires the
separate two-word graft below rather than the common-tail version of 6AJ.

### Proposition 6AK (two-word graft reconnects both source and replacement fibers)

The last provenance limitation of 6AJ has an exact repair in the same
prescribed/two-near-killed branch.  In addition to the source word `W_n`, let
`V_n` be the first-coordinate root segment of the frozen full-replacement
profile `E_n` through `L_n`.  Define two infinite objects:

```text
X_n = source profile with live-root suffix W_n W_(n+1) ...,
T_n = first-player strategy with live-hazard suffix V_n V_(n+1) ...,
Y_n = update X_n first T_n.                          (6AK.1)
```

The other coordinates of `Y_n` are those of `X_n`.  Segmentwise
concatenation gives the literal pair of residual identities

```text
Residual_allC^{L_n}(X_n)=X_(n+1),
Residual_allC^{L_n}(Y_n)=Y_(n+1).                    (6AK.2)
```

Moreover the source prefix of `X_n` is exactly the frozen source word of
`P_n`, while the endpoint prefix of `Y_n` is exactly the frozen
full-replacement word of `E_n`.  Thus `(X_n,T_n)` is one complete stopping-law
source/replacement pair, and conditioning carries that pair exactly to the
later pair `(X_(n+1),T_(n+1))`.  Unlike the common-tail construction in 6AJ,
the source and replacement conditional endpoints are not identified with one
another.

All estimates of 6AJ remain unchanged.  The source comparison uses the two
small labels.  In the endpoint comparison, updating `first` leaves the small
`second` marginal unchanged, so terminal-law disagreement after the common
endpoint prefix has probability at most `epsilon_n`.  Hence (6AJ.4) holds for
the source heads and (6AJ.5) holds for the prescribed atom, while both
identities in (6AK.2) are exact.  In particular this yields, conditionally on
the prescribed branch and the two-near-killed cutoff selection, an exact
reconnection to the recursively grafted later **source-and-replacement** pair,
with fixed labels, terminal orientation, literal frozen prefix clocks, and
vanishing atom loss.  The pair at rank `n+1` has exactly the later frozen
prefix and atom data, but its complete laws retain the recursively selected
future rather than equal the original later frozen complete laws.

This still does not control the rectangle endpoint cap: a deviation by
`second` deletes the small label and can distinguish the tails `Y_(n+1)` and
the frozen endpoint tail at order one.  Nor does the construction produce the
two-near-killed subsequence or an arbitrary-entry availability neighborhood.
In particular it does not yet identify a neighborhood-valued conditioned port
with the original later frozen complete-law fiber.  Those gaps are unchanged
from 6AJ.

### Corollary 6AL (the exact extra deleted port closes the rectangle graft)

The two-word construction also handles the rectangle branch under precisely
the endpoint port missing above.  Retain the hypotheses and notation of 6AK,
but allow the stabilized atom branch to be rectangle.  Assume in addition
that the frozen full-replacement word `E_n` satisfies

```text
Reach_(E_n,-second,L_n) <= epsilon_n,                (6AL.1)
```

with `epsilon_n->0`.  This is the probability that every opponent of the
observer `second` Continues through the endpoint prefix.  It is stronger than
small joint reach and is not implied by the two source-label bounds after
`first` is replaced.

The endpoint profiles `E_n` and `Y_n` share their first `L_n` roots.  For
every fixed behavioral deviation of `second`, the two induced payoff
variables differ only on the event in (6AL.1).  Taking suprema after the
uniform coupling bound gives

```text
|B_second(Y_n)-B_second(E_n)| <= 2*M*epsilon_n.       (6AL.2)
```

Their prescribed payoffs differ by at most `2*M*epsilon_n` as well, so

```text
d_second(Y_n)
 <= d_second(E_n)+4*M*epsilon_n.                     (6AL.3)
```

Apply the same coupling after overriding `second` by the rank's supplied pure
stopping time.  On the source side the surviving first label bounds the
tail-change event; on the endpoint side (6AL.1) does.  Therefore the fixed
rectangle terminal atom changes by at most `2*M*epsilon_n`, with no need to
freeze the pure stopping time across ranks.  Equations (6AK.2), (6AL.2), and
(6AL.3) then give exact source-and-endpoint suffix restart, the same rectangle
orientation and witnesses, and vanishing endpoint observer debt.

Thus the rectangle obstruction in 6AJ is exactly a missing
observer-deleted endpoint clock, not a defect in infinite concatenation.
Condition (6AL.1) may follow from a third near-killed source label unchanged
by the reset, or from a direct bound on the reset mover's replacement word.
Neither source is present in the checked two-label packet, so 6AL is a
conditional consumer rather than a Tier-I producer.

### Corollary 6AL-R (finite repetition makes the grafted labels persistent)

The selected source/replacement chronology can be amplified without any
assumption on the series of frontier scales.  For each selected rank choose

```text
R_n >= 1/(kappa*h_n)
```

and concatenate `R_n` copies of the source word `W_n` before advancing to
`W_(n+1)`.  In the replacement strategy concatenate the matching `R_n`
copies of its first-coordinate endpoint word `V_n`.  The proof of (6AK.2) is
segmentwise and therefore applies to every copy: source and endpoint suffixes
remain literal, and each occurrence retains the same prescribed atom (or,
under 6AL, rectangle atom) with the same rankwise error.

Each copy contributes at least `kappa*h_n` marginal Quit hazard for both
fixed labels.  Hence rank `n` contributes at least one unit to each label and

```text
sum_t q_(t,first)=sum_t q_(t,second)=infinity.      (6AL-R.1)
```

Deleting any player leaves at least one of these two divergent hazard streams,
so the exact joint and every one-player-deleted survival products vanish from
every suffix.  Starting inside one finite word or macro-rank deletes only a
finite hazard prefix and does not affect divergence.

Thus conditional selected grafting supplies the full persistent-clock field
on the same literal roots, even if `sum_n h_n<infinity`.  This does not repair
the local-system interface: it still offers only the preselected repeated
scales, not a packet for every legal scale below a positive port radius, and
it introduces no Tier-II artificial candidate or seam bound.

### Corollary 6AM (the killed-source paid row has no local fixed-charge exactification)

The flat charged-circulation branch already has at least two distinct positive
debtors at the minimum base: the aligned `first,second` of 6V both belong to
`positiveDebtSupport`.  The actual frozen source profiles converge in terminal
semantics to that base.  Therefore the compactness/rigidity argument of
Proposition 62 in `CODEX_CEDAR__PAID_ROW_REENTRY` applies directly to these
source profiles:

> If `V_n` are boxed floor tails with
> `||V_n-U(source_n)||_infinity -> 0` and `q_n` are exact endpoint-Nash roots
> at `V_n`, then `absorption(q_n)->0`.

Indeed, a positive-charge subsequence would converge to an exact root at the
minimum prescribed payoff.  The positive debt of `first` forces every other
player, including `second`, to Continue purely; the positive debt of `second`
then forces `first` to Continue purely as well.  The limiting root is all
Continue, contradicting positive charge.

Consequently the paid first-disagreement row extracted in 6AF from an
exploitably killed **frozen source** cannot be turned into a fixed-charge exact
edge by a floor tail asymptotic to that same source payoff.  Its behavioral
gain is still valid, but any exact charged dispatch must make a nonlocal tail
excursion and later repay it, or use a route not encoded by one exact local
root.  This conclusion is specific to the original minimum-source sequence.
It must not be transferred to the off-minimum full-replacement paid source of
the finite-support-rank alternative; that source mismatch is recorded in the
independent Section 62 feedback.

### Proposition 6AN (tail-only grafts have collapsing entrance radius)

The arbitrary-entry limitation in 6AJ--6AK is quantitative, not merely a
missing selection argument.  Fix one source word `W_n` from the two-near-
killed branch and vary only the continuation attached after `L_n`, keeping all
prefix roots fixed.  For every player `i`, the player-deleted reach is at most
`epsilon_n`.  Hence for any two bounded semantic tails `z,z'`, common-prefix
coupling gives

```text
|F_(W_n)(z)^B_i-F_(W_n)(z')^B_i|
  <= epsilon_n*|z^B_i-z'^B_i|
  <= 2*M*epsilon_n.                                  (6AN.1)
```

The first inequality is the exact cap-tail Lipschitz bound; it is uniform
over unrestricted deviations before the supremum is taken.  Thus the entire
tail-varying image of the fixed word has diameter at most
`2*M*epsilon_n` in every cap coordinate.  In particular, if an entrance
availability notion requires the image to contain a full `l_infinity`
semantic ball of radius `rho_n`, then

```text
rho_n <= M*epsilon_n -> 0.                            (6AN.2)
```

Suppose such radii were inserted into the additive availability ledger

```text
rho_(n+1) >= rho_n-chi_n,       chi_n>=0.             (6AN.3)
```

Summing (6AN.3) through rank `N` and using `rho_N->0` gives

```text
sum_n chi_n >= rho_0.                                 (6AN.4)
```

Therefore a compiler hypothesis requiring total declared availability loss
strictly below the initial radius cannot be satisfied by upgrading the
near-killed graft through **tail variation alone**.  The exact selected chain
of 6AJ--6AK remains valid, but a neighborhood-valued Tier-I producer must
also move the prefix roots, use a nonadditive/invariant port chart, or switch
to a different source branch.  Proposition 6AN does not obstruct such root-
changing reprojectors; it only rules out silently promoting the fixed-word
tail graft to a budget-stable arbitrary-entry theorem.

The qualification “if an entrance availability notion requires” is
essential.  The checked `QuittingBudgetStablePacketSystem.radius` is only a
legal-scale budget; it is not required to be the radius of a semantic image
ball.  Proposition 6AO below therefore shows that 6AN is **not** an
obstruction to populating the literal current packet-system type.  It is an
obstruction only to the stronger full-semantic-neighborhood interpretation.

### Proposition 6AO (periodic self-loop and the missing scale/mesh field)

There is a different exact promotion of the same conditional branch which
shows that the current local-system fields do not by themselves express the
intended vanishing-mesh requirement.

Fix **one** sufficiently late prescribed-atom packet from 6AK.  Write `W` for
its source root word of length `L`, `V` for the mover's full-replacement word
through the same cutoff, `h_0>0` for its genuine frozen scale, and `kappa>0`
for the two-label hazard coefficient.  Suppose both retained source labels
have survival at most `epsilon` through `L`, and suppose the source word has
strictly positive joint all-Continue reach.  (The 6AH regularized family
provides this strict finite-cutoff positivity.)  Define

```text
X = W W W ...,
T = V V V ...          in the mover coordinate,
Y = update X mover T.                                  (6AO.1)
```

These are legal behavioral stopping laws by `quittingCyclicBehaviorProfile`
and `quittingProfileLiveRoot_cyclicBehaviorProfile` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Cycles/PeriodicCompiler.lean`.  Periodicity gives
the exact pair of reached-port identities

```text
Residual_allC^L(X)=X,
Residual_allC^L(Y)=Y.                                  (6AO.2)
```

The positivity hypothesis makes the first identity a genuinely reached
nominal successor rather than only a counterfactual behavioral suffix.  The
replacement identity is provenance data and remains meaningful even if its
counterfactual prefix has zero reach.

The first `L` roots of `X` are exactly the frozen source roots, and the first
`L` roots of `Y` are exactly the frozen full-replacement roots.  The source
comparison `P` versus `X` can disagree only after the common prefix, whose
joint reach is at most the survival of either retained label.  The endpoint
comparison `E` versus `Y` has the same bound because the unchanged second
label survives its endpoint prefix with probability at most `epsilon`.
Consequently, for the fixed prescribed terminal `C`,

```text
|A_C(X,Y)-A_C(P,E)| <= 2*M*epsilon.                    (6AO.3)
```

Thus, if `K` is the number of terminal outcomes and
`2*K*M*epsilon <= q/4`, a frozen charge-`q` prescribed atom remains at least
a charge-`q/2` prescribed atom on the actual periodic source/replacement
pair.  The mover, observer, terminal orientation, first/second labels, and
prefix provenance are literal and recur unchanged after every block.

Each block contributes at least `kappa*h_0` marginal Quit hazard for each
retained label.  Hence the same root word also satisfies

```text
kappa*s <= hazard_i(W)        (i=first,second)          (6AO.4)
```

for **every** `0<s<h_0`.  If a one-point port system is given radius `h_0`,
successor equal to itself, and `chi=0`, then (6AO.2)--(6AO.4) meet the literal
Tier-I availability, restart, label, provenance, hazard, and uniform-bound
fields for every legal declared scale.  The actual roots do not depend on
the declared `s`.  More explicitly, annotate the unique port by the literal
terminal semantic pair of `X` and annotate each internal offset by the pair of
the corresponding cyclic phase.  The semantic prefix identity gives every
exact internal step, (6AO.2) makes both endpoint seams zero, and the global
reward-table bound gives the prescribed/debt bounds.  Thus this is not merely
a clock loop; apart from the separate small-debt seed/implementation problem,
it populates the current `QuittingBudgetStablePacketData` fields exactly.

This does not prove the intended vanishing-scale reprojection theorem.  It
instead isolates a type/interface gap.  `QuittingBudgetStablePacketData`
stores only the lower bounds `kappa*scale <= hazard`; it has no field asserting
that each root hazard, a root mesh, or any other literal packet size is
`O(scale)`.  Accordingly one fixed packet may be relabelled at all smaller
scales.  Any Tier-II seam bound derived from the genuine frozen scale `h_0`
rather than from exact periodic closure cannot honestly be charged to the
arbitrarily smaller declared `s`.

There are therefore two sharply different readings of the local target.

1. Under the **literal current packet-system type**, the prescribed/two-near-
   killed branch admits this exact one-port Tier-I self-loop.
2. Under the intended **vanishing-mesh** reading, one must add an explicit
   upper-size field, for example

   ```text
   max_(offset<L, i) q_(offset,i) <= C*scale,
   ```

   or another named literal mesh quantity.  With that repair, the periodic
   fixed word no longer answers arbitrary smaller scale queries, and the
   root-moving/nonadditive entry problem remains.

The construction is conditional on the prescribed atom branch and the
two-near-killed cutoff already used in 6AJ--6AK.  It says nothing about the
mixed-posterior branches, the rectangle cap, or Tier II.  Its purpose is to
prevent the scale parameter from silently being treated as a mesh bound that
the current type does not contain.

### Proposition 6AP (a nonsingleton atom forces nonvanishing exact root mesh)

The missing upper-mesh field in 6AO cannot be added innocently while retaining
arbitrary fixed terminal orientations on both the source and full-replacement
words.  There is an exact probability obstruction.

Let `q_(t,i)` be the marginal Quit probabilities of any ordinary product-root
sequence, fix `mu in [0,1]`, and suppose

```text
q_(t,i) <= mu                  for every t,i.          (6AP.1)
```

For a fixed nonempty terminal coalition `C` of cardinality `k>=2`, write
`p(C)` for the probability that `C` is the exact first-quitter coalition.
Then

```text
p(C) <= mu^(k-1).                                    (6AP.2)
```

Indeed, fix one label `i_0 in C`.  If `S_t` is joint survival to row `t`, the
row-`t` contribution is

```text
S_t * product_(i in C) q_(t,i)
    * product_(j notin C) (1-q_(t,j))
 <= mu^(k-1) * S_t * q_(t,i_0).
```

Joint survival is at most the marginal survival of `i_0`.  Summing over `t`
therefore bounds the last factors by the probability that `i_0` ever Quits,
which is at most one.  This proves (6AP.2), including infinite words and
positive Never mass.

Now let `P,E` be a source/full-replacement pair, let rewards be bounded by
`M>0`, and suppose a prescribed atom at this same `C` has absolute value at
least `a>0`:

```text
|(Pr_P(C)-Pr_E(C))*r_o(C)| >= a.                    (6AP.3)
```

Then

```text
max(Pr_P(C),Pr_E(C)) >= a/M.                        (6AP.4)
```

Consequently, if **both** complete root words have marginal mesh at most
`mu`, (6AP.2) gives the fixed lower bound

```text
mu >= (a/M)^(1/(k-1)).                              (6AP.5)
```

The same conclusion applies to finite prefixes once they already carry the
atom mass in (6AP.3).  In the decoder normalization one simply substitutes
the actual lower bound on the reward-weighted atom, for example `a=q/(4*K)`
when the retained prescribed charge and outcome-cardinality convention give
`K*A_C>=q/4`.

There are two important qualifications.

1. If an upper-mesh field constrains only the nominal source block, (6AP.5)
   obstructs that block only in the sign orientation for which the source
   itself carries the fixed positive `C`-mass.  In the opposite orientation
   the coarse root may occur only in the counterfactual full-replacement
   word.  A source-and-replacement provenance interface is obstructed without
   this sign qualification.
2. A rectangle atom is evaluated after overriding the observer by a pure
   stopping time.  When the supplied `Option Nat` is a finite time, that
   counterfactual word already contains a sure-Quit root and therefore has
   mesh one if the mesh requirement includes atom-witness profiles.  A
   `Never` witness does not have this consequence, and in either case nothing
   here implies that the nominal packet word has coarse mesh.

Thus a genuine vanishing-root-mesh repair of the Tier-I type must make an
explicit choice.  It can select a singleton terminal orientation, constrain
only the executed nominal word and permit a coarse counterfactual endpoint,
weaken exact terminal orientation/provenance, or use a scale notion other
than maximum marginal root hazard.  A blanket `max q<=C*h` field on both
literal words is incompatible with a fixed nonsingleton atom as `h->0`.

This proposition is a conditional interface obstruction, not a
positive-minimum counterexample: the current atom decoder may stabilize on a
singleton `C`, and the current checked packet type does not contain the
upper-mesh premise used here.  Its role is to make the possible type repair
mathematically exact rather than treating “frontier scale” and literal root
mesh as interchangeable.

### Concrete next selection question

At the atom-aligned cutoff supplied by the frozen packet, apply (6AD.5) to
both retained labels.  Can one select a subsequence and cutoff so that either

```text
* both scaled likelihoods h*Q/S stay bounded and every adverse Q<S branch
  has 1-Q/S=o(1), while the 6E prefix-atom loss also vanishes; or

* both labels enter the near-killed branch, and either the stabilized atom
  branch is prescribed so that 6AJ--6AK apply, or the rectangle endpoint gains
  a third small opponent clock / pair-deleted bound controlling its cap?
```

The first alternative would give sublinear conditioned availability and
same-orientation renewal on the existing ray.  Propositions 6AJ--6AK supply an
exact recursively grafted source/replacement chronology in the prescribed
part of the second alternative, but not equality with the original later
complete laws or the arbitrary-entry neighborhood field.  The rectangle part
still needs the stronger deleted-port datum.  The current frontier controls
neither selection.  This is narrower than asking again for the whole Tier-I
kernel and is the next unresolved check for this notebook.

### Proposition 6AQ (optimal one-row posterior actuator)

There is an exact source/replacement fiber connector for the mixed-posterior
branch, but its least possible **executed-mixture** hazard is precisely the
posterior mismatch.  Thus it removes the algebraic reconnection problem while
leaving the quantitative selection problem intact.  Counterfactual component
roots are accounted for separately below.

Let a two-component complete stopping-law mixture arrive at a live row with
posterior target weight

```text
0<p<1.
```

Fix a desired successor weight `0<b<1`.  At this row one may modify the two
component laws differently, while leaving every earlier root unchanged.  If
`p<=b`, let the target component Continue surely and let the source component
Continue with probability

```text
u = p*(1-b)/((1-p)*b).                              (6AQ.1)
```

If `b<=p`, let the source component Continue surely and let the target
component Continue with probability

```text
v = b*(1-p)/(p*(1-b)).                              (6AQ.2)
```

The displayed numbers lie in `[0,1]`.  Conditional on Continue at the
aggregate behavioral root, the target posterior is exactly `b`.  The
aggregate Quit probability at that root is

```text
Lambda(p,b) =
  (b-p)/b       if p<=b,
  (p-b)/(1-b)   if b<=p.                            (6AQ.3)
```

For example, in the first case the aggregate Continue probability is

```text
(1-p)*u+p = p/b,
```

and the surviving target mass divided by this quantity is `p/(p/b)=b`.
The second case is symmetric.

This actuator is optimal among all one-row componentwise survival repairs for
the aggregate root actually executed by the mixture.
Indeed, let `u,v in [0,1]` be arbitrary component Continue probabilities and
suppose the posterior after Continue is `b`.  There is a scalar `c>=0` such
that the two surviving masses are

```text
(1-p)*u=c*(1-b),       p*v=c*b.                    (6AQ.4)
```

Consequently

```text
c <= min((1-p)/(1-b),p/b).                          (6AQ.5)
```

Here `c` is exactly the aggregate Continue probability.  Maximizing it gives
(6AQ.1)--(6AQ.3), so no one-row actuator has smaller aggregate Quit hazard.
In particular

```text
|p-b| <= Lambda(p,b),                               (6AQ.6)
```

and, if `b in [rho,1-rho]` for fixed `rho>0`,

```text
Lambda(p,b) <= |p-b|/rho.                           (6AQ.7)
```

Thus on an interior target fiber the actuator has `o(h)` hazard exactly when
the posterior mismatch is `o(h)`.  In the regularized killed model of 6AI,
where `p->1` and the target is the original fixed outer weight `b=w<1`, the
optimal cost tends to one.  No choice of a different one-row reweighting
repairs that branch cheaply.

The construction is a legal ordinary behavioral strategy, not an observable
latent-state controller.  Define the two complete stopping-law components so
that they agree with the frozen components through the selected cutoff, use
the componentwise Continue coins (6AQ.1) or (6AQ.2) at the next row, and after
Continue attach any desired pair of component tails.  Their complete-law
mixture is one stopping-time law and therefore has its ordinary behavioral
hazard representation.  The latent two-component description is only a
calculation of that law.  Its actual residual after Continue is the desired
mixture of the attached tails with weight `b`.

Applying these actuators at successive private rows, one coordinate at a
time, gives the exact finite-dimensional port connector.  At the end of the
actuator word, every coordinate has its prescribed next weight and prescribed
pair of component tails.  In particular, after a frozen radial prefix one may
attach the later frozen source and inner-reset component laws and land
**exactly** in the chosen later outer-mixture fiber.  Strict input and target
weights make both actuator component survivals, hence the source connector
reach in that coordinate, positive.  For a product-profile successor to be
actually reached, every unsteered coordinate must also have positive prefix
survival.  The all-player Never regularization in 6AH supplies this extra
fact; the checked two-label available-kernel predicate alone does not.

If the source mixture's target component is itself the named full-replacement
law, the same construction is automatically coupled to the replacement
fiber: give that component survival `v` and the later replacement tail.  The
actual frozen radial packet is nested differently.  Its outer target is the
**inner reset mixture**, while the atom endpoint is the original full
replacement.  There one uses the actuator only to graft the later frozen
source and later inner-reset laws behind the two outer components, and grafts
the later original replacement separately behind the frozen endpoint prefix.
This keeps all three frozen prefixes literal.  It yields an actually reached
later source fiber when the source connector reach is positive; the separately
grafted endpoint remains counterfactual provenance unless its own prefix has
positive reach.  No endpoint-cap conclusion follows from this raw graft.

If an upper-mesh convention also charges the latent component words, it must
count their Quit probabilities, not only the aggregate value `Lambda(p,b)`.
When `p<=b`, the source-component cost is

```text
1-u = (b-p)/(b*(1-p)) = Lambda(p,b)/(1-p),           (6AQ.8)
```

and the target-component cost is zero.  When `b<=p`, the source-component
cost is zero and the target-component cost is

```text
(p-b)/(p*(1-b)) = Lambda(p,b)/p.                    (6AQ.9)
```

These are comparable to the aggregate cost only when the relevant reached
component mass is bounded away from zero.  The current literal Tier-I fields
constrain the executed source hazards, but component-provenance or fixed
source/replacement mesh proposals must retain this distinction.

This is not yet Tier I.  Three costs remain external to the algebraic
connector:

```text
* the sum of the optimal actuator hazards Lambda(p_i,b_i);
* any source/endpoint reach equalization needed to retain a prescribed atom;
* the observer-deleted endpoint port needed to retain a rectangle cap.
```

Equations (6AQ.6)--(6AQ.7) show that the first cost is operationally
sublinear precisely on a selected branch with sublinear posterior mismatch.
They do not produce that selection.  Nor does the actuator preserve a fixed
terminal atom by itself: its absorption row can add a singleton terminal, and
the source and full-replacement connector words can have different total
reach.  Proposition 6X is the exact prescribed-arm repair after those reaches
are controlled; Corollary 6AL records the still stronger rectangle port.

The gain over the previous status is therefore narrow but exact.  Cross-rank
mixed-component identification is not an additional topological obstruction:
it can always be implemented by an ordinary finite root word when the reached
and target weights are strict.  The remaining mixed-posterior Tier-I theorem
is the quantitative selection

```text
sum_i Lambda(p_i,b_i) + atom/cap repair cost = o(h),
```

together with a forward choice of the later frozen source/replacement fiber.

### Proposition 6AR (nested-radial gauge removes the outer-posterior seam)

For the actual frozen radial law, the outer posterior is not the intrinsic
coordinate.  Collapsing the nested mixtures gives an exact zero-row-cost
chart, at the price of allowing the next inner scale to change.

Let `A` be a source stopping law and `R` its original full replacement.  For
`0<h<1` define the inner reset

```text
B_h=(1-h)*A+h*R,
```

and for one fixed `0<w<1` define the executed outer radial law

```text
L_(w,h)=(1-w)*A+w*B_h=(1-w*h)*A+w*h*R.              (6AR.1)
```

At a finite cutoff let `S,Q` be the survivals of `A,R`, respectively, and
assume the mixed survival

```text
D=(1-w*h)*S+w*h*Q
```

is positive.  Conditional on survival, the intrinsic posterior weight of
the original replacement is

```text
e = w*h*Q/D.                                        (6AR.2)
```

Whenever `e<=w`, put

```text
h^+ = e/w = h*Q/D.                                  (6AR.3)
```

Then the actual residual law has the exact refactorization

```text
(1-e)*A^cut+e*R^cut
 = (1-w)*A^cut+w*((1-h^+)*A^cut+h^+*R^cut).         (6AR.4)
```

Thus the outer weight returns to the same fixed `w` with no actuator root and
no outer-availability loss.  Only the inner radial scale changes from `h` to
`h^+`.  The admissibility condition `e<=w` is equivalently

```text
h*(1-w)*Q <= (1-w*h)*S.                             (6AR.5)
```

If `S>0` and `r=Q/S`, the scale update is

```text
h^+ = h*r/(1-w*h+w*h*r).                            (6AR.6)
```

For fixed `w`, along `h->0` this new scale tends to zero if and only if

```text
h*r -> 0.                                           (6AR.7)
```

The equivalence follows because the right side of (6AR.6) is
`x/(1-w*h+w*x)` with `x=h*r`.  In particular, `r->1` gives
`h^+/h->1`, whereas the near-killed regime `h*r` bounded away from zero does
not produce a vanishing next scale even when the outer two-sided posterior
radius remains positive.

As in 6AQ, one may choose the future component tails before forming the
complete laws.  If the frozen prefixes of `A,R` are retained and their
post-cutoff tails are chosen to be a later source/replacement pair, (6AR.4)
lands exactly in the later nested radial fiber with outer weight `w` and
inner scale `h^+`.  The original full-replacement orientation is retained
because `R^cut` is still the named endpoint component.  For a product profile
this requires positive prefix survival in every coordinate to make the
successor actually reached.  This conclusion is coordinatewise.  With active
coordinates `j`, conditioning generally produces the vector

```text
h_j^+ = h*Q_j/((1-w_j*h)*S_j+w_j*h*Q_j),            (6AR.8)
```

whose entries need not agree.  The whole successor is therefore a vector-
inner-scale radial profile, not automatically a member of the original
common-inner-scale frozen family.

This chart is potentially better than the actuator: it spends no new root
hazard and keeps the balanced outer circulation weights fixed.  It is not a
Tier-I solution.  Proposition 6AS below removes the scalar obstruction that a
chosen adaptive scale need equal one of the frontier's recorded scales, but
it does not align the vector (6AR.8).  A whole-profile restart still needs a
vector-scale packet estimate, a low-cost scale equalizer, or a selection
forcing the relevant `h_j^+` to agree.  In addition, (6AR.7) requires the
unresolved cutoff selection `h*Q/S->0` for every coordinate whose next scale
must vanish.  The killed/near-killed examples show that this condition does
not follow from positive component survival alone.

Accordingly the mixed branch has two exact reconnection mechanisms:

```text
fixed later vector:     pay the coordinatewise actuator costs from 6AQ;
adaptive inner vector:  use the zero-row-cost gauges (6AR.8), then solve
                        vector-scale packet control/alignment.
```

Neither mechanism supplies the rectangle observer-deleted endpoint port.

### Proposition 6AS (the frozen head admits an independent inner scale)

The first limitation stated after 6AR is not substantive for Tier I.  Once a
late frozen source, its full replacements, two positive outer weights, gain
floors, and one atom have been selected, the literal packet may be rebuilt at
an arbitrary sufficiently small **inner** scale `s`; it need not use the
frontier's recorded scale at that rank.

Fix one late rank and write `A` for its common source, `R_j` for each active
full replacement, and `w_j in [0,1]` for the already selected balanced outer
weights.  Assume for the fixed labels `first,second`

```text
g_i <= U_i(update A i R_i)-U_i(A),       g_i>0,      (6AS.1)
```

and retain a prescribed or rectangle atom of charge `q>0` and endpoint error
`e` for the selected mover/observer/orientation.  Let `M>0` bound terminal
rewards, let `n=|I|`, and let `K` be the number of terminal outcomes.

For any `0<s<=1`, define

```text
B_(j,s)=(1-s)*A_j+s*R_j,
P_s(j) =(1-w_j)*A_j+w_j*B_(j,s)
       =(1-w_j*s)*A_j+w_j*s*R_j                    (6AS.2)
```

on active coordinates, leaving inactive coordinates at `A`.  These are
ordinary complete stopping-law mixtures.  If

```text
w_first*s<=1/2,       w_second*s<=1/2,              (6AS.3)
```

the same endpoint-payoff argument used by
`nestedRadialEverQuitMass_ge_weight_mul_gain_div` gives

```text
w_i*s*g_i/(2*M) <= EverQuitMass(P_s(i))             (6AS.4)
```

for `i=first,second`.  Therefore, with

```text
kappa=min(w_first*g_first/(4*M),
          w_second*g_second/(4*M))>0,               (6AS.5)
```

`exists_finiteCutoff_two_marginalHazardSums` supplies a finite literal cutoff
at which each fixed label contributes raw marginal hazard at least
`kappa*s`.  The proof uses only the full endpoint gain (6AS.1), not the
normalized tangent identity at the originally recorded frontier scale.

The atom estimate is also uniform in this free scale.  Relative to `A`, each
active coordinate changes by the single effective complete-law weight
`w_j*s<=s`.  Telescoping terminal laws over the active face gives total
probability perturbation at most `n*s` on each of the source and full-endpoint
sides.  The unrestricted-cap coupling is uniform over every observer
deviation.  Consequently the calculation of Proposition 6S gives

```text
HasQuittingStoppingLawVanishingDebtAtomAlternative
  reward P_s mover observer (R_mover) (q/2) (e+4*M*n*s)       (6AS.6)
```

whenever

```text
2*K*M*n*s <= q/8.                                   (6AS.7)
```

The same terminal and, in the rectangle arm, rankwise pure-time orientation
are retained.  The global reward bound controls every literal semantic suffix
as before.

Thus any **one common** adaptive value `s` can be used as the inner scale of a
packet at a sufficiently late frozen rank satisfying the same fixed gain/atom
data; exact equality with a later value of `frontier.scale` is not needed.
One chooses the later rank for its source, replacement, gain, and atom error,
then inserts the independently prescribed `s` in (6AS.2).  This consumes 6AR
only in one coordinate or under an added alignment hypothesis
`h_j^+=s` for all active coordinates.  In general 6AR produces the unequal
vector (6AR.8), which (6AS.2) does not yet realize.

This removes only the common-scalar parametrization obstruction.  It does not
align coordinatewise successor scales or prove that their recursion stays
vanishing: by 6AR that still requires `s*Q_j/S_j->0` at the selected cutoffs.
It also does not turn `s` into a maximum root-mesh bound (the obstruction in
6AP remains), guarantee positive reach of all components without
regularization, or supply the observer-deleted endpoint clock required by the
rectangle branch.

### Corollary 6AT (a retained atom bounds the intrinsic endpoint posterior)

The intrinsic replacement weight in 6AR cannot approach one along a chronology
which retains a fixed atom against that same full replacement.

Let `A,R` be two complete mover laws in one fixed opponent environment and
let

```text
L_e=(1-e)*A+e*R,             0<=e<=1.
```

For every fixed terminal coalition `C`, complete-law affinity gives exactly

```text
Pr_(L_e)(C)-Pr_R(C)
  =(1-e)*(Pr_A(C)-Pr_R(C)).                         (6AT.1)
```

The same identity holds after overriding a distinct observer by any fixed
pure stopping time, because that override changes only the common environment
of the mover mixture.  Hence, with reward bound `M`, every prescribed or
rectangle terminal atom at `C` satisfies

```text
|A_C(L_e,R)| <= M*(1-e).                            (6AT.2)
```

In particular, a retained reward-weighted atom of absolute size at least
`a>0` forces

```text
e <= 1-a/M.                                         (6AT.3)
```

Under a decoder convention such as `K*A_C>=q/4`, substitute
`a=q/(4*K)`.

This is a useful consistency check for 6AR.  The near-killed conditioning
which sends the intrinsic endpoint posterior to one necessarily destroys the
same fixed terminal atom before any fresh reconnection.  Conversely, atom
retention keeps the endpoint posterior uniformly away from one, but it does
not make that posterior tend to zero.  Therefore (6AT.3) supplies a uniform
nondegeneracy bound, not the vanishing adaptive scale required by (6AR.7).

### Proposition 6AU (two-sided Never regularization gives literal endpoint reach)

The source-only regularization in 6AH can be strengthened to the original
full replacements.  This removes exact-zero reach from all three laws used by
6AR--6AS: frozen source, inner reset, and original atom endpoint.  It still
does not control the size of their likelihood ratios.

At old rank `r`, write `h_r` for the frontier scale and put

```text
epsilon_r=h_r^4,
(s_r)^2=h_r^2+4*M*n*epsilon_r.                      (6AU.1)
```

As in 6AH, mix every source marginal with `Never` at weight `epsilon_r`.
In addition, replace every active mover's original replacement law by

```text
R'_(r,m)=(1-epsilon_r)*R_(r,m)+epsilon_r*Never.      (6AU.2)
```

At the full replacement endpoint for mover `m`, all `n` coordinates have
changed from the old endpoint by complete-law weight at most `epsilon_r`.
A sequential coupling gives coordinatewise prescribed and unrestricted-cap
changes at most `2*M*n*epsilon_r`, hence debt change at most
`4*M*n*epsilon_r`.  Therefore

```text
d_m(update sigma'_r m R'_(r,m))
 <=h_r^2+4*M*n*epsilon_r=(s_r)^2.                   (6AU.3)
```

The base-debt half bound follows after a finite truncation exactly as in
6AH.

The limiting tangent is unchanged.  At the same interpolation parameter,
mixing both endpoints with the same `Never` law commutes with their reset
mixture:

```text
(1-h_r)*[(1-epsilon_r)*sigma_r+epsilon_r*Never]
 +h_r*[(1-epsilon_r)*R_(r,m)+epsilon_r*Never]
= (1-epsilon_r)*[(1-h_r)*sigma_r+h_r*R_(r,m)]
  +epsilon_r*Never.                                 (6AU.4)
```

Thus the same-parameter source and reset profiles move by only
`O(n*epsilon_r)` in terminal semantics.  Changing the parameter from `h_r`
to `s_r` has the same `O(|s_r-h_r|)` cost as in 6AH.  Division by `s_r`,
together with

```text
epsilon_r/s_r->0,       |s_r-h_r|/s_r->0,
```

preserves every normalized debt direction and the source excess limit.
Reindexing after the finite endpoint/base truncations therefore gives another
positive-minimum tangent family with the same base, active support, and
tangent.

Every new source and every new regularized replacement has Never mass at least
`epsilon_r`; every inner reset at a strict scale has positive survival as
well.  Consequently any finite word cut from these laws has positive
component and endpoint reach.  The source connector of 6AQ/6AR is then an
actually reached port, while the separately grafted regularized replacement
has literal positive-reach residual provenance rather than only an arbitrary
post-kill gauge.  Its roots are not those of the old unsmoothed replacement.
A previously selected atom is therefore retained only perturbatively; for
literal source/replacement provenance, rerun the atom decoder after the new
tangent family has been constructed and reindexed.

This is still only qualitative.  In the killed model of 6AI, both source and
replacement survivals may be positive while their ratio is of order
`epsilon_r/h_r` or its inverse, so the posterior/scale collapse remains.
Positive endpoint reach also does not provide the small observer-deleted
endpoint survival needed for a rectangle cap.  Proposition 6AU removes a
zero-denominator obstruction, not the operationally sublinear selection.

### Proposition 6AV (vector inner scales remove the common-scale restart gap)

The coordinatewise values in (6AR.8) do not have to be aligned before the
frozen exposure and atom arguments can be rerun.  Both arguments extend
literally to a vector of inner scales.  What remains is a scale-availability
problem, not a common-parameter identity.

Fix a frozen source `A`, full replacements `R_j`, and outer weights
`w_j in [0,1]` on the active face.  For arbitrary inner scales

```text
0 <= s_j <= 1
```

put

```text
B_j=(1-s_j)*A_j+s_j*R_j,
P_s(j)=(1-w_j)*A_j+w_j*B_j
      =(1-w_j*s_j)*A_j+w_j*s_j*R_j.                (6AV.1)
```

Inactive coordinates remain at `A`.  Retain the data of 6AS: two distinct
fixed labels `first,second`, positive gain floors `g_first,g_second`, an atom
mover/observer/orientation of charge `q` and error `e`, reward bound `M>0`,
and terminal-outcome cardinality `K`.  Assume explicitly that

```text
0<w_first<1,              0<w_second<1.
```

Write

```text
L(s)=sum_(j active) w_j*s_j.                        (6AV.2)
```

Let `h>0` satisfy

```text
h <= s_first,       h <= s_second,
w_first*s_first <= 1/2,
w_second*s_second <= 1/2.                          (6AV.3)
```

Then the coordinatewise proof of (6AS.4) gives

```text
w_i*s_i*g_i/(2*M) <= EverQuitMass(P_s(i))
```

for `i=first,second`.  Consequently, for the same constant

```text
kappa=min(w_first*g_first/(4*M),
          w_second*g_second/(4*M))>0,              (6AV.4)
```

both ever-Quit masses are at least `2*kappa*h`.
`exists_finiteCutoff_two_marginalHazardSums` therefore gives one finite
literal cutoff at which the raw marginal hazard of each retained label is at
least `kappa*h`.

The whole-face atom calculation depends only on the effective complete-law
weights `w_j*s_j`.  Sequential terminal-law coupling gives

```text
|Pr_(P_s)(C)-Pr_A(C)| <= L(s),                      (6AV.5)
```

and the corresponding full-replacement comparison has the same upper bound.
The unrestricted observer cap is controlled uniformly over every behavioral
deviation exactly as in (6S.3).  Hence, if

```text
2*K*M*L(s) <= q/8,                                  (6AV.6)
```

the vector-scale head satisfies the same atom alternative with charge `q/2`,
the same terminal and pure-time orientation, and error

```text
e+4*M*L(s).                                         (6AV.7)
```

This already proves that equality of the inner scales was never used by the
exposure or whole-face atom estimates.

There is also an exact vector-scale successor identity.  At the selected
cutoff let `S_j,Q_j` be the prefix survivals of `A_j,R_j`, and suppose they
are positive.  Put

```text
D_j=(1-w_j*s_j)*S_j+w_j*s_j*Q_j.
```

For `w_j>0` define

```text
s_j^+ = s_j*Q_j/D_j,                                (6AV.8)
```

and put `s_j^+=0` when `w_j=0`.  Whenever `s_j^+<=1` for every positive-
weight coordinate, the all-Continue residual of (6AV.1) is exactly

```text
(1-w_j)*A_j^cut
  +w_j*((1-s_j^+)*A_j^cut+s_j^+*R_j^cut).          (6AV.9)
```

This is (6AR.4) coordinate by coordinate.  If the component tails behind the
cutoff are chosen to be a later frozen source `A^+` and its replacements
`R_j^+`, (6AV.9) is therefore the later vector-scale profile `P_(s^+)`
literally, not merely semantically.  If the selected mover's separate full-
replacement prefix also has positive reach and is grafted to `R_m^+`, the
counterfactual endpoint reaches exactly

```text
update P_(s^+) mover R_m^+.
```

Thus source and replacement provenance, terminal orientation, and the two
labels reconnect across the cutoff.  Proposition 6AU supplies positive
finite-prefix reach for its regularized source and replacement laws, subject
to rerunning the atom decoder on that reconstructed family.

The outer two-sided availability does not deteriorate in this refactor.  Its
weights remain exactly `w_j`, so for the two retained strict weights one may
keep the constant chart radius

```text
rho_outer=min(w_first,1-w_first,w_second,1-w_second)>0. (6AV.10)
```

The common-scale mismatch identified after 6AR is therefore removed: a
vector-scale port can be exposed, retain its atom, and reconnect exactly to a
later vector-scale port.

This is not the full Tier-I producer.  For a fixed port the legal exposure
scale above is bounded by

```text
h <= min(s_first,s_second),                         (6AV.11)
```

whereas the budget-stable system requires a packet at every requested
`0<h<rho(port)`.  The constant outer radius (6AV.10) does not imply (6AV.11).
If instead one declares the usable radius to be
`min(s_first,s_second)`, its successor loss is governed by the likelihood
ratios in (6AV.8) and can be order one relative to an arbitrarily smaller
requested `h`.  Moreover atom reuse requires `L(s)` small, while the two-label
progress uses the minimum of only two coordinates.  A full producer still
needs one of the following quantitative statements:

```text
* a source-matched way to lower/reselect the vector scales at cost o(h);
* a bound min(s_first^+,s_second^+) >=
      min(s_first,s_second)-chi(h) with chi(h)=o(h); or
* a different port radius which both implies (6AV.11) for every legal h and
  has summable loss.
```

No such statement follows from positive component reach.  Proposition 6AI
still supplies order-one likelihood-ratio drift after Never regularization.
The rectangle branch also retains its separate observer-deleted endpoint-cap
premise from Corollary 6AL.  Proposition 6AV narrows the open Tier-I issue to
scale availability and that rectangle port; it does not solve either one.

### Proposition 6AW (varying only the cutoff cannot pay the scale-radius loss)

There is a two-label rational regression showing that the last qualification
in 6AV is substantive even when every component has positive reach and the
outer availability radius is fixed.  It rules out a prefix-only implementation
of the budget-stable availability field; it does not rule out moving the first
root or changing the source chart.

For each of two distinct labels, let `A` Continue forever.  Let `R` Quit at
the first row with probability `1/2` and, conditional on Continue, follow
`A`.  Take

```text
w=1/2,             s=1/4.
```

The nested law

```text
P=(1-w)*A+w*((1-s)*A+s*R)
```

has first-row Quit probability

```text
w*s*(1/2)=1/16,                                    (6AW.1)
```

and no later Quit hazard.  Both source and replacement component survivals
through the first row are strictly positive.  Conditional on Continue, the
exact refactored inner scale from (6AV.8) is

```text
s^+ = [s*(1/2)]/[1-w*s*(1/2)]
    = (1/8)/(15/16)=2/15.                           (6AW.2)
```

Every positive-length cutoff has this same residual, because the component
tails agree after the first row.

Use the natural vector-port scale radius

```text
rho=min(s_first,s_second,w,1-w)=1/4.
```

At the successor it is

```text
rho^+=2/15,
rho-rho^+=7/60.                                     (6AW.3)
```

For every requested `0<h<rho`, the one-row word already gives each retained
label hazard `1/16 >= (1/4)*h`.  Thus the same literal prefix supplies the
required `kappa*h` progress with `kappa=1/4` at arbitrarily small requested
scales.  Nevertheless any availability inequality

```text
rho-chi(h) <= rho^+
```

forces

```text
chi(h) >= 7/60                                      (6AW.4)
```

for all those scales.  In particular `chi(h)` cannot tend to zero, much less
be operationally sublinear.

The example is exactly the vector nested-mixture chart of 6AV; there is no
zero denominator, posterior ambiguity, or common-scale mismatch.  The loss
comes from one unavoidable likelihood update before any positive block can
end.  Therefore accepting vector scales and shortening the same literal
prefix is not enough for Tier I.  A successful construction must move or
split the entrance root, choose a different actual source port, or prove a
frontier-specific likelihood cancellation which excludes (6AW.2).

The every-`h` statement here uses only the checked lower-progress interface:
the same root of hazard `1/16` is reused for all smaller declared scales.  It
is not a vanishing upper-mesh construction.

This is an abstract conditional-port regression, not a positive-minimum game
counterexample.  It does not include the tangent-family minimum, atom decoder,
or terminal exploitability source required for a negative answer to the
question.  Its only conclusion is that the remaining root-movement/selection
step cannot be omitted from the vector-scale route.

### Proposition 6AX (exact vector-port availability ledger)

The local availability loss of the vector chart can be written without an
unspecified continuity modulus.  This identifies the precise quantity which
a frontier-specific selection or root move must make operationally
sublinear.

Keep the notation of 6AV, use the halved circulation weights so that
`w_j<=1/2`, and suppose every positive-weight coordinate has `0<s_j<1`.
Then the effective-half premise in 6AV is automatic.  Let

```text
c_atom=q/(16*K*M),
L(s)=sum_j w_j*s_j,
rho_outer=min(w_first,1-w_first,w_second,1-w_second).
```

On the finite positive-weight set `J+`, define the usable vector-port radius

```text
rho(s)=min( rho_outer,
            s_first, s_second,
            c_atom-L(s),
            min_(j in J+) (1-s_j) ).                (6AX.1)
```

The condition `rho(s)>0` simultaneously supplies strict outer availability,
the two inequalities needed in (6AV.11), strict atom slack in (6AV.6), and
room below the upper inner-scale face.

At one cutoff, let `S_j,Q_j,D_j,s_j^+` be as in (6AV.8), and set

```text
Delta_j=s_j^+-s_j.
```

Direct subtraction gives the exact identity

```text
Delta_j
 = s_j*(1-w_j*s_j)*(Q_j-S_j)/D_j.                 (6AX.2)
```

In particular the sign of the scale motion is exactly the sign of the
replacement-versus-source survival difference; positivity of both survivals
does not control its magnitude.

Define the one-packet loss account

```text
Xi=max( 0,
        -Delta_first, -Delta_second,
        sum_j w_j*Delta_j,
        max_(j in J+) Delta_j ).                    (6AX.3)
```

Then

```text
rho(s)-Xi <= rho(s^+).                              (6AX.4)
```

Indeed, the second and third entries of (6AX.3) pay any decrease of the two
clock scales.  The weighted-sum entry pays the increase
`L(s^+)-L(s)` which reduces atom slack.  The last entry pays every increase
of an inner scale, which reduces `min_j(1-s_j)`.  The outer radius is constant
and `Xi>=0`.  Comparing `rho(s)-Xi` separately with the five entries in
(6AX.1) proves (6AX.4).

Consequently a source-matched packet selection satisfying

```text
Xi(port,h) <= chi(h)                                (6AX.5)
```

would populate the exact `radius_successor` field of the budget-stable packet
system with the radius (6AX.1).  The remaining analytic obligation is not a
generic continuity statement: it is to select actual cutoffs, later frozen
component tails, or entrance-root moves so that the right side of (6AX.2)
has an operationally sublinear common majorant in the requested scale `h`.
Proposition 6AW has `Xi>=7/60` for all sufficiently small `h`, showing that
component positivity alone cannot prove (6AX.5).

This ledger still assumes that the successor scales in (6AV.8) stay in
`[0,1]` and that the prescribed/rectangle atom data are freshly available at
the later frozen component pair.  It does not supply the rectangle
observer-deleted endpoint port or any Tier-II candidate-anchor seam.

### Proposition 6AY (zero-cost deflation of a reached reset coefficient)

The displayed inner coefficient is not itself intrinsic to an actual reached
port.  It can always be made smaller without changing one root, one semantic
coordinate, or the named full endpoint.  What cannot be manufactured this
way is a larger amount of the endpoint law inside the reached law.

Let `A,R` be two complete stopping-time laws for one player and let

```text
P=(1-p)*A+p*R,                  0<p<1.              (6AY.1)
```

For every `a` with `0<=a<=p`, define

```text
A[a]=((1-p)/(1-a))*A+((p-a)/(1-a))*R.              (6AY.2)
```

Here `a<=p<1`, so the normalization is legal.  Both coefficients in (6AY.2)
are nonnegative and sum to one, and direct
expansion gives

```text
P=(1-a)*A[a]+a*R.                                  (6AY.3)
```

Moreover, for every bounded payoff functional `f` which is affine in this
player's complete stopping law,

```text
E_R[f]-E_(A[a])[f]
  = ((1-p)/(1-a))*(E_R[f]-E_A[f]),                 (6AY.4)

E_R[f]-E_P[f]
  = (1-a)*(E_R[f]-E_(A[a])[f].                     (6AY.5)
```

Apply this coordinatewise to a product profile `X`, keeping the opponents at
their actual marginals.  The profile `X`, its full endpoint
`update X j R_j`, their prescribed payoffs and unrestricted caps, every
terminal atom between them, and every fixed pure-time witness are literally
unchanged: only the latent source component used to describe `X_j` has been
replaced.  Hence this is a genuine nonadditive port **chart change**, not a
semantic seam or a root actuator.

It also preserves the quantitative exposure argument.  Suppose terminal
rewards have absolute value at most `M>0`, put

```text
G_j=U_j(update X j R_j)-U_j(X)>0,
```

and choose `0<a<=min(p,1/2)`.  From (6AY.5), the endpoint gain from `A[a]`
to `R` in the actual opponent environment is `G_j/(1-a)`.  Coupling the two
component laws shows that their payoff difference is at most
`2*M` times the sum of their ever-Quit masses.  Since

```text
EverQuitMass(P)
 = (1-a)*EverQuitMass(A[a])+a*EverQuitMass(R)
 >= a*(EverQuitMass(A[a])+EverQuitMass(R)),
```

one obtains

```text
EverQuitMass(P) >= a*G_j/(2*M*(1-a))
                >= a*G_j/(2*M).                    (6AY.6)
```

Thus, whenever the actual source/full-endpoint gain and atom data have already
been retained, the *same actual source profile* may answer every smaller
declared exposure scale `h<=min(p,1/2)` by using the chart (6AY.2) with `a=h`.
Neither the source atom nor its endpoint has to be re-proved after that
deflation.

Conditioning commutes with the same calculation.  If a finite prefix has
positive mixed reach and positive endpoint-component reach, its actual
residual has a posterior representation

```text
P^+=(1-p^+)*A^++p^+*R^+.
```

Every `a^+<=p^+` gives another exact representation of this *same* residual
by (6AY.2).  Consequently the coordinate-specific stored values in 6AV can
always be deflated after arrival; their signed loss `Xi` is not an invariant
obstruction if the port type is quotiented by these chart changes.

The intrinsic resource is the available endpoint **submeasure**.  Formula
(6AY.3) cannot increase the largest coefficient `p_max` for which
`P-p_max*R` is a nonnegative measure.  Since complete stopping-time laws live
on the countable space `Omega=N union {infinity}`, this coefficient has the
exact formula

```text
p_max(P,R)=inf_(omega : R(omega)>0) P(omega)/R(omega). (6AY.7)
```

The infimum lies in `[0,1]`; coefficient `a` is legal in (6AY.3) exactly when
`a<=p_max(P,R)`.  If `E` is a positive-probability conditioning event for
both laws, then

```text
p_max(P(.|E),R(.|E))
 = [R(E)/P(E)] * inf_(omega in E, R(omega)>0) P(omega)/R(omega). (6AY.8)
```

Thus the intrinsic successor carrier can be strictly larger than the
posterior of one previously chosen decomposition: some of the endpoint law
may also have been hidden inside its old source component.  But the exhibited
coefficient obtained by conditioning (6AY.1) is only

```text
p^+=p*Q/((1-p)*S+p*Q),                              (6AY.9)
```

which may tend to zero.  A positive budget-stable radius bounded by this
carrier coefficient therefore still needs a uniformly positive lower bound
along the selected chronology, equivalently a domination/likelihood-ratio
condition of the kind isolated in Proposition 6AB, or a fresh endpoint
injection supplied by root movement.  Zero-cost deflation does not provide
that lower bound, does not retain an atom after changing the endpoint law,
and does not address the rectangle observer-deleted cap port.

At the opposite boundary, a retained fixed terminal atom bounds the intrinsic
coefficient away from one, not only a chosen mixture coordinate.  Indeed
`P=p_max*R+(1-p_max)*A_max` and total-variation contraction to terminal
outcomes give

```text
terminalAtomMagnitude <= M*(1-p_max).               (6AY.10)
```

Thus the atom supplies the upper side of a two-sided intrinsic carrier.  The
only missing side is the uniform positive lower domination addressed above.

The gain is a sharper formulation of the remaining Tier-I question.  One need
not control the motion of an arbitrarily chosen inner coordinate `s` itself.
One must instead preserve or replenish a positive amount of the **same named
replacement law** inside the actual reached marginal while retaining its
gain and atom orientation.  Equivalently, the selected actual conditional law
must dominate the named conditional endpoint law by one uniform positive
constant, or a root move must inject a fresh dominated endpoint.  Ordinary
Bayes positivity supplies a positive displayed amount at each finite cutoff,
but not this uniform domination constant.

This also sharpens the interpretation of 6AW.  Before its firing row the
maximal coefficient of the full law `R` inside the actual law is `1/8`.
After Continue, both the actual conditional law and the conditional endpoint
law are `delta_infinity`, so their intrinsic coefficient is `p_max=1`, even
though the particular nested-coordinate value is `2/15`.  Thus the numerical
loss `7/60` is genuinely a failure of that coordinate chart, not an invariant
loss of measure domination.  Restart still fails, for the more important
reason that the conditional source and endpoint now coincide: their gain and
terminal atom are zero.  A useful availability radius must therefore measure
an **oriented endpoint carrier**--domination together with retained endpoint
contrast--rather than a raw posterior coefficient alone.

### Proposition 6AZ (zero-Never periodicization bypasses posterior comparability)

There is a second exact way around the posterior ledger on the prescribed
branch.  Uniform likelihood-ratio comparability in Proposition 6AB is needed
to remain on the original conditional two-component fiber.  It is not needed
if two actual labelled marginals already have zero Never mass and one is
allowed to replace the complete tails by a literal periodic continuation.

Let `P` be an actual behavioral profile and let

```text
E=update P first R_first
```

be a named full endpoint.  Fix `second!=first`.  Suppose

```text
NeverMass(P_first)=NeverMass(P_second)=0,            (6AZ.1)
```

and suppose every finite prefix of `P` has strictly positive joint
all-Continue reach.  (The 6AH Never regularization is one way to guarantee
this finite-reach condition.)  Suppose also that the pair `P,E` carries a
prescribed atom of charge `q>0` with fixed mover `first`, observer, and
terminal orientation `C`.  Assume rewards are bounded by `M`.

For a cutoff `L`, let `W_L` be the first `L` product roots of `P`, and let
`V_L` be the corresponding word for `E`.  The words differ only in the
`first` coordinate.  Define the periodic profiles

```text
X_L = W_L W_L W_L ...,
Y_L = V_L V_L V_L ... .                             (6AZ.2)
```

Equivalently `Y_L` is obtained from `X_L` by replacing only `first` with the
periodic repetition of its endpoint word.  These are legal ordinary
behavioral profiles, and by the finite-reach hypothesis,

```text
Residual_allC^L(X_L)=X_L,                           (6AZ.3)
```

as an actually reached port.  The corresponding endpoint identity holds as
literal replacement provenance whenever its reach is positive and as a
counterfactual suffix identity otherwise.

Let `beta_L` and `beta_L^E` be the two words' joint all-Continue reaches.
Because the `second` marginal is unchanged at the endpoint, (6AZ.1) gives

```text
beta_L -> 0,                beta_L^E -> 0.           (6AZ.4)
```

The two profile pairs agree through their respective first `L` roots.
Coupling on survival through the common prefix therefore gives, for the
fixed prescribed terminal coordinate,

```text
|A_C(X_L,Y_L)-A_C(P,E)|
  <= M*(beta_L+beta_L^E).                            (6AZ.5)
```

In particular, for all sufficiently large `L`, the same mover, observer,
terminal orientation and prefix provenance give a prescribed atom of charge
`q/2` on the exact periodic source/endpoint pair.

The actual source semantic pair also returns to the frozen one.  For a player
`i`, let `delta_(L,-i)` be the all-Continue reach of the first `L` source
roots after deleting `i`.  Two distinct zero-Never labels imply

```text
delta_(L,-i) -> 0                  for every i:      (6AZ.6)
```

if `i=first`, use `second`; if `i=second`, use `first`; otherwise either
label remains.  Common-prefix coupling, uniform over every deviation of
player `i` before taking the best-response supremum, now gives

```text
|U_i(X_L)-U_i(P)| <= 2*M*beta_L,
|B_i(X_L)-B_i(P)| <= 2*M*delta_(L,-i).               (6AZ.7)
```

Thus the full terminal semantic pair of `X_L` converges coordinatewise to
that of the actual frozen source `P`, not merely in prescribed payoff.

Finally choose `L` beyond any finite cutoff which captured positive hazard
sums for both retained labels.  Every period of `X_L` contains those same
positive sums.  Hence both marginal hazard series diverge from every suffix,
and joint and every one-player-deleted survival vanish.  The one port `X_L`
with successor itself may use a constant positive legal-scale radius and zero
availability loss exactly as in Proposition 6AO; the same finite word answers
all smaller declared scales under the current lower-progress-only packet
interface.

This is a genuine conditional prescribed-arm Tier-I kernel.  It is stronger
than the fixed-fiber side of 6AB in one respect: no likelihood-ratio bound
between source and endpoint components is assumed.  Periodicization discards
the old complete tails instead of following their posterior fiber.

It is not the requested arbitrary-entry theorem.  The constructed source is
`X_L`, which is close to `P` but need not equal an independently supplied
reached port.  The proof also relies on two actual zero-Never labels, a
prescribed atom, and the lower-progress-only scale convention.  The frozen
radial source at one rank need not satisfy (6AZ.1), and the rectangle arm
still needs the observer-deleted endpoint-cap port.  Proposition 6AZ therefore
identifies an exact solved subbranch rather than closing Tier I.

The literal periodic identities use `quittingCyclicBehaviorProfile` and
`quittingProfileLiveRoot_cyclicBehaviorProfile` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Cycles/PeriodicCompiler.lean`.  The cap coupling
is the same unrestricted-deviation finite-splice estimate audited in 6W and
6AL; no bounded-controller approximation is used.

### Corollary 6BA (zero-Never replacement closes the periodic rectangle port)

The prescribed restriction in 6AZ has one exact conditional relaxation.
Keep its actual source hypotheses, set `observer=second`, and suppose the atom
alternative is the rectangle branch with pure stopping time `tau`.  In
addition assume

```text
NeverMass(R_first)=0.                                (6BA.1)
```

Construct `X_L,Y_L` from the source and full-endpoint prefixes exactly as in
6AZ, and apply the same pure-time override `tau` to `observer` on the original
and periodic source/endpoint profiles.

On the source side, after deleting the observer, the unchanged mover law
`P_first` has survival tending to zero by (6AZ.1).  On the endpoint side the
corresponding deleted-observer reach is bounded by the survival of
`R_first`, which tends to zero by (6BA.1).  Thus all four overridden terminal
profiles have the required common-prefix tail-forgetting property.  The
rectangle terminal atom on `X_L,Y_L` converges to the original one.

More importantly, for every behavioral deviation of `observer`, the original
full rectangle endpoint and its periodic version can differ only after all
of the observer's opponents survive the first `L` rows.  That probability is
at most the `R_first` survival.  Taking suprema after the uniform coupling
bound gives

```text
|B_observer(periodicEndpoint)-B_observer(originalEndpoint)|
  <= 2*M*Survival_(R_first)(L),                     (6BA.2)
```

and the same bound holds for prescribed payoff.  Hence the periodic endpoint
observer debt exceeds the original endpoint debt by at most

```text
4*M*Survival_(R_first)(L) -> 0.                     (6BA.3)
```

For sufficiently large `L`, the exact self-loop therefore retains the same
rectangle orientation and pure-time witness, a fixed smaller atom charge,
and the vanishing endpoint-debt estimate.  This is precisely the extra
observer-deleted endpoint port assumed abstractly in Corollary 6AL.

Corollary 6BA is again a conditional periodic-source kernel, not a producer
from the frozen frontier.  The checked two-label packet gives zero Never mass
for neither the actual labels nor the replacement law.  Nor does this result
enter an independently supplied reached source.  It says only that, once the
three displayed zero-Never facts and positive finite source reach are
available, the rectangle cap creates no further periodic-restart obstruction.

### Proposition 6BB (zero-Never component-pair smoothing preserves the radial port)

The zero-Never premise in 6AZ has one exact componentwise producer which also
retains the nested radial stopping-law provenance.  It is conditional on the
frozen source **and replacement** laws of both retained labels already having
zero Never mass; the tangent family does not currently supply that premise.

Let `G` be a fixed stopping law of full support on `N` and zero mass at
`infinity`, for example `G(t)=2^(-(t+1))`.  For `0<epsilon<1` define

```text
Smooth_epsilon(mu)=(1-epsilon)*mu+epsilon*G.         (6BB.1)
```

This is an ordinary complete stopping law.  It is coupled to `mu` with
mismatch probability at most `epsilon`, has positive survival through every
finite date, and has zero Never mass whenever `mu` does.

Fix one aligned frozen simultaneous radial source with common source
marginals `A_j`, full replacements `R_j`, outer weights `w_j`, and inner scale
`h`.  Its active marginal is the exact collapsed law

```text
P_h(j)=(1-w_j*h)*A_j+w_j*h*R_j.                     (6BB.2)
```

Suppose the retained labels `first!=second` satisfy

```text
Never(A_j)=Never(R_j)=0,       j=first,second.       (6BB.3)
```

For a strict radial coefficient `0<w_j*h<1`, this premise is also necessary
for a zero-Never executed marginal while retaining the literal two-component
decomposition.  Indeed

```text
Never(P_h(j))
  =(1-w_j*h)*Never(A_j)+w_j*h*Never(R_j),            (6BB.3a)
```

and both summands are nonnegative with strictly positive coefficients.  Thus
`Never(P_h(j))=0` if and only if both component Never masses vanish.

For every active coordinate replace `A_j,R_j` by their smoothed laws and
rebuild the nested radial marginal with the **same** `w_j,h`; for an inactive
coordinate smooth its unchanged actual marginal.  By affinity, every rebuilt
active marginal is also

```text
P'_h(j)=Smooth_epsilon(P_h(j)).                     (6BB.4)
```

Thus the whole simultaneous cube retains its outer and inner mixture
identities, source-component and full-replacement labels, and reset
orientations literally.  Write `P'_h` for the resulting product profile and
let

```text
E'_h=update P'_h first Smooth_epsilon(R_first).      (6BB.5)
```

Every coordinate has positive survival through every finite date.  The two
retained actual marginals and the named `first` replacement have zero Never
mass.  If `n=|I|`, coordinatewise coupling gives source mismatch at most
`n*epsilon`, and endpoint mismatch at most `n*epsilon`.  Hence, for reward
bound `M`, every prescribed or unrestricted-cap coordinate of the source or
endpoint moves by at most

```text
2*M*n*epsilon,                                      (6BB.6)
```

and every debt coordinate moves by at most `4*M*n*epsilon`.  A fixed
reward-weighted terminal atom between the source and endpoint moves by at
most

```text
2*M*n*epsilon.                                      (6BB.7)
```

The last estimate couples the source pair and endpoint pair separately and
uses the terminal orientation's reward bound.  Therefore any fixed atom
charge, mover/observer labels, terminal orientation, and pure-time witness
survive with their usual smaller constants when `epsilon` is sufficiently
small.  A vanishing endpoint-debt tolerance remains vanishing when
`epsilon->0`; if the original field requires an `O(h^2)` bound, choose for
example `epsilon=o(h^2)`.  Taking `epsilon=o(h)` also preserves every positive
`kappa*h` exposure bound after an arbitrarily small reduction of `kappa`;
alternatively the zero-Never retained marginals each have total ever-Quit
mass one and a fresh common finite cutoff can be selected directly.

Apply 6AZ to the smoothed source/endpoint pair in the prescribed arm.  In the
aligned rectangle selection `first=mover`, `second=observer`, apply 6BA: its
additional endpoint zero-Never premise follows from (6BB.3) for `R_first`.
In both arms all finite source prefixes are
actually reachable, and the periodic construction gives an exact self-loop,
two persistent labels, the retained orientation and component provenance,
zero availability loss, and the global payoff/debt bounds.

Thus Tier I is closed on the conditional branch (6BB.3), for both atom arms.
The condition is stronger than zero Never of the executed radial marginals,
but it is exactly what prevents the smoothing step from destroying the named
source/replacement decomposition.

This strength is necessary for this particular port repair by (6BB.3a).  If
`Never(R_first)>0`, any actual source law which contains `R_first` with a
positive mixture coefficient also has positive Never mass.  Relocating that
Never atom into finite time can make the executed source zero-Never, but then
the original `R_first` is no longer a positive submeasure of it; the literal
stopping-law mixture provenance is lost.  Changing `R_first` by an order-one
amount can in turn destroy the atom and endpoint cap.  Hence source-only
zero-Never is insufficient for this provenance-preserving construction.

Nothing here proves (6BB.3) along the positive-minimum frontier or enters an
independently supplied reached port.  The smoothing displacement can be made
`o(h)`, but it is still an explicit source change, not equality with a prior
actual continuation and not the Tier-II external candidate-anchor adapter.

### Proposition 6BC (finite/Never localization of an atom or gain)

The four zero-Never component premises in 6BB have a finite exact precursor.
Every complete stopping law is a mixture of its conditional finite-quit law
and `Never`; an affine contrast therefore localizes to one of four component
pairs.  A small mover debt at the original endpoint also localizes
quantitatively to the selected replacement component.

Fix one mover `m` and one opponent profile.  For a stopping law `mu`, put

```text
f_mu=1-mu(infinity),              n_mu=mu(infinity).
```

When `f_mu>0`, let `mu^F` be `mu` conditioned on a finite stopping time; let
`mu^N=delta_infinity`.  Zero-weight components may be assigned arbitrarily
because they will never be selected.  Then

```text
mu=f_mu*mu^F+n_mu*mu^N.                              (6BC.1)
```

The `F` component has zero Never mass and the `N` component is pure Never.

Let `A,R` be a source/replacement pair.  Suppose `Phi` is affine in the
mover's complete stopping law and has oscillation at most `L>0`:

```text
|Phi(mu)-Phi(nu)|<=L                                 (6BC.2)
```

for all stopping laws.  If, for one sign `s in {+1,-1}`,

```text
s*(Phi(A)-Phi(R))>=q>0,                              (6BC.3)
```

then there are types `u,v in {F,N}` with positive product weight

```text
omega=a_u*r_v,
a_F=f_A, a_N=n_A, r_F=f_R, r_N=n_R,                 (6BC.4)
```

such that

```text
omega*s*(Phi(A^u)-Phi(R^v))>=q/4,
omega>=q/(4*L),
s*(Phi(A^u)-Phi(R^v))>=q/4.                         (6BC.5)
```

Indeed affine expansion gives

```text
s*(Phi(A)-Phi(R))
 =sum_(u,v) a_u*r_v*s*(Phi(A^u)-Phi(R^v)).          (6BC.6)
```

One of the four summands is at least `q/4`.  It is positive, so its weight is
positive; (6BC.2) then gives the weight lower bound, and `omega<=1` gives the
last inequality.

Assume additionally that `Phi` is accompanied by the mover's endpoint debt
bound

```text
B_m-U_m(R)<=epsilon,                                (6BC.7)
```

where `B_m` is the unrestricted cap against the fixed opponents.  The cap is
independent of the mover's prescribed stopping law, and every component law
is an allowed deviation.  Hence

```text
epsilon
 >=sum_v r_v*(B_m-U_m(R^v)).                        (6BC.8)
```

All summands are nonnegative.  Since the selected replacement weight
`r_v>=omega`, its endpoint debt satisfies

```text
B_m-U_m(R^v)<=4*L*epsilon/q.                        (6BC.9)
```

There are two applications.

1. For a fixed prescribed terminal orientation `C` and observer `o`, take

   ```text
   Phi(mu)=r_o(C)*Pr_(update source m mu)(C).
   ```

   If rewards are bounded by `M>0`, its oscillation is at most `M`.  A charged
   atom of magnitude at least `q` therefore selects one of the four exact
   source/replacement types with the same signed orientation at charge at
   least `q/4`, product weight at least `q/(4*M)`, and selected endpoint mover
   debt at most `4*M*epsilon/q`.

2. For the mover prescribed payoff, suppose

   ```text
   U_m(R)-U_m(A)>=g>0.
   ```

   Its oscillation is at most `2*M`.  The same proof, with the order of the
   contrast reversed, selects a type pair with gain at least `g/4`, product
   weight at least `g/(8*M)`, and endpoint mover debt at most
   `8*M*epsilon/g`.

Thus both the atom and the exposure-producing gain admit a finite four-type
dispatch:

```text
(F,F), (F,N), (N,F), (N,N).                          (6BC.10)
```

The `(F,F)` output is exactly the zero-Never source/replacement component
input consumed by 6BB after full-support smoothing.  The other three outputs
are explicit pure-Never component residuals rather than an uncontrolled
continuum of stopping laws.

This does not yet prove Tier I.  Applying the atom and gain localizations to
the same mover can select different cells of (6BC.10); no argument here
co-localizes both positive contrasts.  The second retained clock label has a
gain localization but no supplied atom.  Nor is a source component selected
for one mover automatically compatible with the common source components
selected for every other mover.  The exact next finite question is therefore
whether the frozen balance/debt signs force the atom and both mover gains
onto compatible `(F,F)` cells, or whether one of the three displayed Never
patterns has a separate strategic consumer.

### Proposition 6BD (atom and mover gain can be co-localized by one fixed patch)

The first nonclaim after 6BC can be removed for one mover.  The atom-selected
pair already has an almost nonnegative mover gain because its replacement is
almost a best response.  Mixing it with the gain-selected pair retains a
fixed atom fraction and creates a fixed positive gain on one source/
replacement pair.

Use the atom application of 6BC to choose `(A_0,R_0)` with

```text
Atom(A_0,R_0)>=q/4,
d_m(R_0)<=delta_0:=4*M*epsilon/q.                    (6BD.1)
```

The atom is written with its already selected sign.  Use the gain application
to choose `(A_1,R_1)` with

```text
Gain(A_1,R_1)>=g/4,
d_m(R_1)<=delta_1:=8*M*epsilon/g.                    (6BD.2)
```

All four laws face the same fixed opponents.  Since `R_0` is within
`delta_0` of the unrestricted cap and `A_0` is itself an allowed deviation,

```text
Gain(A_0,R_0)=U_m(R_0)-U_m(A_0)>=-delta_0.           (6BD.3)
```

Set

```text
theta=q/(16*M+4*q),
A_theta=(1-theta)*A_0+theta*A_1,
R_theta=(1-theta)*R_0+theta*R_1.                    (6BD.4)
```

Here `0<theta<1`.  Any signed terminal atom with reward bound `M` is at least
`-M` on `(A_1,R_1)`.  Affinity in both complete laws therefore gives

```text
Atom(A_theta,R_theta)
 >=(1-theta)*q/4-theta*M
 =q/4-theta*(M+q/4)
 =3*q/16
 >=q/8.                                             (6BD.5)
```

The mover gain is also affine:

```text
Gain(A_theta,R_theta)
 >=theta*g/4-(1-theta)*delta_0.                     (6BD.6)
```

Consequently, once

```text
epsilon<=theta*g*q/(32*M),                           (6BD.7)
```

the patched pair has

```text
Gain(A_theta,R_theta)>=theta*g/8>0.                 (6BD.8)
```

Finally the cap is invariant under changing the mover's own prescribed law,
so its endpoint debt is exactly affine and satisfies

```text
d_m(R_theta)
 <=(1-theta)*delta_0+theta*delta_1 ->0.             (6BD.9)
```

Thus different atom and gain cells in (6BC.10) are not a co-localization
obstruction.  One fixed mixture produces a single named pair with a retained
fixed atom, a retained fixed positive mover gain, and vanishing endpoint
debt.

The Never pattern remains finite and exact.  Because `0<theta<1`, the patched
source has zero Never mass exactly when **both** `A_0,A_1` are `F` components;
the patched replacement has zero Never mass exactly when both `R_0,R_1` are
`F` components.  Hence the good input to 6BB is the `(F,F)` atom cell together
with an `(F,F)` gain cell.  Every other output records which side of one of
those two selected cells is pure Never.

This still does not solve simultaneous packet selection.  The construction
modifies one mover's source marginal while all opponents are frozen.  Applying
it independently to the two circulation movers and then combining their new
source marginals can change each mover's opponent environment by order one,
so neither gain estimate automatically survives.  The remaining compatibility
problem is now entirely across players, not between the atom and gain for one
player.

### Proposition 6BE (literal persistent clocks force zero Never mass)

There is a global obstruction to interpreting "sufficiently close actual
reached port" as an arbitrary terminal-semantic neighborhood condition.  It
does not depend on the radial chart.

Let `sigma` be an actual behavioral profile.  Suppose finite literal root
words `W_0,W_1,...` concatenate in their displayed order to the root sequence
of `sigma`; equivalently, after every word the advertised successor is the
actual all-Continue residual of the preceding profile.  Fix one label `i`.
Suppose there are `kappa>0` and positive scales `h_n` such that

```text
sum_(root in W_n) q_i(root) >= kappa*h_n,
sum_n h_n = infinity.                               (6BE.1)
```

Here the last display means that the nonnegative partial sums tend to
`+infinity` (equivalently, the positive sequence `(h_n)` is not summable).

Then

```text
Pr_(sigma_i)(Never)=0.                              (6BE.2)
```

Indeed, (6BE.1) implies that the sum of all marginal Quit hazards in
`sigma_i` diverges.  More directly, if its Never mass were `z>0`, then its
survival `S_t` before every finite row would satisfy `S_t>=z`.  Writing
`p_t=S_t*q_t` for the unconditional mass of quitting at row `t` gives

```text
sum_t q_t <= (1/z)*sum_t p_t <= 1/z,                (6BE.3)
```

a contradiction.  Applying this argument to the two retained labels shows
that every literal Tier-I chronology consumed by a divergent-scale schedule
must start with zero Never mass in both labelled marginals.

This conclusion also follows directly from the checked budget-stable
iteration once its external actual-port provenance is imposed.  The selected
scales have divergent sum, each block contributes at least `kappa*h_n`, and
exact successor provenance says that flattening the selected words recovers
one actual root chronology.  Notice that this uses an external field which
`QuittingBudgetStablePacketSystem` deliberately does not store: without the
literal residual-profile identification, arbitrary root words need not be
the roots of the advertised actual source.

The obstruction is topologically sharp.  Given any stopping law `mu` and
`0<epsilon<1`, put

```text
mu_epsilon=(1-epsilon)*mu+epsilon*Never.             (6BE.4)
```

Then `mu_epsilon` has Never mass at least `epsilon`, while its stopping-time
law is coupled to `mu` with mismatch probability at most `epsilon`.  Apply
this to the two retained coordinates of an arbitrary product profile.  For
terminal rewards bounded by `M`, sequential coupling gives, conservatively,

```text
|Delta U_i| <= 4*M*epsilon,
|Delta B_i| <= 4*M*epsilon,
|Delta debt_i| <= 8*M*epsilon                       (6BE.5)
```

for every player `i`; the cap estimate is uniform over every behavioral
deviation before taking the supremum.  A reward-weighted terminal atom
between a source and a named endpoint changes by at most `8*M*epsilon` if
the same two-coordinate perturbation is made on both sides.  Thus all strict
finite atom, gain, orientation and endpoint-error inequalities survive after
an arbitrarily small perturbation of their constants.

This can be done **without changing the displayed packet roots**.  Fix any
finite source word of length `L` having positive all-Continue reach.  Keep
every root through `L` and replace only the conditional tail of each retained
label by

```text
(1-epsilon)*(old conditional tail)+epsilon*Never.  (6BE.5a)
```

If `S_i(L)>0` is that label's prefix survival, its new complete law has Never
mass at least `S_i(L)*epsilon>0`.  Its total-law coupling mismatch from the
old law is at most `S_i(L)*epsilon<=epsilon`.  The same tail splice may be
made behind the named full-replacement prefix when that prefix has positive
reach; a zero-reach endpoint tail may instead be left unchanged, since it has
no effect on the complete law.  Hence the source and endpoint words,
mover/observer labels, terminal orientation, and every raw root in the frozen
packet remain literal, while the semantic and atom estimates are still
bounded by (6BE.5).  This fixed-prefix version directly applies to an actually
reached finite packet; if its source joint reach were zero, it could not
advertise a reusable reached successor in the first place.

The perturbation can preserve the complete radial provenance, not only the
semantic pair.  Replace every relevant source component `A_j` and named full
replacement `R_j` by (6BE.4) and rebuild the same nested mixtures.  Affinity
retains the exact component labels and reset identities, while every rebuilt
source and replacement component has positive Never mass.  With a
rank-dependent choice such as `epsilon_r=h_r^4`, Proposition 6AU records that
the positive-minimum tangent-family limits and endpoint tolerances are
unchanged.  Therefore positive-Never actual ports occur arbitrarily close in
all of the finite semantic and strict atom/reset data currently used to state
entry.

Consequently:

```text
No terminal-semantic neighborhood of a frozen packet can be an
arbitrary-entry domain for a literal persistent-two-label Tier-I kernel.
                                                               (6BE.6)
```

Any positive theorem must instead do one of the following:

```text
* select or construct a zero-Never actual port before iteration;
* restrict the port domain by an explicit zero-Never/persistent-tail field;
* or provide a genuine source-changing implementation adapter.
```

This is a capstone-level interface obstruction, not a finite quitting-game
counterexample.  It does not show that a suitable zero-Never port cannot be
selected from a positive-minimum frontier, and it does not obstruct a solved-
game disjunct.  It does rule out the universal arbitrary-nearby-port reading
of Tier I and shows why the Never regularizations in 6AH/6AU can be used for
finite reach diagnostics but cannot themselves be the literal seeds of the
persistent chronology.

Two boundary tests are exact.  A stationary law with Quit probability
`c in (0,1)` has zero Never mass and may be partitioned into one-row blocks;
its hazard budget is genuinely infinite.  At the opposite boundary,
`(1-epsilon)*mu+epsilon*Never` has positive Never mass even when `mu` has
zero Never mass, so no reblocking of its literal roots can satisfy (6BE.1).
Zero Never is only necessary: it does not by itself retain an atom, endpoint
contrast, or a two-sided conditional component chart.

For a Lean handoff, the scalar implication is already nearly the theorem
`ScalarHazard.neverMass_eq_zero_of_tendsto_sum_atTop` in
`MathUE/Probability/DiscreteHazardStopping.lean`; the Boolean-profile bridge
uses `quittingHazardNeverMass` and its stopping-law identity in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Paths/BehaviorStoppingLaw.lean`.  The block
flattening calendar and the positive nonsummable scale field are in
`MathUE/SublinearCostSchedule.lean`, while the actual packet selection is in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Debt/Dynamic/BudgetStableCompatiblePacketIteration.lean`.
The perturbation (6BE.4) is the ordinary behavioral strategy
`quittingStoppingLawMixtureBehaviorStrategy` from
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Paths/StoppingLawMixture.lean`, not public
randomization.
No controller or new observation is being introduced: the private mixture is
represented by its ordinary date-by-date hazard, and the cap bounds in
(6BE.5) quantify over every unilateral behavioral deviation.  Conversely,
replacing the positive-Never residual by a newly selected strategy after a
block would be a source change, not execution of the advertised actual
behavioral continuation; that is exactly the external adapter left open.
The new formal field would be the external equality between the flattened
selected roots and the live hazards of the advertised actual seed profile;
it must not be inferred from `QuittingBudgetStablePacketSystem`, which stores
no such profile.

### Revised capstone target

The remaining positive Tier-I target must now include an actual zero-Never
entrance selection.  On such a selected port, close the `(F,F)` component
compatibility/rectangle branch using 6BB--6BD, or replenish the same named

Absent that selection, semantic closeness is insufficient by Proposition

problem from the maintained question.

## 6BF. A killed source reduces to one uncontrolled cap coordinate

This proposition records the sharp local content of a literal sure-Quit row.
It does **not** dispatch the killed-source branch, because the mover's own cap
is exactly the remaining obstruction.

Let `P` be a product behavioral profile and suppose player `m` Quits surely
at a live row `T`.  Fix `s != m` and a later date `K>T`.  Form `P[K,s]` by
changing only `s`'s conditional law at and after `K`, forcing it to quit by
date `K` (equivalently, cap its old stopping time at `K`).  Then

```text
U(P[K,s]) = U(P),                                   (6BF.1)
B_i(P[K,s]) = B_i(P)       for every i != m.         (6BF.2)
```

Indeed, prescribed play terminates no later than `T`, so the modification is
unreachable and (6BF.1) is literal.  If `i != m` makes an arbitrary behavioral
deviation, the coordinate `m` is still unchanged and still Quits surely at
`T`; hence that deviated play also terminates before the changed tail.  The
payoff equality holds uniformly before taking the supremum over `i`'s
deviations, proving (6BF.2).  Consequently

```text
Sem(P[K,s])-Sem(P) is supported only in the cap coordinate B_m,
debt(P[K,s])-debt(P) is supported only at m.          (6BF.3)
```

Thus `P[K,s]` is an exact actual source with two zero-Never marginal laws:
`m` by its old sure-Quit row and `s` by the new finite cap.  This is stronger
than a semantic perturbation and preserves every frozen prefix root through
the sure row.

It does not make the killed `m`-component survive the packet cutoff.  Its
conditioned posterior weight is still zero.  Therefore this construction
addresses only the literal two-zero-Never entrance obstruction isolated in
6BE; a Tier-I availability repair still has to soften or replace the killed
component and reconnect the resulting positive posterior to a later rank.

There is a corresponding endpoint statement.  Let

```text
E = update P m R_m,
E[K,s] = update P[K,s] m R_m,
```

where the replacement law `R_m` is held fixed.  Updating distinct coordinates
commutes.  Under the natural coupling, the two endpoint profiles can differ
only if the play reaches date `K` under `R_m`; hence, for rewards bounded by
`M`,

```text
|U_i(E[K,s])-U_i(E)| <= 2*M*S_Rm(K),                 (6BF.4)
|B_i(E[K,s])-B_i(E)| <= 2*M*S_Rm(K)   for i != m,    (6BF.5)
```

where `S_Rm(K)` is the replacement marginal's survival to `K`.  The cap bound
again holds uniformly over every deviation of `i != m`, because that deviation
does not remove the common replacement law of `m`.  If `R_m` has zero Never
mass, these errors tend to zero.  The same coupling retains any fixed terminal
atom or pure-time contrast whose observer is distinct from `m`, up to its
usual bounded reward times `S_Rm(K)` loss.

No analogous estimate follows for `B_m`: a deviation by `m` deletes both its
old sure row at the source and its replacement law at the endpoint, so it can
reach the newly installed sentinel with probability one.  Standard one-seed
finite-cap sharpness examples show that this is not merely a missing factor in
the coupling estimate.  Proposition 6BF therefore converts the killed-source
residual into the following one-scalar problem:

```text
Choose the late sentinel so that it does not raise B_m, or turn a fixed
positive B_m rise into an independently solved strategic branch.           (6BF.6)
```

This reduction is internal only.  It supplies neither a declared decrease nor
a restartable Tier-I kernel, and it is not an export candidate.

The obstruction has an exact min--max formulation.  For the retained
`s != m`, let

```text
chi_ZN(m,s) = inf { B_m(rho) : Pr_(rho_s)(Never)=0 },
```

where the other coordinates of `rho` are arbitrary opponent behavioral laws
and `m`'s coordinate is overwritten in `B_m`.  The ordinary punishment value
is the same infimum without the zero-Never restriction, so
`chi_m<=chi_ZN(m,s)`.

If equality holds, the killed branch can be repaired to arbitrary accuracy.
At a late splice date retain the old opponent prefix and append a tail with
`s` zero-Never and cap at most `chi_m+epsilon`.  The old suffix cap is at least
`chi_m`.  Backward optimal-stopping recursion is monotone and nonexpansive in
the terminal cap, hence the new entrance cap is at most the old one plus
`epsilon`.  Equations (6BF.1)--(6BF.5) then give an actual two-zero-Never port
with every semantic loss arbitrarily small.

Thus the unresolved branch is precisely

```text
chi_ZN(m,s) > chi_m.                                 (6BF.7)
```

This strict gap cannot be dismissed by stationary-minmax equality.  For
example, if `m`'s solo and Never payoffs are zero while every opponent-only
terminal coalition pays `m` one, all-Continue holds `m` to zero, but every
opponent environment in which `s` quits almost surely lets `m` Continue and
earn one.  This example is only a sharpness test for the scalar implication;
it is not a positive-minimum frontier or a quitting-game counterexample.
Consuming (6BF.7) by a paid, sure-exit, deletion, or other checked solved-game
branch remains necessary before 6BF becomes capstone-level.

## 6BG. The proposed Tier-II same-root anchor is globally impossible at positive minimum

The direct-seam barrier understates the obstruction.  Even if no direct seam
from the actual source to the artificial anchor is declared, an exact
candidate chain on the **same persistent literal roots** already determines
its own comparison with the actual terminal semantic pair of those roots.
This is the checked theorem
`QuittingBoundedSeamChain.actualDebtGap_le_two_mul_card_eta` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Debt/Dynamic/ChronologicalSeamReduction.lean`.

Here is its exact specialization.  Let `I` be finite and let `D_*>0` satisfy

```text
D_* <= sum_i debt(Sem(sigma))_i                         (6BG.1)
```

for every ordinary behavioral profile `sigma`.  Let `C` be any
`QuittingBoundedSeamChain`; in particular its candidate annotations are
uniformly bounded, its candidate debts are nonnegative, its roots are literal
product roots, and its candidates satisfy the exact semantic-prefix recursion.
Assume:

```text
* every prescribed seam series and cap seam series is summable;
* joint survival on the roots tends to zero;
* every one-player-deleted survival tends to zero;
* debt(C.candidate(0))_i <= eta for every i; and
* sum_t prescribedSeam_i(t) + sum_t capSeam_i(t) <= eta
  for every i.
```

Then

```text
D_* <= 2*|I|*eta.                                      (6BG.2)
```

No root-Nash hypothesis occurs here.  The proof compares `candidate(0)` with
the semantic pair of the literal infinite root profile.  Joint-survival
uniqueness controls the prescribed coordinate; player-deleted-survival
uniqueness controls the unrestricted behavioral cap; subtraction controls
debt.  Summing (6BG.1) for that literal profile gives (6BG.2).

For the positive-minimum tangent family, take

```text
D_* = debtSum(family.base).
```

Every actual terminal semantic pair belongs to the semantic carrier, so
`family.base_minimum` proves (6BG.1), while `family.base_positive` gives
`D_*>0`.  Positive minimum also implies that the player type is nonempty.
Choosing

```text
eta = D_*/(4*|I|)
```

in (6BG.2) yields `D_* <= D_*/2`, a contradiction.

Consequently, the maintained Tier-II fields cannot all coexist with positive
minimum after Tier I supplies persistent literal roots.  In particular, a
separately named all-behavior implementation relation between an actual port
`R(x)` and an artificial anchor `A(x)` cannot repair the contradiction:
the `actualPair` in the checked comparison already uses the supremum over all
unilateral behavioral deviations, and it is constructed from the same roots.
The extra relation is never used.

The block compiler makes the conflict literal.  The checked theorem
`QuittingBudgetStablePacketSystem.exists_summableSeamSource_of_seed` supplies
the exact chain, survival, initial-debt, and total-seam hypotheses above from
the proposed packet system and an arbitrary-accuracy seed.  Hence a Tier-II
adapter of the displayed kind is not an independent local ingredient: in the
positive-minimum branch its construction is already the conjecture-closing
contradiction.

More explicitly, Tier-II items 1--5 map to the checked obstruction as follows.

```text
items 1--2  -> concatenate the internal candidates and successors;
               entrance/exit anchoring plus exact internal prefixes give
               QuittingBoundedSeamChain.exact_step on the flat calendar;

item 3      -> prescribedSeam and totalSeam are bounded blockwise by omega;

item 4      -> the operational sublinear schedule keeps the selected ports
               available while making the omega-cost series summable;
               Tier I's two persistent labels give joint and every
               player-deleted survival tending to zero;

item 5      -> candidate(0)=A(x_eta) has coordinatewise debt <=eta.
```

These are exactly the fields assembled by
`QuittingBudgetStablePacketSystem.exists_summableSeamSource_of_seed` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Debt/Dynamic/BudgetStableCompatiblePacketIteration.lean`.
Its flattened prescribed-seam tsum and total-seam tsum are each at most the
requested budget.  Since `totalSeam=prescribedSeam+capSeam`, the hypothesis
of (6BG.2) has the advertised constant, with no extra factor hidden between
block and calendar notation.  Tier-II item 6 is not among the hypotheses.

This map is a statement about the maintained **typed composition**.  The five
informal bullets alone do not mention all fields of
`QuittingBoundedSeamChain` and `QuittingBudgetStablePacketSystem`: uniform
annotation bounds, nonnegative candidate debt, persistent literal-root
survival, and an actually summable selected schedule must also be present.
Those fields are, however, explicitly required by the intended composition
through the checked packet compiler.  The qualification therefore does not
leave the maintained same-root Tier-II architecture open.

The strongest clean one-chain statement is therefore:

> **Positive-minimum persistent-seam impossibility.**  For a positive-minimum
> tangent family and a bounded seam chain on any literal root sequence with
> vanishing joint and all player-deleted survival, no chain can simultaneously
> have coordinatewise initial candidate debt at most `eta` and coordinatewise
> total seam tsum at most `eta` when
> `eta < debtSum(family.base)/(2*card(I))`.

The global-gap input to the checked theorem is literally

```text
family.base_minimum
  (quittingTerminalSemanticPair reward profile)
  (quittingTerminalSemanticPair_mem_carrier reward profile).
```

Here `family.base_minimum` is a field of
`QuittingPositiveMinimumDebtTangentFamily` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Diagnostics/Quitting/StoppingLaw/PositiveMinimumDebtTangentFamily.lean`,
and `quittingTerminalSemanticPair_mem_carrier` is in
`UniformEquilibrium/Diagnostics/Quitting/TerminalSemanticPlateauIncidence.lean`.
The quantitative conclusion is precisely
`QuittingBoundedSeamChain.actualDebtGap_le_two_mul_card_eta` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Debt/Dynamic/ChronologicalSeamReduction.lean`.

A narrow Lean handoff can specialize that declaration directly; no new
probability or optimal-stopping proof is needed.  The likely theorem shape is

```text
QuittingPositiveMinimumDebtTangentFamily.not_exists_persistentSmallSeamChain
  (chain : QuittingBoundedSeamChain reward)
  (eta : Real)
  (hsmall : eta < debtSum family.base / (2*card I))
  (hsummable, hjoint, hopponent, hinitial, htotal) : False.
```

The proof instantiates the global gap as above, applies

this rules out a **single** sufficiently accurate chain; no limiting family of
chains and no compactness argument is required.

This leaves only three honest architectural exits:

```text
1. prove an independent solved-game disjunct;
2. change the literal roots/profile whose terminal semantics are compared; or
3. abandon either arbitrary-small seed debt or summably vanishing total seam.
```

This section is an internal status correction, not an export candidate.  Its
mathematical content is already formalized by the named seam-chain theorem;
the only contribution here is the exact specialization to the maintained
two-tier interface.  It does not prove that a positive-minimum game exists or
give a counterexample to the quitting-game conjecture.

## 6BH. The corrected finite Tier-II interface must export a terminal residual

The sharp local statement stops before the infinite summable-seam chain.  It
keeps the terminal discrepancy as an explicit output instead of attempting to
erase it with a separate implementation field.

Let `C` be a bounded seam chain and fix a finite length `L`.  Write

```text
E^U_t(i) = C.actualPair(t).U_i - C.candidate(t).U_i,
E^B_t(i) = C.actualPair(t).B_i - C.candidate(t).B_i,

beta_L   = product_(t<L) jointContinue(C.root(t)),
beta_i,L = product_(t<L) opponentContinue(C.root(t),i).
```

Then the one-step prefix inequalities in
`ChronologicalSeamReduction.lean` iterate finitely to

```text
|E^U_0(i)| <= sum_(t<L) prescribedSeam_i(t)
                + beta_L*|E^U_L(i)|,                 (6BH.1)

|E^B_0(i)| <= sum_(t<L) capSeam_i(t)
                + beta_i,L*|E^B_L(i)|.               (6BH.2)
```

No summability or asymptotic survival hypothesis is used.  The proof is the
finite induction already appearing inside the private helper
`abs_le_tsum_of_discounted_recursion`: apply `prescribedError_step` or
`capError_step`, use nonnegativity of the discount, and use discount at most
one to discard the weights on the finite seam costs.  The last error retains
the full survival product.

Since debt is `B-U`, (6BH.1)--(6BH.2) give

```text
debt(C.actualPair(0))_i
 <= debt(C.candidate(0))_i
    + sum_(t<L) totalSeam_i(t)
    + beta_L*|E^U_L(i)|
    + beta_i,L*|E^B_L(i)|.                            (6BH.3)
```

At a positive minimum `D_*`, summing (6BH.3) and applying the global carrier
lower bound to the literal root profile yields

```text
D_* <= sum_i [
          debt(C.candidate(0))_i
          + sum_(t<L) totalSeam_i(t)
          + beta_L*|E^U_L(i)|
          + beta_i,L*|E^B_L(i)| ].                   (6BH.4)
```

In particular, if the initial candidate debts and the finite total seams are
each at most `eta` coordinatewise, then

```text
sum_i [beta_L*|E^U_L(i)| + beta_i,L*|E^B_L(i)|]
  >= D_* - 2*|I|*eta.                                (6BH.5)
```

Thus for `eta<=D_*/(4*|I|)`, every valid finite local adapter must export at
least `D_*/2` of survival-weighted terminal semantic discrepancy.  This is
the exact boundary which disappears in 6BG: on a persistent infinite chain,
boundedness and vanishing joint/deleted survival force the terminal term to
zero, producing the contradiction.

The corrected local Tier-II interface is therefore not

```text
actual source -> small-debt same-root anchor with an unspecified external
implementation relation.
```

It is instead

```text
actual source -> finite exact candidate block
              -> explicit terminal residual port carrying (6BH.5).
```

A useful producer must then do something nonlocal and checkable with that
residual: send it to an independently solved-game branch, move to genuinely
different literal roots, or prove a well-founded decrease of a declared

### Proposition 6BK (strategically precompact watchdogs cannot force a fixed gap)

Status: complete ordinary proof, self-audited, awaiting independent review.
This is an incentive-gadget architecture no-go.  It strictly extends the
finite-watchdog theorem to profile-dependent infinite families and isolates
the genuinely unbounded-time feature that any surviving witness selector must
have.

#### Self-contained setting

Let `I` be finite and let `r` be any finite quitting reward table.  A
behavioral strategy of one player is identified with its induced quit-time
law on

```text
Omega = N union {infinity}.
```

For player `i`, define the reward-dependent strategic pseudometric

```text
d_i(tau,tau') = sup_(rho_-i)
  |U_i(tau,rho_-i)-U_i(tau',rho_-i)|,                (6BK.1)
```

where the supremum ranges over all ordinary independent behavioral opponent
profiles.  Let `D_i` be a nonempty family of behavioral strategies for each
player, and assume `D_i` is totally bounded for `d_i`: for every `eps>0`
there is a finite `F_i subset D_i` such that every `tau in D_i` is within
`eps` of some `f in F_i`.

#### Theorem 6BK.1 (precompact-watchdog impossibility)

For every `eps>0`, there is an ordinary behavioral profile `sigma` such that

```text
U_i(tau,sigma_-i) <= U_i(sigma)+eps
for every i and every tau in D_i.                    (6BK.2)
```

Consequently no strategically totally bounded watchdog families can certify
a fixed positive all-profile exploitability gap, even when the watchdog
chosen from `D_i` depends arbitrarily on the candidate profile.

#### Proof

Choose finite `eps`-nets `F_i subset D_i`.  Form the finite strategic-form
game whose pure strategies for player `i` are the elements of `F_i`, with
payoff equal to the quitting game's exact terminal payoff.  Nash's theorem
gives a mixed equilibrium `lambda` of this finite game.

Independently sampling a pure stopping law from each `lambda_i` gives a
mixture of probability laws on `Omega`.  A mixture of quit-time laws is again
one quit-time law, and therefore has the behavioral hazard representation

```text
q_t = Pr(T=t | T>=t).
```

Let `sigma_i` be that behavioral law.  Independence of the players' finite-
game mixtures makes the joint terminal distribution exactly the product of
the marginal mixture laws, so the finite-game equilibrium inequalities say

```text
U_i(f,sigma_-i) <= U_i(sigma)                         (f in F_i). (6BK.3)
```

For arbitrary `tau in D_i`, choose `f in F_i` with `d_i(tau,f)<eps`.
By `(6BK.1)` at the particular opponent profile `sigma_-i`,

```text
U_i(tau,sigma_-i)
  <= U_i(f,sigma_-i)+eps
  <= U_i(sigma)+eps.
```

This proves `(6BK.2)`.  Notice that `sigma_i` need not belong to `D_i`; the
finite-watchdog escape profile is allowed to be any ordinary behavioral law.

#### Corollary 6BK.2 (profile-dependent selector obstruction)

Suppose an architecture assigns to every profile `sigma` a player
`i(sigma)` and a deviation `W(sigma)` satisfying

```text
U_i(W(sigma),sigma_-i)-U_i(sigma) >= g              (6BK.4)
```

for one fixed `g>0`.  For each player let

```text
D_i={W(sigma) : i(sigma)=i}.
```

At least one nonempty `D_i` is not totally bounded in `d_i`.  Otherwise
pad every empty `D_i` by an arbitrary singleton behavioral law.  The padded
families are nonempty and totally bounded, and padding an empty selector range
adds no deviation that can be chosen by `(6BK.4)`.  Theorem 6BK.1 with
`eps<g` then produces a profile contradicting `(6BK.4)`.

Thus allowing the profitable deviation to depend on the profile does not by
itself evade the watchdog no-go.  Its range must be strategically
nonprecompact.

#### Corollary 6BK.3 (literal tail escape in total variation)

Let

```text
M=max(1, max_(S,i)|r(S)_i|).
```

For quit-time laws `mu,nu`, maximal coupling of the two clocks while keeping
the opponents fixed gives

```text
d_i(mu,nu) <= 2*M*TV(mu,nu).                         (6BK.5)
```

Indeed, coupled terminal coalitions agree whenever the two player clocks
agree; on the mismatch event the two bounded payoffs differ by at most
`2*M`.

On the countable space `Omega`, a family of laws is totally bounded in total
variation if and only if it is uniformly tight in the discrete sense

```text
forall eps>0, exists finite A subset Omega,
  sup_(mu in D) mu(A^c)<eps.                         (6BK.6)
```

For the forward implication, take a finite total-variation net and a finite
high-mass set for each net point, then union those sets.  For the reverse
implication, move the mass outside one common finite high-mass set to a fixed
point of that set; the resulting laws lie in a finite-dimensional simplex,
which has a finite total-variation net.

By `(6BK.5)`, total-variation total boundedness implies strategic total
boundedness.  Hence the selector in Corollary 6BK.2 has some player `i` and
some constant `kappa>0` such that, for every `N`, one of its selected
deviations satisfies

```text
Pr(N<T_i<infinity) >= kappa.                         (6BK.7)
```

To see this, failure of strategic total boundedness implies failure of
total-variation total boundedness and hence failure of `(6BK.6)`.  Thus there
is `eps0>0` such that for every finite `A`,

```text
sup_(mu in D_i) mu(A^c) >= eps0.
```

The supremum need not be attained.  Set `kappa=eps0/2`; by the definition of
supremum there is a selected law with complement mass strictly greater than
`kappa`.  Apply this to the finite set

```text
A_N={0,1,...,N,infinity}.
```

The complement is exactly the finite times strictly after `N`.

In particular, if the selected witnesses are pure times, some player's
selected finite witness times are unbounded.  The stronger mixed statement
`(6BK.7)` says that a fixed amount of witness mass must escape beyond every
finite horizon; merely adjoining `Never` to a uniformly tight finite-time
family does not suffice.

#### Theorem 6BK.4 (all-behavior existence for precompact complete reply classes)

The preceding obstruction becomes a universal strategy-architecture
existence theorem when the watchdog class is best-response complete.  Assume
that each `D_i` is strategically totally bounded and that for every arbitrary
behavioral opponent profile `rho_-i`,

```text
sup_(all behavioral tau_i) U_i(tau_i,rho_-i)
  = sup_(tau_i in D_i) U_i(tau_i,rho_-i).             (6BK.8)
```

Then the quitting game has terminal `eps`-Nash profiles for every `eps>0`
against **all** behavioral deviations, and consequently has a uniform-
equilibrium payoff.

Indeed, choose `sigma` from Theorem 6BK.1.  Inequality `(6BK.2)` for every
member of `D_i`, followed by the supremum and `(6BK.8)`, gives

```text
sup_(all tau_i) U_i(tau_i,sigma_-i) <= U_i(sigma)+eps
```

for every player.  Thus `sigma` is an unrestricted terminal `eps`-Nash
profile.  Apply the checked equivalence
`quittingGame_exists_uniformEquilibriumPayoff_iff_terminalNash_all_errors`.

Therefore no incentive-gadget counterexample can belong to the precisely
defined class of quitting tables admitting strategically precompact complete
reply families.  A candidate proof may use a compact continuum of deviations
only if that continuum is incomplete for some opponent clock law; if it is
complete, the finite-net Nash escape already supplies all-error terminal
approximate equilibria.

#### Boundaries and examples

1. A finite watchdog menu is totally bounded, so Theorem 6BK.1 recovers the
   existing finite-watchdog impossibility.
2. Any family with a common finite horizon, or with uniformly exponentially
   decaying finite tails, is total-variation totally bounded.  This includes
   geometric clocks with hazard uniformly bounded below, even after adjoining
   the isolated `Never` strategy.
3. Geometric clocks with hazards tending to zero are not uniformly tight in
   total variation: their mass escapes to later finite times before the
   zero-hazard `Never` boundary.  The theorem deliberately does not exclude
   this genuinely noncompact architecture.
4. The pure-time family `{0,1,2,...,Never}` is not total-variation totally
   bounded, since distinct point masses have distance one.  It is the exact
   boundary used by profile-dependent stopping-time best responses.

#### Consequence for `INCENTIVE_GADGET`

A proof of the requested fixed exploitability gap may of course use the full
unrestricted best response.  Proposition 6BK says precisely what no compact
watchdog substitute can do: any profile-dependent witness selector proving a
fixed gap must have nonprecompact strategic range and, therefore, a player
whose selected clock laws place fixed mass arbitrarily far out in finite
time.  Theorem 6BK.4 also rules out, with an unrestricted all-behavior
consumer, the universal architecture in which a precompact family is complete
for best responses.  This rigorously confirms that the remaining gadget
mechanism must be genuinely nonlocal in time.  It does not construct the
reward table or rule out reward tables whose best responses have this tail
escape.  The reviewed standalone packet is
`exports/STRATEGICALLY_PRECOMPACT_WATCHDOG_IMPOSSIBILITY.md`.

### Proposition 6BL (one exceptional nonprecompact player is still harmless)

Status: complete ordinary proof with one independent PASS/no repair; second
review and packet gate pending.  This strengthens Proposition 6BK at the
exact threshold where its simultaneous global finite nets first fail.

Retain the quitting game, strategic pseudometrics `d_i`, and nonempty
behavioral reply families `D_i`.  Fix one distinguished player `e`.  Assume
only that

```text
D_i is totally bounded for d_i whenever i != e.     (6BL.1)
```

No compactness, tightness, or bounded-horizon condition is imposed on `D_e`.

#### Theorem 6BL.1 (one-exception finite-net escape)

For every `eps>0`, there is an ordinary behavioral profile `sigma` such that

```text
U_i(tau_i,sigma_-i) <= U_i(sigma)+eps
for every player i and every tau_i in D_i.           (6BL.2)
```

#### Proof

For every `i!=e`, choose a finite `eps`-net `F_i subset D_i` in `d_i`.  Put

```text
A_-e = product_(i!=e) F_i.
```

This is a finite nonempty set of pure opponent profiles.  Map the completely
arbitrary exceptional family into the finite-dimensional payoff cube by

```text
Phi(tau)(a_-e)=U_e(tau,a_-e),
Phi : D_e -> [-M,M]^(A_-e).                          (6BL.3)
```

Every bounded subset of a finite-dimensional normed space is totally bounded.
Therefore the image of `Phi` has a finite `eps`-net in the sup norm, with
representatives chosen from the image.  Choose a finite `E_e subset D_e` such
that, for every `tau in D_e`, some `f in E_e` satisfies

```text
max_(a_-e in A_-e)
  |U_e(tau,a_-e)-U_e(f,a_-e)| < eps.                (6BL.4)
```

Form the finite strategic game with pure sets `E_e` for the exceptional
player and `F_i` for every other player.  Let `lambda` be a mixed Nash
equilibrium.  As in Proposition 6BK, replace each marginal mixture of
quit-time laws by its single behavioral hazard representation, obtaining an
ordinary independent behavioral profile `sigma`.  The finite-game payoff and
every finite-game deviation payoff are unchanged.

For `i!=e`, approximate any `tau_i in D_i` by `f_i in F_i` in the global
pseudometric `d_i`; the finite Nash inequality gives `(6BL.2)`.  For the
exceptional player, its opponents' mixed action profile is a probability law
on the finite set `A_-e`.  Averaging `(6BL.4)` against that law gives

```text
|U_e(tau,sigma_-e)-U_e(f,sigma_-e)| < eps,
```

and the finite Nash inequality for `f in E_e` again gives `(6BL.2)`.  No
metric control on `D_e` against opponent profiles outside `A_-e` was used.

#### Corollary 6BL.2 (two distinct unbounded-time identities are necessary)

Suppose a profile-dependent architecture selects, at every profile `sigma`,
a player `i(sigma)` and deviation `W(sigma)` with gain at least one fixed
`g>0`.  Let

```text
D_i={W(sigma):i(sigma)=i}.
```

Then at least **two distinct** nonempty ranges `D_i` are not totally bounded
in their strategic pseudometrics.  Otherwise pad empty ranges by arbitrary
singletons, choose the sole possible nonprecompact range as `D_e` (or choose
any `e` if none exists), and apply Theorem 6BL.1 with `eps<g`.  The selected
deviation at the resulting profile contradicts `(6BL.2)`.

In particular, even a completely unrestricted, profile-dependent pure-time
selector cannot certify an all-profile fixed gap if it always names the same
deviating player.

Combining this with `(6BK.5)--(6BK.7)`, there are two distinct players `i,j`
and constants `kappa_i,kappa_j>0` such that each identity has selected laws
with fixed finite mass arbitrarily late:

```text
for every N, some mu in D_i has mu(N<T<infinity)>=kappa_i,
for every N, some nu in D_j has nu(N<T<infinity)>=kappa_j.       (6BL.5)
```

The two laws in `(6BL.5)` need not be selected at the same candidate profile.
The theorem gives an identity-level necessity, not simultaneous two-clock
exposure.

#### Theorem 6BL.3 (one-exception complete classes imply a uniform payoff)

Assume `(6BL.1)` and suppose every `D_i`, including the arbitrary exceptional
family, is complete for unrestricted best responses:

```text
sup_(all behavioral tau_i) U_i(tau_i,rho_-i)
  = sup_(tau_i in D_i) U_i(tau_i,rho_-i)             (6BL.6)
```

for every arbitrary behavioral opponent profile.  Then the game has an
unrestricted terminal `eps`-Nash profile for every `eps>0`, and hence a
uniform-equilibrium payoff.  Indeed, take `sigma` from Theorem 6BL.1, take
the supremum in `(6BL.2)`, use `(6BL.6)`, and apply
`quittingGame_exists_uniformEquilibriumPayoff_iff_terminalNash_all_errors`.

Thus a universal reply architecture may contain one genuinely nonprecompact,
unbounded-time player and still be solved.  A possible counterexample must
have at least two strategically nonprecompact best-response identities.

#### Exact scope

The exceptional-player approximation is deliberately profile-set dependent:
it sees `D_e` only through finitely many payoff coordinates after the other
players have been netted.  With two arbitrary nonprecompact players, neither
can be finitely coordinatized before the other, and this proof stops.  The
result therefore does not iterate to eliminate two exceptional identities.

For `INCENTIVE_GADGET.md`, this rules out every architecture in which all
profile-dependent unbounded witness times belong to one clock player while
the remaining watchdog ranges are strategically precompact.  It does not
force the two late identities in `(6BL.5)` to coexist at one profile, and so
does not by itself produce the two strict-first target atoms required by the
square-root clock contradiction.

strategic total boundedness.  The present proof uses a different asymmetric
step: after netting all ordinary players, it compactifies the exceptional
family only through its finite vector of payoffs against those net points.
A narrow search for `all but one`, `one noncompact`, `exceptional player`, and

ordinary ingredients reviewed for 6BK.

This is a universal strategy-architecture theorem, not a claim that the
quitting game itself has only one strategically nonprecompact best-response
identity.  The two-identity obstruction is necessary for a fixed-gap selector
and is not asserted to be sufficient for one.

### Proposition 6BM (one proper sentinel compactifies every other clock)

Status: complete ordinary proof with one independent PASS/no repair; second
review and packet gate pending.  This uses the special topology of
quitting-time laws and is stronger than 6BL in a different direction.

Call a stopping law **proper** if it Quits at a finite time almost surely.  A
reply family `D_s` is **properly strategically approximable** if, for every
`eps>0`, there is a finite nonempty collection `F_s` of proper behavioral
laws—not necessarily members of `D_s`—such that

```text
for every tau in D_s, some f in F_s has d_s(tau,f)<eps.  (6BM.1)
```

The empty family satisfies this convention by taking any proper singleton.

#### Theorem 6BM.1 (proper-sentinel escape)

Fix one player `s` whose family satisfies `(6BM.1)`.  No restriction at all
is imposed on the other players' deviation families.  For every `eps>0`
there is an ordinary behavioral profile `sigma` such that

```text
U_s(tau_s,sigma_-s) <= U_s(sigma)+eps       (tau_s in D_s),
U_i(tau_i,sigma_-i) <= U_i(sigma)           (i!=s, every behavioral tau_i).
                                                            (6BM.2)
```

#### Proof: the sentinel restores weak continuity

Give

```text
Omega=N union {infinity}
```

its one-point compactification topology and let `P(Omega)` carry the weak
topology.  It is a compact convex metrizable set, and its points are exactly
behavioral stopping laws.

Choose a finite proper `eps`-net `F_s` from `(6BM.1)`.  Let the sentinel's
auxiliary strategy set be the finite-dimensional simplex `conv(F_s)`, viewed
as the corresponding compact convex set of stopping laws.  Give every other
player the full compact convex strategy set `P(Omega)`.

The quitting terminal payoff is jointly continuous on this restricted
product.  To check the only nonstandard point, let `M` bound absolute rewards.
Because `F_s` is finite and every one of its laws is proper, for every
`eta>0` there is `H` such that

```text
sup_(nu in conv(F_s)) nu(T_s>H)<eta.                 (6BM.3)
```

On the complementary event `T_s<=H`, absorption occurs by date `H`.  The
terminal payoff restricted to this event is a finite sum of products of the
coordinates

```text
mu_i({0}),...,mu_i({H}), mu_i({H+1,H+2,...,infinity}),
```

all of which are weakly continuous because the displayed sets are clopen in
the one-point compactification.  Discarding `T_s>H` changes any payoff by at
most `M*eta`, uniformly over every other stopping law.  Hence the actual
terminal payoff is a uniform limit of continuous finite-coordinate
polynomials and is jointly continuous.

It is affine in every player's own law.  The Fan--Glicksberg/Kakutani theorem
therefore gives a Nash profile of this compact auxiliary game.  It is already
an ordinary independent product of behavioral stopping laws.  Every player
`i!=s` can choose any law in `P(Omega)`, so its inequality in `(6BM.2)` is
exact.  For the sentinel, auxiliary Nash controls every `f in F_s`; approximate
an arbitrary `tau_s in D_s` in the global strategic pseudometric and obtain
the first inequality of `(6BM.2)`.

The argument precisely avoids the standard weak-compactification seam: an
all-Never boundary cannot be approached jointly because the proper sentinel
absorbs almost surely, and `(6BM.3)` makes that absorption uniform over its
finite auxiliary simplex.

#### Corollary 6BM.2 (every player must carry a late-or-Never obstruction)

For a fixed-gap profile-dependent selector, let `D_i` be the selected range
of player `i`.  Then **no** player range is properly strategically
approximable.  If one `D_s` were, apply Theorem 6BM.1 with `eps<g`: deviations
of every other player are controlled exactly, while the selected sentinel
deviation is controlled within `eps`, a contradiction.  In particular every
player identity must occur somewhere in the selector range; an empty range
would be properly approximable.

There is a literal tail consequence.  Proper finite approximation in total
variation is equivalent, on `Omega`, to finite-time uniform tightness

```text
for every eta>0, exists H,
  sup_(mu in D_s) mu(T_s>H)<eta,                     (6BM.4)
```

where `T_s>H` includes `T_s=infinity`.  For the forward implication, take a
finite proper net and one common finite tail cutoff.  For the reverse
implication, truncate every law to `{0,...,H}` and net the resulting finite
simplex.  Since `d_s<=2M*TV`, failure of proper strategic approximation
implies failure of `(6BM.4)`.  Thus, for every player `s`, some `kappa_s>0`
satisfies

```text
for every H, some selected mu in D_s has
  mu(H<T_s<=infinity)>=kappa_s.                     (6BM.5)
```

Precisely, failure of `(6BM.4)` gives `eta_s>0` for which the relevant
supremum is at least `eta_s` at every `H`; take `kappa_s=eta_s/2`, so no
attainment of the supremum is assumed.

This mass may be Never.  Combining 6BM.2 with 6BL.2 sharpens the architecture
boundary:

```text
every player identity must exhibit late-or-Never mass,
and at least two distinct identities must exhibit genuinely late finite mass.
                                                            (6BM.6)
```

The witnesses in `(6BM.6)` still need not occur at the same candidate profile.

There is a sharper description of the identities which are not among the two
nonprecompact ones.  Let `P_s` denote the set of all proper laws.  If a range
`D_s` is strategically totally bounded but is not properly strategically
approximable, then there are `tau_s in D_s` and `zeta_s>0` with

```text
inf_(p in P_s) d_s(tau_s,p) >= zeta_s.              (6BM.6a)
```

Indeed, failure of proper approximation gives an error `eta>0`.  Take a
finite `eta/3`-net of `D_s`.  If every net center were within `eta/3` of one
proper law, those finitely many proper laws would form a `2eta/3`-net,
contradicting the chosen error.  Hence one center has positive distance from
every proper law.  Such a `tau_s` necessarily has positive Never mass, since
a proper law has distance zero from `P_s`.

Thus every strategically precompact calibrator range in a surviving gadget
must contain an **essential Never witness** separated in payoff from every
almost-surely finite clock.  The other possibility is genuine strategic
nonprecompactness, which occurs for at least two identities by 6BL.

#### Theorem 6BM.3 (one proper complete class implies a uniform payoff)

Suppose one player `s` has a properly strategically approximable reply family
`D_s` which is complete for its unrestricted best responses against every
behavioral opponent profile.  There is no hypothesis on any other player's
reply class.  Apply Theorem 6BM.1 and use completeness to upgrade the
sentinel's first inequality in `(6BM.2)` to its unrestricted cap; every other
player is already controlled against all behavioral laws.  Hence terminal
`eps`-Nash profiles exist for every `eps>0`, and
`quittingGame_exists_uniformEquilibriumPayoff_iff_terminalNash_all_errors`
gives a uniform-equilibrium payoff.

This is a universal all-behavior existence class with only one structured
player.  It does not assert that an arbitrary quitting table has such a
properly approximable complete class.

#### Boundaries and source comparison

Finite menus of finite quit times satisfy `(6BM.1)`.  Uniformly finite-time
tight families do as well.  A family consisting of `Never` can fail it when
late proper clocks remain strategically separated from Never; an unbounded
pure-time family can fail through the usual relative-time tests.  Those are
the intended surviving seams.

There is also a nontrivial exact `Never` test.  Suppose the sentinel's solo
reward and every terminal reward received while it is absent are zero.  Let
`mu_L` be uniform on `L` distinct finite dates.  Against arbitrary opponents,
`mu_L` and Never differ only when the sentinel ties the opponents' first
coalition: preemption pays the zero solo row, and waiting pays a zero absent
row.  The tie probability is at most `1/L`, so

```text
d_s(mu_L,Never) <= M/L.                             (6BM.7)
```

Thus the singleton family `{Never}` is properly strategically approximable
even though its sole law is not proper.  If in addition all rows containing
the sentinel pay it at most zero, `{Never}` is best-response complete and
Theorem 6BM.3 applies.  This boundary is consistent with the theorem: a
weakly indifferent zero-payoff sentinel may use a diffuse proper clock to
compactify the other players without improving its own payoff.

`notes/CODEX_CEDAR__STOPPING_TIME_COMPACT_GAME.md` records why the full weakly
compact stopping-law game is discontinuous when all clocks escape together:
finite common quit times can converge to all Never while retaining a tie
payoff.  Proposition 6BM does not invoke a generic discontinuous-game theorem;
it removes exactly that seam with one uniformly proper sentinel and then uses
ordinary continuous compact-game Nash existence.  No checked declaration
found in the narrow stopping-law/exceptional-owner search states this
reply-family criterion.

For `INCENTIVE_GADGET.md`, the conclusion is again an architecture no-go, not
the requested reward table.  A surviving gadget proof must make every player
identity capable of carrying a nonvanishing boundary tail, with at least two
identities escaping through finite times.  The theorem supplies no
simultaneous target-pair mass and no leftover upper bound.

In particular, extra calibrators cannot remain strategically passive in a
fixed-gap construction.  Each added calibrator must itself be selected as a
profitable deviator at some candidate profile and its selected range must
carry the late-or-Never obstruction `(6BM.5)`.  Adding a finite or otherwise
properly approximable calibrator menu creates a sentinel and triggers the
compact-game escape.
