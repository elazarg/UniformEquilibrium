## Bottom line

The lane contains much more permanent mathematics than its raw size suggests,
but most of that mathematics has already moved into `formalized/` or has been
subsumed by a stronger checked construction.  The remaining useful material
does not reveal a hidden completed Fin4 consumer.  It does, however, isolate
three genuinely live connection themes:

1. select a near-minimum deterministic-clock component while retaining both a
   concentrated atom and a paid endpoint;
2. install a response on the successor side of an actual source-attached seam;
3. consume the positive-Never law arm by a literal late-release chronology.

The formerly missed all-player escape social-surplus identity and its
sign-chamber attainment theorem are now proved in Lean in the production
terminal-semantic escape-account modules.
They solve a real class, although they do not consume the surviving strict
positive-social arm or the general hard residual.  The
positive-Never late-release result has unusually strong actual-source data and
two favorable reviews, but it still lacks the strategic consumer required by
the export gate.
