# Cap switching and trace friction: reduction audit

Identity: CODEX_ROOT  
Date: 2026-08-31  
Status: two useful conditional mechanisms; no exhaustive Fin4 reduction is
proved.

## Claims separated

The submitted argument combines three mathematically distinct assertions:

1. finite reset-cube nonadditivity localizes to one two-dimensional square;
2. a replayable block with error (e=o(a)) relative to charge (a>0) can be
   repeated many times; and
3. every surviving Fin4 source must supply either such a replayable family or
   a consumed normalized obstruction.

The first two are credible under explicit hypotheses. The third is the
missing theorem, not a consequence of the first two.

## 1. Finite-cube localization

For a fixed finite Boolean cube, the following algebraic principle is sound.
Compare a full macro increment with the sum of its first-order edge columns.
Along any ordering of the movers, their difference telescopes into finitely
many mixed two-face differences. Therefore, if the first-order circulation
cancels, but the full macro fails to be (o(\lambda_n)), then after a finite
pigeonhole extraction one fixed coordinate and one fixed two-face have mixed
difference of order (lambda_n).

This requires the reset amplitudes, weights, and the definition of the
first-order columns to be co-indexed literally. It should be stated as a
standalone finite-cube lemma before attaching it to cap semantics.

### Two missing implications

First, a debt-vector identity

\[
d_n(A)-d_n(\varnothing)=o(\lambda_n)
\]

is not a semantic or law near-return. The existing consumers require the
appropriate prescribed-payoff, unrestricted-cap, law, and ancestry fields.
Debt cancellation alone permits equal and opposite payoff/cap motion.

Second, prescribed payoffs and terminal laws are separately affine in each
player's mixing direction, hence multi-affine on the reset cube; they are not
jointly affine. Their mixed square need not vanish exactly. It is
(O(\lambda_n^2)), and hence (o(\lambda_n)), only after proving uniform
order-(lambda_n) bounds for both reset amplitudes on one common literal
source family. With that quantitative input, first-order debt curvature does
localize to first-order cap curvature.

Thus the honest output is presently:

\[
\text{full semantic/law return supplied separately}
\quad\lor\quad
\text{one persistent two-reset cap square}.
\]

The claimed full near-return does not follow from the displayed debt fields
alone.

## 2. Minimum-face cap-switching lemma

The proposed statement that unique all Continue forces every normalized
mixed cap difference to vanish is an open consumer, not a consequence of root
uniqueness. A cap is a supremum over complete stopping-time deviations.
Two reset directions can jointly activate a pure stopping time which is
suboptimal in each one-direction comparison. This can create first-order
mixed curvature while leaving the current root correspondence unchanged.

The useful residual is therefore real:

> one fixed player, two fixed source-matched reset directions, and one fixed
> cube context carry first-order mixed curvature of the unrestricted
> best-response cap.

Consuming it requires chronology of the competing stopping times. If their
first disagreement remains bounded, a finite reached-row certificate is
plausible. If it escapes to infinity, one needs a source-attached suffix
bubble theorem. Neither implication is proved here.

The suggested “earliest stopping-time switch” is not yet a finite rank:
stopping dates range over (mathbb N\cup\{\infty\}), and switches may move
arbitrarily far outward. A well-founded rank appears only after proving that
an escaping switch either becomes (o(\lambda)) or regenerates a genuinely
smaller finite passport.

## 3. Zeno amplification arithmetic

The scalar lemma is correct. If

\[
a_n>0,
\qquad a_n\le1,
\qquad r_n=e_n/a_n\to0,
\]

then, after treating (e_n=0) separately,

\[
m_n=\left\lfloor\frac1{a_n\sqrt{r_n}}\right\rfloor
\]

satisfies

\[
m_na_n\to\infty,
\qquad m_ne_n\to0.
\]

This is worth retaining as a generic quantitative device.

### Why it is not yet an applied theorem

The hypotheses doing the mathematical work are “replayable” and “compose
linearly.” They are not supplied by compact recurrence.

- A block's terminal absorption probability does not add linearly under
  repetition; survival weights later copies. One can instead use additive
  logarithmic survival, or prove directly that (m_na_n\to\infty) forces
  repeated survival to zero.
- Payoff charge and Nash defects in chronological compilers are generally
  survival-weighted, not raw copies of one scalar.
- Repeating a block changes the continuation cap seen by the next copy.
  Bellman, Nash, law, and passport errors need a uniform subadditive estimate.
- Source ancestry and the paid marked row must survive every restart, not
  merely the first splice.

A formalizable amplification theorem should therefore take a typed block
object with exact restart closure, a subadditive complete seam metric, a
survival estimate, and a passport-preservation field. The scalar calculation
then proves its repetition-count conclusion. Constructing such blocks from a
Fin4 source is the hard producer.

## 4. Checked support descent does not consume all leakage

The checked renewable canonical support descent has one recursive branch:
flat, no-entry, minimum-fibre full replacement. That branch strictly lowers
positive-debt support and can recur only finitely often.

It also has three nonrecursive residual exits:

1. positive total tangent slope;
2. flat support entry; and
3. an off-minimum paid first-disagreement endpoint.

The formalization record explicitly states that these exits and the backward
compiler remain unconsumed. Therefore it is too strong to say that all
minimum-fibre leakage has ceased to be a residual regime. Only the recursive
flat/no-entry part is finitely ranked.

## 5. Trace-friction formulation

For one already supplied replayable family, failure of every
(e_n/a_n\to0) subsequence does yield a positive lower ratio after a
subsequence. Calling its normalized limit a trace-friction tangent is a
reasonable research language.

What is not proved is the proposed exhaustive decomposition of that tangent.
In particular:

- a nonzero clock coordinate is not automatically a checked persistent-clock
  consumer without the exact source and survival interfaces;
- coalition timing must be converted into an actual reached packet;
- finitely many player/role labels do not make the continuous semantic/law
  state recurrent or composable; and
- a normalized matrix obstruction need not lift to an actual behavioral
  chronology.

Thus the proposed charge-normalized trace-closure theorem is a clean new
question. It is not presently a reduction theorem.

## Verdict

The submission contributes:

- a plausible finite-square localization target for first-order cap leakage;
- a correct elementary charge-relative repetition lemma; and
- a useful conjectural description of the missing source-replay interface.

It does not establish

\[
\text{Fin4 counterexample}
\Longrightarrow
\text{exact inert stall or positive linear trace friction}.
\]

That conclusion still needs an unconditional source adapter and an exhaustive
consumer for normalized trace defects. The material should remain a research
note rather than enter the export queue.
