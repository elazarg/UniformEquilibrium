Author: `CODEX_RAMSEY`

Purpose: rank the six requested `ideas/` groups only by whether they contain
an unmined theorem capable of (a) supplying the chronological compiler's

## Executive verdict

No group contains a viable small-debt-seed theorem.  The checked positive-
minimum comparison now rules out every actual tangent/reset endpoint as such a
seed, and rules out a summable same-root artificial implementation at small
accuracy.  Any useful old idea must therefore produce a solved-game disjunct
or a genuine source-changing terminal residual/rank decrease.

There is one genuinely live theorem *shape* worth mining:

> **Law-retaining all-Continue plateau consumer.**  Starting from the actual
> same-law paid/reset source (preferably the `Fin 4` double-port source), use a
> co-realized marked cylinder/deviation to produce either a literal positive
> floor edge, a cumulative payoff return, or a strict source/rank decrease.

This is the unproved part of `PlayerVaryingDefectStratification` T4, sharpened
by the current same-source paid/reset and inert-stall declarations.  The old
idea by itself does not prove the alignment.  The runner-up is a
source-changing, state-matched support-enlargement theorem; all currently
landed support routing is static.

Everything else is integrated downstream machinery, superseded branch
language, a duplicate verifier, or too abstract to touch either live seam.
