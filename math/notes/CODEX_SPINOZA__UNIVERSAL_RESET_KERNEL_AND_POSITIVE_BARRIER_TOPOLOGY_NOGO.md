# Universal reset kernels do not force a zero-debt point by convexity or topology

Author: CODEX_SPINOZA

## Status

**Exact ordinary mathematics; global barrier obstruction, not
Lean-checked.**  The semigroup of all quitting-root prefix maps has a unique
least nonempty closed invariant set.  It is generated by the tail-independent
deterministic nonsingleton roots and is contained in the universal-prefix
hull of every nonempty passport.

This observation does not produce a smaller conjecture-facing barrier.  A
Fin4 table has a strictly positive universal reset kernel, of debt floor
\(1/4\), even though its full carrier contains the zero-debt all-Never point.
Moreover convexification cannot manufacture a diagonal point from
nonnegative-debt semantic states.  Hence neither connectedness,
convexification, nor minimal invariant-set topology forces a positive local
barrier containing a finite response passport to meet the diagonal.

If the all-Never base is imposed, the least closed invariant set is the full
carrier, and the claim that it meets the diagonal is exactly the original
controller--tester sign question.  The response passport is redundant at
that level.

## 1. The universal reset kernel

Let

\[
 \mathcal Z_M=[-M,M]^I\times[-M,M]^I
\]

be the terminal-semantic box, and write \(T_x\) for prefixing one product
Quit/Continue root \(x\in[0,1]^I\).  For a finite word \(w\), write \(T_w\)
for the iterated prefix map, including the empty word.

If \(S\subseteq I\) has at least two members, let \(1_S\) be the deterministic
root at which exactly \(S\) Quits.  Unilateral deviation by any one player
cannot remove all quitters at that stage.  Consequently

\[
 c_S:=T_{1_S}z
\tag{1.1}
\]

is independent of the tail state \(z\in\mathcal Z_M\), in both its prescribed
payoff and complete behavioral cap coordinates.

Define

\[
 \mathcal R_r
 :=\overline{\{
 T_wc_S:
 |S|\ge2,\ w\text{ a finite root word}
 \}}.
\tag{1.2}
\]

### Proposition 1.1 (least nonempty closed invariant set)

The set \(\mathcal R_r\) is nonempty, compact, and invariant under every
prefix map.  If \(C\subseteq\mathcal Z_M\) is any nonempty closed set with

\[
 T_x(C)\subseteq C
 \qquad(x\in[0,1]^I),
\tag{1.3}
\]

then

\[
 \boxed{\mathcal R_r\subseteq C.}
\tag{1.4}
\]

#### Proof

Choose \(z\in C\).  Invariance and tail independence give

\[
 c_S=T_{1_S}z\in C
 \qquad(|S|\ge2).
\]

Repeated invariance puts every \(T_wc_S\) in \(C\), and closedness gives
(1.4).  Conversely, continuity of every \(T_x\) shows directly that the
closure (1.2) is invariant.  Compactness follows from closedness inside the
compact box.  `QED`

For every nonempty seed set \(P\subseteq\mathcal Z_M\), its closed universal
prefix hull therefore satisfies

\[
 \mathcal R_r\subseteq\mathcal H(P).
\tag{1.5}
\]

The inclusion uses no payoff, debt, cap-response, or response-cycle field of
the passport \(P\).  It follows solely from allowing every controller root.

## 2. Convexification cannot create the diagonal

Every terminal-semantic carrier point satisfies

\[
 b_i-u_i\ge0\qquad(i\in I).
\tag{2.1}
\]

Let \(C\) be any subset of this nonnegative-debt cone.  If a finite convex
combination \(z=\sum_a\lambda_a z^a\) lies on the diagonal, then for every
coordinate \(i\),

\[
 0=b_i(z)-u_i(z)
  =\sum_a\lambda_a\bigl(b_i(z^a)-u_i(z^a)\bigr).
\tag{2.2}
\]

All summands are nonnegative.  Hence every \(z^a\) with
\(\lambda_a>0\) is already diagonal.  In finite dimension, Caratheodory's
theorem gives the exact identity

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{conv}(C)\cap\{b=u\}
 =\operatorname{conv}\bigl(C\cap\{b=u\}\bigr).}
\tag{2.3}
\]

For compact \(C\), the same holds with closed convex hulls.  Thus averaging
rotating debtor labels can lower maximum debt, but it cannot make every debt
zero unless a zero-debt constituent was present already.

There is a second independent problem: the cap coordinate of \(T_x\) is a
maximum of a Quit branch and an affine Continue branch.  The prefix map is
not affine in full semantic state.  Therefore the convex hull of an invariant
carrier subset need not itself be invariant, and a correlated convex
combination need not be the semantics of any ordinary behavioral profile.

## 3. An exact positive Fin4 reset kernel

Take \(I=\operatorname{Fin}4\) and define

\[
 r_i(S)=
 \begin{cases}
 -1,&i\in S,\\
 0,&i\notin S.
 \end{cases}
\tag{3.1}
\]

Consider any finite root word ending in a deterministic nonsingleton reset
\(c_S\).  Prescribed play absorbs surely in a nonempty terminal coalition
\(K\).  Its payoff is

\[
 u_i=-\Pr(i\in K).
\tag{3.2}
\]

Every player can obtain zero by Never: if someone else eventually Quits the
player is an outsider, and if no one Quits the all-Never payoff is zero.
No deviation can exceed zero, because every terminal payoff coordinate is
either \(-1\) or zero.  Hence the complete unrestricted cap is exactly

\[
 b_i=0,
 \qquad
 d_i=b_i-u_i=\Pr(i\in K).
\tag{3.3}
\]

Therefore

\[
 \sum_i d_i=\mathbb E|K|\ge1,
 \qquad
 \max_i d_i\ge\frac14.
\tag{3.4}
\]

The terminal coalition space is finite.  Every limit of the laws in (1.2)
still has total mass one on nonempty coalitions, so (3.2)--(3.4) pass to the
closed reset kernel.  Thus

\[
 \boxed{
 \min_{z\in\mathcal R_r}\max_i d_i(z)\ge\frac14.}
\tag{3.5}
\]

Equality is attained.  Prefix four singleton hazards

\[
 \frac14,\quad\frac13,\quad\frac12,\quad1
\tag{3.6}
\]

for players \(0,1,2,3\), respectively, before any nonsingleton reset.  The
last singleton root absorbs surely, so the deeper reset is not reached, and
the terminal law is uniform on the four singleton coalitions.  It has

\[
 u_i=-\frac14,
 \qquad b_i=0,
 \qquad d_i=\frac14.
\tag{3.7}
\]

This point also has the strong local fields which make a naive topological
argument tempting.  All Continue is its unique exact cap root; the singleton
moats equal one; and replacing player \(0\)'s complete strategy by Never is a
paid cap response of gain \(1/4\) which rotates the remaining debts from

\[
 (1/4,1/4,1/4,1/4)
 \quad\text{to}\quad
 (0,1/3,1/3,1/3).
\tag{3.8}
\]

Nevertheless the full carrier also contains the all-Never semantic point
\((u,b)=(0,0)\).  Hence the table's global controller--tester value is zero.
The positive reset kernel is a proper local invariant subset, not a negative
certificate.

## 4. Consequence for passport-generated barriers

Let \(P\) be a finite response-cycle or paid-port passport and let
\(\mathcal H(P)\) be its closed universal-prefix hull.  The exact hull-floor
identity gives

\[
 \min_{z\in\mathcal H(P)}d(z)
 =\inf_{p\in P}Q(p),
\tag{4.1}
\]

where \(Q\) is the greatest target-free Bellman barrier.  Displayed profile
debt and horizontal cap gain do not lower-bound \(Q\), because \(Q\) tests
every future controller prefix.  Proposition 1.1 adds that every such hull
contains \(\mathcal R_r\), but (3.5) shows that even the least invariant core
can remain uniformly away from the diagonal.

If the all-Never base \(e_\infty\) is added, then

\[
 \mathcal H(P\cup\{e_\infty\})=\mathcal K_r,
\tag{4.2}
\]

because the all-Never universal-prefix hull is the whole canonical carrier.
Thus a minimal closed invariant barrier which is a genuine controller
certificate is the carrier itself.  Proving that it meets the diagonal is
precisely the original sign problem; the finite passport supplies no smaller
topological target.

## 6. Boundary and nonclaims

- Tail independence of \(c_S\) requires \(|S|\ge2\).  At a deterministic
  singleton root, the owner's Continue deviation reaches the tail cap.
- The reset kernel is invariant under prefix maps only.  It need not be
  closed under horizontal complete-strategy cap responses.
- The negative-membership table has global debt minimum zero and is not a
  counterexample to the uniform-equilibrium conjecture.
- The theorem does not refute a future argument using a special algebraic
  invariant of the particular Fin4 response passport.  It refutes the bare
  inference from minimality, connectedness, convexification, or universal
  prefix closure.
