## Interpretation

Several failures can be correlated evidence against one proof mechanism
rather than independent evidence against the conjecture. Enlarging a state,
refining a paid response, and finding another semantic recurrence may all
encounter the same unproved temporal compilation.

This is not a reason to discard the existing route: it has produced genuine
theorems. It is a reason to reserve research effort for constructions that
are not required to pass through it.

Likewise, "not local" must not become an indiscriminate ban on local lemmas.
A local identity used inside a new global existence argument differs from
a claimed universal local consumer. Each no-go must be applied with its exact
hypotheses and conclusion.

## How to judge a proposed new mechanism

- What mathematical information or operation does it use that the failed
  implication did not?
- Which exact no-go applies, and which hypothesis would actually change?
- Does it preserve independent product behavior and unrestricted testing, or
  has it silently introduced correlation, extra observation, or a cutoff?
- Can its first test explain a known solved game whose equilibrium is absent
  from the proposed restricted class?
- Does success produce a profile, a genuine global obstruction, or a lemma
  with an explicit remaining producer? Merely satisfying more necessary
  conditions is recorded separately.
- What small, decisive calculation could falsify the first proposal before
  another large interface is built?

Do not require every first lemma to settle the conjecture. Require a clear
account of the new mechanism and the still-missing implication. Conversely,
do not call a reformulation a new route merely because its symbols changed.

## Horizontal decomposition versus end-to-end coverage

Research hypothesis: splitting work into representation, compactness,
replacement, and consumer layers can favor individually valid constructions
whose witnesses do not match. This is a possible organizational explanation
for repeated source/subsequence/seam failures, not a mathematical proof that
the chosen route is incomplete or less promising than another route.

Three outputs must be counted separately:

- a theorem giving UE for every reward table in a specified class removes
  that class from the possible-counterexample search;
- a theorem restricting the witness attached to every hypothetical
  counterexample may simplify the residual without removing any additional
  reward table;
- a theorem disproving a proposed proof mechanism restricts our methods,
  not the possible-counterexample class.

No percentage or geometric size reduction follows from counting packet
fields, branches, modules, or necessary conditions. In particular, reducing
many source descriptions to one description is not a claim that a comparable
fraction of reward tables has been solved.

A bounded connectivity test follows one actual chain from its table
assumptions. At each edge, check that the previous theorem supplies the exact
next input, on the same witness whenever that theorem requires it. Stop at
the first missing property; do not count later conditional theorems as a
connected extension. A vertical research task may target a restricted table
class, but should own that entire chain through a full-response equilibrium
or a contradiction.

### A precise overrestriction exposed by the canonical tests

The reviewed exact-menu example supplies mathematical evidence for one
specific architecture risk. For its fixed table, minimizing unrestricted
regret among exact menu equilibria leaves regret 18/49 at every deadline.
Yet explicit approximate menu laws have unrestricted regret 2^(-N). Thus
making menu error vanish at each fixed deadline before selecting a larger
deadline is not equivalent to selecting deadline and error jointly. The
two limit orders are proved unequal in the preserved submission and its
independent review.

This is not just an unidentified lack of compactness or cap leakage. It
falsifies a concrete universal exactification strategy while displaying a
successful approximate construction for the same game. Conversely it does
not refute arbitrary use of finite games, local identities inside global
constructions, or exact equilibria when a separate producer really supplies
the required missing-date control.

The empirical-product experiments expose a separate methodological warning:
even a full best response can chase a small boundary collision bonus and
increase the product law's other debts. Correlated history-regret bounds
would not repair this, because the actual output is an independent product.
The tolerance-aware success in CODEX_HILBERT's owned notebook is an exact
proof draft for one test table, with no independent gate or general
convergence claim. Its initial law, tie rules, and tolerance all differ from
the earlier run; their individual causal contributions have not been
separated.

The next useful test is a genuine approximate-selection mechanism that also
handles the cyclic canonical example, or a new raw-table existence class.
Another exact-selector failure or a longer numerical plateau alone would
have diminishing value. Neither of these research requirements imposes
source ancestry across accuracies or a temporalization of best-response
arrows on every possible positive proof.

### Current tests: avoid counting an overlap or strengthening a constraint

The completed proof draft
`CODEX_RENY__INVERSE_POSITIVE_SIGNED_FOUR_CYCLE_PRODUCER.md` produces a
cycle from a positive full inverse, but its exact determinant split places

coverage nor a weakening of the entire exported condition. This attempt
has stopped as a class-extension claim; it remains a mathematical tool.
The draft and its overlap proof have not received an independent gate.

The one-pivot regularization test requires another exact distinction.
For the pivot's own survival S(t), a daily forced-Quit clock imposes

    S(t+1) <= (1-delta) S(t) for every t.

A global geometric envelope imposes only

    S(t) <= (1-delta)^t for every t.

The first implies the second, not conversely. A clock quitting with hazard
one half at dates 0,3,6,... and zero hazard elsewhere has
S(t)=2^(-ceil(t/3)). It satisfies the global envelope whenever
delta <= 1-2^(-1/3), but violates every positive daily hazard floor.
The cyclic canonical example already has an equilibrium with precisely
such a pivot clock.

Consequently any uniqueness or bad-selection theorem for the daily-floor
regularization cannot be cited against the global-envelope regularization.
The former preserves its constraint under every reached suffix; the latter
can accumulate slack and need not preserve the same envelope after
conditioning. Reusing a suffix-induction proof would silently strengthen
the actual global constraint.

The separate author draft
`CODEX_HILBERT__ONE_PIVOT_FORCED_CLOCK_REGULARIZATION.md` now records the
full daily-floor uniqueness calculation; the coordinator read it completely,
without assigning an independent-review or Lean seal. On the canonical
cyclic table its unique restricted equilibrium has pivot full regret tending
to 2/5. The weaker global-envelope domain contains both this bad stationary
equilibrium and the known zero-regret periodic equilibrium. Thus its
remaining issue is selection, not existence of a compact restricted game.

For fixed opponents, the draft also computes the envelope-restricted pivot
cap exactly: privately sample a geometric deadline G and choose the best
finite stopping date at or before G. The value is the expectation of that
running maximum. It converges to the unrestricted cap for fixed opponents,
but no uniform convergence along moving equilibrium opponents is proved.
The surviving global-envelope selection problem remains a research
candidate, not an established arbitrary-game producer.

## Target preservation is stronger than target-free existence recovery

A convexified payoff/cap point need not be realizable with its prescribed
payoff. This does not by itself refute recovery for an existence proof:
the recovered profile may have a different payoff, provided its complete
behavioral debts are small. The terminal-all-errors consumer can select
one payoff target afterward. Neither a chosen relaxed target nor a common
realizing strategy across accuracies is an input to that consumer.

The ordinary mathematical recovery theorem in
`CODEX_TURING_BOX__STATE_INFORMATION_AND_RELAXATION.md`, Sections 3–6,
makes this distinction quantitative. Let F be a nonempty prefix-closed
family of complete payoff/cap pairs in the reward box, with nonnegative
debts, and let eta be its infimum maximum debt. Convexify once and apply
any finite independent-root word. If its resulting maximum debt is
epsilon and its joint prefix survival is S, then

    eta <= n epsilon / S                         if S > 0,
    eta <= epsilon + 2R sqrt(S) + 2RS             for every S.

The first branch can select an unprefixed constituent; the second selects
one constituent and retains the word. It is essential that extraction is
not required to retain the original source or its payoff. One constituent
controls all caps simultaneously: choose its cap small for a player with
maximal deleted-player survival, while every other cap is screened by at
most sqrt(S). These are unrestricted cap estimates, not finite-menu tests.

The bounds give a recovery modulus independent of word length. They also
iterate through a fixed finite bound d on publicly observed future draws.
Their small-error exponent deteriorates roughly as 1/3^d, so they do not
give recovery when d grows arbitrarily with accuracy. The proof and its
bounded independent mathematical check are recorded in the owned note and
`../feedback/CODEX_TURING_BOX__STATE_INFORMATION_AND_RELAXATION__BY_CODEX_EMMY_BOX.md`.
This is not a Lean check or an arbitrary-game producer.

Thus fixed-target decoding, target-free recovery, and recovery uniform in
an increasing number of signal rounds are three different mathematical
questions. A no-go for the first must not be used to reject the second.
Conversely, solving the second is not permission to interchange the depth
and accuracy limits in the third. A concrete next test is an existence
producer in this exact relaxation together with the quantitative recovery
it needs; convexity alone supplies neither.

## Changing permitted operations is different from changing the objective

An objective cannot select a successful profile if every profile in its
permitted output class has a positive unrestricted regret floor. The exact
test in `CODEX_EMMY_BOX__OBJECTIVES_AND_GLOBAL_SELECTION.md` makes this
distinction explicit. In one three-player singleton game, every infinite
deterministic owner word with the selected owner quitting with hazard one
half has maximum complete debt at least 1/12. Yet simultaneous independent
stationary hazards tending to zero approach the same positive equilibrium
payoff. The obstruction covers all such owner words, not just periodic or
finite-state ones. The calculations have a bounded independent check.

This is an operation-level control experiment on a solved game, not new
counterexample-class coverage. The existing CEDAR one-active obstruction
already treats a stronger variable-hazard restriction on a different table.
The lesson is not that nonlinear objectives are useless on the full
strategy space. It is that changing an objective while retaining an
insufficient root alphabet does not change the available strategies.

The same example gives a whole-tree test, not just a one-word test. Finite
trees with H fresh uniform owner signals and a literal Never leaf have
complete debt 2^(-H). Every finite half-hazard owner word followed by Never
has debt at least 1/48. Thus recursive child selection and keeping or
dropping existing prefixes cannot have a recovery loss uniform in signal
depth, even with only three players. The proof and its bounded check are
in the Emmy note. This does not disprove arbitrary-root recovery: that
restricted word family is closed only under its three allowed roots, not
under all product roots. Introducing a new sure-collision root already
solves this particular table.

There is also a positive operation with precise limits. In Section 9 of
`CODEX_TURING_BOX__STATE_INFORMATION_AND_RELAXATION.md`, an exogenous public
signal may determine the product root separately at each calendar date,
with independent fresh signals and no retained public state. Suppose its
conditional total Quit hazard is at most delta at every date and signal.
Replacing each player's hazard by its signal average gives an actual
private profile with

    private maximum debt <= public maximum debt + 8R delta.

The proof compares first stopping coalitions for every deleted-player
system, uniformly over all deterministic deadlines and Never. It allows
calendar variation and positive Never mass. It does not require a lower
bound on opponent absorption. The ordinary proof has a bounded independent
check; it is not a general-game producer or a Lean declaration.

Neither of its two main hypotheses is cosmetic. A rare public sure
grand-coalition signal has small expected hazard but large conditional
hazard; replacing it by independent marginal hazards leaves a fixed regret
gap. A public signal that permanently assigns a sole owner can instead
have uniformly tiny conditional hazards and exact public equilibrium,
while averaging away that retained state also leaves a fixed regret gap.
The latter example excludes the particular averaged payoff as a private
uniform payoff, not the existence of other private equilibrium targets.

Nor can the small-hazard source be imposed on every table. The participant
indicator game, in which a player receives one exactly when it quits,
has an exact sure-collision equilibrium but a positive regret floor for
uniformly diffuse public protocols. A stronger source comparison in the
Turing note uses the checked Solan--Vieille boundary table: serialization
and its complete one-active regret floor exclude vanishing-error,
vanishing-hazard, no-retained-state public families, although that table
has a checked period-two equilibrium. These are solved-game restrictions
on an attempted construction, not positive-gap counterexamples.

Thus the constructive alternative must retain the freedom to change joint
roots, use substantial simultaneous quitting where needed, and select a
different equilibrium target. Target-free finite-tree extraction remains
an available operation, but its dependence on signal depth is an actual
missing estimate. Small-root averaging is another available operation,
but general history-dependent public strategies do not satisfy its input.
Neither operation should be presented as a universal replacement for the
existing temporal route without its own source theorem.

The direct-selection test in
`CODEX_NASH_BOX__DIRECT_EXISTENCE_BEYOND_TEMPORALIZATION.md` distinguishes
another pair of quantifiers. A single auxiliary game with small private
Never bonuses can have both a bad exact finite-menu equilibrium branch
and a good branch whose original unrestricted debt tends to zero. The bad
branch even disproves a proposed weak-continuity hypothesis, while the
good branch remains available in that same full equilibrium correspondence.
Consequently neither an arbitrary-selector failure nor failure of that
topological theorem refutes existential global selection. The calculation
of the two global limit orders has a bounded independent mathematical
check; the other auxiliary claims remain ordinary proof drafts. None
provides new reward-table coverage or a Lean check.
The unresolved positive task is to select from the entire correspondence
while choosing the menu and approximation parameters jointly; the local
bonus-transport identity alone only reproduces Nash--Bellman recursion in
augmented payoffs and does not solve that task.

## A direct reward-table route does not need a temporal seam

There is a complete contrasting mechanism. Fix signed singleton data with
strictly positive inverse singleton-difference matrix. Give each recipient
one selected containing coalition of size dᵢ+1, with reward aᵢL^{dᵢ},
where dᵢ≥1 and aᵢ>0, and keep its other nonsingleton rewards bounded.
For sufficiently large fixed L, a normalized radial fixed-point argument
produces an actual full-support stationary equilibrium, not merely a
normalized limiting direction. The complete result is
`../exports/POSITIVE_INVERSE_COALITION_JACKPOTS_UNIFORM_EQUILIBRIUM.md`.

The mathematical operation is explicit. Substituting hazards q=z/L
cancels each coalition's reward scale, giving an exact polynomial
indifference field with a positive nonlinear leading term. The positive
inverse places its image in a compact interior direction simplex. Inner
and outer radial inequalities survive the bounded signed perturbation,
so a fixed point exists at positive finite hazards. Every deleted-opponent
system then contracts, controlling all stopping times and Never and
delivering the actual fixed uniform payoff. No selected minimum source,
source renewal, or best-response-to-temporal-edge identification enters
the proof.

This is evidence for diversifying mechanisms, not for abandoning the
source-preserving route. The theorem narrows an explicit raw-table class;
it does not resolve the general minimum residual or force arbitrary
inverse-positive tables to have positive coalition rewards at these
scales. In particular, the four-active negative-triple stress family is
not covered. Its full-response calibration and burst obstructions remain
tests of a different construction. Their failure cannot be counted again
as failure of the positive-inverse producer, or as evidence of a positive
all-behavior gap.

## Correlation removal does not supply independent existence

Three concrete tests distinguish failure of a selection operation from
failure of UE. Their supporting arguments are ordinary mathematics in
the named notebooks; no new Lean or counterexample-class seal is asserted
here.

First, Section23 of
`CODEX_NASH_BOX__DIRECT_EXISTENCE_BEYOND_TEMPORALIZATION.md` starts from
a full coarse-obedient law of complete clocks. Independent root coins
preserve full coarse obedience when their product root is Nash against
the law's actual payoff. They also erase some clock correlation whenever
the selected root has sufficient activity. Both are honest operations.
But on the perturbed Solan calibration, a strict coarse anchor with payoff
(4/3,4/3,4/3) has only the all-Continue root in its ENTIRE root-Nash
correspondence. Every finite permitted prefix is a common delay, and its
distance from every product clock law stays at least 1/32. The game has UE.
This falsifies universal recovery from an arbitrary fixed anchor, not
joint selection of another source, another payoff and independent clocks.

Second, Section35.3 of
`CODEX_TURING_BOX__STATE_INFORMATION_AND_RELAXATION.md` explains why an
ordinary convex-duality argument cannot supply that joint selection.
For finite joint laws, total correlation is nonnegative and vanishes on
every pure joint atom. For any nonempty linear coarse-obedience set,
its ordinary nonnegative-multiplier Lagrangian dual has value exactly zero:
the inner minimization is attained at a pure atom maximizing the combined
linear constraint, and feasibility makes the resulting dual value
nonpositive. This remains true with a strict feasible anchor. It is a
duality-gap obstruction to this particular proof operation, not a positive
lower bound on the primal correlation minimum. Independence has not become
a convex constraint by choosing entropy as the objective.

Third, Section13.7 of
`CODEX_EMMY_BOX__OBJECTIVES_AND_GLOBAL_SELECTION.md` tests fast public
regeneration. When every instantaneous hazard is small relative to the
public reset rate, honest averaging produces a private stationary profile
with controlled complete regret. A universal producer with both errors
tending to zero would therefore produce stationary profiles with vanishing
total hazard. The homogeneous complementarity obstruction excludes that
conclusion on an R0 singleton matrix with a positive own singleton. The
whole fast-regenerating source is too restrictive there, even though the
game may have a macroscopic or nonstationary equilibrium.

The common methodological risk is demanding an exact auxiliary invariant
while removing the information that makes it easy to satisfy. These tests
do not prove that every successful proof must violate that invariant, or
that correlation methods are exhausted. They do justify changing the
operation rather than refining a frozen-source selector or invoking
ordinary Slater duality again. Concrete alternatives may vary payoff and
clock laws jointly, retain strategic memory on the absorption scale, or
allow controlled auxiliary errors while directly decreasing original full
regret. Each still needs its own producer and unrestricted-response proof.

## A preserved actual-carrier chamber changes the selection question

The reviewed mathematical theorem in
`../exports/TWO_LEDGER_PROPER_SUPPORT_CHAMBER_UNIFORM_EQUILIBRIUM.md`
provides a complete alternative to temporal compilation on its raw reward
class. Its conclusion is original UE against every behavioral deviation,
not equilibrium in a correlated or discounted auxiliary model. Its finite
guards are reward inequalities and coalition-graph conditions; no strategic
witness is supplied as an input. The new raw adapter remains ordinary
mathematics, not Lean-checked.

Let U be prescribed terminal payoff, B the complete cap vector, and
E=max_i(B_i-U_i). The proof produces an actual seed in the compact region

    min_i(B_i-s_i)<=0,
    sum_i a_i(B_i-s_i)>=lambda E,          lambda>0.

At a positive-E point, one raw ledger excludes full-support cap-Nash roots.
Proper-coalition leaving incentives preserve the low-cap condition, while
a DIFFERENT raw ledger preserves the weighted inequality. Every remaining
exact root therefore stays in the region and satisfies E'=cE, where c is
joint Continue mass. At a positive attained minimum, either some cap is
strictly below its own singleton, or the equality coordinates contain a
sink of the insertion graph. The former excludes the all-Continue root;
the latter explicitly supplies a nonzero axis root. Both contradict
minimality. A zero-debt point in the closed ACTUAL carrier then supplies
actual approximants and one fixed uniform target.

Three distinctions are essential to the research diagnosis.

1. **Minimizer escape is weaker than pointwise escape.** The exact actual
   point with unique all-Continue root in Section15 of
   `CODEX_EMMY_BOX__OBJECTIVES_AND_GLOBAL_SELECTION.md` does not refute
   escape at a constrained minimum. Its arbitrary-point premise is weaker
   than the additional minimizing property the proof uses. The theorem
   does not claim that the simple low-cap/surplus region is preserved on
   every standard-Q table.
2. **A compact state need not support temporal iteration.** The proof uses
   continuity of one fixed-root semantic map and one contradiction at a
   compact minimizer. It needs no continuous root selector, attained best
   response, common infinite root word, or recurrence of source passports.
   Thus failures of those stronger architectures do not obstruct this
   particular class theorem.
3. **A successful global invariant is not a universal invariant.** Standard-Q
   supplies the positive singleton seed but does not supply the two reward
   ledgers, proper-coalition leavers, or insertion-graph guard. The theorem
   includes a full sixty-coordinate Fin4 open region, but gives neither a
   fraction of solved tables nor a transformation of every counterexample
   into that region.

This is concrete evidence for the missing-global-theorem form of the
sufficient-state hypothesis on this class, not evidence against the
conjecture. The important untested extension is global selection of a
different invariant region, possibly with a different objective or with
preservation under a selected root rather than every root. A construction
of such a region must begin from arbitrary surviving reward data and end
at the actual diagonal; naming a region whose nonemptiness or preservation
is assumed would only restate the selection problem.

`quittingTerminalSemanticDebt_prefix_eq_continueMass_mul_of_capNash` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Root/CapNashRootStack.lean` and

## Row-wise continuation and a two-parameter next target

The [complete complex-spectrum ray](../exports/COMPLETE_COMPLEX_SPECTRUM_RAY_UNIFORM_EQUILIBRIUM.md)

connected curve of three-owner stationary equilibria covers the remaining

alone are insufficient; the interval proofs retain their full incentive
comparisons, and the stationary proof identifies roots at every seam.

A useful next extension has two reward parameters affecting only rows0,1.
Freeze their hazards q0=z0,q1=z1 and put h=(1-z0)(1-z1). For owner2, write
its forced-Quit and passive offers as Q2(u)=Q0+u DeltaQ and
P2(u)=P0+u DeltaP, where u=q3. Since H2=h(1-u), its stationary field
F2=(1-H2)Q2-P2 is

    F2=h DeltaQ u^2+[(1-h)DeltaQ+hQ0-DeltaP]u+(1-h)Q0-P0.

It does not depend on q2. Similarly F3 is a quadratic in q2 alone. Thus the
two opponent equations decouple into scalar quadratics, with at most four
isolated proper root pairs. Identically zero polynomials and multiple roots
must be retained as degeneracies; there is no universal four-root bound.
Quiet, mixed and sure choices give nine support patterns in the complete
two-owner complementarity problem.

On any continuous proper branch where the relevant coefficients are nonzero,
the two remaining stationarity equations recover the two reward parameters
as a map of(z0,z1). A Poincare--Miranda boundary-sign argument could then cover
a whole parameter rectangle, even when the recovery map folds. This is an
explicit next producer question, not a proved arbitrary-game reduction:
proper branches, boundary signs, and compatibility at branch changes still
have to be produced from the reward data.

The following analytic extension is proved ordinary mathematics, retained

The construction is by product_cover_fresh_review, with the larger row2 cone

Write r_i(S)=s_i+W_i(S), W_i({i})=0, and set W_i(empty)=B_i(empty)=0.

participant rewards themselves may decrease without bound. Row3 is arbitrary.

## A finite-scale global alternative from real algebraic geometry

This is a proved supporting procedure, not a UE decision algorithm or a new
reward-class exclusion. Its useful feature is that neither a polynomial
degree nor a calendar length must be fixed before the search. All statements
below concern native reward tables with zero own singletons and Never payoff.

For n players, a root q in [0,1]^n and continuation w, put
h=product_i(1-q_i), a=1-h, H_i=product_(j!=i)(1-q_j). Let Q_i be the forced
Quit offer, P_i the nonempty-opponent passive offer, C_i=P_i+H_i w_i, and

    U_i=q_i Q_i+(1-q_i)C_i,
    d_i=max(Q_i,C_i)-U_i.

On K_b=[-b,b]^n, R(Z,epsilon,b) contains every triple(w,q,u) for which
|u_i-U_i|<=epsilon a and d_i<=epsilon a for each i. An edge's charge is a.
A finite budget bounds the sum of charges along EVERY finite path; shared
vertices must literally agree. A potential satisfies P(w)>=a+P(u) on every
edge. These are the definitions `IsQuittingFloorFreeRobustEdge` and
`quittingFloorFreeRobustChargedRelation` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Projective/RobustChargedRelation.lean`.

If native tables Z,Z' differ by at most delta in every entry, then

    |U_i^Z-U_i^Z'|<=delta a,
    |d_i^Z-d_i^Z'|<=2 delta a,
    R(Z,epsilon,b) subset R(Z',epsilon+2delta,b).       (A)

Indeed only nonempty prescribed coalitions affect the first difference.
The identical own singletons mean that each of Q_i and C_i changes by at
most delta(1-H_i). The function
d_i=max((1-q_i)(Q_i-C_i),-q_i(Q_i-C_i)) is 1-Lipschitz in Q_i-C_i,
and 1-H_i<=a. This also proves (A) at a=0. Fixing the own levels is essential.

The existing theorem
`exists_quittingRobustChargedRelation_rationalPotential_of_finiteBudget`
in `UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Projective/RobustChargedRelationPolynomialSeparator.lean`
produces a rational polynomial potential for R(Z,epsilon/4,b) from finite
budget for R(Z,epsilon,b+1), for bounded rewards and 0<epsilon<=1.
It supplies the polynomial; its existence is not an added hypothesis below.

### Uniformity at a fixed positive tolerance

Let F be a compact bounded family of native tables, 0<eta<=1, and let
A_eta be the tables in F with finite budget for R(Z,eta,b+1).
There is one finite C such that every Z in A_eta has budget C for
R(Z,eta/2,b+1). To prove this, take Z in closure(A_eta) and choose
Z' in A_eta within eta/8. By (A), R(Z,3eta/4,b+1) has a finite budget C_Z.
For every Y within eta/8 of Z, R(Y,eta/2,b+1) is contained in this relation.
Extract a finite subcover of compact closure(A_eta) and take max C_Z.
If A_eta is empty take C=0. No closedness or recognition algorithm for
A_eta is assumed.

There is also a finite catalogue of rational polynomials such that every
Z in A_eta accepts one as a potential for R(Z,eta/16,b). At each Z in the
same closure, finite budget at eta/2 on K_(b+1) gives a polynomial at eta/8
on K_b. By (A), that SAME polynomial works at eta/16 for every Y within
eta/32 of Z. Compactness again gives a finite catalogue. In particular its
degree and rational coefficient height have uniform finite bounds. These
bounds are existential; no numerical bound or lower bound on eta is proved.

If F is semialgebraic, let S be F intersected with the union of the
acceptance sets of this finite catalogue. Quantifier elimination makes S
semialgebraic, and, writing A(t,c) for finite budget on R(Z,t,c),

    F intersect A(eta,b+1) subset S subset F intersect A(eta/16,b).

The two boxes differ. The second inclusion follows by telescoping a bounded
polynomial on K_b, not by an assertion that the two budget problems coincide.

### A terminating search for a finite cover

Input: n is a natural number; M,b,eta,C are rational with b,C>0 and
0<eta<=1; and F is given by a rational semialgebraic formula, with the
promise that it is compact and consists of native tables bounded by M.
There is an algorithm producing a finite cover of F by acceptance regions
of the following two kinds:

- a rational polynomial valid as a potential for R(Z,eta/16,b);
- a rational finite path in R(Z,eta,b+1) whose total charge exceeds C.

Enumerate all rational polynomials and all finite lists of rational boxed
vertices and roots, interleaving the two enumerations fairly. Reject a path
unless its rational charge exceeds C. For each remaining object compute its
semialgebraic acceptance region. For a polynomial this universally quantifies
boxed w,q,u and tests the edge implication; for a path it is a finite list
of inequalities in the reward entries. Absolute values and maxima are finite
Boolean combinations of polynomial inequalities. After each finite stage,
quantifier elimination decides whether the accumulated regions cover F.

Here is the termination proof. For each Z, consider R(Z,eta/2,b+1).
If its budget is finite, the polynomial theorem gives a witness at eta/8
on K_b, which is accepted at eta/16 throughout a neighborhood of Z by (A).
If its budget is infinite, take a finite path with charge>C. A zero-charge
edge has q=0 and u=w, so delete such edges. On each remaining edge, raising
tolerance from eta/2 to 3eta/4 leaves strictly positive slack. There are
finitely many edges. Joint continuity therefore permits simultaneous rational
rounding of every vertex and root, preserving all inequalities and charge>C.
Choose a shared path vertex once, not separately for its two incident edges.
Rational points are dense even at the box boundary because b+1 is rational.
The same rounded path is accepted at eta throughout a neighborhood of Z.
These acceptance neighborhoods cover compact F, so finitely many objects
suffice; the fair enumeration eventually finds them. The algorithm never
needs to decide the finite-budget versus infinite-budget alternative.

The labels need not be exclusive. A path label supplies only the requested
tolerance and charge, not a single fixed UE target across all accuracies.
A polynomial label supplies a native-box budget certificate. To infer an
original-game counterexample through
`quittingGame_not_exists_uniformEquilibriumPayoff_iff_noSureRoot_and_rationalPotential`
in `UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Projective/PolynomialForwardCertificateCharacterization.lean`,
one must separately supply Fin4, normality, a positive original singleton,
absence of the stated punishment-vector sure root, and the original box of
radius rewardBound+2. Own-level translation shifts the box and may turn
rational polynomial coefficients irrational. Use finite capacity and the
packet characterization, or reapply the separator with its required box
margin, rather than silently applying the rational-polynomial consumer.

The systematic research question is therefore concrete: can these finite
covers be produced economically on the surviving parameter families, and can
one show that their polynomial side is empty at every accuracy? The procedure
proves termination at a specified scale, not that the desired label wins.

## Information relaxation: what may and may not be frozen

An occupation measure on literal Nash edges and a public lottery over
continuation values are different objects. In the former, each edge retains
its own exact successor; a sufficiently absorbing path can be fixed as a
deterministic calendar. In the latter, a root may be indifferent only against
the MEAN successor. Revealing the lottery in advance then gives a deviator
advance information about a future stopping reward.

The exact one-node price is elementary. For one mixing owner, let
Q=P+H mu, mu=E W, where W is the full cap of an exact continuation revealed
only after surviving the root. Freezing W while retaining the root gives debt

    H[(1-q)(mu-W)_+ + q(W-mu)_+].

Its expectation is H E|W-mu|/2, independent of q. This is a Wasserstein-1
dispersion cost. It concerns one root with exact continuations, not a proved
infinite-tree error bound. A derandomization argument needs vanishing
dispersion or a modified strategy, rather than a constant-factor prophet bound.

Here is a complete strict-positive test. Use native own/Never0 and

    Gamma=[0,-1,3,-1; -1,0,-1,3; 3,-1,0,-1; -1,3,-1,0].

Every coordinate of every pair/triple reward is1/10, except increments
Z_1(12)+=1, Z_0(23)+=2, Z_1(23)+=1; grand rewards are arbitrary.
The pure tails12 and23 are Nash, with values
a=(1/10,11/10,1/10,1/10) and b=(21/10,11/10,1/10,1/10).
At root q=(1/2,1/2,0,0), a fair public choice of these tails after survival
makes Q=C=1/20 for owners0,1, and Q=3/40<C=11/20 for owners2,3.
Thus the public profile is exact Nash. Freezing EITHER tail creates debt1/4
for owner0 and zero for everyone else. All deviations absorb by the second
date, so adding arbitrary signed row offsets preserves this comparison.

Put J_ii=0 and J_ij=Z_i({i,j}) for i!=j. Thus J=(E-I)/10+e_1 e_2^T,
where E is the all-ones matrix. Both Gamma^-1 and (Gamma-J)^-1 are strictly
positive. The former has diagonal
2/15, opposite entries7/15, and other entries1/5. For the latter, put
B=Gamma-(E-I)/10. Its inverse has diagonal
(10/7+10/51-20/29)/4>0, opposite entries
(10/7+10/51+20/29)/4>0, and cross entries110/357<1.
Since Gamma-J=B-e_1 e_2^T, the Sherman-Morrison identity gives a strictly
positive inverse. This example has pure sure exits and excludes no new UE
class. It tests a proposed operation even inside the positive-inverse
geometry; it does not refute selection of endpoint-compatible Nash paths.

The external literature points to the same distinction.
[Jenkinson--Li--Liao--Zhang (2025), Theorem3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18092v2)
extracts paths from a supplied invariant measure for a compact closed
multivalued dynamics, but does not supply positive absorption. The relevant
path mechanism is already available in the conference's ergodic recurrence
work; no additional UE coverage follows from this citation.
[Ingalls--Sontag--Wang (2003), Theorem1](https://www.sontaglab.org/FTPDIR/relaxation-di-as-appeared.pdf)
shadows a supplied convexified differential-inclusion trajectory over an
infinite horizon. It permits a perturbed initial point and requires local
Hausdorff-Lipschitz regularity unavailable at general root support changes.
Neither theorem supplies the missing game-specific producer.

The unresolved constructive target is a finite-amplitude controlled invariant
set of ACTUAL Nash transitions. A legal transition must retain its
continuation value and all full deviation comparisons. Certified parameter
continuation and support-dependent interval boxes offer a concrete way to
test this target without assuming a continuous global root selector.
