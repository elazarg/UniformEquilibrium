## 1. Exact question

Fix a checked

```text
FinFourSingletonBaseResetRepairPaidCapDoublePort reward M residual e.
```

Its original singleton source and literal owner-Continue repair use the same
selected free stationary root.  In the double-inert arm, both independently
selected cap-prefix sequences are all Continue and preserve their respective
literal profiles.  The original complete law is also retained by the checked
`QuittingFixedLawResetDispatch` stored in the producer.

Does the affine source/repair law identity now put a positive marked event on
the *same* law as the reset dispatch strongly enough to force one of:

1. a positive exact punishment-floor Bellman edge;
2. a cumulative payoff return; or
3. a strict semantic-debt/support decrease?

The answer is no from the displayed local data.  The affine bridge does give
an exact same-law marked event in one half of its dichotomy, but an
all-Continue cap root tests only singleton rewards against the cap tail.  It
does not test any nonsingleton membership toggle in the retained suffix law.

## 2. The strongest positive same-law consequence

Let `F=Fin 4 \ {e}`.  Write `mu_src` for the terminal law of the sure-owner
singleton source and `mu_rep` for the law after replacing `e` by Always
Continue.  Let

```text
a = 1 - product_(i in F) Pr(i Continues in one free row).
```

The checked producer gives

\[
 a\ge {\Gamma\over \Gamma+2M}>0.                    \tag{2.1}
\]

The reviewed affine-law theorem selects a nonempty `A subset F` with

\[
 \mu_{\rm rep}(A)\ge {\Gamma\over 28M}               \tag{2.2}
\]

and at least one of

\[
 r_e(A)-r_e(\{e\})\ge {\Gamma\over2},                \tag{S}
\]

\[
 r_e(A)-r_e(A\cup\{e\})\ge {\Gamma\over2}.           \tag{L}
\]

### Proposition 2.1 (quantitative source-law owner-leave atom)

In alternative `(L)`, the original source law itself contains the coalition
`A union {e}` with

\[
 \mu_{\rm src}(A\cup\{e\})
   \ge {\Gamma^2\over 28M(\Gamma+2M)},               \tag{2.3}
\]

and at that exact source-law atom owner `e` has the leave gain

\[
 r_e(A)-r_e(A\cup\{e\})\ge\Gamma/2.                  \tag{2.4}
\]

#### Proof

The affine identity is

\[
 \mu_{\rm src}(A\cup\{e\})=a\mu_{\rm rep}(A)
\]

for every nonempty `A subset F`.  Multiply (2.1) and (2.2).  The payoff
inequality is exactly `(L)`.  QED.

Thus the favorable half of the repaired-atom split is not merely a label or
separate-law passport.  It is a quantitative owner-leave toggle on the
literal law retained by the original fixed-law dispatch.  This is the most
one can obtain without a new strategic conversion.

Alternative `(S)` does not have this conclusion: it compares a repaired-law
atom with the solo row and need not be a membership toggle on a
source-supported coalition.

## 3. Exact stationary double-profile regression

The following table shows that even perfect owner/law/atom alignment in the
leave arm does not force cap absorption.

Identify `Fin 4` with `{0,1,2,3}`.  For every nonempty coalition `S`, define

\[
 r_0(S)=\begin{cases}
  1,&0\notin S,\\
  0,&0\in S,
 \end{cases}                                         \tag{3.1}
\]

\[
 r_1(S)=\begin{cases}
  0,&1\notin S,\\
  0,&\{0,1\}\subseteq S,\\
 -1,&1\in S,\ 0\notin S,
 \end{cases}                                         \tag{3.2}
\]

and, for `i=2,3`,

\[
 r_i(S)=\begin{cases}
  0,&i\notin S,\\
 -1,&i\in S.
 \end{cases}                                         \tag{3.3}
\]

Let `sigma` be the stationary profile in which `0,1` Quit surely and `2,3`
Continue surely.  Let `tau` be its literal owner repair: player `0` is
changed to Always Continue, while the other three stationary actions are
unchanged.  Thus `tau` has only player `1` Quitting surely.

### Proposition 3.1 (aligned marked law with a unique allC cap root)

The table and profiles above satisfy all of the following.

1. Their semantic pairs are

   \[
   U(\sigma)=(0,0,0,0),\qquad B(\sigma)=(1,0,0,0),   \tag{3.4}
   \]

   \[
   U(\tau)=(1,-1,0,0),\qquad B(\tau)=(1,0,0,0).      \tag{3.5}
   \]

   Hence the source has positive-debt support `{0}` and the repair has
   positive-debt support `{1}`.

   The free product point (`1` sure Quit, `2,3` Continue) is an exact induced
   Nash point over singleton base `{0}`.  All three free coordinates have
   zero unrestricted source debt and are punishment-floor safe.  The
   singleton owner floor excess is one, the free absorption probability is
   one, and `{0,1}` is a strict-superset source atom of mass one.  Thus all of
   the local stationary-source fields used by the checked singleton producer
   hold with `M=Gamma=delta=1`.

2. Player `0` has a full unit paid row on `sigma`: changing its pure time
   from date zero to any later date (or Never) changes the terminal coalition
   from `{0,1}` to `{1}` and raises its payoff from zero to one.  Player `1`
   has a full unit paid row on `tau`: changing date zero to literal Never
   raises its payoff from `-1` to zero.  (A later finite Quit would still pay
   the singleton value `-1` and is not used.)

   The repair identities are also exact: player `0`'s repaired payoff and cap
   both equal its source cap `1`; player `1` is a distinct free debtor with
   repaired debt one.  The repaired floor alternative is its explicit
   under-floor arm, since `U_1(tau)=-1<P_1=0`.

3. The complete laws are

   \[
   \mu_{\rm src}=\delta_{\{0,1\}},\qquad
   \mu_{\rm rep}=\delta_{\{1\}}.                     \tag{3.6}
   \]

   They obey the exact affine bridge with `a=1`.  Its selected atom
   `A={1}` has repaired mass one and the aligned source-law owner-leave gain

   \[
   r_0(\{1\})-r_0(\{0,1\})=1.                       \tag{3.7}
   \]

4. Player `2` is a zero-debt reset label at the source, and its opponent
   incidence with owner `0` under `mu_src` is one.  The same law supports the
   strict member-leave toggle (3.7).

5. At the common cap tail

   \[
   V=(1,0,0,0),                                      \tag{3.8}
   \]

   all Continue is the unique exact product root.  Consequently every
   canonical cap prefix of either stationary profile is all Continue, has
   zero charge, and preserves its suffix semantic pair and paid row.

6. The tail `V` is punishment-floor safe.  In fact every punishment value is
   zero.

7. Taking the all-Never semantic pair as the minimum, the source law and
   source semantic pair admit a literal static
   `QuittingFixedLawResetDispatch` with reset label `2`, marked opponent `0`,
   and returned point equal to the source pair.  Its `dynamic_exit` is the
   all-Continue cap-face arm, not an absorbing descent.

#### Proof

Both profiles absorb at date zero.  Against the singleton base `{0}`, free
player `1` is indifferent between tying at `{0,1}` and leaving `{0}`, while
players `2,3` strictly prefer Continue to joining.  This proves the induced
Nash assertion.  At `sigma`, player `0` gets zero by
Quitting and one by omitting itself from `{0,1}`.  Player `1` gets zero both
by tying and by leaving player `0` alone.  A deviation by player `2` or `3`
which Quits at date zero pays `-1`; continuing pays zero.  Pure-time
extremality therefore gives (3.4) against arbitrary behavioral deviations.

At `tau`, player `0` is an outsider to `{1}` and gets one; joining pays zero.
Player `1` gets `-1` by its prescribed Quit and zero by waiting forever.
Players `2,3` again get zero by continuing and `-1` by joining.  This proves
(3.5) with unrestricted caps and proves the two paid-row assertions.
Equation (3.6), the affine identity, (3.7), and the incidence assertion are
immediate.

It remains to check the cap root.  Against any opponent product law, player
`0` gets exactly one more from Continue than from Quit: at the empty opponent
corner compare tail `V_0=1` with solo payoff zero, and at every nonempty
corner compare (3.1) before and after inserting `0`.  Thus every exact root
has quit probability `q_0=0`.

Conditional on `q_0=0`, player `1` gets zero from Continue and `-1` from Quit
at every opponent corner, including the empty corner where the continuation
value is `V_1=0`.  Hence `q_1=0`.  The same strict comparison holds globally
for players `2,3` by (3.3), so `q_2=q_3=0`.  This proves uniqueness of all
Continue.  Prefixing it leaves both the continuation value and literal suffix
unchanged, proving item 5.

Every player guarantees zero by Never.  Opponents can choose all Never, after
which the player's best payoff is zero (the solo rewards are zero for player
`0` and `-1` for the others).  Hence all punishment values are zero.
Together with (3.4)--(3.5), this proves the floor, owner-excess, repaired
handoff, and support assertions.  The absorption and atom claims follow from
the sure Quit of player `1`.

Finally let `minimum=((0,0,0,0),(0,0,0,0))`, let `target=returned=Sem(sigma)`,
and let the retained law be `mu_src`.  The reset debt of player `2` is zero;
`D(minimum)=0<=D(returned)=D(target)=1`; the transfer inequality has zero on
its left and a nonnegative sum on its right; (3.7) supplies the supported
toggle; and the unique all-Continue root supplies the static `dynamic_exit`
arm.  These are exactly the local fields of a
`QuittingFixedLawResetDispatch`.  QED.

## 4. Why the retained toggle is invisible to the cap root

The regression exposes the structural mismatch, not merely a bad choice of
labels.  At an all-Continue root, no nonempty first-stage coalition is played.
Its Nash inequalities are precisely

\[
 r_i(\{i\})\le V_i\quad\text{for every }i.            \tag{4.1}
\]

The retained law and (3.7) concern the *suffix* coalition `{0,1}`.  The
strict comparison

\[
 r_0(\{0,1\})<r_0(\{1\})

does not negate any inequality in (4.1).  In fact it makes Continue more,
not less, attractive to player `0`.  Positive source-law mass, full observer
reach, exact label matching, and a unit toggle gain therefore coexist with
zero cap charge.

This also explains why the checked fixed-law dispatcher legitimately retains
both a `supported_toggle` and an all-Continue `dynamic_exit` arm.  The former
is a terminal-law certificate; the latter is a one-stage cap-Nash statement.
No declaration identifies them as the same strategic event.

## 5. Verdict and the minimal missing implication

The sharpened T4 implication is **false from the local paid/reset/double-port
fields alone**, even in its best-aligned owner-leave cell.  Proposition 2.1
does supply an actual same-law quantitative atom, so the remaining loss is no
longer law provenance.  It is the cap-to-suffix strategic conversion.

A successful theorem must use the ambient positive-minimum/hard-residual data
to add at least one of the following genuinely new conclusions on a
source-matched state:

```text
some i has solo_i > capTail_i,
```

which directly excludes the all-Continue cap root; or

```text
the supported suffix toggle is implemented by an exact floor-admissible
root/block whose other-player endpoint inequalities are all controlled;
```

or a nonlocal operation which changes the semantic source and proves a strict
debt/support rank decrease while retaining regeneration provenance.

Merely retaining the law, atom, paid row, reset incidence, or lossless shifted
row cannot do this.  The regression realizes all those local mechanisms but
has no positive edge, return charge, or strict reset descent.

The precise hard-residual datum absent from the regression is global: the
all-Never profile is an exact terminal equilibrium, so `D_*=0` and there is no
positive terminal exploitability witness.  The example therefore does not
refute a theorem which uses those ambient hypotheses to force one of the new
conclusions above.  It does prove that such a theorem cannot be obtained by
unpacking the existing local packet fields alone.
