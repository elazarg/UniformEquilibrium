# Control theory translation: repair barriers and finite switching

Identity: CODEX_LARCH_JOINT.

## Status and disposition

**Outside-field theory sketch with exact positive and negative tests.**
No Lean work, export, or arbitrary-game UE producer is claimed. The useful
translation from control theory is approximation of a simultaneous motion by
finite coordinate motions while respecting a quantitative safety margin.
The attractive nonholonomic/Lie-bracket analogy fails for fixed marginal
resets: these commute exactly.

The surviving statement is a conditional construction: a supplied joint
descent curve of order k produces one-law repair sequences with arbitrarily
small temporary exploitability overshoot. The number of replacements reflects
the order of descent: logarithmic in the reciprocal overshoot for k=1 and
O(η^(-(k−1)/k)) for k>1, up to a harmless logarithmic term. An exact canonical
game has matching η^(−1/2) order for quadratic descent on a fixed face.

This may clarify which coordinate-repair impossibilities matter and give a
small algorithmic consumer of the common-curvature certificate. It does not
produce that certificate, and it is lower priority as a direct UE attack.

[Independent review](../feedback/CODEX_LARCH_JOINT__OUTSIDE_FIELD_CONTROL_AND_REPAIR_BARRIERS__BY_CODEX_LARCH.md)
checks the finite construction, switching bounds, exact calibration, and
counterexamples. It found no unresolved mathematical objection within this
conditional scope.

## 1. Outside-field source and translation contract

The bounded reading started with
[the nonlocality catalogue](../ideas/NONLOCALITY_TECHNIQUE_CATALOGUE.md),
especially NL-10, NL-12, NL-15, NL-17, and
[its control-theoretic recommendations](../ideas/CURRENT_NONLOCALITY_RECOMMENDATIONS.md).
Those recommendations correctly demand an executable transition and a renewed
invariant, rather than just the language of a controller.

In control theory, switching among restricted controls can approximate a
motion generated by their relaxed combination; constrained variants must
also control intermediate constraint violations. For an external primary
reference see Vasudevan, Gonzalez, Bajcsy, and Sastry,
[Consistent Approximations for the Optimal Control of Constrained Switched Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.0062).
No theorem from that paper is invoked here: the exact product-space argument
below is simpler and is given in full.

Here the state is an actual independent stopping-law profile p, the objective
E is its full terminal exploitability, and one control replaces one entire
marginal law. This is a construction in the space of candidate strategies.
Its sequence index is not a date or a reached public history in the game.
Every intermediate object is an actual product profile, but this alone does
not make the sequence a Nash–Bellman chronology or a repeated-play strategy.

## 2. Why mixed response squares are not Lie brackets

For fixed target laws q_i define the unilateral relaxation field

    X_i(p)_i=q_i−p_i,       X_i(p)_j=0 for j≠i.

Different fields depend on disjoint coordinates. Their derivatives therefore
give [X_i,X_j]=0 for i≠j; equivalently, the two marginal replacements commute
exactly. A four-step reversible loop in these coordinates returns to its
starting law. A nonzero mixed difference of a scalar response gain does not
change this fact. It is curvature of an observation of the product state,
not a net displacement generated by a commutator loop.

The distinction is visible in
[the quadratic-repair calibration](CODEX_LARCH_JOINT__MULTILATERAL_REPAIR_THEORY.md):

    E(a,b)=1+|a−b|−ab.

The active gains have a nonzero −ab interaction, yet the a and b coordinate
motions commute. Calling their response square a nonholonomic displacement
would predict a phenomenon that the actual law motions do not have.

State-dependent target selectors could make vector fields noncommute, but
this would require an actual locally regular selector, feasible reverse
motions at simplex boundaries, and control of all changing caps. These are
substantial new game hypotheses. Moreover all whole-law motions are already
legal in an unrestricted profile search, so a bracket cannot enlarge its
full tangent cone. It could matter only for a separately specified constrained
control family. For the usual control interpretation of a bracket as switched
noncommuting flow, see the authors' teaching material for
[Modern Robotics](https://modernrobotics.northwestern.edu/nu-gm-book-resource/13-3-2-controllability-of-wheeled-mobile-robots-part-3-of-4/).

## 3. A finite switching theorem with a literal cap budget

Fix a nonempty finite set of quitting players and a reward bound M>0. Equip profiles
with distance D(p,q)=Σ_i TV(p_i,q_i). Uniform opponent coupling gives

    |E(p)−E(q)|≤4M D(p,q).                            (1)

This bound includes all complete responses; it is not a finite-tester
approximation. Let p(s), 0≤s≤S, be a supplied curve of actual product laws
such that, for constants L,c>0 and integer k≥1,

    D(p(s),p(t))≤L|s−t|,
    E(p(s))≤m−c s^k,       m=E(p(0)).                 (2)

For every η>0 there is a finite sequence from p(0) to p(S), changing only
one marginal at each step, such that

    E(intermediate profile)≤m+η,
    E(final profile)≤m−c S^k.                         (3)

To prove this, put K=4ML. At parameter s choose

    h=min(S−s,(η+c s^k)/(2K)).                        (4)

Replace the players' marginals one at a time from p_i(s) to p_i(s+h).
Every intermediate product uses one of these two endpoint marginals in each
coordinate, so its distance from p(s) is at most Lh. By (1)–(2),

    E(intermediate)≤m−c s^k+Kh
                  ≤m+η/2−c s^k/2≤m+η.

After at most n replacements the actual state is exactly p(s+h), and the
same invariant restarts with that literal state. The process terminates
because every unclipped h is at least η/(2K). No profile averaging or
correlated corner draw occurs. The coordinate moves can also be filled in
continuously along marginal mixture chords without worsening the bound:
the same distance estimate holds along each chord.

For a quantitative count, set r=(η/c)^(1/k), assuming r<S. The initial
parameter interval [0,r] takes at most 2Kr/η+1 blocks. In the band
[2^j r,2^(j+1)r], every step beginning there is at least
η2^(jk)/(2K), so at most

    2K c^(−1/k) η^(-(k−1)/k) 2^(−j(k−1))+1

blocks begin there. Sum the geometric series for k>1; the extra ones add
only O(log(cS^k/η)). For k=1 each band's bound is constant and the total
is O(log(cS/η)). Multiply by n to count individual replacements. Constants
depend on the supplied curve, not on η.

Thus the common second-order criterion in the linked note, once it supplies
E(p(s))≤m−c s² on a small interval, has a genuine finite coordinate-update
consumer. A finite smooth curve on a fixed calendar is Lipschitz in D.
Boundary mass representations from the pivot LP must first be implemented
with error smaller than the available budget; an unimplemented relaxed mass
point is not an intermediate law.

## 4. Exact quadratic calibration and a lower bound on switching

Use the fully specified canonical table in the linked repair note: pivot 0
quits at zero, player 3 plays Never, and the other two players join the pivot
independently with probabilities a,b. Their complete exploitability is

    E(a,b)=1+|a−b|−ab.

The path (s,s), 0≤s≤1/2, has E=1−s². Section 3 supplies a finite sequence
of one-coordinate changes reaching (1/2,1/2), with final E=3/4 and every
intermediate E≤1+η, in O(η^(−1/2)) replacements. This gives a concrete
meaning to small temporary cap violations; the eventual improvement can
remain fixed while the allowed initial overshoot tends to zero.

The rate cannot be uniformly improved on this face. Assume 0<η≤1/16 and
let r be the current maximum of a,b before it first reaches √η. If one
coordinate is changed to a new value u above the unchanged coordinate v,
the cap budget requires

    1+u−v−uv≤1+η,
    u≤(v+η)/(1−v)≤(r+η)/(1−r).

Hence the maximum can increase by at most

    (η+r²)/(1−r)≤(8/3)η                while r≤√η.

Starting at zero therefore needs at least (3/8)η^(−1/2) replacements before
the maximum reaches √η. Reaching (1/2,1/2) must do so. This lower bound
allows arbitrary ordering and decreases of the coordinates; it does not
assume fixed step size or monotone coordinate paths.

At η=0 no first nontrivial coordinate move is possible: E(a,0)=1+a and
E(0,b)=1+b. Thus exact monotonicity and arbitrarily small positive overshoot
have qualitatively different reachability. The lower bound is only on this
fixed two-coordinate face; allowing pivot changes may create shortcuts.

## 5. Exact missing game hypothesis and falsifiers

The missing source hypothesis is (2), or enough actual response information
to produce it. For quadratic descent the common-curvature test asks for one
feasible velocity and a common acceleration improving every zero-slope tied
response. The switching theorem cannot manufacture their signs. At a genuine
positive unrestricted global minimum no curve satisfying (2) exists; any
claim to derive one must use additional stopping-game structure to contradict
that hypothetical minimum.

Three falsifiers prevent an inflated translation:

1. A nonzero response square is not a Lie bracket: the commuting calibration
   in Section 2 disproves this exact identification.
2. Coordinate best responses are not the controls in Section 3. The prescribed
   short moves may temporarily increase exploitability or fail to optimize
   their mover's payoff. Replacing them by selected coordinate minimizers can
   lose both the path and the budget. The complete coordinate trap already
   demonstrates the danger of insisting on nonincrease at every step.
3. A fixed positive descent endpoint alone is insufficient for arbitrarily
   small overshoot. For a generic scalar objective on a product domain, two
   points can be separated by a positive sublevel barrier. Assumption (2)
   supplies an entire low-objective connecting path, not only its endpoint.
   For example the multiaffine function f(x,y)=2−xy−x/4 on [−1,1]² has
   endpoint values f(−1,−1)=5/4 and f(1,1)=3/4, but every continuous path
   between them crosses x=0 and has f=2 there. This is a generic control
   falsifier, not a quitting-game counterexample; endpoint comparisons alone
   do not prove path access, even for a separately affine function.

## 6. Overlap with chronology and holonomy no-gos

[MAXWELL's response-chart holonomy note](CODEX_MAXWELL__RESPONSE_CHART_HOLONOMY_AND_OBSERVER_SWITCH_NOGO.md)
already shows that rotating observer charts need not form a fixed scalar
potential. The present construction uses the same full E throughout and
has no frozen observer chart. It gains no chronological charge from a loop.

[LEIBNIZ's source-faithful chord boundary](CODEX_LEIBNIZ__SOURCE_FAITHFUL_RESPONSE_CHORD_COCYCLE_BOUNDARY.md)
already shows that literal profile fidelity and exact caps do not preserve
previously killed debts under later updates. Our intermediate profiles are
allowed to recreate those debts inside the explicit budget. Consequently
the construction does not imply the persistent-zero rank theorem or repair
that note's missing renewable field.

[HAHN's infinitesimal-corner audit](CODEX_HAHN__KKT_INFINITESIMAL_CORNER_ALIGNMENT_BOUNDARY.md)
identifies a first-order semantic seam versus a second-order bubble. Finite
switching does not convert its weak bubble into escaped terminal mass or
remove the need for a common first-order-flat path. It merely tracks a path
after that missing compatibility has been supplied.

## 7. Research decision

Keep the quantitative switching statement as a small potential consumer of
actual joint curvature, and reject the bare Lie-bracket metaphor for fixed
law resets. Its useful question is whether vanishing permitted overshoot
changes the complete coordinate trap under a source-derived joint curve.
The existing first-order joint escape already answers that question locally
for HILBERT's solved fixture; the quadratic calibration adds a sharp switching
cost that first-order analysis misses.

Do not build a general control library yet. A meaningful next test would
find a real source family with certified common curvature and bound the
switching cost uniformly against its source and tail errors. Without that
input this is a mathematically valid translation with limited UE force,
not a new arbitrary-game algorithm and not a reached-gameplay chronology.
