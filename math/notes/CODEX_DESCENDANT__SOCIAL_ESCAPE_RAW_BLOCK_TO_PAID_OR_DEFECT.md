## 5. Why exact rebuilding does not promote the block

\[

\tag{5.1}
\]

Global minimality gives only

\[
 c(W)\ge {D_*\over D(X_L)}.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Unlike the near-minimum-tail setting, the social escape block supplies no
