# Fin4 program blindspots and decisive tests

Identity: PAIRED_HULL_REVIEW  
Date: 2026-08-31  
Status: big-picture audit; no new chamber theorem is claimed

## Executive verdict

The program is focused on the right **semantic** obstruction but is spending
too much effort refining entrances to it.

The current checked top-level reduction has only two terminal chambers:

\[
\text{full debt}\quad\lor\quad\text{reset rigid}.
\]

Both feed the same downstream waist:

\[
\text{actual paid response or reset}
\longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
\text{charged return},\\
\text{minimum-fibre rank transition},\\
\text{strictly off-minimum inert port}.
\end{cases}
\]

The first two outputs have consumers only when their exact source and
renewal hypotheses are supplied.  The third does not.  Most recent
constructions produce stronger atoms, better reaches, extra cap rectangles,
two cuts, social surplus, or better normal forms, but then land at this same
waist.

The repeated loss is not plausibly one forgotten scalar field.  It is the
absence of a **commuting actual successor seam**: after a horizontal response,
reset, minimization, or compactification, one needs an actual finite forward
block whose literal successor is the next source and whose unrestricted caps
remain controlled.  The architecture notes prove that this seam cannot in
general be reconstructed after reach has vanished or after only compact
semantic data have been retained.

Accordingly, the next result should count as conjecture-facing progress only
if it does at least one of the following:

1. closes one of the two chambers;
2. constructs an extension-compatible forward block and invokes a named
   terminal or capacity consumer;
3. gives a renewable finite rank on the complete source packet; or
4. produces an exact positive controller--tester barrier for one table.

Another atom, local paid edge, cap trichotomy, compact limit, or real-valued
decrease should be recorded as producer progress but not as shrinkage of the
remaining strongly connected component.

## 1. Established dependency waist

The following are established project facts, not speculation.

### 1.1 Complete top-level reduction

The checked global-minimum and law-tight construction gives positive minimum
debt, a source-attached positive finite terminal atom, and the exhaustive
full-debt/reset-rigid split.  The former singleton/Never third chamber is
excluded by the global singleton moat.  See
`formalized/GLOBAL_MINIMUM_SINGLETON_MOAT_AND_FIN4_TWO_CHAMBER_REDUCTION.md`.

Thus the two maintained chamber questions are genuinely exhaustive:

- `questions/FIN4_FULL_DEBT_CHAMBER_CONSUMER.md`;
- `questions/FIN4_RESET_RIGID_CHAMBER_CONSUMER.md`.

They are not known to be independent.  Their descendants repeatedly enter
the same off-minimum paid-cap/inert component.

### 1.2 Bounded exact-block capacity is mandatory in a counterexample

Unbounded finite exact Nash--Bellman hazard capacity produces a persistent
spine and hence a uniform-equilibrium payoff.  Therefore a Fin4 counterexample
has bounded exact-block capacity.  See
`formalized/UNBOUNDED_FINITE_HAZARD_CAPACITY_COMPILER.md`.

This is stronger than a qualitative compactness statement.  Any proposed
renewable construction of exact blocks with an unbounded total hazard charge
would already close the positive theorem.  The missing issue is that the
known paid or absorbing pieces are not yet literal successive blocks of one
forward construction.

### 1.3 Full debt already has actual reached profitable responses

The full-debt line has progressed beyond terminal-law atom discovery.  It has
source-attached behavioral forks with positive whole-profile gain and actual
joint reach.  Fresh maximal-root recomputation then gives charge, reset-rigid
entry, or a strictly off-minimum vanishing-root port.  The latter can be
neutralized by descendant-slice minimization only to reset-rigid entry or a
strictly off-minimum unique-all-Continue port.  See
`formalized/VANISHING_RESPONSE_MAXIMAL_ROOT_RESET_REDUCTION.md` and
`formalized/FOUR_PROFILE_DESCENDANT_SLICE_NEUTRALIZATION.md`.

What is absent is not another profitable row.  It is an extension-compatible
rebase after the response changes other players' caps.

### 1.4 Reset rigidity already has finite static geometry

At a reset-rigid minimum, the all-Continue exact root is unique, the reset
owner has zero debt, and the debt transferred out of that coordinate is
exactly compensated by other coordinates.  Static terminal toggles and cap
rectangles can produce paid rows or response switches, but these are
horizontal profile comparisons.  Cap-switch curvature has already been
localized to a full-chord paid row while the finite-splice cemetery shortcut
has an exact Fin4 regression.  See
`formalized/CAP_SWITCH_RECTANGLE_FULL_CHORD_AND_FINITE_SPLICE_BOUNDARY.md`.

Thus the reset problem is not lack of finite geometry.  It is the failure to
turn that geometry into a same-source forward return or zero-preserving
minimum-fibre transition.

### 1.5 Several complete consumers are conditional producers' endpoints

The following implications are real and useful, but their inputs are not
forced by the current chambers:

- a source-selected exact spine with a nonsummable fixed marginal clock is
  consumed;
- unbounded exact-block capacity is consumed;
- normal S.3 or its well-supported source interface is consumed;
- a supplied uniformly reached post-mark two-cut block is dispatched;
- a supplied charged near-return or renewable minimum-fibre rank edge is
  consumed.

The direct AGKRS forward trichotomy is checked, and the normal S.3 consumer is
checked.  Neither constructs the approximate-equilibrium/S.3 premise from a
Fin4 hard residual.  See
`formalized/AGKRS_FORWARD_TRICHOTOMY_BY_REFUSAL_COMPACTIFICATION.md` and
`formalized/NORMAL_S3_DELAYED_SWITCH_UNIFORM_PAYOFF.md`.

### 1.6 The exact negative route exists

The controller--tester formulation gives the exact value of the original
all-behavior problem and an exact barrier language.  The exact scale
semidecision gives finite positive certificates when the value is positive.
What is missing is a concrete positive table/certificate, not soundness of
the certificate target.  See
`exports/QUITTING_CONTROLLER_TESTER_VALUE_AND_BARRIER_DUALITY.md`,
`arch/DECIDE_CONTROLLER_TESTER.md`, and
`formalized/EXACT_FIN4_SCALE_RESOLUTION_AND_COUNTEREXAMPLE_SEMIDECISION.md`.

## 2. Information repeatedly forgotten

These are not stylistic warnings.  Each has an exact regression or no-go in
the maintained architecture.

### 2.1 Horizontal construction is not chronology

Response replacement, source selection, suffix recentering, minimization,
normalization, and compactification are horizontal operations.  A cycle made
from such operations need not be one playable time sequence.  Even prefixing
all Continue and then deleting that row makes a zero-charge two-cycle.
`arch/RECURRENCE_ARCHITECTURE_OBSTRUCTION.md` rules out recurrence arguments
which do not type their edges.

### 2.2 Vanishing reach destroys the successor

At a fixed positive reach, conditioning is stable.  When reach tends to zero,
the closed suffix relation has an arbitrary target fibre.  The target cannot
be reconstructed from the named limiting source by protected finite
prefix/suffix programs.  This is the exact content of
`arch/VANISHING_REACH_SUFFIX_NO_GO.md`.

Consequently, retaining more limiting payoff, law, cap, or atom coordinates
does not by itself recover the missing actual successor.

### 2.3 Exact profile information and compact all-depth suffix control are
incompatible

Marginal stopping laws are an exact sufficient state for all unilateral
behavioral deviations, but the exact state is not compact in a topology
supporting one uniform all-depth suffix modulus.  Compact coordinatewise
states support only fixed finite programs; operational total variation
supports exact arbitrary-depth operations but is noncompact.  See
`arch/SUFFICIENT_STATE.md`, `arch/STATE_TOPOLOGIES_AND_APPROXIMATION.md`, and
`arch/SUFFIX_INFORMATION_OBSTRUCTION.md`.

This is evidence against the hypothesis that one more finite semantic
passport will solve the problem.  A successful proof is more likely to
construct its forward trace before compactifying, or to use an external
finite phase/rank which exits before reach collapses.

### 2.4 Unrestricted caps are nonsmooth and nonlocal

Changing one player's complete stopping law preserves that player's cap but
can raise several other caps by activating remote stopping times.  Global
minimality then forces the mover's debt reduction to be compensated by
cross-coordinate leakage.  A cap rectangle may detect this, but a response
curl is not a cap curl, and neither is a chronological root.

This is why debt-killing does not imply growth of the zero-debt set and why
support ranks reset after off-minimum reconstruction.

### 2.5 Minimum-fibre exact prefixing is inert

At positive global minimum debt, every exact cap--Nash root on the law-tight
minimum face is all Continue.  It has survival one, preserves the point, and
spends no charge.  Exact prefix rays are useful off the minimum and as
negative clock-escape results, but they cannot generate minimum-fibre motion.

### 2.6 A conditional consumer is not a source theorem

The normal S.3 compiler, persistent-spine compiler, two-cut compiler,
near-return compiler, and renewable-rank compiler are strong.  None shows
that the two chambers supply its input.  Several research loops have
effectively reconstructed the consumer interface without producing the
same-sequence input.

### 2.7 Subsequences cannot be spliced after the fact

An atom, a paid response, a minimum endpoint, and a good cap root extracted
on separate subsequences cannot be combined unless a common refinement
retains all four profiles and all literal dates.  The four-profile descendant
packet is a genuine repair of this issue inside one construction, but its
compact descendant still does not preserve one literal prehistory or make a
replayable block.

## 3. Why the last attempts keep reaching the same place

The common pattern is:

1. **localization succeeds:** one finds an atom, first disagreement, paid
   response, cap switch, premium, social-surplus witness, or two cuts;
2. **exactification changes the target cap:** old exact roots are no longer
   root Nash for the modified continuation;
3. **recomputation succeeds only pointwise:** a new maximal root exists but
   leads to reset rigidity or a strictly off-minimum inert target;
4. **compactification loses ancestry:** the limiting target has the desired
   semantic data but is not the literal successor of the limiting source;
5. **a proposed rank is not renewable:** it decreases only within one
   minimum-fibre lane and can be reset by the off-minimum re-entry.

This explains the apparent repeated “last ten percent.”  Producer
classification really has improved, but the remaining component is closed
under the operations used to refine it.  Renaming its state by clock,
curvature, normalized flow, two-cut, or premium data does not remove an
edge from that component.

There is no evidence here that a counterexample exists.  No positive table
or accepted positive barrier is known.  There is also no theorem saying that
the commuting seam must exist.  The nonsmooth cap and vanishing-reach
regressions show a coherent mechanism by which a counterexample could avoid
it.  Both possibilities therefore remain live.

## 4. Underused leverage

### 4.1 Use bounded capacity as the target of every renewal attempt

The strongest global counterexample restriction is not merely positive
minimum debt; it is bounded exact-block hazard capacity.  A local charged
piece becomes decisive only when it is made extension-compatible.  Once that
is achieved, one should concatenate immediately and invoke the capacity
compiler instead of proving another local charge estimate.

### 4.2 Use the barrier formulation as an independent program

The controller--tester state is complete and finite-dimensional along every
finite controller word.  Positive barriers are exact negative certificates.
This route does not need to solve source reprojection.  Exact search should
target barrier/tree certificates and the two chamber sign patterns, rather
than only stationary or bounded-clock exploitability.

### 4.3 Use the full-debt actual-reach fork, not older deleted-survival atoms

Actual joint reach and a legal complete response are already available in
the full-debt line.  Further atom-production work below that source has low
value unless it also solves re-exactification at the changed cap.

### 4.4 Treat the two chambers as one downstream component

Full debt can enter reset rigidity, while reset-side paid responses can exit
off minimum and re-enter the common paid-cap/inert machinery.  Separate local
normal forms are still useful, but the conjecture-facing rank or return must
be global across those transitions.

### 4.5 Audit law-support contractions, but do not confuse them with a
consumer

Recent reviewed product-law and pure finite-clock results remove genuine
entrance branches and turn finite-clock minima into off-minimum paid ports.
They should be used to simplify the reset law support.  They do not consume
the resulting paid port.  Any new law-support split should be judged by
whether one output leaves the common component.

## 5. Highest-value attacks

### Priority 1: construction-specific commuting-seam theorem

Choose one already source-attached paid fork—preferably the full-debt
actual-reach fork—and ask for one exact statement:

> After the legal response changes the continuation cap, construct a finite
> exact or summably approximate Nash--Bellman block whose literal successor is
> the response profile, while preserving the source reach and the full
> unrestricted-cap error budget.

The output must immediately give one of:

- unbounded extension-compatible charge, contradicting bounded capacity;
- a checked charged near-return;
- a complete reset-rigid child; or
- a renewable finite rank on the same source packet.

This is narrower and more falsifiable than a general “source reprojection”
theorem.

### Priority 2: decide reset leakage under positive global minimality

Local zero-minimum regressions show that static reset passports permit
rotation of the zero-debt set.  The decisive test is whether the same response
square can coexist with **positive global minimum debt and the actual
source-attached minimum law**.

Either prove that positive minimality forces a zero-preserving response or an
off-minimum charge, or produce an exact finite table/finite-tree relaxation
realizing the whole reset passport at positive certified value.  Success in
either direction would materially change the conjecture.

### Priority 3: approximate forward packets at moving successors

The maintained approximate-forward question deliberately allows small root
Nash errors.  Attack it at the moving Bellman successor, not at one frozen
cap.  A useful theorem must keep one error tolerance for an arbitrarily long
packet and must not let the cumulative error grow with length.

This is the natural positive response to bounded exact capacity.  A local
quadratic-error row is valuable only with a renewable moving-source theorem.

### Priority 4: exact barrier search targeted to the residual

Run the complete positive semidecision against normalized rational tables,
but bias candidate generation toward the table-only hard-residual normal
forms and the two chamber passports.  Require exact proof-producing lower
certificates.  Failure at finitely many scales is evidence only; a positive
barrier is a complete negative result.

### Priority 5: source-selected persistent spine

This remains a complete positive route.  It deserves effort only when a
specific selector uses positive-minimum or hard-residual information to avoid
the canonical all-Continue phantom spine.  Abstract compact spine existence
or another all-summable prefix ray is not useful.

## 6. Routes to deprioritize

The following should receive low priority unless accompanied by a new direct
consumer interface.

1. Further AGKRS compactification: the forward trichotomy and conditional
   S.3 consumer are already checked; the missing premise is external.
2. Source-free normalized matrix, ballistic, occupation, or parity states:
   these do not retain the absolute cap/law chronology needed downstream.
3. More same-stage coalition cycles or terminal toggles: horizontal finite
   geometry is already abundant and is not game time.
4. More generic state/grammar architecture: the exact sufficient state,
   topology tradeoff, executable grammar, and adapter no-gos are already
   understood.
5. New real-valued debt descents without a renewable packet rank.
6. New compact limits whose source and target are not limits of one common
   literal execution relation.
7. New packet wrappers which end in the named off-minimum paid-cap or inert
   port without closing an outgoing edge.

## 7. Falsifiable tests for proposed progress

Every new conjecture-facing proposal should pass all applicable tests below.

### Test A: literal successor test

Exhibit actual profiles and finite root words on one indexed sequence.  State
which endpoint is literally the suffix after the word.  Equality only of
semantic limits or terminal laws fails this test.

### Test B: full-cap test

Control the supremum over all pure stopping times and Never for every player,
not only current root endpoints, stationary responses, or a selected finite
menu.

### Test C: positive-reach test

Any suffix conditioning must retain a uniform reach floor, or keep the
prelimit execution and quantify the reach-weighted error.  Promoting a
zero-reach conditional limit to a new source fails this test.

### Test D: extension-compatibility test

After one block, reapply the same construction to its literal successor.
If the next source must be independently reselected, the block cannot
contradict bounded capacity and is not a renewable transition.

### Test E: charge-relative error test

If the useful charge tends to zero, prove that the replay/seam error is
summable or is little-o of charge.  Mere convergence of both quantities to
zero is insufficient.

### Test F: rank-renewal test

Define the rank on the complete global state type and verify it decreases
after off-minimum reconstruction and chamber changes.  A phase tag may be
spent once; it may not be recreated by selecting a fresh source.

### Test G: named-consumer test

Name the checked consumer and list every one of its hypotheses.  If any
source, law, target, reach, response, or fixed-payoff field is newly selected
after the producer, the interface is not closed.

### Test H: exact negative-certificate test

For a counterexample candidate, produce a sound finite barrier/tree
certificate controlling escape to late times and all behavioral responses.
Stationary, bounded-clock, or floating-point exploitability does not count.

## 8. Concrete next experiments

1. **One-fork commuting square.**  Instantiate the strongest full-debt
   actual-reach fork on a small exact table.  Recompute maximal roots at all
   four response corners and test whether one literal root word can be kept
   Nash, or whether a quantitative cap seam is unavoidable.  Record the seam
   as a controller--tester barrier candidate rather than discarding the
   failure.
2. **Positive-minimum reset feasibility.**  Encode the finite reset-rigid
   equalities, singleton moat, source-law incidence, and a finite exact
   controller lower bound.  Search for a rational model.  A model is a
   serious counterexample seed; an exact infeasibility certificate suggests
   the missing chamber inequality.
3. **Capacity replay attempt.**  For one current paid-port producer, specify
   the exact data needed to re-run it at its target.  Attempt two consecutive
   iterations before proving an omega theorem.  Failure should identify the
   first noncommuting cap or ancestry field precisely.
4. **Barrier-first exact search.**  Search directly for positive invariant
   barriers in the 17-dimensional forward tester ledger, using chamber
   normal forms only as candidate-generation constraints.
5. **Audit the new reset law-support contraction.**  Verify law and all
   deleted-law convergence under the proposed late all-Continue prefixes.
   If sound, update the reset entrance map; do not count it as chamber closure
   unless its positive-singleton output is consumed.

## 9. Interpretation of the “missing information” hypothesis

The evidence supports a precise weak version and rejects a naive one.

- **Supported:** current compact packets often forget the actual
  source/successor coupling, relative timing, or response code needed for the
  next chronological operation.
- **Rejected as a general remedy:** there is no evident finite list of extra
  semantic coordinates whose compact retention makes every suffix operation
  continuous.  The suffix no-gos prove this impossible in that form.
- **Plausible positive architecture:** carry one proof-relevant finite trace
  with explicit reach and cap-error budgets, execute before compactifying,
  and use a finite phase/rank or bounded-capacity contradiction.
- **Plausible negative architecture:** a table may have a positive invariant
  controller--tester barrier precisely because every attempted horizontal
  response incurs a nonvanishing chronological seam.

This makes the research program scientifically falsifiable.  The next phase
should test whether the seam can be built for one complete chamber source, or
whether the failure itself can be converted into a positive exact barrier.
