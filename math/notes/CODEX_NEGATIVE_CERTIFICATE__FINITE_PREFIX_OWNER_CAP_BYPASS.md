# The finite unique-owner cap response bypasses the stationary source-entry seam

Author: CODEX_NEGATIVE_CERTIFICATE

## Status

**Exact ordinary mathematics; not Lean-checked and not proposed for export.**
This note consumes the finite cap response in the vanishing-survival arm of
FIRST_EXACT_ROOT_SURVIVAL_AND_UNIQUE_SURE_RESET before passing to the
stationary limiting root. Updating the actual finite prefixed source by its
attained owner cap kills that owner's debt exactly. The terminal gap then
selects a distinct debtor and a full-gap paid row on the literal child.

Thus the compact stationary source-entry seam can be bypassed. The resulting
child enters the checked actual-profile paid-cap trichotomy: quantitative debt
descent or an inert stall. If the child is punishment-floor safe, it also
starts the checked marked exact orbit with a summable semantic port and
positive limiting reach of the unchanged paid suffix. A terminal-gap
deviation can then be copied through every finite prefix with its gain scaled
by that positive reach.

This still does not prove renewal or a uniform equilibrium. No punishment
floor is automatic at the cap-updated child, and neither the quantitative
descent nor inert port regenerates the structured source.

   \[

2. there is a player \(j_n\ne k\), a behavioral deviation

   \[
   U_{j_n}(\chi_n[j_n\leftarrow\zeta_{n,j_n}])
      -U_{j_n}(\chi_n)\ge\Gamma;                              \tag{4}

   \quad\text{or}\quad

\[
 U_{j_n}(\chi_{n,H}[j_n\leftarrow\zeta_{n,j_n}^{\,H}])

\[

Thus the floor-safe arm contains an actual source-matched delayed profitable

## Proof

### 1. Literal cap attainment kills the owner debt

At \(Y_n\), the complete cap of \(k\) is

\[
 B'_{n,k}
 =\max\{Q_k(q_{n,-k}),
         C_k(q_{n,-k};B_{n,k})\}.                             \tag{8}
\]

The first-root theorem proves that the second branch is strictly maximal.
Because \(\tau_n\) is stationary, its complete cap is attained by Quit0 or
Never, including the noncontracting opponent boundary. Therefore
\(\beta_{n,k}\) is an actual behavioral strategy and

\[
 U_k(\chi_n)=B'_{n,k}.                                       \tag{9}
\]

A player's unilateral cap depends only on its opponents' strategies.
Updating that player's own strategy to an attained cap response leaves the
cap value unchanged. Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
 B_k(\chi_n)&=B_k(\rho_n)=B'_{n,k},\\
 d_k(\operatorname{Sem}(\chi_n))
 &=B_k(\chi_n)-U_k(\chi_n)=0.
\end{aligned}
\]

The payoff gain in (9) over \(U_k(\rho_n)\) is exactly
\(d_k(Y_n)\), proving item 1.

No stationarity of \(\rho_n\) or \(\chi_n\) is used. Stationarity is used only
to attain the old tail cap inside \(\beta_{n,k}\).

### 2. The terminal gap moves to a distinct debtor on the literal child

Apply \(\mathcal W\)'s terminal exploitability gap to the actual behavioral
profile \(\chi_n\). Fix one player \(j_n\) and one behavioral deviation
\(\zeta_{n,j_n}\) supplied by that application, so (4) holds.

Equation (3) says that no unilateral deviation by \(k\) has positive gain at
\(\chi_n\). Since \(\Gamma>0\), the selected player must satisfy
\(j_n\ne k\).

For this fixed witness, write its payoff and the prescribed payoff as
expectations of the same pure-time payoff function under the receiving and
source stopping laws. The bounded support-pair averaging lemma selects one
receiving atom and one source atom whose payoff difference is at least the
difference of the two expectations, hence at least \(\Gamma\). Applying
exists_quittingPaidFirstDisagreementRow_of_pureTimePayoff_sub to those two
atoms gives a QuittingPaidFirstDisagreementRow at the exact profile
\(\chi_n\), the same observer \(j_n\), and gain \(\Gamma\).

This is the witness-retaining form of the proof of
HasTerminalExploitabilityGap.exists_supported_pureTimePayoff_sub_at; the
currently checked declaration existentially packages the same choice but does
not expose the original behavioral gain in its conclusion. Thus item 2 is
ordinary mathematics with unrestricted behavioral agency, and its Lean
handoff needs the strengthened simultaneous packaging stated below. No
attainment assumption on \(j_n\)'s cap is used.

### 5. Transport the actual terminal-gap deviation through the marked roots

Fix \(H\). By definition, \(\chi_{n,H}\) is obtained by prefixing the first
\(H\) displayed roots to the unchanged suffix \(\chi_n\). Construct
\(\zeta_{n,j_n}^{\,H}\) recursively. At every one of these prefix dates it
uses the same mixed action as the prescribed \(j_n\)-strategy. Conditional on
joint Continue through all \(H\) roots, it switches to the shifted strategy
\zeta_{n,j_n}\) in \(\chi_n\).

At every root-absorbing history, prescribed and deviating laws coincide.
Only the joint-survival event reaches a different suffix payoff. Repeated use
of the exact root-then-continuation payoff identity therefore multiplies the
original difference by

\[
 R_{n,H}
 =\prod_{t<H}\operatorname{JointContinue}(q_{n,t}),
\]

which proves (6). The PositivePaidSuffixReach field gives
\(R_{n,H}\to\ell_n>0\). Hence eventually
\(R_{n,H}\ge\ell_n/2\), and (4) implies (7).

This completes the theorem.

## The under-punishment arm

If (10) fails, one obtains a literal player \(i\) with

\[
 U_i(\chi_n)<\operatorname{punishmentValue}_i
 \le B_i(\chi_n).                                             \tag{11}
\]

Thus \(i\) has positive complete debt, and the actual-profile terminal-gap
port from item 3 still applies. But the checked pair-base floor-repair theorem
does not apply merely from (11): it requires the underfloor player itself to
Quit surely at a stationary source. The cap child \(\chi_n\) is generally
nonstationary, and the underfloor player selected by (11) need not be sure.

Full normality does not contradict (11). Punishment normality compares the
punishment value with a singleton reward or cap benchmark; it does not assert
that every prescribed payoff of every actual profile lies above punishment.
Thus the exact output in this arm is (5) plus a localized underfloor
coordinate, not a floor-safe orbit or a contradiction to the hard residual.

## Boundary and falsification audit

1. **Cap attainment is essential.** For an arbitrary nonstationary source,
   the complete behavioral cap may be only a supremum. Here it is attained
   because the finite prefix strictly selects Continue into the stationary
   tail \(\tau_n\), whose cap is Quit0 or Never.
2. **Debt zero is exact.** Updating the owner to an attained cap response
   changes no opponent strategy, so its cap remains the same number. There is
   no limiting or approximate equality in (3).
3. **The new debtor is distinct.** The terminal gap cannot be paid by the
   updated owner because that owner's complete debt is zero.
4. **No stationarity is smuggled into the child.** The arbitrary-profile paid
   row and paid-cap port explicitly accept \(\chi_n\). Stationarity is not a
   hypothesis.
5. **Floor safety is separate.** The marked exact orbit is asserted only
   under (10). The underfloor arm does not use a sure-Quit floor repair whose
   hypotheses are absent.
6. **Positive suffix reach is per finite source.** The constant \(\ell_n\)
   may depend on \(n\). No uniform lower bound over the tropical sequence is
   claimed.
7. **A delayed fork is not a return.** Equation (7) retains one profitable
   suffix deviation through a literal exact-prefix orbit. It does not make
   an endpoint semantically close to \(\chi_n\), replenish the original cap
   pin, or regenerate the tropical source.
