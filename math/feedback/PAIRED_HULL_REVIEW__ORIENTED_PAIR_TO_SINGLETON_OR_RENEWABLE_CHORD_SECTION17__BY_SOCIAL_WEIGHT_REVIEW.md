## 1. Moving endpoint identities

At every supplied profile \(X_n\), the marked root is the same pure pair and
the target \(Y_n\) changes only player \(q\)'s action at that row. Therefore,
with \(L_n\) the unconditional reach and \(\Delta>0\) the fixed endpoint gap,

\[
 U_q(Y_n)-U_q(X_n)=L_n\Delta,
 \qquad
 d_q(X_n)=L_n\Delta+R_n,
 \qquad
 d_q(Y_n)=R_n.
\]

Earlier absorption is common to both profiles and the other sure quitter
screens the arbitrary post-tail. The law identities are likewise exact:

\[
 \operatorname{Law}(Y_n)
 =\operatorname{Law}(X_n)+L_n(\delta_{K'}-\delta_K).
\]

For the literal one-row mixture \(H_n^s\), independence is preserved and the
same formula holds with coefficient \(sL_n\). In particular the moving mark
itself—not merely the time-forgetting limiting law—carries \(K'\)-mass
\(sL_n\ge s\lambda\).

The target-excess split is exhaustive after subselection: a nonnegative
sequence has either a positive-liminf subsequence or a subsequence tending to
zero. The same observation handles \(R_n\).

## 2. Complete-cap convexity and the minimum squeeze

For \(i\ne q\), every fixed unrestricted behavioral response payoff is
affine in the one-row mixture of \(q\)'s strategy. Its supremum is therefore
convex. For \(i=q\), the opponent profile is unchanged, so the complete cap
is constant. Prescribed payoff is affine for every coordinate. Hence

\[
 d_i(H_n^s)
 \le(1-s)d_i(X_n)+s d_i(Y_n)
\]

for the unrestricted behavioral debt, not merely a finite-response cap.

On the arm \(D(Y_n)\to D_*\), summing and using the global lower bound gives

\[
 D_*\le D(H_n^s)
 \le(1-s)D(X_n)+sD(Y_n)\longrightarrow D_*.
\]

After joint compactification, both sides of the coordinatewise inequalities
sum to \(D_*\). Every nonnegative coordinate slack is therefore zero:

\[
 d_i(h^s)=(1-s)d_i(x)+s d_i(y).
\]

Since \(d_q(y)=0\), \(d_q(x)=L\Delta>0\), and \(D(y)=D_*>0\),

\[
 \varnothing\ne\operatorname{supp}^+d(y)
 \subsetneq\operatorname{supp}^+d(h^s).
\]

This argument absorbs arbitrary nonmover cap leakage into the minimum-fibre
equality; deleted reach cannot break it.

## 4. Rank scope and remaining outputs

The support drop \(h^s\to y\) is strict and renewable once \(y\) enters the
existing tangent trace: any recursive minimum-fibre child lowers the same
support cardinality. The initial transition from the incoming pair source to
\(h^s\) may use a one-use phase tag. Within this construction the tangent
trace does not return to that incoming phase; its other exits are declared
nonrecursive outputs.

Thus the honest supplied-sequence dispatch is:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\liminf R_n>0
 &\Rightarrow \text{uniformly paid supported row strictly before }t_n,\\
R_n\to0,\ \liminf(D(Y_n)-D_*)>0
 &\Rightarrow \text{literal quantitative off-minimum endpoint},\\
R_n\to0,\ D(Y_n)\to D_*
 &\Rightarrow \text{regenerated minimum child with strict support drop}.
\end{array}
\]

The first output still enters the paid-cap trichotomy with open
debt-descent/inert arms. The second is the universal off-minimum paid waist.
The third is genuine finite-rank progress. Section 17 therefore fixes the
actual-source adapter but does not by itself prove Fin4 uniform equilibrium.

I found no counterexample from mixed roots, earlier absorption, arbitrary
tails, moving marks, or large deleted-reach cap motion.
