## Checks

1. Exact root prefixing contracts target error by joint survival and transports
   complete behavioral debt by the stated opponent-survival estimate. The
   quantitative jump recurrence agrees with the diagonal exact-root semantic
   identity.

   stop or the reached tail response, yielding `d_i+2e_i`. An outsider's
   arbitrary stopping rule is a mixture over its first stopping row and the
   reached tail, so its bound is a maximum, not an `N`-row sum. The
   `max((1-p)d_k,2Mh+e_k)` estimate is correct.
3. Iterating the safe scalar recurrence over a fixed length `L` gives
   `(2L+1)eta + 2M sum h_t`. The displayed `JJ`, `JF`, `FJ`, `FF`, and `JFJF`
   constants follow exactly, and fixed-target terminal acceptance gives every
   finite compatible word.
4. For the all-minus-one table at target `(1,...,1)`, all Continue is an exact
   Bellman/root-Nash self-loop, while every actual terminal payoff lies in
   `[-1,0]`. This is a valid obstruction to inferring executable infinite
   closure from compact exact witness syntax alone.
5. The coordinate block action
   `d -> max(0,beta*d-g)` has the correct exact-root cases: pure Continue,
   interior mixing, and pure Quit. Its iterated composition is the right sharp
   cutoff-debt account; the product-survival criterion is only a sufficient
   simplification.
6. The payoff cutoff coefficient is joint survival, whereas cap erasure is
   playerwise opponent survival plus exercise premium. A positive-survival
   infinite branch therefore really needs an attained diagonal semantic
   continuation. The note labels the Zeno compiler and arbitrary-game carrier
   as proposals, not theorems.
