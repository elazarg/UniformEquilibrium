## Necessary corrections to the finite-capacity conclusion

Under the additional composable-history hypothesis, the numerical argument
is correct: fixed-excess returns occur only finitely often, and an infinite
undercharge tail has an off-minimum cluster at whose limiting cap all Continue
is exact.  The conclusion should nevertheless be weakened in three ways.

1. All-Continue exactness does not imply unique all-Continue.  The correct
   residual is unique all-Continue versus a positive-absorption exact root.
2. Compact semantic/law convergence alone gives a carrier cluster, not an
   actual source-faithful omega descendant.  Actualization requires a closed
   ancestry or summable executable decoder.
3. The marked atom is located at the preceding marked row in the current
   packet, not automatically in the escaped continuation.  Its retention must
   be separately proved.

The positive-root arm gives useful additional structure.  Prefixing the
cluster by a positive-absorption exact cap root gives

\[
  D(Y)=(1-a)D(X)<D(X).
\]

If `D(Y)=D_*`, the checked strict-ray handoff regenerates a same-residual
minimum source.  If `D(Y)>D_*`, this is only a strict off-minimum descent; no
uniform decrement or renewable rank is presently known.  This positive-root
arm is not the tangent-family flat-support-entry hypothesis.

## New exactification obstruction

The natural attempt to turn the C1/C2 approximate charged block into an exact
charged history is false from the presently stated fields.  There is an
explicit Fin4 zero-Never family with:

- global `1/n`-Nash error;
- marked-block reach tending to `exp(-1)`;
- block hazard charge exactly one; and
- parent and child payoff coordinates tending to zero,

such that every exact Nash--Bellman block with the same child and an
asymptotically matching parent has total hazard tending to zero.  Exactness
forces the charged player to Continue everywhere; moving the charge to an
opponent changes the parent payoff by an order-one amount.

Thus rootwise exactification, constrained homotopy, and dynamic exact-root
selection cannot preserve a fixed fraction of the approximate charge using
only the two-cut fields.  The regression has zero minimum debt, so it does not
exclude a theorem that essentially uses positive-minimum hard-residual
provenance.

## Recommended continuation

Do not pursue generic rowwise exactification.  The two viable targets are:

1. instantiate the source-faithful summable macro decoder of `arch/GRAMMAR.md`
   and `arch/ADAPTERS_COMPLETE.md` on the C1/C2 blocks, with one ancestry chain,
   summable aggregate Bellman/Nash seams, preserved reach, and co-realized
   annotations; or
2. prove a Fin4 positive-minimum coercivity theorem forcing an exact charged
   exit, a paid/debt exit, or a renewable rank descent from every order-one
   approximate charged block.

The remaining issue is therefore not persistent-spine consumption.  It is the
source-specific construction of a composable or summably decoded chronology.
