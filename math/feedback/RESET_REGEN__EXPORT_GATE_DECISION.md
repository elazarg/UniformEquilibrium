## Decision

Do not export RESET_REGEN.md.

All three reviews agree that a corrected conditional Zeno normal form is
mathematically sound: along a recursively coherent canonical positive-root
orbit, coordinate debts and total debt scale by the joint Continue product,
positive minimum debt prevents that product from vanishing, inherited atoms
remain nonzero, and the canonical absorption sequence is summable.

The submitted packet does not establish the advertised renewal. Its paid-gain
equality is a conservative annotation chosen internally by one constructor,
not a public theorem about arbitrary regeneration records. The physical paid
gap scales by observer-opponent survival. The note also overstates which ranks
are excluded and which future charge vanishes, and it targets the wrong
near-return consumer.

After these repairs, the surviving normal form substantially duplicates
[CHATGPT_EXTERNAL__NONCOLLAPSING_MAXIMAL_PAID_RESET_ORBIT.md](../notes/CHATGPT_EXTERNAL__NONCOLLAPSING_MAXIMAL_PAID_RESET_ORBIT.md)
and checked maximal-ray results. It does not provide a renewable rank,
admissible charged return, terminal approximants, contradiction, or positive-
gap table. The missing result remains a charge-renewal consumer or another
accepted output of the maintained paid/reset regeneration question.

The sharper block absorption, weighted telescope, logarithmic bound, and
semantic/law displacement estimates are retained in the strengthening review
for later use. No file was added to exports/.
