# Source audit of `DECIDE_CONTROLLER_TESTER`

## Verdict

The normalized-rational core is already supported by checked declarations and
the principal synthesis is mathematically sound:

> For a fixed normalized rational Fin4 table, finite rational interval trees
> form a decidable certificate class which is sound and complete for strict
> positive lower bounds on the unrestricted terminal-exploitability infimum.

The same sources also justify a terminating two-sided procedure at each
positive rational scale and the global recursive-enumerability equivalence for
the existence of a positive-gap Fin4 table.

The note nevertheless exceeds the current theorem surface in two places.  Its
generic verifier is stated for an arbitrary rational table although the actual
single-shell soundness theorem assumes normalization.  Its claimed rational
open cover of the entire real table space consequently also needs a literal
rescaling wrapper.  Both repairs are elementary and appear mathematically
valid, but neither is defined or proved in the inspected sources.  Without the
wrapper, the current normalized certificate centers cannot cover arbitrary
large real reward tables.

The computable-real conclusion for each rational table is a genuine new
synthesis from the checked lower and upper searches.  I found no declaration
stating it.  Its proposed dovetail proof is sound after making normalization
and rescaling explicit.

The note does not decide whether the value is zero, prove Fin4 uniform-payoff
existence, or produce a positive table.

## New synthesis that survives

### Complete positivity certificates for a fixed normalized rational table

For normalized rational `r`, define `Verify(r,N,a,T)` to be the existing
single-shell problem's Boolean tree verifier at threshold `a`.  Then:

- an accepted tree gives `a <= A_N(r) <= eta(r)` by tree soundness and the
  shell lower bridge;
- if `a < eta(r)`, choose `N` with `24/N < eta(r)-a`; the shell bracket gives
  `a < A_N(r)`, and strict-margin search completeness supplies an accepted
  tree.

This proves the advertised equivalence for positive `a`.  It is mostly a
packaging theorem over existing declarations, but it is a useful exact answer
to the effective-positive-certificate intermediate question.

### Uniform computability of the value on rational inputs

Fix a rational accuracy `delta`.  Choose `N` with `24/N < delta`.  Dovetail:

1. accepted lower trees at rational thresholds `a`; and
2. verified rational finite-clock profiles with exploitability below rational
   thresholds `b`.

The shell bracket and rational density ensure some pair satisfies

`a <= eta(r) <= Expl(profile) < b` and `b-a < delta`.

Every acceptance test is rational and finite.  This is a correct computable-
real algorithm.  It does not decide the exact zero predicate.

### Rational-open positive locus after rescaling

Once arbitrary rational centers carry a rescaled normalized certificate, the
`2`-Lipschitz estimate proves that certified balls are contained in the
positive locus, while rational density and strict certificate completeness
show that they cover it.  This is mathematically sound and genuinely stronger
than the current global existential semidecision.  It should be presented as a
new theorem requiring the rescaling wrapper, not as already supplied by the
current normalized verifier.

## Required repairs

### 1. Normalize the verifier theorem literally

As written, the opening theorem says `Verify(r,N,a,T)` for every rational
table `r`.  The actual bridge
`finFourSingleShellLower_le_exploitabilityInf` requires
`|r_i(S)| <= 1`, and the Boolean tree checker does not itself validate that
hypothesis.

Use one of two exact formulations:

1. state the theorem for normalized rational reward codes; or
2. define `M(r) = max(1,max |r_i(S)|)`, verify the normalized table `r/M(r)` at
   threshold `a/M(r)`, and include this preprocessing in `Verify`.

The second formulation gives the arbitrary-rational theorem claimed in the
note, since positive homogeneity is checked.

### 2. Repair the open-cover quantifiers

Equation (29) is not literally justified if the union ranges only over the
currently implemented normalized reward codes: normalized centers cannot
approximate an arbitrary large reward table.  Let the centers `q` be arbitrary
rational tables equipped with the rescaled certificate from repair 1.  Then
the radius `a/4` argument is correct.

### 3. Separate source status from mathematical consequence

The following are not currently named checked declarations in the inspected
source and should not be described as already formalized:

- one generic arbitrary-rational `Verify` relation with the stated theorem;
- uniform computability of `eta(r)` as a computable real; and
- the recursively enumerable rational-open cover of the whole positive
  locus.

They are valid mathematical corollaries after repairs 1--2 and are natural
formalization targets.

## Scope

The result answers the question's useful intermediate request for an exact
positive-output algorithm complete for every positive table.  It does not
answer either terminal alternative in
`questions/QUITTING_CONTROLLER_TESTER_DUALITY.md`: no theorem proves all
values zero, and no concrete accepted positive certificate is supplied.

## Final re-audit after revision

The revision correctly repairs the two substantive scope issues:

- it explicitly defines the arbitrary-rational verifier by normalizing with
  the computable positive rational factor `M(r)` and scaling the threshold by
  `1/M(r)`; and
- its open-cover union now ranges over arbitrary rational centers carrying
  these rescaled certificates.

Its opening status paragraph also accurately distinguishes the checked
normalized ingredients from the three ordinary-mathematics syntheses.  I no
longer see a mathematical normalization or source-status overclaim.

One editorial clarification would make the proof literally unambiguous.  At
the start of Sections 2--4, say that `r` has been renamed from `bar r` and the
threshold from `a/M(r)` to `a`.  Otherwise equations (8)--(19), especially
`Verify(r,N,a,T) => a <= A_N(r)`, visually look like statements about the
arbitrary unnormalized wrapper even though their proof is the normalized
single-shell argument.  The outer theorem is sound because equation (5)
rescales the conclusion; only the local notation is ambiguous.

Likewise, the computability paragraph should describe the effective
acceptance test as “a lower tree verifies at threshold `a`” rather than the
real inequality `a <= A_N(r)` itself.  The surrounding text already makes
this intended meaning clear, so this is not a mathematical defect.

Final verdict: mathematically sound after the stated rescaling repair,
source-status honest, and suitable as a maintained meta result.  The only
remaining changes are the two notation/algorithm-wording clarifications above.
