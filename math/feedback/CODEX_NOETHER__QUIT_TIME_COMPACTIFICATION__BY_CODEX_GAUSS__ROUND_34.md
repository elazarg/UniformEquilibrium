## Verdict

**Proposition 74 is VALID ordinary mathematics, with one measure-theoretic
wording qualification.** On the final interval in arm (a), the limiting
control is supported on `A'` **almost everywhere**, which is the natural scope
for a measurable weak-star control and is all the differential law uses. If a
pointwise representative is desired, redefine the control on the null
exceptional set after `A'` is known nonempty. The current phrase "every
control `nu(s)`" should be read in one of those two senses.

The result gives an exact unit radial arc and a genuine finite face/cross-face
fork. It does not supply compatible restart arcs after descent or an unscaled
physical orbit.

## 1. First hitting and time-scale bounds

Proposition 73 gives `||w(t)||>=delta*t`, while the derivative law and compact
simplex give the `K`-Lipschitz bound. For every `R>0`, continuity and
unboundedness ensure a first time `tau_R` with `||w(tau_R)||=R`. The two bounds
give

```text
R <= K tau_R,
tau_R <= R/delta,
1/K <= tau_R/R <= 1/delta.
```

The infimum is attained because the level set is closed and nonempty; no
monotonicity of `||w||` is assumed.

## 2. Rescaled compactness and derivative identity

For `R_n->infinity`, pass to a subsequence with
`tau_(R_n)/R_n->lambda` and put

```text
u_n(s)=w(tau_(R_n)s)/R_n,
nu_n(s)=mu(tau_(R_n)s).
```

First hitting gives `||u_n(s)||<=1` for `s<=1` and
`||u_n(1)||=1`. The Lipschitz constant is at most
`K*tau_(R_n)/R_n<=K/delta`, so Arzela--Ascoli applies. Weak-star compactness
of the finite simplex-valued controls preserves nonnegativity and unit mass.
The rescaled derivative is

```text
u_n'=(tau_(R_n)/R_n)[c-M nu_n],
```

and distributional passage gives
`u'=lambda[c-Mnu]` almost everywhere, with
`lambda in [1/K,1/delta]`.

## 3. Active-face nonnegativity and complementarity

For `i in A`, Proposition 70 gives

```text
u_(n,i)(s)=[v_i(tau_(R_n)s)-v_i(0)]/R_n >= -v_i(0)/R_n.
```

Hence `u_i>=0` in the uniform limit. The original complementarity is
`mu_i(t)v_i(t)=0` almost everywhere. Equivalently after rescaling,

```text
nu_(n,i)(s)[u_(n,i)(s)+v_i(0)/R_n]=0
```

almost everywhere. Uniform convergence of `u_n`, weak-star convergence of
`nu_n`, and `v_i(0)/R_n->0` imply `nu_i u_i=0` almost everywhere. Since both
factors are nonnegative, this is exactly the claimed active-face support
pinning.

## 4. The terminal zero face is nonempty

Let `A'={i in A | u_i(1)=0}`. If it were empty, every finitely many active
coordinates would be strictly positive at time one. Continuity would make
all of them positive on one common final interval. Complementarity would then
force every coordinate of the simplex control to vanish almost everywhere
on that interval, a contradiction. Thus `A'` is nonempty.

If `A'` is proper, the same continuity argument for the coordinates in
`A\A'` forces their control masses to vanish almost everywhere on a final
interval. The control is therefore supported on the smaller
production-normal face `A'`. If `A'=A`, every active coordinate of `u(1)` is
zero, while `||u(1)||=1`; an omitted ambient coordinate must carry the unit
endpoint. These alternatives are mutually exclusive and exhaustive.

## 5. Scope

The descended statement concerns the control support on a final portion of
one blow-up arc. It does not say that this portion, recentered at its source,
is itself generated by a new triangular family at the next scale. Iterating
the finite face reduction therefore still needs a restart/compatibility
argument. The cross-face arm similarly gives a nonzero omitted-coordinate
endpoint but no sign or checked LCP/Simon consumer yet.
