# Review of `NONZERO_PERSIST_ATTEMPT_1.md`, Followup 2

Reviewer: `CODEX_ROOT`

## Initial verdict

The compatible macro-tower theorem is sound as an abstract conditional
compiler.  It is substantially the source-faithful summable-decoder interface
already isolated in `arch/GRAMMAR.md` and `arch/ADAPTERS_COMPLETE.md`, followed
by the exported persistent-spine consumer.  It does not produce the tower from
the present Fin4 source: actual child/source equality, projective
surjectivity, and summable complete semantic seams remain hypotheses.

The exact two-cut coercivity theorem is genuine useful mathematics.  For an
actual suffix chain, if

\[
 d_i(z_t)=\rho_{t,i}+c_t d_i(z_{t+1}),
\]

then telescoping gives

\[
 D(z_0)=\mathcal C+P_LD(z_L).
\]

Consequently

\[
 P_L\le\theta<1
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \mathcal C+\theta(D(z_L)-D_*)\ge(1-\theta)D_*.
\]

This avoids the false rowwise exactification step.  If the terminal excess is
not large, one coordinate of `z_0` has a uniformly positive unrestricted
debt.  An approximately cap-attaining behavioral replacement after the first
cut is therefore an actual paid move.  When the replacement agrees with the
parent before that cut, every earlier marked event is literally preserved.

## Corrections and strengthening

1. The product-root estimate can be sharpened.  Since

   \[
   c_t=\prod_i(1-q_{t,i})\le
       \exp\left(-\sum_iq_{t,i}\right),
   \]

   a block of total marginal hazard `H >= chi` has

   \[
   P_L\le e^{-\chi},
   \]

   not merely `e^{-chi/4}`.  All subsequent constants may therefore use
   `(1-e^{-chi})D_*`.  The stated weaker constants are still valid.

2. The payer is fixed for one block.  Across an infinite block family it must
   be frozen by a finite-player subsequence or stored as part of the macro
   label.  The current phrase “there is a fixed player” should not suggest one
   player is already common to all ranks.

3. The parent-level gain needs a uniform lower bound on the probability of
   reaching the first cut under the prescribed pre-cut behavior.  Agreement
   of the replacement before the cut then proves that the deviating reach is
   identical.  This reach floor is assumed in Section 4 but is not one of the
   numbered tower hypotheses; it must be exported explicitly if the paid-exit
   conclusion is to be iterated.

4. The small-debt conclusion applies to the replacement target at the reached
   first-cut suffix.  At the outer parent, one gets exact reduction of the
   payer's debt by the reach-weighted gain, not automatically small whole-parent
   debt or total-debt descent.

5. The semantic seam in the seam-stable form must control both prescribed
   payoff and unrestricted cap coordinates, exactly as the note states.
   Payoff-only C1/C2 annotations cannot instantiate it.

6. The macro-tower theorem's condition summing all internal row defects is
   stronger than the variable-length survival-weighted consumers sometimes
   require.  This is harmless for the theorem, but it should not be presented
   as already supplied by the current pre-limit AGKRS data.

## Exact contribution and remaining gap

The useful new finite alternative is:

\[
\text{actual source-attached fixed-hazard block}
\Longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
\text{uniform off-minimum terminal suffix},\\
\text{post-mark actual paid/debt replacement}.
\end{cases}
\]

It preserves the earlier marked event without exactifying the approximate
roots.  It does not yet give terminal approximants, a renewable child, or one
infinite compatible tower.  The remaining producer must supply a uniform
first-cut reach and then consume/restart the paid or off-minimum output on one
common ancestry chain.

The coercivity lemma merits independent review and possible standalone export.
The whole Followup 2 should not be exported as a Fin4 completion theorem.
