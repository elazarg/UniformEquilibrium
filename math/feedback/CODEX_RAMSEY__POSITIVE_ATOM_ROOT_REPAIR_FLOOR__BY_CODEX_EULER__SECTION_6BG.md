# Review of Section 6BG: same-root Tier-II rigidity

Reviewer: `CODEX_EULER`

## Claim reviewed

Section 6BG claims that the proposed Tier-II anchor fields, once composed with
the Tier-I persistent literal roots into the checked bounded summable-seam
chain, already contradict positive minimum.  The claimed estimate is

```text
D_* <= 2*|I|*eta,
```

without root Nash and without the separately requested all-behavior
implementation field 6.

## Verdict

**PASS, with one scope qualification that the section already essentially
observes.**  The contradiction applies to fields 1--5 after they have been
realized as the fully typed same-root packet system and flattened to a
`QuittingBoundedSeamChain` with the displayed survival and total-summability
hypotheses.  It is not a theorem about five informal local bullets in the
absence of bounded annotations, nonnegative candidate debt, persistent
literal-root survival, or an actually summable selected schedule.

Under the maintained question's intended composition through
`QuittingBudgetStablePacketSystem`, those extra hypotheses are exactly the
ones already supplied by the packet type, Tier I, and the checked schedule
selector.  Therefore the qualification does not save the stated Tier-II
same-root architecture.


## Why the actual pair is genuinely behavioral

The chain defines

```text
actualPair(t)
  = quittingTerminalSemanticPair reward
      (quittingRootSequenceProfile reward chain.roots t).
```

Thus `actualPair(0)` is the semantic pair of the literal infinite product-root
profile executed by the chain.  Its cap coordinate is the repository's
`quittingContinuationBestResponseValue`, hence already takes the supremum over
the player's unrestricted unilateral behavioral strategies.

The checked proof first establishes

```text
|actual.U-candidate.U| <= prescribed-tsum
```

from joint-survival uniqueness and

```text
|actual.B-candidate.B| <= cap-tsum
```

from player-deleted-survival uniqueness.  Subtraction gives the coordinate
debt estimate.  Summing over players yields exactly the factor
`2*|I|*eta`: one `eta` is the initial candidate debt allowance and one is the
combined prescribed-plus-cap seam allowance.

No additional implementation relation between a reached port and the
candidate can change this comparison, because it is already made to the
literal behavioral profile generated by the same roots.

## Positive-minimum specialization

Let `family` be a `QuittingPositiveMinimumDebtTangentFamily` and set

```text
D_* = quittingTerminalSemanticDebtSum family.base.
```

Every literal semantic pair is in the range of
`quittingTerminalSemanticPair`, hence in
`quittingTerminalSemanticCarrier reward`.  Therefore
`family.base_minimum` supplies

```text
D_* <= debtSum(Sem(sigma))
```

for every behavioral profile `sigma`.  This is exactly the theorem's `hgap`.
The field `family.base_positive` gives `D_*>0`; in particular the finite player
type is nonempty, so `|I|>0`.

Choosing

```text
eta = D_*/(4*|I|)
```

gives `eta>0`, and the checked estimate becomes

```text
D_* <= D_*/2,
```

contradicting `D_*>0`.  The constants and the use of the compactified carrier
are exact.

## Match to the maintained Tier-II question

`QuittingBudgetStablePacketSystem.exists_summableSeamSource_of_seed` selects a
divergent-scale, summable-cost schedule and flattens the exact packet arrays.
Its two fixed positive hazard streams give joint and every player-deleted
survival.  Its packet type includes exact prefix steps, candidate debt
nonnegativity, and uniform prescribed/debt bounds.  From a seed with
coordinate debt at most `eta`, it produces:

```text
* prescribed seam tsum <= eta;
* total two-coordinate seam tsum <= eta;
* the required literal-root survival fields.
```

The flat total seam is pointwise `prescribedSeam+capSeam`.  Its summability
therefore gives summability of the cap seam, and `tsum_add` identifies its
`tsum` with `prescribed-tsum+cap-tsum`.  Thus the total-seam bound supplies
the rigidity theorem's combined bound with the same `eta`, not `2*eta`.
Consequently, the maintained Tier-II fields 1--5, interpreted as
the data required to form this packet system on the Tier-I roots, are already
inconsistent with positive minimum for arbitrary small seed accuracy.

Item 6 is therefore not a remaining independent repair of this architecture.
It could matter only after changing some earlier premise: for example, using
different literal roots/profile, abandoning arbitrary-small candidate debt,
or abandoning a summably vanishing total seam.

## Required status correction

The current question should not continue to list a same-root, summable-seam,
arbitrary-small-debt anchor adapter as an open positive Tier-II target whose
only missing field is all-behavior implementation.  Once fields 1--5 are
supplied in the typed composable form, checked global rigidity already closes
the positive-minimum branch by contradiction.

The honest alternatives are:

```text
1. an independent solved-game disjunct;
2. an adapter changing the literal root profile to which semantics are
   compared; or
3. a different compiler architecture without arbitrary-small seed debt or
   summable total seam.
```

This is a question/interface correction based on an existing checked theorem,
not a new conjecture-level result or an export candidate.
