# Independent review: weighted aggregate-surplus chamber

Reviewer: SOCIAL_WEIGHT_REVIEW

Verdict: **PASS for every stated mathematical claim; strengthen before
export.**

The weighted-minimum theorem, reward-moment upper bound, zero-debt
consequence, subset discussion, and strict-positive cone alternative are all
correct.  I found no counterexample to the stated results.  Two additions
would make an export materially sharper: the common-outcome version of the
weighted inequality, and a nonnegative-costate theorem at the ordinary global
minimum which unifies the weighted chamber with the checked subset chamber.

## Claim checked

For a finite player type of cardinality at least two, a strictly positive
costate \(\theta\), and a carrier minimizer of

\[
 m_\theta=\sum_i\theta_i(B_i-U_i),
\]

the note claims

\[
 A_\theta+(n-1)m_\theta
 \leq \theta\mathbin\cdot U
 \leq R_\theta,
\]

where \(A_\theta=\theta\mathbin\cdot s\) and the maximum defining
\(R_\theta\) includes the Never reward zero.  It concludes that
\(R_\theta\leq A_\theta\) forces zero terminal semantic debt and hence a
uniform-equilibrium payoff.  It also gives the exact strict-positive dual
alternative

\[
 \exists\theta>0:\quad \theta\mathbin\cdot s\geq0,
 \quad\theta\mathbin\cdot(r(S)-s)\leq0\quad(\varnothing\ne S)
\]

versus a nonzero nonnegative vector in the cone generated by
\(-s\) and the vectors \(r(S)-s\).

## Verification of the weighted theorem

The named checked input is exactly strong enough.  In
`TerminalSemanticWeightedAuxiliaryNashBudget.lean`,
`minimumTerminalSemantic_weightedSingletonMargin` says, at the displayed
strictly positive weighted minimum,

\[
 m_\theta\leq\theta_i(B_i-s_i)
\]

for every player.  Summing gives

\[
 nm_\theta\leq\theta\mathbin\cdot(B-s)
 =m_\theta+\theta\mathbin\cdot(U-s),
\]

which is precisely the left side of the claimed sandwich.  No unweighted
minimality is being smuggled into this step.

The right side uses only
`quittingTerminalSemanticCarrier_prescribed_mem_rewardMomentSet` from
`TerminalSemanticMoment.lean`.  The representing mass is a probability mass
on the finite outcomes `none` and `some S`; `none` has reward zero.  Therefore
every weighted outcome reward is at most

\[
 \max\bigl(0,\max_{S\ne\varnothing}\theta\mathbin\cdot r(S)\bigr),
\]

and averaging proves the upper bound.  Full law realizability is not used.
The outer maximum with zero is necessary, as the note's negative-reward
example correctly shows.

If the weighted minimum is zero, strict positivity of every \(\theta_i\)
and carrier debt nonnegativity force every debt coordinate to vanish.  The
same carrier point is then an unweighted global minimum because all carrier
total debts are nonnegative.  For \(n\geq2\), the player type is nonempty, so
`exists_uniformEquilibriumPayoff_of_hasZeroMinimumTerminalSemanticDebt` in
`PositiveMinimumSemanticDebt.lean` applies.  The note does not claim
attainment by one exact profile, and no such claim is needed.

The positive-branch division by \(n-1\), the positive-part unconditional
bound, and the one-player warning are all correct.

## Common-outcome strengthening

The export should include the sharper finite-table certificate

\[
 \boxed{
 \exists\omega\in\{\mathrm{Never}\}\cup
   \{S:S\ne\varnothing\}:\quad
 (n-1)m_\theta
 \leq \theta\mathbin\cdot(r(\omega)-s).}
\]

Moreover, \(\omega\) can be selected with positive mass in one reward-moment
representation of \(U\).  Indeed,

\[
 (n-1)m_\theta\leq\theta\mathbin\cdot(U-s)
 =\sum_\omega\mu(\omega)
   \theta\mathbin\cdot(r(\omega)-s),
\]

so a positive-mass summand is at least its average.  This is the exact
weighted analogue of
`exists_terminalOutcome_subset_singletonSurplus_ge_minimumDebt` and its
support-sensitive companion.  A narrow search found no existing declaration
with an arbitrary positive costate and this common-outcome conclusion.

## Stronger nonnegative-costate theorem

There is a cleaner conjecture-facing theorem that does not require a weighted
minimum.  Let \(z=(U,B)\) be an ordinary positive global minimum, put
\(D=\sum_i(B_i-U_i)\), and let \(\theta_i\geq0\).  Define

\[
 T=\sum_i\theta_i,
 \qquad M=\max_i\theta_i.
\]

The checked unweighted singleton margin gives \(D\leq B_i-s_i\) for every
player.  Multiplication by \(\theta_i\) and summation give

\[
 TD\leq\theta\mathbin\cdot(U-s)+
       \sum_i\theta_i d_i
 \leq\theta\mathbin\cdot(U-s)+MD.
\]

Hence

\[
 \boxed{(T-M)D\leq\theta\mathbin\cdot(U-s).}
\]

The same reward-moment argument selects a positive-mass common outcome
\(\omega\) satisfying

\[
 (T-M)D\leq\theta\mathbin\cdot(r(\omega)-s).
\]

If at least two coordinates of \(\theta\) are positive, then \(T-M>0\).
Consequently the same chamber condition \(R_\theta\leq A_\theta\) forces
\(D=0\), even when some costate coordinates vanish.  Quantitatively, on the
positive branch,

\[
 D\leq\frac{R_\theta-A_\theta}{T-M}.
\]

This one theorem contains both previously separated cases:

* \(\theta=\mathbf1\) gives coefficient \(n-1\);
* \(\theta=\mathbf1_J\) gives coefficient \(|J|-1\), exactly the checked
  subset chamber.

Thus the note is right that zero weights cannot simply be inserted into
`minimumTerminalSemantic_weightedSingletonMargin`; however, zero weights are
valid through this different proof at the unweighted minimum.  I recommend
that an export state both results: the sharp weighted-objective theorem and
the unifying ordinary-minimum costate theorem.

## Exact cone alternative

The signs in the note are correct.  The chamber system is equivalently

\[
 \theta>0,\qquad
 \theta\mathbin\cdot(-s)\leq0,\qquad
 \theta\mathbin\cdot(r(S)-s)\leq0.
\]

Let \(C\) be the finitely generated cone of those displayed generators and
let \(P=\mathbb R^I_{\geq0}\).  If a nonzero \(v\in C\cap P\) exists, every
strictly positive \(\theta\) has \(\theta\mathbin\cdot v>0\), contradicting
the polar inequalities.  Conversely, if \(C\cap P=\{0\}\), the finitely
generated cone \(C\) is closed and is disjoint from the compact simplex
\(\Delta=\{p\in P:\sum_i p_i=1\}\).  Strong separation produces a functional
nonpositive on \(C\) and strictly positive on \(\Delta\).  Conicity forces
the upper bound on \(C\) to be zero, while positivity on every simplex vertex
forces every coordinate of the functional to be strictly positive.  This is
exactly the first arm.  The two arms are exclusive.

The cone statement characterizes failure of the **strictly positive**
costate class.  If the stronger nonnegative costate theorem is adopted, its
failure certificates are face-dependent and should not be conflated with
this single strict-positive alternative.

## Novelty and exact examples

The note correctly identifies its equal-weight theorem as already checked in
stronger common-outcome form by
`minimumTerminalSemantic_subset_singletonSurplus` and
`exists_terminalOutcome_subset_singletonSurplus_ge_minimumDebt`.  The named
all-player social-sign attainment theorem has materially different
hypotheses and does not subsume the mixed-singleton example.

The note's second example proves strict improvement over the all-player
equal-weight test, but it is still caught by several existing subset tests.
It should not be used to suggest improvement over the complete subset
family.  Here is an exact Fin4 example showing that arbitrary weights really
do improve on **every** subset indicator.

Take weights \(\theta=(1,2,3,4)\), put every own singleton vector equal to
zero, and hence put \(s=0\).  For each nonsingleton coalition \(S\), let
\(\ell\) and \(h\) be respectively its least- and greatest-weight members,
and define

\[
 r_\ell(S)=\theta_h,\qquad
 r_h(S)=-\theta_\ell,\qquad
 r_k(S)=0\quad(k\notin\{\ell,h\}).
\]

Give singleton coalitions the zero reward vector.  Then
\(\theta\mathbin\cdot r(S)=0\) for every outcome, so the weighted chamber
holds with equality.  On the other hand, for every player subset \(J\) with
\(|J|\geq2\), use the terminal coalition \(S=J\).  Its \(J\)-social reward is

\[
 \sum_{i\in J}r_i(J)=\theta_h-\theta_\ell>0,
\]

while \(A_J=0\).  Thus every checked subset-indicator chamber fails.  This
establishes genuine novelty of the arbitrary-costate closure, not merely of
its quantitative weighted-minimum formulation.

## Required changes before export

There is no mathematical repair to the statements already in the note.
Before export I recommend:

1. add the positive-support common-outcome weighted certificate;
2. add the ordinary-minimum nonnegative-costate theorem, explaining that it
   unifies the all-player and subset chambers;
3. either add the Fin4 example above or explicitly say that the existing
   weighted example separates only from the all-player equal-weight test;
4. retain the strict-positive Gordan alternative, but label its scope so it
   is not mistaken for the dual of the enlarged zero-allowing costate class.

With those strengthening edits, the result is suitable for the export gate.
