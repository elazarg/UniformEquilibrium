## 1. Exact prefix-orbit closure

For a raw decorated datum

\[
 x=((z,\nu),(w,\omega),m,g)
\]

and a fixed product root \(q\) of joint Continue mass \(c\), the proposed map

\[
 \Phi_q(x)=((T_qz,P_q\nu),(w,\omega),cm,cg)
\]

is exactly the decoration of the literal profile obtained by placing \(q\)
before the raw source and shifting the mark by one.

The whole semantic prefix and terminal-law prefix are continuous. The tail
coordinates are unchanged. Marked stage mass scales by \(c\), and the
specific one-date endpoint payoff difference scales by \(c\), because the
source and its unilateral target copy the same new root and differ only at
the shifted marked date.

If \(\mathcal R\) contains every finite product-root prefix of every member
of the original cofinal family, then

\[
 \Phi_q(\mathcal R)\subseteq\mathcal R.
\]

Continuity therefore gives

\[
 \Phi_q(\overline{\mathcal R})
 \subseteq\overline{\mathcal R}.
\]

This remains true when \(q\) is not Nash, or even approximately Nash, at any
raw approximant. Exactness is not part of orbit membership.

## 2. Compact slice and minimizer

The ambient product is compact:

* both whole and tail semantic/law coordinates lie in the checked compact
  joint carrier;
* \(m\in[0,1]\); and
* \(g\) lies in a fixed bounded interval.

Hence \(\mathcal K=\overline{\mathcal R}\) is compact. The tail-minimum
condition and the two weak normalized inequalities are closed because total
semantic debt is continuous and \(D_*>0\). The initial forced-pair family has
a simultaneous decorated cluster with positive limiting mass and gain, so
strictly smaller positive thresholds make the slice nonempty.

This correctly avoids the circular approximate-infimum step. Debt actually
attains a minimum on the decorated slice.

## 3. Finite-source density loss and reconciliation with the alternative construction

At a finite raw approximant, a root which is exact only at the limit has
total defect \(E_n\ge0\). The exact finite account is

\[
 D(q*X_n)=cD(X_n)+E_n,\qquad
 m(q*X_n)=cm_n,\qquad
 g(q*X_n)=cg_n.
\]

Therefore a finite source saturating \(m_n=\theta D(X_n)\) leaves the
pointwise weak-density class whenever \(E_n>0\):

\[
 {cm_n\over cD(X_n)+E_n}<\theta.
\]

For instance \(D=1\), \(c=1/2\), \(m=\theta\), and \(E_n=1/n\) violate the
finite inequality at every rank although the density tends to \(\theta\).
One fresh exact prefix cannot restore the lost ratio because it scales both
numerator and denominator equally.

This does not contradict the reviewed proof. Its density slice lives on the
decorated **limit carrier**. Literal \(q\)-prefixes of the raw approximants
prove that \(\Phi_q(x)\in\mathcal K\); exactness of \(q\) at the limiting cap
then gives

\[
 D(T_qz)=cD(z)
\]

with no error term. Thus normalized densities are exactly invariant at the
carrier point even though no finite approximant stays on the boundary slice.

My independent limiting-source construction packages the same fact by
storing tuples \((z,m,g)\) which admit cofinal actual witnesses and proving
sequential closure by a diagonal selection. The reviewed construction stores
the extra tail joint point and makes closure automatic by using the orbit
closure under **all** prefixes. The two constructions are mathematically
compatible; the reviewed one is preferable for formalization.

## 4. Elimination of every positive-absorption exact root

Let \(x_0\) minimize whole debt \(\overline D>0\) in the slice, and let \(q\)
be any exact cap--Nash root at its whole cap. Exact cap-prefix scaling and
slice invariance give

\[
 \overline D
 \le D(T_qz_0)
 =c\overline D
 \le\overline D.
\]

Therefore \(c=1\). A product root has joint Continue mass one exactly when
every player Continues surely. Finite root-Nash existence supplies at least
one exact root, so the complete exact correspondence is

\[
 \operatorname{Nash}(z_0^B)=\{\mathbf C\}.
\]

This proves more than exclusion of the particular support-entry root
selected from the original maximal ray: it excludes every positive-
absorption exact root at the decorated minimizer.

## 5. Probability and all-behavior audit

The debt coordinate is the unrestricted terminal-semantic cap minus
prescribed payoff. Exact cap-prefix scaling is the checked all-behavior
identity, not a stationary-deviation approximation.

The marked paid gain is the payoff of one complete unilateral behavioral
replacement which copies the source before the mark and changes the selected
endpoint at the pure pair. Arbitrary earlier absorption cancels in the payoff
difference. The post-mark tail is unreachable after either endpoint because
the marked coalition remains nonempty. Never and arbitrarily late stopping
remain included in the cap coordinates of \(z\) and \(w\).

No stopping-law compactness or behavioral realization of the carrier
minimizer is assumed. Compactness is applied only to finite-dimensional
semantic/law and scalar decorations; actual raw profiles remain available as
an approximating sequence.

The compact minimizer core is generic for any finite player type:

> Fix a source-attached family of actual profiles with one fixed pure
> nonsingleton marked coalition, fixed endpoint labels, a minimum-return tail,
> and positive limiting marked-mass and paid-gain densities. Form the compact
> closure of all arbitrary finite product-root prefixes, decorated by the
> whole joint semantic/law point, post-mark joint semantic/law point, marked
> mass, and paid gain. On every nonempty closed slice defined by lower bounds
> for mass/debt and gain/debt, total debt has a minimizer whose exact cap--Nash
> correspondence is uniquely all Continue.

The conjecture-facing Fin4 corollary is:

> Every strict canonical forced-pair ray stall yields either a
> whole-source-return concentrated collision packet and the checked
> three-role/limit-chord output, or a source-attached off-minimum normalized-
> passport minimizer whose exact cap--Nash root is uniquely all Continue.

This corollary strictly replaces the prior

\[
\text{unique all Continue}\ \lor\ \text{support entry}
\]

reduction once the minor source/consumer naming and boundary-test repairs
listed above are incorporated.

## 9. Scope

The theorem does not:

* prove the decorated minimizer is realized by one behavioral profile;
* preserve cap exactness on raw approximants;
* keep finite actualizers on the exact boundary-density slice;
* produce a uniform-equilibrium payoff in the off-minimum arm; or
* consume the remaining unique-all-Continue normalized-passport minimizer.

Those nonclaims are essential. The result is a compact source-provenance
closure and strict branch elimination, not a terminal equilibrium theorem.
