## Exact conjugacy and a quantitative bridge bound

Write z_j=E_j/G_j for observer caps and fix one observer a. At g=0 the
forward map of a run with label i is

    z_j^+=(z_j-(1-theta)z_i)/(1-(1-theta)z_i).

Thus 1-z_a^+=(1-z_a)/(1-(1-theta)z_i). With
sigma=z_a/(1-z_a), tau_j=(z_j-z_a)/(1-z_a), the map is exactly

    sigma^+=theta sigma-(1-theta)tau_i,  tau^+=tau.

The pivot cap is fixed. The forward pivot prescribed payoff u and joint
survival nu are both divided by 1-(1-theta)z_i, so u/(1-z_a) and
nu/(1-z_a) are fixed as well. These identities require no cap sign assumption
and hold at nonzero small cap data; the coordinate change is uniformly
regular near zero. Center the last coordinate at its tail limit one.

Let A_k denote these exact linear zero-g maps. For an arbitrary sequence,

    sigma_n=(product theta_k)sigma_0
      -sum_k [(1-theta_k) product_(l>k) theta_l] tau_(i_k).

The coefficients in the sum are nonnegative and sum to
1-product theta_k <=1. Consequently every subproduct of the A_k has a
common operator bound M, independent of the number of runs and even at
theta=0. This supplies an explicit bound rather than an assumption about
products of individually bounded matrices.

The exact full maps are A_k y+g_k R_k(y,g_k), with R and its derivatives
bounded on one fixed small neighborhood, uniformly in the compact run
parameters. Their derivatives along the actual tail satisfy
B_k=A_k+Delta_k, norm(Delta_k)<=C g_k. Duhamel expansion and discrete
Gronwall therefore give, for every bridge,

    norm(B_n...B_1) <= M exp(M C sum g_k),
    norm(B_n...B_1-A_n...A_1)
      <= M^2 C exp(M C sum g_k) sum g_k.

Only forward products are used; no inverse across a long run or bridge is
claimed. A run mass g is at most the sum of its observer date hazards.
Positive original Never probability implies summable date hazards, so the
remaining sum of g tends to zero. All conditional tail caps and prescribed
