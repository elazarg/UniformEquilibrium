# Review of `WITNESS_1.md`

**Reviewer:** CODEX_STRENGTHEN  
**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Verdict:** the matrix algebra, reward formulas, fully mixed cap-root family,
and unique limiting root are correct.  The generic pencil reduction is mostly
already contained in the weighted-cocycle reduction, with one potentially
useful boundary strengthening.  The principal mandatory correction is
orientation: recurrence (23) is an outward exact-prefix construction ray.
Its literal chronological edges point in the reverse direction, and the note
does not supply one infinite forward Nash--Bellman chronology or any
terminal-semantic carrier seed.

## 1. Claim checked

The note makes four separable claims.

1. Eventual positive finite current hazard in every coordinate strengthens
   the normalized omega relation from complementarity to the equality
   `M tau_n + rho_n C c_n = 0`.
2. Renewal then gives the fixed matrix-pencil recurrence and, when `C` is
   invertible, a nonnegative stable eigenmode.
3. The displayed rational matrices and quadratic-coalition reward formula
   produce exact fully mixed roots and the scalar recurrence (23).
4. This is presented as an exact Zeno cap chronology whose only missing datum
   is a source-attached terminal-law seed.

Claims 1--3 survive with qualifications below.  Claim 4 is too strong.

## 3. Full-support equality

### 3.1 The proposed strengthening is valid

Fix one omega offset and use its actual source-coordinate subsequence.  The
assumed eventual positivity of every finite current normalized hazard makes
the checked finite complementarity identity force

\[
 \operatorname{endpointSlack}(t,i)=0
\]

at all sufficiently late selected dates.  The ballistic source supplies a
positive lower bound on the renewal ratio.  Passing the checked normalized
endpoint decomposition, tail-normalized cap flow, and collision-error limit
along the same source subsequence therefore gives `W_{n,i}=0`, even if the
limiting normalized current coordinate is zero.  Repeating this for the
finite set of players and each fixed integer offset proves (5).

This should be stated as ordinary mathematics.  A Lean wrapper must retain
the original eventual-positivity hypothesis explicitly: the structure
`FinFourUniformlyBallisticNormalizedSource` stores the ratio floor and full
binding, but not eventual positivity.

### 3.2 What is redundant

If one instead assumes a uniform positive lower bound on every *omega*
current coordinate, then (5) is immediate from the already checked
`current_work_eq_zero`.  Moreover, the theorem in
`GATE_STRENGTHENER__BALLISTIC_WEIGHTED_COCYCLE_COLLAPSE.md` already proves the
stronger generic alternative, without assuming `C` invertible:

\[
 \text{one omega-current coordinate tends to zero}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \exists r\in[\eta,1],\ y>0:\ (M+rC)y=0.
\]

The new equality is useful only in the boundary arm: it still yields a
descriptor trajectory when some omega coordinates vanish.  Its resulting
eigenvector may then have proper support, so it does not improve the existing
strictly positive fixed-mode arm.

## 4. Matrix-pencil step

Equations (6), (9), and (10) are algebraically correct:

\[
 (M+C)\tau_n=(1-\rho_n)C\tau_{n+1},
 \qquad T\tau_n=(1-\rho_n)\tau_{n+1}.
\]

For the closed cone generated by the `tau_n`, `T` preserves the cone and is
an `l1` contraction there.  If `T` kills a nonzero cone vector, that vector
already gives eigenvalue zero.  Otherwise normalization of `Tx` on the
compact simplex section of the cone gives a fixed ray and hence

\[
 (M+(1-\lambda)C)x=0,
 \qquad 0\le\lambda\le1-\eta.
\]

Two wording corrections are needed.

* The generic eigenvector is nonnegative and nonzero, not necessarily
  strictly positive.  Proper support is a genuine possible output.
* If an omega ratio equals one, the scale recursion
  `a_{n+1}=(1-rho_n)a_n` reaches zero.  It is then a nonnegative trajectory
  with a zero-eigenvalue branch, not a positive trajectory at every index.

## 5. Exact audit of the rational table

The displayed calculations are correct.

* The matrix `C` has determinant `17`, every row sum is `-2`, and every
  symmetric off-diagonal sum is strictly negative.
* For the uniform vector `x`, `Mx=x` and `Cx=-2x`, hence
  `(M+C/2)x=0` and `C^{-1}(M+C)x=x/2`.
* Formula (18) gives exactly the advertised singleton and collision matrices.
* Direct product expansion gives

  \[
  G_i(q,b)=(Cq)_i-b_i\prod_{k\ne i}(1-q_k)
  \]

  and

  \[
  F_i(s+b,q)-s_i=
  \Bigl(\prod_k(1-q_k)\Bigr)b_i+(Mq)_i+q_i(Cq)_i.
  \]

* With `q_i=a/4` and the displayed scalar `b(a)`, every endpoint difference
  is zero and the successor cap is exactly `s-a/4`.
* The scalar map in (23) has a unique solution `a_{t+1}` in `(0,1)` for each
  `a_t` in `(0,1)`, with `a_{t+1}<a_t/2`; its construction-direction hazard
  sum is finite.

The unique-limit-root proof is also correct.  If at least two coordinates of
`q` are positive, exact Quit-support complementarity would give
`q^T C q>=0`, while the negative symmetric pair sums give `q^T C q<0`.  If
exactly one coordinate is positive, its designated collider continues with
strictly positive endpoint difference.  Thus only all Continue is exact at
cap `s`.

## 6. Mandatory orientation correction

Let

\[
 B_t=s+b(a_t)\mathbf 1.
\]

The verified identities say

\[
 q(a_t)\text{ is exact against }B_t,
 \qquad
 F_{q(a_t)}(B_t)=B_{t+1}.
\]

Thus `B_{t+1}` is obtained by **prefixing** `q(a_t)` before the tail cap
`B_t`.  The literal chronological edge is

\[
 \boxed{B_{t+1}\longrightarrow B_t,}
\]

not `B_t -> B_{t+1}`.  Increasing `t` is the outward prefix-construction
direction toward the inert boundary.  For each `N` it gives a valid finite
chronological block

\[
 B_N\to B_{N-1}\to\cdots\to B_0,
\]

but these blocks start at different caps.  At the limit cap `s`, the unique
root is all Continue, so the construction does not provide a positive first
edge leaving `s`.  Reversing the scalar recurrence makes the hazards grow
rather than producing the claimed summable forward tail.

Consequently the phrases “exact fully mixed Zeno orbit,” “cap chronology,”
and “what is missing is not a Nash--Bellman chronology” must be narrowed.
The surviving object is an **exact outward cap-prefix tower** or an
arbitrarily deep family of finite exact Nash--Bellman blocks with moving
initial caps.  It is not one infinite forward chronological root sequence.

## 7. Missing semantic source

The reward table is a genuine finite quitting table, but the draft defines
only roots and declared caps.  It supplies no prescribed payoff coordinate,
behavioral profile, terminal outcome law, membership in
`quittingTerminalSemanticCarrier`, or proof that any `B_t` is the unrestricted
behavioral cap of one actual tail.  It therefore does not instantiate
`QuittingForwardExactCapTail`, despite matching that structure's algebraic
prefix orientation.

This omission is independent of the orientation issue.  Even a carrier seed
at every finite level would still leave an outward prefix tower rather than
one forward infinite ancestry.  A source theorem would have to supply both
carrier realization and an extension-compatible projective choice of the
finite blocks.

## 8. Novelty and strongest surviving statement

Relative to
`BallisticNormalizedSelectedChainRegression.lean`, the explicit table is a
real strengthening: it lifts one positive fixed pencil mode from normalized
matrix data to exact product roots, exact cap-Nash equations, and exact
Bellman cap prefixing.  The older checked regression is matrix-level only and
uses a different collision matrix.

Relative to `GATE_STRENGTHENER__BALLISTIC_WEIGHTED_COCYCLE_COLLAPSE.md`, the
generic pencil conclusion is mostly redundant and weaker because it assumes
`C` invertible.  The genuinely new generic observation is that eventual
finite full support can force `W_n=0` even at an omega boundary coordinate.

The strongest accurate headline is:

> Eventual finite full support upgrades every source omega window to the
> exact descriptor equality.  A rational quadratic Fin4 table realizes one
> strictly positive stable pencil mode by an exact, summable outward
> cap-prefix tower whose limit cap has unique all Continue.  The tower has no
> supplied terminal-semantic realization and does not form one forward
> infinite Nash--Bellman chronology.

That result is useful as a sharp bounded-capacity/Zeno regression.  It is not
a source producer or a counterexample mechanism.
