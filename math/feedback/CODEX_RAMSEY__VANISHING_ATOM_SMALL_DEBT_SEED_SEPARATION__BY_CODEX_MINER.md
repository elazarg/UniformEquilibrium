## 1. Actual endpoint and carrier audit

Let

```text
D_* = quittingTerminalSemanticDebtSum family.base.
```

The fields `family.base_mem`, `family.base_minimum`, and
`family.base_positive` give a positive global minimum over the literal
terminal-semantic carrier.  Every endpoint class named in the note is covered:

- `family.source rank`, every full-replacement profile, every common-response
  endpoint, and every reset-cube face are literal behavioral profiles, so
  `quittingTerminalSemanticPair_mem_carrier` applies;
- `FullReplacementCluster.cluster` has the explicit field `cluster_mem`; and
- any other claimed carrier limit is covered only after its carrier membership
  is supplied, as the note correctly states.

Therefore every such semantic pair has total debt at least `D_*`.  If every
coordinate were at most `eta`, finite summation would give

```text
D_* <= card(I) * eta.
```

The contradiction for `eta < D_*/card(I)` is valid.  Nonemptiness is available
in the tangent-family setting; in particular `D_*>0` itself rules out an empty
player type, and the atom access more strongly stores distinct mover and
observer labels.

The relocation bounds are also correct.  From one observer debt at most `e`,
nonnegativity and the total minimum give

```text
sum_{i != observer} debt_i >= D_* - e.
```

Since `observer != mover`, `card(I)-1>0`, so when `D_*-e>0` some other
coordinate has debt at least `(D_*-e)/(card(I)-1)`.  The full-replacement
specialization uses the checked
`FullReplacementCluster.mover_debt_eq_zero`.  With two distinct zero/small
coordinates on `Fin 4`, one of the two remaining coordinates indeed carries
at least half of the remaining total debt.  No coordinatewise endpoints from
different profiles are silently pasted together.

## 2. Same-root seam theorem and constant

`QuittingBoundedSeamChain.actualDebtGap_le_candidate_add_seams` compares the
candidate at time zero with the unrestricted terminal-semantic pair of the
literal infinite profile generated by exactly `chain.roots`.  It requires:

- summability of the prescribed and cap absolute seam streams;
- joint survival to zero; and
- every player-deleted survival to zero.

Applying `family.base_minimum` to the actual root-sequence profile supplies
its `hgap` with `delta=D_*`.  If candidate debt is at most `eta` in every
coordinate and

```text
tsum prescribedSeam_i + tsum capSeam_i <= eta,
```

then `actualDebtGap_le_two_mul_card_eta` gives exactly

```text
D_* <= 2 * card(I) * eta.
```

The factor two is one `eta` for initial candidate debt plus one `eta` for the
whole two-coordinate seam toll.  There is no additional factor from keeping a
separate prescribed budget.

The budget-stable output matches this convention.  Its packaged
`QuittingSummableSeamSource` stores:

```text
prescribed_summable,
total_summable,
prescribed_tsum_le,
total_tsum_le,
```

where `totalSeam = prescribedSeam + capSeam` pointwise.  Since the two seam
streams are nonnegative, `capSeam <= totalSeam`; hence `total_summable`
implies cap summability.  After that,
`tsum_add` identifies `tsum totalSeam` with
`tsum prescribedSeam + tsum capSeam`, so `total_tsum_le` is exactly the
`hseam` premise above.  The two persistent literal marginals give the required
joint and deleted-player survival, and the seed field gives the initial debt
bound.  Thus `exists_summableSeamSource_of_seed` really enters the obstruction
with no missing factor or survival quantifier.

## 3. Required scope wording

The opening phrase “hide the replacement in a summable same-root seam chain”
should be read, or preferably rewritten, as:

```text
hide it in a persistent same-root chain whose joint and every deleted-player
survival vanish and whose per-coordinate total absolute seam tsum is <= eta.
```

Summability alone, with a large fixed total seam or without survival
contraction, is not ruled out by the checked theorem.  Likewise the result
does not exclude an artificial-anchor architecture that leaves this
same-root absolute-seam interface—for example, one with a separate solved-game
disjunct or a genuinely different source transition whose terminal discrepancy
is consumed by another checked mechanism.  Sections 5--6 and the note's final
nonclaim already have the correct narrower intent; this qualification merely
makes the summary match them.

## 4. Export assessment

The quantitative same-root obstruction is already Lean checked, and the
actual-endpoint specialization is an elementary application of the maintained
minimum field.  The note is useful as an internal anti-duplication boundary,
but it adds no new export theorem and should remain internal.
