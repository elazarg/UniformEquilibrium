## 1. Delta mathematics

The punishment-priced finite calculation checks.

Let \(F=I\setminus\{i\}\), select a product Nash point \(x\) of the induced
three-player binary game with \(i\) fixed to Quit, and let \(p(A)\) be its
cell law.  With the full complement as the free set there are no outside
join conditions.  Thus \(C_P\le Q\) constructs exactly a
`QuittingSingletonBaseCertificate`; its checked compiler covers all
behavioral deviations, including Never and unbounded finite clocks.

Under no uniform payoff,
`exists_uniformPayoff_or_singletonBase_pos_gap` supplies a fixed
\(\gamma_i>0\) on the entire compact induced-Nash set.  Punishment normality
removes the empty-cell term, so one of the seven nonempty free cells obeys

\[
p(A)\bigl(r_i(A)-r_i(A\cup\{i\})\bigr)\ge\gamma_i/7.
\]

The reward bound gives \(p(A)\ge\gamma_i/(14M)>0\).  This also proves
\(M>0\) in the live branch.

For an actual profile \(P_n\) reaching \(t_n\) with probability \(L_n\),
overwriting only the four marginals at \(t_n\) leaves every pre-mark hazard
unchanged.  Putting the free players at \(x\) and \(i\) at Continue gives

\[
\Pr_{Y_n}(\text{first terminal coalition at }t_n=A)=L_np(A).

Thus the proposed half-size resolution
\(L_*\gamma_i/(28M)\) is safe.  The singleton packet constructor applies

screening accepts an arbitrary actual profile and retains the source stage

The final `consumerResult` is correctly typed with the retained

Accordingly the widened statement really does need only a finite mark with a
uniform joint-reach floor.  Neither paid gain nor first-disagreement

## 2. Why this is not a strict paid-port contraction

The widened hypothesis is vacuous as a paid-port discriminator.  In every
quitting game one may take the all-Continue profile, date zero, and joint
reach one.  The same marked-row overwrite then manufactures the positive
cell and strong packet.  Thus, under the hard residual, the theorem's packet
conclusion is available without a paid port, its source ancestry, its gain,
its continuation, or its orientation.

The checked downstream result is precisely the interface normal form already
recorded in `FIN4_STRATEGIC_CONCENTRATED_ARM_ACTION_NORMAL_FORM`:

\[
\text{Continue/source-attached singleton mode}
\quad\lor\quad
\text{Quit/source-attached collision-minimum residual}.
\]

That formalized packet explicitly says that neither arm is consumed and that
restating or iterating the universal static handoff is not a consumer.  The
new construction supplies no relation which makes either arm smaller when it
came from a paid port.

In particular, the type returned by `consumerResult` retains the newly
manufactured packet and the minimum source, but not:

- the incoming profitable first-disagreement edge;
- the paid gain or marked observer;
- the literal paid continuation;
- a Nash--Bellman predecessor/successor identity;
- a phase which cannot recur; or
- a rank decreasing on regenerated complete sources.

Forgetting a stronger object and landing in an already open universal waist
does not prove that the stronger object's inert mode is “not an independent
terminal mode.”  It only proves coexistence with a packet waist which was
already reachable.

## 3. Comparison with the maintained paid-port question

`FIN4_QUANTITATIVE_PAID_PORT_CONSUMER` accepts terminal approximants, a
charged Nash--Bellman return, a finite well-founded regenerated-source rank,
a contradiction, or an exact counterexample.  Its nonanswers explicitly
include a horizontal endpoint construction and finite replacement ancestry
treated as temporal play.

The present output is none of the accepted conclusions.  It makes four
horizontal row replacements, discards the paid data, and returns two
unconsumed static packet modes.  There is no proof that the packet modes
cannot regenerate another paid port or reset the same outer atlas phase.

Therefore the claims “strict branch contraction” and “later/inert port is not
an independent terminal mode” should not be used as conjecture-facing
conclusions.  The honest theorem is a useful supplied-mark adapter:

> Under the Fin4 no-UE hard residual, any uniformly reached literal mark can
> be overwritten into a strong singleton packet whose standard consumer
> returns the existing strategic/collision-minimum interface.

That exact statement is valid but belongs in `notes/` unless a later theorem
uses the retained relation to consume one output or defines a nonresetting
phase/rank.

## 4. Export disposition

The candidate fails `exports/README.md` criteria 4 and the “strict named
change” requirement.  Formalization could still be convenient as an internal
helper, but it would duplicate a universal packet waist rather than close or
strictly narrow a maintained question.

Recommended disposition: retain the proof internally, remove the strict-
contraction language, and use it only if a downstream argument exploits the
same marked prefix in a way represented by the output type.  Do not export in
its current form.
