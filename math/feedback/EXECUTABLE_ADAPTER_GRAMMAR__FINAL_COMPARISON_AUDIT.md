## 2. Required wrapper corrections

### Constrained-face qualifier

The export title, body Section 11, conclusion, and final nonclaims have the
right scope. The opening exact statement still says:

> a trace-visible same-source maximal-root operation

Replace this by:

> a trace-visible same-source constrained-face absorption-maximal exact-root
> operation

Likewise, in “Conjecture-facing change,” replace

> one explicit rational Fin4 construction

by

> one explicit rational Fin4 constrained-face exact-root construction

or add the same qualifier to the preceding selected-root phrase. Without
this correction, the headline can be read as claiming nonclosedness of an
unrestricted globally maximal cap-root selector. The body expressly does not
prove that.

The broader sentence

> the grammar cannot admit every exact selected-root construction

is correct: one constrained selected-root construction suffices to refute the
universal claim.

### Terminal-limit consumer

The Source correspondence section says that when a concrete program supplies
“vanishing debt with one payoff limit,” the consumer is

quittingGame_isUniformEquilibriumPayoff_of_terminalNash_exact.

That declaration consumes one exact terminal Nash profile. For a sequence of
terminal approximate equilibria whose errors tend to zero and whose payoffs
tend to one target, the matching declaration is

quittingGame_isUniformEquilibriumPayoff_of_terminalNash_tendsto.

Either cite the latter, or change the sentence to discuss one exact terminal
Nash profile. The current combination is not literally correct.

### Declared decoder class

The conjecture-facing summary should say

> all uniformly summable finite-macro decoders of the declared SD form

rather than an unqualified “all uniformly summable finite-macro decoders.”
The theorem uses compact inverse-limit code, continuous finite macros,
continuous visible output, and the uniform budgets in the SD contract. It
does not classify every possible object someone might call a decoder.

## 4. Effect of EXECUTABLE_COMPLETE.md

The newer note contains two genuine architectural refinements.

1. Its normal-form theorem eliminates every certified finite grammar
   expression into one compact executable adapter. In particular,
   trace-visible bounded rank adds no new topological primitive once all of
   its branch, child, terminal, and backward data are trace-safe.
2. Its two-player calculation proves that a coherent
   absorption-maximal-root **return** with simultaneous positive reach,
   literal suffix-back provenance, vanishing strategic trace error, and
   vanishing maximality error is impossible at the displayed limit. This
   also excludes a pointwise rank whose declared outcome type is precisely
   that impossible return.

These sharpen the accepted architectural negative. They do not close any
current Fin4 producer edge:

- the example has the exact all-Never equilibrium and therefore is not a
  positive-minimum Fin4 residual;
- its own absorption-maximal root supplies an exact terminal Nash profile, so
  a ranked construction may legitimately terminate through a different
  consumer;
- the pointwise-rank no-go excludes only ranks required to output the
  impossible coherent return, not ranks allowed to produce terminal
  approximants, charged returns of another form, or renewable source descent;
  and
- no comparison transport, total-variation decoder, regenerated source, or
  terminal consumer is produced for the actual Fin4 escape or minimum-return
  packets.

Accordingly, EXECUTABLE_COMPLETE.md does not answer a further portion of the
Fin4 executable-program question. It makes the standards for an answer more
explicit and removes one tempting universal maximal-root-return route.

## 5. Status of the revised Fin4 question

questions/FIN4_RECURRENT_COMPONENT_CONSUMER_OR_MODEL.md remains genuinely
open.

Its input assumes \(D_*>0\) and the checked source-preserving forced-pair
stream. The constrained-root no-go table and the two-player normal-form table
have minimum debt zero and are not source-attached instances of either
terminal component. They therefore do not satisfy even the question's
partial-answer criterion.

The question also does not duplicate the generic architecture result:

- the generic result supplies a sufficient grammar and proves that no such
  grammar can universally realize every exact constrained optimizer;
- the Fin4 question asks for source-specific adapters or alternative
  consumers for the actual escape and minimum-return operations.

Its existing maximal-root bullet is already correctly phrased:

> exact-root selection ... without assuming that maximal absorption among
> exact roots is a closed selection rule.

That wording permits exactly the routes left open by the no-go: prove
source-specific comparison transport, record a nonmaximal exact root, postpone
a pointwise choice until after reconstruction, or use a different terminal
consumer. No revision is needed.

Once the export is accepted, the now-answered generic
QUITTING_COMPOSITIONALLY_SUFFICIENT_STATE.md should leave questions/ under the
directory's resolved-question policy. Removing that generic question does not
affect the Fin4 question or its README entry.

## Final disposition

After the three wrapper corrections, the export candidate is faithful,
properly scoped, and ready for final gate review. The new normal-form note is
valuable follow-up architecture work but should not be used to claim progress
on either Fin4 terminal component without a separate source-specific adapter
or consumer.
