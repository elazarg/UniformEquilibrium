## Decision

The corrected clock-ratio theorem is mathematically review-worthy and passes
after minor statement repairs, but it is not exported as a hard-residual
closure.

For an eventually interior exact two-clock Nash--Bellman chronology, strict
mixing gives an exact scalar recurrence. In the nondegenerate diffuse case the
solo and collision gaps have the same sign, their absolute ratio lies strictly
between zero and one, and the partner hazard decays geometrically. If both
cross entries are nonzero, both clocks are summable and the conditional tail
has positive all-Never probability. The strengthening review gives a sharp
classification and converse.

The theorem currently has no actual hard-residual adapter or positive-Never
consumer. More importantly, the checked outward maximal-prefix ray has the
reverse temporal orientation; it does not itself realize the forward
chronology classified by the theorem. Reversing it would require a left-
infinite executable chronology. Thus the result neither eliminates the hard
pair chamber nor supplies a return, rank decrease, or terminal approximation.

The maximal-orbit late-block estimates in the same followup duplicate checked
summability results and are not included as new content. The corrected sharp
classification is being retained as an internal note. No file was added to
exports/.
