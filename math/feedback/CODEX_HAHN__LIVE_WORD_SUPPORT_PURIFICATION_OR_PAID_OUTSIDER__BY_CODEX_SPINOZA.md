## Reconstruction and adversarial checks

The final joint-reach floor implies both every intermediate reach
\(A_{n,t}\ge\eta\) and every displayed marginal Continue probability
\(c_{n,t,i}\ge\eta\).  Thus a row defect bounded below gives a literal
one-row deviation with unconditional gain \(A_{n,t}h_{n,t,i}\), and the
vanishing complete host debt excludes the host as the eventual fixed payer.

For one player \(i\), deleting all selected bad Quit choices is a genuine
single behavioral replacement.  The exact performance-difference expansion
against the old continuation value is

\[
 U_i(Y'_n)-U_i(Y_n)
 =\sum_{t\in S_n}A'_{n,t}q_{n,t,i}
      \bigl(C_{n,t,i}-Q_{n,t,i}\bigr).
\]

The use of the modified occupancy \(A'_{n,t}\) is essential and is correct:
backward subtraction telescopes the propagated continuation difference with
the new-policy survival factors.  Removing Quit mass gives
\(A'_{n,t}\ge A_{n,t}\), so the reached-mass lower bound in the aggregate
branch has the stated orientation.  There is no illicit summation of
incompatible one-row deviations.

In the diagonal branch, the original reached bad-Quit ledger controls the
unweighted deletion mass because every original reach is at least \(\eta\):

\[
 S_n\le M_n(\delta_n)/\eta\to0.
\]

The rowwise maximal coupling controls the full profile, every suffix, and
each pure-current-action endpoint uniformly by the same union-bound total
\(S_n\).  Each endpoint payoff moves by at most \(2RS_n\), so a supported
action's loss moves by at most \(4RS_n\).  Continue stays supported and its
old loss is at most \(H_n/\eta\); a retained Quit action has old loss at most
\(\delta_n\).  This proves exactly the displayed
\(\varepsilon_n=\delta_n+4RS_n\) support loss.  Simultaneous deletions across
players do not affect this coupling argument.

The Bellman identities are definitional prescribed-payoff recursions for the
new actual word.  Deleting Quit mass increases its final survival.  Finally,
with row absorption \(a_{n,t}\),

\[
 \sum_t a_{n,t}\le -\log\prod_t(1-a_{n,t})
 \le-\log\eta,
\]

so the charge is uniformly bounded, in the wrong direction for the existing
arbitrarily-large-charge forward producer.  No punishment floor, source
return, or endpoint renewal is inferred.

## Boundary tests

- Without the fixed final-reach floor, a tiny reached bad-Quit ledger can hide
  order-one unweighted strategy changes, and Continue support losses cannot
  be divided by a common \(\eta\).
- Replacing the modified reaches in the policy-improvement identity by
  arbitrary or old reaches would generally destroy exactness; the note uses
  the correct modified occupancy and only then the monotonicity comparison.
- The TV coupling is uniform over suffix endpoints because each suffix sees
  a subset of the changed Bernoulli coordinates; it does not assert
  convergence of an unrelated infinite continuation.
- The paid A/B outputs are complete behavioral deviations with fixed gain,
  but their horizontal targets need not preserve the old roots.  The note
  correctly makes no consumer claim.

## Recommendation

The theorem is mathematically useful as a note-level local purification and
paid-outsider screen.  It is not by itself an exportable conjecture-facing
consumer: its C branch lacks punishment admissibility, divergent charge, and
a renewable tail, while A/B stop at the familiar horizontal paid-port waist.
