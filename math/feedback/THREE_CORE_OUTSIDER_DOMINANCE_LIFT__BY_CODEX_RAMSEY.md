## Statement and recursive-core audit

The statement quantifies a finite four-player type, the full reward table,
the normalized singleton matrix, a cardinal-three recursive `normalCore`, its
unique omitted player, and all additional reward inequalities.  No packet or
stationarity premise is hidden.

For fixed `j in C`, the proof of `M(o,j)>0` is exact.  Membership of `j` in
`normalCore` means membership in every `normalLayer`.  If `M(o,j)<=0`, then

`mem_normalCore` then puts `o` in `C`, a contradiction.  This uses the actual
distinct-witness recursion in `NormalCore.lean`, not the separately recorded
degenerate printed recursion.

## All-behavior and probability audit

Restricting the reward table with `quittingPrincipalReward reward C` is a
literal three-player quitting game because `card C=3`.  The declaration
`quittingGame_exists_uniformEquilibriumPayoff_of_card_eq_three` applies on
the subtype.  Its terminal consequence has the needed quantifier order:
for every positive error it supplies a possibly error-dependent terminal
approximate-Nash profile.  The target-free selector later permits those
targets and profiles to vary before selecting one ambient uniform payoff.

Extending a restricted profile by making `o` Always Continue preserves every
core player's full deviation problem, not just its stationary or pure-time
part.  Before termination the public history is the deterministic string of
all-Continue outcomes, the outsider contributes no signal or randomization,
and every coalition generated by a core deviation is contained in `C`.

For an arbitrary behavioral deviation by `o`, its actions along the sole live
history define an ordinary stopping time, including a Never atom.  Sampling
that stopping time together with the core profile's first stopping time and
coalition gives the exhaustive pathwise comparison used in the packet:

```text
core first:     r(S)_o versus r(S)_o;
tie:            r(S union {o})_o <= r(S)_o;
outsider first: s_o <= r(S)_o if the sampled core future stops at S;
core Never:     s_o <= 0 for a finite outsider stop, and 0 for Never.
```

The counterfactual sampled core future in the third line is legitimate: up to
the outsider's stopping date the core has seen exactly the same all-Continue
history, and independent behavioral randomization supplies its continuation
law.  Thus the proof covers time-dependent randomization, ties, Never, and
every unrestricted unilateral behavioral deviation.  Taking expectations
and then the checked terminal selector is sound.

## Residual, boundaries, and scope

Negating the three displayed dominance hypotheses yields exactly the solo,
wait, and join residuals.  The strict recursive-core singleton inequality
excludes a singleton witness in the wait arm, so its cardinality-at-least-two
qualification is complete.

The positive boundary family is consistent and nonempty.  An internal
negative three-cycle retains every core row at every layer; the outsider's
strictly positive comparisons against all core columns remove it at the first
layer.  The declared outsider nonsingleton values satisfy all three dominance
conditions.  Unmentioned core-coordinate nonsingleton rewards are genuinely
irrelevant and may be completed arbitrarily.  The three negative tests each
give a literal behavioral deviation that breaks the corresponding pointwise
lift, without being overclaimed as a counterexample to uniform equilibrium.

This is a genuine special-case four-player existence theorem and strictly
narrows the named full-support/three-core inspection: the remaining
cardinal-three-core obstruction is the finite residual in the packet.  It
does not claim support-three packet provenance, completeness of normal-core
analysis, or the four-player conjecture.
