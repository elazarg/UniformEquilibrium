# Cross-audit of paid-cycle recharge versus two-tier shadowing

Reviewer: `CODEX_GATE_FALSIFIER`

## Verdict

**FAIL as a completed connection.**  The cited ingredients do not compose to
the two-tier chronological-shadowing producer, and the missing bridge is not
already proved under another name.  It also does not follow by a short
algebraic composition.

There is a genuine and useful partial connection already checked in Lean:
the minimum-target arm of the paid-cycle result regenerates its source from
the literal endpoint profiles and dates, and arbitrary supplied
two-counterfactual response contrasts transport through the newly added exact
cap--Nash prefix with a multiplier tending to one.  This closes the old
*realizer reselection* loss.  It does not close response reentry, full
semantic seams, successor compatibility, or the independent small-debt seed.

Consequently the proposed section should not say that one reentry-loss lemma
would inhabit the packet requested by
`questions/FIN4_TWO_TIER_CHRONOLOGICAL_SHADOWING.md`.  At most it identifies a
possible attack on one recursively regenerated minimum-cycle subarm.  No new
export-worthy theorem is presently obtained by combining the cited results.

## The decisive mismatch

The response transport theorem carries a difference between **two shifted
counterfactual responses**.  It deliberately does not compare the shifted
response with the newly prefixed profile's prescribed strategy.  The
observer may Quit in the new cap--Nash prefix, whereas both transported
counterfactuals are forced to Continue through it.  Therefore it gives no
transport of:

- the raw prescribed-to-response gain;
- the response endpoint's small debt relative to the new actual source;
- the cap displacement of any other player; or
- the complete semantic endpoint seam.

This limitation is explicit in
`formalized/SOURCE_FAITHFUL_MINIMUM_ENDPOINT_CAUSALIZATION_AND_RESPONSE_MENU_TRANSPORT.md`.
It is also mathematically necessary: the checked theorem
`not_isQuittingCrossSeamContrastTransport_finFour` in
`Research/Quitting/CrossSeamResponseContrastNoGo.lean` shows that one
player's full replacement can change another player's Quit-now-versus-Never
contrast from zero to one.  Its example has `D_*=0`, so it does not refute a
new positive-minimum theorem; it does refute any purported general algebraic
splice.

The remote-bubble regression is sharper for the exact proposed seam.  It has
the same response label, fixed response gain, zero target-observer debt, a
unit nonsingleton atom, unrestricted caps, and exact semantic/law equality
across regeneration, yet its reentry loss equals the full gain at every
step.  Its global minimum is also zero.  Thus positive-minimum provenance is
the only remaining possible source of a reentry estimate; no currently
checked local field implies it.

## Why a small reentry estimate is still not the two-tier packet

Even if one added, as a new hypothesis, sublinear reentry loss for one fixed
observer/response chart, four independent requirements of
`QuittingBudgetStablePacketSystem` and its intended source-attached
strengthening would remain.

1. **Literal successor compatibility.**  One needs a packet at every legal
   source and requested scale whose terminal candidate is close in every
   payoff and cap coordinate to the next port annotation.  A bound on one
   response value is not the eight-coordinate Fin4 `total_endpoint` bound.

2. **Fixed persistent labels.**  The paid cycle fixes a mover and observer
   only within one outer subsequence.  The next regenerated cycle may select
   a different observer, mover, routed coalition, or response.  Finite
   pigeonhole gives recurring labels, not adjacent compatible blocks, and it
   does not account for the intervening blocks' clocks.  The atom decoder's
   terminal can also differ from the routed cycle coalition.

3. **Operationally sublinear availability and seam cost.**  The source-
   faithful regeneration provides exact profiles, marks, and asymptotically
   lossless prefix transport.  It provides no positive-radius local selection
   theorem, no `radius_successor` estimate, and no common operationally
   sublinear majorant for full semantic seams.

4. **Tier-II seed or direct consumer.**  Every actual positive-minimum carrier
   point has total debt at least `D_*`, so it cannot be the all-coordinate
   small-debt seed at arbitrary accuracy.  The all-behavior implementation
   inequality and the checked same-root seam barrier show that a vanishing-
   error implementation would already be UE-strength.  Paid-cycle recharge
   and response reentry supply no such implementation.

There is an additional quantifier mismatch.  The cocycle requires literal
closure of the outgoing response at one step with the incoming response at
the next step, including every prefix clock shift.  The atom decoder chooses
an approximately optimal response independently at each rank.  Source-
faithful menu transport can retain a response once supplied, but it does not
force the next decoder to select that response, nor prove that it remains
near-cap against the next prescribed environment.  Observer rotation is a
separate known obstruction.

## Strongest rigorous composition currently available

The present declarations justify only the following source-attached statement:

```text
paid nonsingleton cycle
  -> fixed spectator recharge and atom alternative
  -> paid singleton, or strict off-minimum endpoint,
     or minimum regeneration on the literal endpoint sequence;

minimum regeneration + any supplied finite two-response menu
  -> exact transport of that counterfactual contrast through the new prefix
     with multiplier tending to one.
```

No conclusion identifies this transported contrast with the next paid-cycle
response, bounds a reentry loss, or produces a compatible port successor.

A separately supplied infinite chain with one fixed observer, literally
closed response labels, gains bounded below by `q>0`, and cumulative reentry
loss `o(N)` would be impossible by the response cocycle.  That is a correct
conditional consumer, not a producer from the Fin4 source.  Moreover, its
finite failure may land in the paid-singleton or off-minimum arms rather than
in a terminal or rank-decreasing output.

## Exact new theorem that would move this lane

The missing result must be substantially stronger than “reentry loss is
small.”  One adequate theorem would construct, from every recursively
regenerated minimum-cycle source, a fixed-observer, source/successor-compatible
block family such that:

- the outgoing response is literally the next incoming shifted response;
- its prescribed and all-cap endpoint seam has a common operationally
  sublinear bound;
- two fixed actual players accumulate the required marginal hazards;
- availability is renewed with operationally sublinear loss; and
- either an external all-coordinate small-debt implementation is supplied or
  the construction exits directly to a UE/terminal consumer.

Alternatively, a theorem could bypass the two-tier interface by using the
exact cocycle to eliminate all infinite minimum-regeneration cycles and then
consume both finite exit arms.  Neither theorem is presently in Lean or in
the reviewed notes.

## Export assessment

The paid-cycle recharge and source-faithful response-menu transport are
already separately formalized results.  The response reentry cocycle is a
correct internal conditional consumer/no-go.  Their proposed connection adds
no proved arrow, eliminates no maintained branch, and does not furnish either
tier of the shadowing question.  It should remain a candidate connection in
the mining note, with the claim weakened from “one missing lemma feeds the
two-tier packet” to “a source-level full-semantic reentry/availability theorem
could connect these existing modules.”
