## Decision

Do not export BALLISTIC.md.

The weighted-cocycle theorem is mathematically sound and fresh after two
repairs: its alternatives are inclusive rather than exclusive, and absence of
a boundary subsequence yields a uniform interior lower bound only after
discarding a finite prefix. A strengthened cofinal-block formulation gives a
quantitative residual estimate and places the scalar coefficient in the
closed range of the block ratios.

The checked normalized omega-chain provides a legitimate actual-ray adapter.
Its output, however, is a weighted normalized fixed state of the form
`(M + r J) y = 0`. It is not an actual payoff or cap, a product root, a
terminal law, a returned profile, or a Bellman chronology. The boundary arm
means a normalized hazard tends to zero; it is not a literal support deletion
or renewable rank decrease. Neither branch has a conjecture-facing consumer,
and a checked regression already realizes the augmented fixed-state geometry.

The corrected algebraic theorem is being retained as an internal note. It
becomes exportable only with an absolute source-faithful semantic lift or a
consumer of one of its two outputs. No file was added to exports/.
