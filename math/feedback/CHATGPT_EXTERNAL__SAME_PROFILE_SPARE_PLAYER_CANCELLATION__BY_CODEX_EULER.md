## Claim audited

The player set is the disjoint union

\[
I=\{b_0,b_1\}\mathbin{\dot\cup}\{f_0,f_1\}
  \mathbin{\dot\cup}\{s\}.
\]

At an actual behavioral profile `sigma^0`, both base players quit surely in
the first stage, the spare continues, and the free players use fixed first
stage probabilities.  The family `sigma^q` changes only the spare's first
stage quit probability to `q` and uses `sigma^0` after the all-Continue
outcome.  The proposal assumes that the two base debts are positive at
`q=0`, that the two free coordinates are complementary at both endpoints,
that the base endpoint differences have crossed to the nonnegative side at
`q=1`, that both endpoint payoff vectors dominate punishment, and that

\[
q_*\kappa\le D_0.
\]

It concludes exact formulas for every debt coordinate, an epsilon-Nash
interval, an exact charge-one floor edge when `kappa=0`, and a strict
positive-debt-support drop at a global minimum when `kappa>0`.

## 1. Unrestricted behavioral deviations really collapse

This is the main semantic point, and it is correct.  If the deviator is one
base player, the other base player still quits surely at date zero.  If the
deviator is free or is the spare, both base players remain sure quitters.
Thus, under every unilateral behavioral replacement, absorption occurs at
the first stage with probability one.  Later behavior, private randomization,
and history dependence are irrelevant.

Consequently, if the deviator's first-stage quit probability is `alpha`, its
payoff is exactly

\[
\alpha Q_i(q)+(1-\alpha)C_i(q),
\]

and its unrestricted behavioral cap is exactly `max(Q_i(q),C_i(q))`.  This is
not merely a stationary-strategy cap.  The source-level checked ingredients
which support this reduction are `QuittingRootHasSureQuitter` and
`quittingRootExpectedPayoff_eq_of_hasSureQuitter` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Root/FirstBranch.lean`, together with the literal
root/profile splice identity
`quittingTerminalPayoff_rootThenContinuation_eq`.

No coalition of three or more can be newly generated by a *unilateral*
deviation except through the fixed simultaneous actions of the other
players at date zero; those outcomes are already included in `Q_i(q)` and
`C_i(q)`.  There is no missing later-time deviation arm.

## 2. The `Phi_p` formula and affine insertion are exact

Write `Delta=Q-C`.  At prescribed quit probability `p`, the payoff is
`p Q+(1-p)C`, while the cap is `max(Q,C)`.  Directly,

\[
\max(Q,C)-[pQ+(1-p)C]
  =(1-p)(\Delta)_+ + p(-\Delta)_+=\Phi_p(\Delta).
\]

This checks all endpoint cases `p=0,1`; no division is involved.

For every old player, conditioning on the spare's first-stage Bernoulli
action gives

\[
\Delta_i(q)=(1-q)\Delta_i^0+q\Delta_i^1.
\]

The terminal-law statement is equally exact: since `s` has zero probability
at the source and the base ensures nonempty absorption, every old first-stage
atom `S` splits into `S` and `S union {s}` with weights `1-q` and `q`.

For each free player, `Phi_{p_f}` is convex and nonnegative.  Its value is
zero at both endpoint differences, so it is zero on their whole affine
segment.  This proves `d_f(q)=0`, including the pure boundary cases:

- if `p_f=0`, both endpoint differences are nonpositive;
- if `p_f=1`, both are nonnegative;
- if `0<p_f<1`, both endpoint differences are zero.

For a base player `p_b=1`, hence

\[
d_b(q)=(-\Delta_b(q))_+=(a_b-q\lambda_b)_+.
\]

Because `Delta_b^0=-a_b<0` and `Delta_b^1>=0`, one has
`lambda_b=a_b+Delta_b^1>0` and
`tau_b=a_b/lambda_b in (0,1]`.  Therefore
`q_*=max(tau_{b_0},tau_{b_1})` kills both base debts.

For the spare, `Delta_s=-kappa<=0` and its prescribed probability is `q`, so

\[
d_s(q)=q\kappa.
\]

Thus at `q_*` the debt vector is exactly zero off `s`, total debt is
`q_* kappa`, and maximum exploitability is also `q_* kappa`.

## 3. Floors and the exact epsilon interval

Every prescribed payoff coordinate is affine:

\[
u_i(q)=(1-q)u_i^0+q u_i^1.
\]

The behavioral punishment vector belongs to the fixed five-player game and
does not depend on `q`.  Endpoint floor domination therefore gives floor
domination for the entire segment.

Since the free debts vanish identically, the exact exploitability formula is

\[
\max\{q\kappa,
  (a_{b_0}-q\lambda_{b_0})_+,
  (a_{b_1}-q\lambda_{b_1})_+\}.
\]

For `epsilon>=0`, solving the three scalar inequalities gives exactly the
displayed interval `[L_epsilon,U_epsilon]`.  When the lower endpoint exceeds
the upper endpoint the interval is empty, as it should be.  The formula also
handles `kappa=0`, `epsilon=0`, `tau_b=1`, and pure free-player probabilities.

## 4. The `kappa=0` floor edge is valid, with its direction stated precisely

Let `r_*` be the first-stage product root of `sigma^{q_*}`.  It contains both
sure base quitters, hence its absorption charge is one.  The literal profile

\[
r_* :: \sigma^0
\]

is exactly `sigma^{q_*}` under the proposal's definition, including the
unreached continuation.  Because every unilateral replacement leaves at
least one sure opponent quitter, both the payoff equation and every root
Nash inequality are independent of the continuation payoff.

When `kappa=0`, all five debts vanish, so `r_*` is exact Nash against the tail
payoff `u^0`.  The predecessor payoff is `u^{q_*}`.  Both payoff vectors are
bounded terminal payoffs and both dominate punishment.  Therefore one gets
an exact `QuittingPunishmentFloorAdmissibleEdge` with

\[
\text{tail payoff}=u^0,
\qquad
\text{current payoff}=u^{q_*},
\qquad
\text{charge}=1.
\]

Under the checked relation
`quittingPunishmentFloorAdmissibleChargedRelation` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Bellman/Finite/PunishmentFloorAdmissibleChargedRelation.lean`,
the relation source is `edge.tail` and its target is `edge.current`.
Behavioral chronology is read oppositely: the current root is prefixed in
front of the actual tail continuation `sigma^0`.

This precise formulation is stronger and safer than saying only that the
edge is "attached" to the source.  It also avoids confusing this result with
the cap-Nash/prescribed-Nash seam documented by
`capNash_isZeroNash_at_prescribed_iff_surcharge_eq_liveDebt` in
`PairBasePaidResetEndpointSeam.lean`: here the two sure quitters kill the seam
directly.

## 5. The `kappa>0` minimum-fiber support drop is valid only because `(SC)` is supplied

Let the minimization domain contain every actual behavioral semantic pair;
in particular it must contain both `sigma^0` and `sigma^{q_*}`.  If
`sigma^0` is a global total-debt minimizer, then

\[
D_0\le D(\sigma^{q_*})=q_*\kappa.
\]

The explicit hypothesis `(SC)` supplies the reverse inequality.  Equality
follows.  Since `a_b>0` gives `q_*>0`, `kappa>0` makes `s` the unique positive
debt coordinate at `sigma^{q_*}`.  Hence both points lie in the same minimum
fiber and support cardinality drops from two to one.

This argument must **not** be cited as proving `(SC)`.  Without `(SC)`, global
minimality gives only the opposite inequality

\[
D_0\le q_*\kappa,
\]

and is entirely compatible with strict increase.  In particular, the
producer inequality `q_* kappa <= D_0` cannot be smuggled out of minimum
debt.

record the orientation `tail u^0 -> current u^{q_*}` and the literal prefix
identity `r_* :: sigma^0 = sigma^{q_*}`.  I regard this as a typing
clarification rather than a mathematical repair.

## Final assessment

After those clarifications, the theorem is mathematically correct at its
stated conditional scope.  The all-behavior reduction is genuine, the
`Phi_p` and affine formulas are exact, endpoint complementarity persists,
the floor segment is safe, the `kappa=0` edge has charge one, and `(SC)` plus
global minimality gives a real support-cardinality decrease.

The unresolved conjecture-facing problem is exactly the one the proposal
admits: produce the endpoint complementarity and, above all,
`q_* kappa <= D_0` from a source-native five-player extension of the checked
four-player pair-base window.  No declaration found in the narrow source
audit provides that producer.
