## Exact claim and verdict

let δ=D_*>0 be the numerical infimum of SUM complete terminal deviation
debts over actual independent finite stopping laws. Its produced marked
ordered-calendar minimum cannot have both positive Never mass for every
player and zero mass at every finite calendar point for every player.

The supporting erasure theorem says that, at a produced actual minimum,
an available nonatomic cut whose removed marginal masses have sum at most
δ/(4M) can be erased by independent conditioning without changing the global
minimum value or omitting any cap test. Section38 iterates this finite-charge
move until a final small suffix would make all Never a global minimum.

exclusion. I found no unresolved mathematical objection or required repair.
The argument uses the actual global minimum, includes binding finite and

It is a genuine restriction on produced positive-minimum sources, not merely

## Prefix erasure: complete caps and actual minimality

The true singleton moat b_i−s_i≥δ is correctly a SUM-minimum result. Since
both cap and singleton lie in [−M,M], it implies δ≤2M and hence κ≤1/2.
Each removed mass x_i is strictly below1. Positive x_i coordinates are
interior; zero-prefix coordinates must remain fixed, as the proof specifies.

Every test before a pays s_i unless an opponent selects the prefix, an event
of probability at most Σ_{j≠i}z_j. Its uniform upper bound
s_i+2MΣ_{j≠i}z_j is valid for either sign of s_i and all simultaneous outcomes.
At the original source this is at least δ/2 below the full cap. At or after
a, including Never, the exact cap is the passive prefix contribution plus
the opponent suffix product times the COMPLETE conditional cap. Taking
a supremum commutes with this identity because the product is nonnegative.
Thus the local complete-debt branch is genuinely multi-affine.

The local interior minimum forces that polynomial to be constant. The given
small-cube averaging proof is valid: multi-affine interpolation expresses the
center as the average of its corners, and local minimality makes all corners
equal; interpolation then gives constancy on an open cube and hence everywhere.
This step alone would not justify extending the cap identity to z=0.

The continuation argument supplies precisely that missing justification.
For EACH parameter point, fixed original-sequence reweighting gives actual
finite approximants with every cap converging. At any point of the closed
minimum set C, its represented pair is therefore in the actual carrier and
is again a global minimum. The same singleton moat gives a uniform strict
gap against all earlier tests throughout the path z(t)=(1−t)x. Hence no early
cap switch can occur while leaving C, and local equality with the constant
polynomial makes C relatively open. The connectedness argument is sound.
Binding caps at or after a never needed a unique selector or a strict gap.

At the endpoint z=0, every removed finite test pays exactly s_i, already
paid by the available empty test a. Cropping the earlier calendar therefore
does not remove a possible best response, including when s_i<0 and Never
is strictly better. This would be false at an unmarked cut or a positive
atomic first date without the required empty response.

## Fixed charge, gapped calendars and termination

Atomlessness makes H_a(b)=Σ_i q_i^a([a,b)) continuous in the real cut coordinate.
When total remaining finite mass Λ(a)>κ, the intermediate value κ occurs
strictly between a and c. If its real cut lies in a gap of T, the cumulative
mass is constant across that gap. Its endpoints belong to T and give the same
mass. Neither endpoint can be a or c at that intermediate value. This covers
Cantor-like supports as well as calendars containing irrelevant empty points.
The chosen endpoint remains nonatomic, so Section36 is applicable.

The exact product identity R(b)=R(a)∏_i(1−x_i) and Σ_i x_i=κ give
R(b)≤R(a)e^(−κ), whereas R(b)≥ρ=∏_i α_i>0 at EVERY step. Choosing finite N
with e^(−Nκ)<ρ proves finite termination at a minimum suffix with Λ≤κ.
No macroscopic atom or uniform lower bound on conditional finite mass is
assumed. Global minimality keeps δ and hence κ fixed at every step.

Every subsequent available nonatomic cut removes at most κ. Cuts approaching
the finite-support endpoint leave no finite mass in the limit. If that endpoint

claimed contradiction for arbitrary signed rewards.
