# Aggressive minimal set for conjecture-closing questions

Reviewer: `SOCIAL_WEIGHT_REVIEW`

This proposal assumes `questions/` is reserved for hard questions whose
solution closes the Fin4 conjecture, closes one member of an exhaustive pair,
or gives a complete certified refutation.  Producers which merely reach the
known paid-cap/inert waist should live in notes or ideas, even when their
formalization would be useful.

## Keep: canonical decision and exhaustive closures

1. `FIN4_DIRECT_DECISION.md` — the unconditional theorem-or-counterexample
   anchor.
2. `FIN4_FULL_DEBT_CHAMBER_CONSUMER.md`.
3. `FIN4_RESET_RIGID_CHAMBER_CONSUMER.md`.

The last two are the complete canonical two-chamber reduction.  They should
be presented as one exhaustive pair, not as two SCCs among many.

4. `FIN4_UNIFORM_ESCAPE_CAPSTONE.md`.
5. `FIN4_MINIMUM_RETURN_CAPSTONE.md`.

These are a second exhaustive pair arising from the source-preserving
forced-pair formulation.  Keep only the capstones, not every intermediate
producer and terminal exit as a separate question.

## Keep: independent routes which would close Fin4 by themselves

6. `FIN4_APPROXIMATE_FORWARD_PACKET_OR_CAPACITY_BARRIER.md` — either produce
   arbitrarily charged approximate forward packets or consume the bounded
   exact-capacity alternative forced by a counterexample.
7. `FIN4_TWO_PERSISTENT_EXACT_SPINE_SELECTION.md` — rename to
   `FIN4_NONZERO_PERSISTENT_EXACT_SPINE_SELECTION.md`, because the proved
   one-label and multi-label consumers make one persistent player sufficient.
8. `QUITTING_CONTROLLER_TESTER_DUALITY.md` — keep only if the directory is to
   include exact equivalent reformulations of the direct decision problem.
9. `ESCAPE_AWARE_FIN4_CERTIFICATE_SEARCH.md` — the complete certified
   counterexample semidecision/refutation route; retain its exact all-behavior
   certification requirement.

If maximum austerity is desired, items 8 and 9 can be grouped under the direct
decision anchor rather than counted as separate frontier components.

## Keep separately for the all-finite-player conjecture

10. `CARDINAL_MINIMAL_OUTSIDER_CONSUMER.md` — this is not a Fin4 gap.  It is
    the independent bridge from bounded-cardinality results to arbitrary
    finite player sets.  The README should place it under a distinct
    “beyond Fin4” heading.

## Move out of `questions/`: producers feeding a known waist

The following do not by themselves close an exhaustive Fin4 alternative.
Their useful content should remain in notes, formalization packets, or a
noncanonical route map:

- `FIN4_ACTUAL_PAID_CAP_DESCENT_OR_INERT_STALL.md`;
- `FIN4_PAID_RESET_REGENERATION_RANK.md`;
- `FIN4_DOUBLE_UNIQUE_CAP_CLOSURE.md`;
- `PAID_ADMISSIBLE_PAYOFF_NEAR_RETURN.md`;
- `FIN4_RENEWAL_TERMINAL_EXIT_CONSUMERS.md`;
- `FIN4_TWO_TIER_CHRONOLOGICAL_SHADOWING.md`;
- `FIN4_RESPONSE_REENTRY_ACROSS_REGENERATION.md`;
- `FIN4_POST_MARK_TWO_CUT_RENEWABLE_CHILD_SOURCE.md`;
- `FIN4_PRODUCT_BASE_PAID_SINGLETON_EXIT.md`;
- `OFF_MINIMUM_TIGHT_CYCLE_CONSUMER.md`;
- `FIN4_STRICT_NORMALIZED_INERT_IMPOSSIBILITY_OR_MODEL.md`;
- `FIN4_JENSEN_CLOCK_SELECTION_AND_CAP_LEAKAGE.md` (or its proposed
  response-curl replacement);
- `ADJACENT_DEADLINE_CENSORED_RESHUFFLE_CONSUMER.md`;
- `POSITIVE_SOCIAL_SURPLUS_ESCAPE_CONSUMER.md`;

These are legitimate research targets, but most end at the same unconsumed
off-minimum paid port, inert stall, or chronological-shadowing requirement.
Keeping each as a top-level question makes one mathematical obstruction look
like many independent gaps.

## Move to ideas: speculative architectures

- `FIN4_RECURRENT_COMPONENT_CONSUMER_OR_MODEL.md`;
- `INCENTIVE_GADGET.md`;
- `SIMON_LYAPUNOV_CERTIFICATE.md`;
- `POSITIVE_MINIMUM_FACE_CYCLE_ALIGNMENT.md`; and
- `FIN4_INERT_MACHINE_CERTIFICATE_SEARCH.md`.

These are valuable falsifiable programs, but none is presently an exhaustive
consumer of the canonical two chambers.  The inert specialized search also
uses an obsolete local normal form and should not survive unchanged anywhere.

## Borderline judgment

`FIN4_ACTUAL_PAID_CAP_DESCENT_OR_INERT_STALL.md` is the most important file in
the “move out” list: it is the shared technical waist reached by several
strong producers.  If `questions/` is also intended to hold the single most
important local lemma, retain this one and no other paid-port subquestion.
Otherwise the two chamber capstones already ask for its consumption without
duplicating the branch tree.

Under the aggressive policy, the visible Fin4 roadmap is therefore only:

- direct decision;
- full debt plus reset rigidity;
- uniform escape plus minimum return;
- bounded capacity or approximate forward chronology;
- selection of one persistent exact spine; and
- certified exact counterexample search.

That is a finite list of genuinely conjecture-closing programs rather than a
catalogue of every useful producer.
