independently in [1,6/5], all four within-partner member entries independently
in [1,2], and the other entries are fixed. The class is not defined as
tables admitting a desired strategy or certificate.

The claimed chain produces its strategic witnesses: explicit elimination
from the four active rewards gives two secondary rates as functions of two
primary rates; uniform face signs and Miranda produce the latter. The

four-unequal-rate equilibrium. It is not merely lowering rewards at one
unchanged common-c profile: independent changes to active member rewards
require recomputing its active ties.

## Why keep it internal

The mechanism and strategic support are the same robust two-phase branch
already present at c=1 and along the frozen c-family. The useful technical
addition is a uniform, finite-sized independent-parameter continuation
certificate, including large allowed changes to inactive within-pair
rewards. That is stronger than the tiny previously certified balls, but
the current packet does not identify a new structural boundary crossed by
this enlargement.

In particular, 6/5 is the endpoint of a successful fixed-square estimate,
not a demonstrated strategy or existence threshold. The note explicitly
leaves open how failure of its signs or quiet bounds selects another
matching, a longer calendar, or a stationary exit on the SAME arbitrary
input table. It neither solves the full twelve-member [1,2] box nor
strictly narrows the arbitrary paired-singleton cylinder by a source-wide
alternative.

I therefore do not affirm the independent conjecture-facing importance
needed for a standalone export now. The rectangle is useful internal
producer material for the actual table-wide coverage/disjunction problem;
its correctness and formalizability alone do not justify an additional
export. This decision requests no full proof gate, constants optimization,
or revision of the frozen paired-collision packet.
