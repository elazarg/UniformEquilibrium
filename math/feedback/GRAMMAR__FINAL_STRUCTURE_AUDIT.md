## Initial verdict before revision

The three-file consolidation is substantively lossless and has no competing
live draft.  The intended ownership split is now clear:

| File | Canonical responsibility |
|---|---|
| `meta/EXECUTABLE_COMPACT_STATE.md` | stopping-law semantics, elementary operations, quantitative stability, tight fusion, the terminal-Nash consumer at the elementary level, and the late-mass obstruction |
| `meta/GRAMMAR.md` | the sufficient non-elementary trace/control schema, its coherent-diagonal theorem, ranked control, and the maximal-root boundary |
| `meta/VANISHING_REACH_SUFFIX_NO_GO.md` | the narrowly scoped zero-reach conditional-suffix obstruction |

The old `GRAMMAR_2.md`, `EXECUTABLE_ADAPTERS.md`, and
`MAXIMAL_ROOT_ADAPTER_NO_GO.md` are absent, and no living file outside
historical feedback refers to those retired names.  Their valid mathematical
content is represented in the three files above.  I found no substantive
mathematical regression in the consolidation.

I would nevertheless describe `meta/GRAMMAR.md` as the canonical theorem
surface, not yet a completely clean canonical map.  Two live surfaces should
be aligned before the organization is called final:

1. `meta/EXECUTABLE_COMPACT_STATE.md` still presents a bare
   “ranked-regeneration passport” as one of the passports licensing a proof
   transition and ends by asking for a “closed-limit executable adapter or a
   renewable rank.”  The corrected grammar proves that bare pointwise rank is
   not trace-safe: it may be used only after reconstruction, while a
   trace-visible ranked edge needs the stronger closed tagged
   `TraceRank(K)` certificate.  The compact-state note does say that rank is
   an external adapter, so this is not an outright theorem contradiction, but
   its passport and conclusion language predates and obscures the canonical
   two-sort boundary.
2. The maintained
   `questions/QUITTING_COMPOSITIONALLY_SUFFICIENT_STATE.md` still asks for a
   convergent subsequence from *every* increasing compatible execution
   sequence.  The constant root diagram with laws `delta_m` refutes that
   statement in total variation.  It needs the tight-fusion assumptions used
   by Theorem 7.  It also still says “every nonterminal component” instead of
   the dispatch-selected successor relation, and its negative clause
   quantifies over “every proposed renewable ranked enlargement” without
   defining such a class.  Thus the canonical answer is sound while its live
   question still asks for a stronger false or undefined contract.

These are cleanup issues in the surrounding live surfaces, not defects in the
central adapter theorems as currently stated.

### Earlier `GRAMMAR.md`

Its strongest valid contribution was the vanishing-reach `CSR_1` example.
That material is preserved in `meta/VANISHING_REACH_SUFFIX_NO_GO.md`:

- arbitrary conditional target laws in the fibre over `delta_0`;
- unit protected-source law separation for the `delta_infinity` target;
- the law-intrinsic rank self-loop;
- the reach-weighted-consumer and external-rank nonclaims; and
- the off-path behavioral-provenance qualification.

One useful sharpening from the conditioning review is not stated in the
standalone note.  Section 2 is titled “Exact closure at depth one,” but proves
only that the zero-reach fibre contains every target law.  The short converse
argument gives the exact characterization

$$
\mathsf{CSR}_1
=
\{(\mu,S_1\mu):q_1(\mu)>0\}
\;\cup\;
\bigl(\{\delta_0\}\times\Delta(\mathbb N\sqcup\{\infty\})\bigr).
$$

Indeed, at positive limiting reach the fixed-floor conditioning estimate
forces the target to be `S_1 mu`, while zero depth-one reach forces
`mu = delta_0`.  This is not a lost proof from a surviving draft—the full
fibre and every consequence used later are present—but adding the equality
would make the heading and the canonical no-go maximally precise.

## Duplication audit

There is no longer a duplicate live proof of either the non-elementary adapter
schema or the maximal-root example.  The remaining overlap is mostly proper
interface repetition:

- `meta/GRAMMAR.md` repeats the stopping-law carrier and elementary operation
  names so that its constructor types can be read locally;
- its exact-root prefix estimate is the concrete `ClosedSelect` instance of
  the elementary prefix estimate; and
- Theorem 7 necessarily restates the tight-fusion inputs before adding the
  non-elementary edge induction.

One passage is genuinely duplicative.  Section 9.1 of `meta/GRAMMAR.md`
repeats the proof of the terminal-Nash consumer already given as Theorem 2 of
`meta/EXECUTABLE_COMPACT_STATE.md`.  The grammar adds only the named checked
terminal-to-uniform consumer.  A cleaner ownership split would cite the
elementary theorem, state that Theorem 7 supplies its hypotheses for the
enlarged grammar, and retain only the terminal-to-uniform corollary and its
producer nonclaim.

The suffix no-go's repetition of the fixed-floor conditioning estimate is
reasonable: that estimate is the local positive theorem needed to identify
the exact boundary of its counterexample.

## Residual literalizations in `meta/GRAMMAR.md`

The independent post-edit reviews passed the mathematics but left several
minor points that remain visible in the final file:

1. `Root(mu,x)` is described as satisfying “the exact finite product-root
   inequalities” without displaying or naming those inequalities.  The
   canonical note should define the relation or cite its exact source.
2. In the proof of Theorem 6, child convergence should be obtained from the
   `TraceEdge` certificate before closedness is applied to the full tagged
   relation containing that child coordinate.  The hypotheses support this
   order, but the prose currently states the steps in the opposite order.
3. Theorem 7's extraction list omits the compact branch-witness bundles of
   `TraceRank`, though the standing compact-auxiliary convention covers them.
4. Hypothesis 8 says branch tags stabilize “along the extracted subsequence”
   before the theorem performs the extraction.  Finiteness makes this a
   conclusion of diagonal extraction, not a separate hypothesis.
5. The rank-one example leaves its singleton outcome types and unique backward
   map implicit.  Stating them would make it literally instantiate every
   field of the pointwise rank interface.

There is also a harmless cross-file metric convention mismatch:
`meta/EXECUTABLE_COMPACT_STATE.md` and `meta/GRAMMAR.md` work with full
`l1` distance while `meta/VANISHING_REACH_SUFFIX_NO_GO.md` writes `d_TV`,
apparently in the standard half-`l1` convention.  Its bounds remain valid,
but one sentence fixing the convention would make the canonical trio easier
to compose quantitatively.

## Recommended final organization

Retain the three files and their present mathematical ownership.  No retired
draft should be restored.  The minimum cleanup is:

1. make the compact-state rank passport explicitly pointwise/post-limit and
   defer trace-visible rank to `meta/GRAMMAR.md`;
2. align the maintained question with the tight-fusion and selected-dispatch
   quantifiers;
3. reduce the duplicated terminal-Nash proof in the grammar to a corollary;
4. add the exact `CSR_1` equality or rename its section; and
5. add navigable links and the small literalizations listed above.

After those changes, `meta/GRAMMAR.md` would be a clean canonical map over one
elementary foundation and one focused boundary note, rather than merely the
correct canonical theorem surface it is now.
