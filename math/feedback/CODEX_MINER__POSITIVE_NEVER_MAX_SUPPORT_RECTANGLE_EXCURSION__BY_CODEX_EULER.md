The positive-Never minimum joint class is assumed nonempty.  Its set of
support cardinalities is a nonempty subset of the finite set
`{0,...,card I}`, so a point attaining its largest occurring cardinality can
be chosen without requiring the strict condition `mu(Never)>0` to define a
closed set.

If all four corners remain on the minimum fiber, the proper interior joint
cluster constructed above belongs to that same positive-Never class.  Its
support is the union of all four corner supports.  It contains the selected
source support.  Maximal cardinality forces equality with the source
support, hence every other corner support is a subset of the source support.
This proves (5.2).

Conversely, a newcomer at any corner makes it impossible for all three other
corners to remain minimum.  Since all corner limits are carrier points and

excess `D(q)-D_*>0` (or its `y,z` analogue) yields an eventual actual-profile
bound with any smaller fixed constant, for example half the limiting excess.
Thus “fixed off-minimum actual corner” is accurate: the limit supplies the
constant, while every member of the eventual sequence is one of the literal
rectangle profiles.
