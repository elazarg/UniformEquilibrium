# Review of the marked-row cap-defect floor

Reviewer: CODEX_DESCENDANT

Date: 2026-08-31

Verdict: **PASS, with a precise novelty boundary.**  The scratch theorem is
sound and genuinely strengthens the aggregate through-mark account at an
impure marked root.  It localizes a fixed cap-side defect at one actual date.
Combined with the checked cap-defect decomposition, it yields a one-date
singleton-atom or actual-paid-endpoint disjunction.  At an already pure
nonsingleton marked row, the existing collision-screening theorem is stronger.
Neither form controls cross-coordinate cap leakage after the paid update.

## The floor itself

If a terminal coalition has unconditional marked stage mass at least
\(\kappa>0\), the marked live mass is at least \(\kappa\), and the marked
root's absorption is also at least \(\kappa\).  Convergence of the literal
post-mark caps to a unique-all-Continue cap puts every late marked root inside
the checked robust absorption/defect moat.  Hence one fixed \(r_0>0\)
satisfies

\[
r_0\le R_{n,m_n}
\]

eventually, where \(R_{n,m_n}\) is total cap-side Nash defect against the
actual post-mark cap.  Multiplication by live mass gives the reached floor

\[
\kappa r_0\le L_{n,m_n}R_{n,m_n}.
\]

The proof uses no Nash property of the marked root and no stationary
restriction on the continuation cap.  The near-minimum-tail corollary
correctly obtains unique all Continue from the existing table-level radius.

## Exact one-date consequence

At one row, the checked coordinate inequality is

\[
R_i-P_i\le E_i,
\]

where \(P_i\) is the own-Quit option budget and \(E_i\) is the literal
prescribed-payoff endpoint defect.  After multiplication by marked live mass,
\(\sum_iP_i\) is at most \(2M\) times the total marked singleton stage mass.
Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c}
\text{some singleton has marked stage mass at least }
  \kappa r_0/(16M),\\
\text{or some one-date best-endpoint deviation has actual gain at least }
  \kappa r_0/8.
\end{array}}
\]

If the option budget pays at least half the reached moat, divide first by
\(2M\) and then among four singleton labels.  Otherwise the literal-defect
sum pays at least half, and division among four player coordinates gives the
paid endpoint.  This is stronger than the earlier through-mark ledger because
no calendar-time diffusion survives.

The note should not call the cap-defect floor itself an unconditional
prescribed-payoff row-gain floor: the singleton-option arm is real.  The
displayed disjunction is the exact claim.

## Pure-pair boundary

At a pure nonsingleton root, every own-Quit option coefficient vanishes and
the continuation is screened coordinatewise.  The checked identity
quittingTerminalSemanticDebtSum_pureNonsingletonRow_eq_totalDefect and global
minimality already give

\[
D_*\le R_{n,m_n}=E_{n,m_n}.
\]

With marked live mass at least \(\kappa\), the existing screened dispatch
selects an actual endpoint gain at least \(\kappa D_*/4\).  Thus on the
forced-pair branch the new moat identifies the same charge, with a weaker
unspecified constant.  Its genuine novelty is the impure-root localization.

## Conjecture-facing limit

Both alternatives land in known source-attached objects: a concentrated
singleton atom or a paid one-date endpoint.  Updating the payer reduces its
own debt exactly but may create equal or larger debt in the other three caps.
The theorem supplies no return, renewable zero-set rank, or exact cap-root
charge.  It removes time diffusion at the marked row; it does not consume the
cap-leakage residual.
