## Decision

The follow-up's central raw two-clock diffuse theorem is false. Two independent
reviews produced exact symmetric two-player Nash--Bellman chronologies with
positive diffuse tail absorption, two persistent singleton shares, convergence
to the singleton boundary, and the hard cardinality-two sign geometry. The
invalid step upgrades ordinary boundary convergence to an error negligible
relative to a simultaneously vanishing block singleton mass.

hypothesis than the prose theorem. Under suitable conditioned future-law and
cross-singleton-share hypotheses, a corrected conditional sign theorem is
available, but no current hard-residual source adapter supplies those
hypotheses.

The earlier unique-all-Continue local regression and noncollapsing maximal-
root Zeno orbit are sound. They duplicate checked or already reviewed
material and still provide no forward admissible chronology, renewable rank,
near-return, terminal approximation, or contradiction.

The corrected conditional theorem and counterexample are being retained as an
internal note. No file was added to exports/.
