## 3. Product-root gluing does not fill the live producer seam

### 3.1 The chronological atom packets already contain the roots

The concentrated reprojection packet stores an actual behavioral profile, a
literal marked date, its live root, the shifted actual tail, and the exact
semantic prefix identity.  The diffuse packet likewise stores actual
profiles and their literal stage clocks.  Its checked
`eventually_matchedChronology` already gives, at every fixed finite depth,

\[
 X_t=\operatorname{Prefix}(q_t,X_{t+1})
\]

for the actual live root `q_t` of the same profile.

Thus square minors reconstruct nothing missing at a retained calendar row:
the complete coalition law is already a product law by construction.  If one
forgets the profile and retains only the packet's selected coalition clock or
opponent-absorption clock, the opposite problem occurs: one has only one
scalar per row, not all `2^|I|` coalition fluxes required by the face/minor
criterion.

The diffuse alternative also explains why taking limits does not repair this.
Its mesh tends to zero while its cutoff tends to infinity.  Fixed-calendar
compactification can therefore lose the positive window mass to increasing
times.  Selecting clock quantiles retains mass but skips variable stretches
of calendar time.  Square minors are intrarow equations; they do not identify
the tail after one selected mark with the source at the next selected mark.

### 3.2 The exact compilers require strategic data, not probability data

`QuittingBudgetStablePacketData` already assumes literal roots.  In addition
it asks for exact semantic steps, a literal entrance annotation, a controlled
successor seam, two fixed marginal-hazard labels, and a legal successor port.
The all-error compiler separately needs a coordinatewise small initial debt.
The checked positive-minimum seed barrier shows that an actual positive-debt
source cannot itself be such a seed and that a small artificial anchor pays a
nonvanishing direct semantic seam.

The gluing theorem proves none of those fields.  In particular, exact
probability conservation

\[
 \sum_S m_t(S)=H_t,\qquad H_{t+1}=m_t(\varnothing)
\]

does not imply root Nash complementarity or a payoff/cap seam estimate.

### 3.3 Stronger rectangle incompatibility

The rectangle route is not merely missing a full flux table.  Its target-row
consumer produces a `QuittingLiteralPositiveActualRowPacket`: an actual row
with a strictly positive one-stage behavioral gain.  The checked theorem
`not_exists_literalNashBellmanEmbedding` says that this row cannot be embedded
as an exact Nash--Bellman edge while retaining its current payoff, literal
root, and literal shifted-tail payoff.  The approximate theorem
`gain_le_nashError_of_literal_root_tail` says that any unchanged-root/tail
repair must pay at least the retained gain as Nash error.

There is also a direct full-flux formulation of this obstruction.  Let `H` be
the live mass of such a row, `q` its actual root, and

\[
 m(S)=H p_q(S)\qquad(S\subseteq I).
\]

The packet's positive mass on one nonempty coalition forces `H>0`.  If a
second product root `q'` realizes the same complete flux table, division by
`H` gives `p_{q'}=p_q`; uniqueness in the reviewed boundary product-law
characterization gives `q'=q`.  Hence a square-minor reconstruction of the
full literal flux recovers exactly the already obstructed root.  It cannot
make the row exact Nash--Bellman without changing the flux, source payoff, or
tail payoff, or without charging the positive Nash error.

This is the sharpest failed implication in the probability lane:

> full literal reached-flux reconstruction + positive rectangle row does not
> imply an exact strategic prefix preserving that row's semantic fiber.

The failure is complementarity, not product compatibility.

## 4. Robust curl gives a hard-residual diagnostic, not a consumer

`PunishmentNormalResidualHardClass` is an LCP condition on the normalized
singleton matrix, restricted to the punishment-normal principal set.  The
Fin4 hard residual adds a terminal-gap witness, a full-support singleton
packet, and all-player punishment normality.  Neither structure contains
`IsAffineQuittingMembershipGain reward bias coefficient`, a coefficient
matrix for nonsingleton membership gains, positive symmetrizing weights, or
an approximate symmetrizer.

These are genuinely different data.  For each player `i`, an affine
membership table can be built by fixing the empty-background value at zero,
choosing arbitrary values on nonempty coalitions not containing `i`, and then
setting

\[
 r_i(S\cup\{i\})
 =r_i(S)+a_i+\sum_{j\in S}M_{ij}.
\]

The off-diagonal singleton values `r_i({j})` are among the freely chosen
background values, while `r_i({i})=a_i`.  Consequently a prescribed
normalized singleton matrix can coexist algebraically with arbitrary affine
curl coefficients `M`.  In particular the `ResidualHardClass` predicates,
which depend on that normalized singleton matrix, do not force small curl.
This algebraic independence does not assert preservation of the additional
Fin4 terminal witness or punishment values.

There is one valid quantitative composition.  Suppose a Fin4 hard residual
also comes with an affine representation `(a,M)`.  Put

\[
 \gamma=\texttt{witness.terminalGap},\qquad
 w_i=\texttt{packet.mass}(i),
\]

and let

\[
 \mu={1\over 1+3(2\,\texttt{bound}/\gamma)}.
\]

The packet fields give `w_i >= mu > 0`.  The terminal witness and the robust
weighted-potential profile imply `E(w)>=gamma`.  Therefore some player `i`
satisfies

\[
 \sum_{j\in C(i),\,j\ne i}
   |w_iM_{ij}-w_jM_{ji}|
 \ge 2\gamma w_i
 \ge 2\gamma\mu.
\tag{4.1}
\]

This is a clean, conjecture-facing necessary condition for an **affine** Fin4
counterexample.  It does not contradict any hard-residual field.  The hard
class supplies no weight sequence with curl tending to zero; its
nonprojective-Q-bar condition is not a symmetrizability statement.  Thus
(4.1) has no current downstream consumer and is not an answer to the
maintained semantic-closure question.

The paired-singleton calibration reinforces the scope boundary:
`periodTwo_residualHard_fullCore_nonstationary_but_uniform` shows that even
residual-hard membership plus full core and stationary exact nonexistence is
not counterexamplehood.  Algebraic hard-class information must not be read as
the missing semantic obstruction.

## 5. Social surplus reaches the inert wall rather than closing it

The reviewed escape identity needs

\[
 r_i(\{i\})\ge0\quad\text{for all }i,
 \qquad
 \sum_i r_i(S)\le0\quad\text{for every nonempty }S.
\tag{5.1}
\]

Neither condition follows from the current hard residual.  All-player
punishment normality says only

\[
 \text{punishmentValue}_i\le r_i(\{i\});
\]

it does not compare the singleton reward with zero.  Aggregate signs on
nonsingleton coalitions are absent entirely.  At the singleton-LCP level this
absence is exact: changing a nonsingleton payoff coordinate leaves the
normalized singleton matrix, and hence `ResidualHardClass`, unchanged while
it can reverse the aggregate sign of that coalition.

If (5.1) is added by hand, the social-surplus theorem does interact with the
live all-nonproper compactness residual: it produces an actual profile
attaining the positive minimum debt value.  But the maintained paid-cap
question already records the consequence of exact attainment.  The minimum
fiber contraction forces total absorption and cap displacement of the lifted
paid port to be zero, and `inertStall_of_initialDebt_eq_minimum` puts the port
in the literal inert arm.

Therefore the sign-class theorem converts

\[
 \text{all-nonproper nonattainment}
 \quad\longrightarrow\quad
 \text{attained exact-minimum inert stall},
\]

not to a uniform payoff or contradiction.  It is also incompatible with the
premise needed by retained-premium Candidate C: at an exact-minimum source the
absorption is zero, whereas Candidate C requires an eventual fixed positive
absorption floor.  No current checked theorem consumes the resulting inert
profile merely from (5.1).

## 6. Disposition and next admissible interfaces

No export was created.  None of these compositions supplies a theorem of the
three accepted kinds in the maintained questions.

The exact incompatible fields are:

| proposed composition | data already present | missing or contradicted field |
|---|---|---|
| minors + concentrated/diffuse atom | actual product root and literal semantic prefix | all-player root Nash, small seed, and summable source/endpoint matching |
| minors + positive rectangle row | complete actual product row | exact same-fiber Nash--Bellman compatibility; the positive gain proves its negation |
| robust curl + hard residual | singleton LCP hard class; in Fin4, a terminal gap and positive packet weights | affine coefficient representation and a produced small-curl weight; the gap instead forces the positive lower bound (4.1) |
| social surplus + hard residual | compact minimum sequence and terminal witness | singleton/nonempty-coalition sign chamber; after adding it, an inert-stall consumer |
| social surplus + retained Candidate C | actual exact minimum | Candidate C's positive absorption floor; contraction forces absorption zero |

A future product-gluing connection would become real only if a producer
returned **all** stage coalition fluxes and conservation while not already
returning roots, together with semantic annotations that survive the same
reconstruction.  A future curl connection needs a checked hard-residual
adapter producing affine coefficients and an approximate symmetrizer.  A
future social-surplus connection needs a theorem consuming the attained
exact-minimum inert stall.  None of those producer fields is currently
available.

The product-minor theorem remains the note most at risk of being forgotten:
it passed an adversarial review, appears nonduplicate, and is a clean
`MathUE`/Research formalization candidate.  Its proper scope is a probability
verifier/reconstructor, not the current source producer.
