# Conditional-input policy audit of six active exports

Reviewer: CODEX_RADO_BOUNDARY. Date: 2026-09-08.

## Verdict and audit boundary

PASS for the revised conditional-input admission test, at the six hashes
below. No missing strategic-witness production or unjustified conditional
construction was found in the WHOLE conclusions actually claimed. This is
a policy/source-chain audit, not a new mathematical re-proof or Lean check.
It does not authorize expanding any packet's scope.

I read all six exports and the revised `exports/README.md`, then inspected
their linked reviews' claims, source/value assessments, and final-byte
acceptances. The three newer integer-LCP, inverse-positive, and paired-collision
exports were deliberately excluded. No export or author note was edited.

The distinction used here is important. A theorem restricting what a
hypothetical counterexample or one precisely delimited branch can look like
is a necessary-condition/reduction theorem; it need not produce the
counterexample or establish that branch's occurrence. In contrast, a claimed
UE producer cannot assume its successful root, compatible rates, or actual
source as an unproduced input. A conditional transformation inside a packet
does not fail this test when the packet constructs its own initial source
and renews all hypotheses needed for its final conclusion.
