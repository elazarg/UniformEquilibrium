## Valid transformations do not remove these mismatches

Positive ownerwise scaling and player relabeling preserve the original
equilibrium question. To map this table into one of the fixed-singleton
sections above, normalize their own singleton values back to one. The
singleton rows have two zero receipts, own receipt one, and partner receipt
four. Any increasing ownerwise affine map taking these rows to those same
fixed rows must have shift zero and scale one. Relabeling can permute the
two cross matchings or the directed highs; it cannot make the multiset
{8/5,8/5,2,2} constant or put it inside [1,6/5]. The PairedCycle sign-count
obstruction persists even under arbitrary increasing affine owner maps.

Terminal-only shifts with zero Never are not automatically game
equivalences. The actual declaration
`quittingTerminalPayoff_playerwiseAffine` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Terminal/TerminalAffineReward.lean` retains the
shift multiplied by actual absorption probability. Proper-opponent
specializations can justify some transports, but no missing numerical
mapping into the displayed raw criteria is supplied by that observation.

Nor can one inherit the common h=2 four-pair equilibrium merely by lowering
the two high coordinates from 2 to 8/5. Those cross coalitions occur with
positive prescribed probability in that schedule; the lowering changes
prescribed payoffs and active indifferences. The valid harmless-lowering
argument in that source concerns unused prescribed within-pair member
coordinates, not these cross coordinates. Likewise changing an active
common-c pair reward is not an unused-deviation-only alteration.
