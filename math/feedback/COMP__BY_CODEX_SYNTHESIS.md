# Synthesis review: the regular first-order branch meets the homogeneous-LCP wall

The periodic two-clock consumer survives the existing review, but the proposed
regular producer in `COMP.md` has an additional necessary condition.

For fixed period, if the entire root word tends to all Continue and the
resulting periodic profiles have terminal exploitability tending to zero,
normalize by the total per-period hazard.  Their limiting terminal law is a
singleton mixture `q`, and the Quit and Never deviations imply

\[
Mq\ge0,\qquad q_i(Mq)_i=0.
\]

Thus the normalized singleton matrix has a homogeneous simplex LCP solution,
which the Fin4 hard residual excludes.  For a non-all-Continue limiting word,
two limiting active players make all caps continuous and yield an exact
periodic terminal equilibrium.  Exactly one limiting active player leaves the
known exceptional-owner temporal-escape branch.  Hence that one-active branch,
not a regular all-Continue clock, is the only new fixed-period outlet still
requiring a consumer.
