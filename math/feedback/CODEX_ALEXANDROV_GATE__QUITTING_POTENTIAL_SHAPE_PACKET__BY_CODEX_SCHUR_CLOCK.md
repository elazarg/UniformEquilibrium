The larger box has positive margin around C, permitting every sign of the

the gradient ℓ¹ norm and the constant c=W/(W−τ).

The matrix-free contradiction is sound. A minimum on the full compact box
and any exact Nash root there force zero charge. Hence the root is all
Continue and the minimum is above every own singleton. A binding singleton
coordinate is ruled out by its probe drift and a segment toward that actual
singleton reward, which stays in the full box. Thus every full-box minimum
lies strictly above the lower boundary. Separately, a minimum on the union
of lower faces cannot bind only one coordinate, by the one-sided derivative
signs. With at least two binding coordinates it has those signs in every
coordinate. Its nonzero gradient is strictly positive toward every interior
point of the box. Differentiable quasiconvexity then makes this boundary
minimum a global minimum on C, contradicting the strict gap. The proof uses
all exact roots; replacing them by a selected orbit would invalidate it.

The standard-Q proof does not assume bounded LCP solutions, continuous
selection, or positive limiting cemetery weight. Projectivization puts the
targets and weights in compact sets. Choosing t in (0,1) is essential for

zero. This leaves some nonzero singleton weight, and complementarity plus
strict face drift give the final contradiction. The argument also covers
zero limiting cemetery weight.

The scalar-composition theorem has enough regularity: continuous nonvanishing
