## 1. Exact claim and independent proof check

Let Z_R=[−R,R]^8 with R>0. Suppose permitted maps include constant resets
to points p_j whose affine span is all of R^8, with at least one p_j in
the ordinary interior of the box. If a real polynomial h of degree at
most two obeys h(z)≤h(p_j) for every z in the box and every j, then h
is constant on R^8.

Indeed every reset point is a global maximizer on the box and all have
the same value. At an interior reset p, write

    h(p+v)=h(p)+g·v+vᵀHv,    H symmetric.

Small displacements of both signs are allowed in every ambient direction.
The first-order inequality gives g=0 and the second-order inequality
gives H negative semidefinite. For every reset difference v_j=p_j−p,
equal maximal values imply v_jᵀHv_j=0. Negative semidefiniteness implies
Hv_j=0, not merely a scalar orthogonality condition. Since those
differences span the ambient space, H=0. This also eliminates the original
linear coefficients. Boundary reset points cause no problem: only the
one point used to establish global negative semidefiniteness must be
interior.

For the raw maximum debt d(u,b)=max_i(b_i−u_i), a constant h≤d on the
FULL independent box satisfies h≤−2R by using u_i=R and b_i=−R for all
i. It therefore cannot be positive at the Never seed. The constancy
argument itself needs neither this minorant nor the seed condition.

Explicit falsification attempts confirm the role of the hypotheses:

- Without an interior reset, h(z)=‖z‖² is nonconstant yet has equal
  global maxima at every box vertex, whose affine span is full.
- Without full affine span, h(z)=−z₁² has an interior maximizer and
  remains maximal at all reset points in the hyperplane z₁=0, but is
  nonconstant.
- Merely requiring monotonicity on the actual semantic carrier does
  not authorize either two-sided ambient variations or the point with
  raw debt −2R. That weaker domain is not the theorem's hypothesis.

## 2. Actual reset vectors and exact determinant

I rebuilt the table directly from the reward formula, not from the
displayed reset matrix. With β>0 and a>max(1,β), its singleton vector is
(1,0,0,0), its exceptional outsider reward is r₁({0})=a, every other
singleton outsider reward is −a, and its nonsingleton rewards are

    r_i(S)=β ε_i t_i(S)t_(i+1)(S),
    ε=(1,1,1,−1),    t_i(S)=2·1_(i∈S)−1.

For a deterministic coalition S with at least two quitters, every player
faces at least one OTHER sure quitter after either of its current
actions. No continuation is reached even by a complete unilateral
behavioral replacement. Consequently the full prefix map is the constant

    p_S=(r_i(S), max(r_i(S),r_i(S△{i})))_i.

This formula is valid on every formal box point, independently of whether
its cap and payoff coordinates are jointly realizable. It does not replace
the unrestricted cap with a stationary cap.

The nine regenerated rows agree with all eight coordinates of the
original table. Subtracting p_01 from the remaining rows in the stated
order gives precisely

    det D=−256 β⁷(a−β).

Two independent exact computations were used:

1. A symbolic determinant from reset rows generated by the reward rule
   and the explicit maximum cases a>β>0.
2. Rational Gaussian elimination after normalizing β=1. The determinant
   is affine in ρ=a/β, because ρ occurs in only one matrix column. At
   ρ=1 it is zero; at ρ=2 the elimination pivots are
   (−2,2,−2,−4,2,−1,4,1), with three row swaps, giving −256.
   Thus the normalized determinant is −256(ρ−1), which proves the
   displayed homogeneous formula. The additional ρ=3 check gives −512.

The reset p_23 has all coordinates equal to ±β, so it is strictly
interior for EVERY R≥a. All reward coordinates have magnitude at most a.
Thus applicability is not an artifact of enlarging a deficient reward
box, and it also holds for the production box discussed below.

The function-barrier domain is literally the product of two coordinate
interval boxes. It does NOT include a hidden b≥u constraint, carrier
membership, punishment inequality, or attainable-law condition. Its
`le_prefix` field quantifies over every box point and every independent
root simplex. The canonical bound is the sum of absolute reward entries,
not their maximum; it is at least a, so the strict interior check survives.
Its raw maximum-debt objective is not clipped at zero off the carrier.

A quadratic restricted to this box is bounded and continuous, hence
upper semicontinuous. If its obstacle and monotonicity inequalities held,
the quoted interface would therefore accept it. Iterating monotonicity
from Never and then applying the obstacle bounds its seed value by every
finite-word debt. The checked duality identifies their infimum with the
unrestricted semantic value. Thus a POSITIVE seed really would certify
a positive all-behavior gap; the theorem proves only that quadratics
cannot supply such a seed on this family.

The closed-set interface is different: `prefix_mem` quantifies only over
points of the selected set. A quadratic inequality defining such a set
does not imply that its defining function is globally prefix-monotone.
The reset-rank proof cannot be used to exclude that interface. The
SUM-potential construction has global decreasing potential inequalities
and a different objective/zero-set contract; it is not the MAX function
barrier merely under another name.
