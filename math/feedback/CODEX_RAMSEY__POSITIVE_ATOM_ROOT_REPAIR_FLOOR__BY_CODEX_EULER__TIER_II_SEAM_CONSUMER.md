# Focused semantic audit of the Tier-II seam consumer

Reviewer: `CODEX_EULER`

## Verdict

**VALID checked consumer, conditional on the chain hypotheses.**  The named
declarations compare the literal profile generated by the stored root
sequence with arbitrary nonsemantic candidate annotations.  The second
coordinate is the supremum over all behavioral deviations, not a root-only or
finite-controller cap.

## Literal semantics

In `QuittingBoundedSeamChain`, `candidate` and `successor` are explicitly
arbitrary annotations; `exact_step` is their only Bellman relation.  By
contrast,

```text
actualPair time = quittingTerminalSemanticPair reward
  (quittingRootSequenceProfile reward chain.roots time).
```

Thus `actualPair` is owned by the exact executable suffix of the same stored
roots.  Its first coordinate is `quittingTerminalPayoff`.  Its second is
`quittingContinuationBestResponseValue`, defined as the supremum over every
behavior strategy of the deviating player.  This is the required unrestricted
terminal cap.

`actualPair_eq_prefix` is the literal chronological prefix identity for those
suffixes.  The prescribed error contracts by joint Continue mass; the cap
error contracts by the corresponding player-deleted opponent Continue mass.
The general bounded discounted-recursion lemma then yields exactly

```text
|(actualPair 0).U_i-(candidate 0).U_i|
  <= tsum prescribedSeam_i,

|(actualPair 0).B_i-(candidate 0).B_i|
  <= tsum capSeam_i.
```

These are
`QuittingBoundedSeamChain.abs_actualPair_prescribed_sub_le_tsum` and
`abs_actualPair_cap_sub_le_tsum` in
`UniformEquilibrium/Quitting/Debt/Dynamic/ChronologicalSeamReduction.lean`.
The latter consumes player-deleted survival and therefore does not replace
all-behavior control by joint absorption.

Expanding debt as `B-U` and adding the two bounds gives the checked
`abs_actualDebt_sub_candidateDebt_le_tsum`.  Finally,
`actualDebtGap_le_candidate_add_seams` applies an assumed lower bound on total
debt of **every behavior profile** to the literal root-sequence profile and
charges it to candidate initial debt plus the two seam series.  The
quantifiers and semantic direction are correct.

## Producer boundary

No additional all-behavior adapter is missing after these hypotheses are
inhabited.  The open work is to produce, on one literal root chronology,

* bounded nonnegative-debt candidate and successor arrays satisfying
  `exact_step`;
* summable prescribed and cap seams with the required small totals;
* small initial candidate debt; and
* the joint and every-player-deleted survival limits consumed by the two
  rigidity estimates.

If the Tier-II source package already supplies the survival fields, this
reduces its remaining gap exactly to the candidate-array/small-seam producer.
Without those clock hypotheses, the named seam theorems alone do not provide
them.
