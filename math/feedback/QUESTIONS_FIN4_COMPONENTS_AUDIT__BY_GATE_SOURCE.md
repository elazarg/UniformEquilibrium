# Source audit of seven Fin4 component questions

Reviewer: `GATE_SOURCE`

## Verdict

None of the seven mathematical problems is fully solved.  Two documents need
substantive replacement, one contains a wrong source attribution, and one
roadmap edge is not justified by the checked interfaces.

The current dependency picture is:

```text
minimum atom
  -> source-attached concentrated singleton
  -> source-attached forced pair
  -> minimum return or uniform escape

minimum return
  -> finite renewable support descent
  -> positive slope | flat support entry | off-minimum paid row

every positive-minimum tangent frontier
  -> vanishing-debt atom access
     (support entry can fix the zero-debt entrant as observer)
  -> [open source-attached two-tier shadowing producer]

off-minimum paid row
  -> actual-profile paid-cap port
  -> charged return | quantitative descent | inert stall
       charged return is consumed
       descent and the same-source inert stall are open

special hard-residual double port
  -> source regeneration | repaired regeneration | paired unique caps
       regeneration has no finite rank
       paired unique caps are open

strict normalized inert source
  -> minimum return | zero-density Zeno source | fixed-cap barrier
       the zero-density source contracts to a host endpoint or a
       source-attached concentrated packet
```

The important correction is that the generic paid terminal exit and the
specialized paired unique-cap obstruction are not one source-attached chain.
The former produces one arbitrary actual paid-cap source.  The latter is
constructed independently from a stationary singleton-base source and its
literal owner repair.  A terminal-exit question may use the paired theorem as
an independent same-table route, but may not call it the terminal exit's inert
output.

All caps in the inspected sources are suprema over complete unilateral
behavioral strategies.  The fully screened cap comparisons are uniform over
that same class.  No stationary-only interpretation is needed below.

## 1. `FIN4_ATLAS_CONCENTRATED_SINGLETON.md`

### Status

**Open but deliberately redundant.**  It is a legitimate direct-consumer
shortcut, not a remaining producer branch.  Current source-preserving
constructions already turn every minimum-atom entrance into a strong
concentrated packet and then into a fixed forced-pair stream.

The exact sources are
`MinimumSingletonClockCompression.lean`,
`SourcePreservingSingletonFrames.lean`, and
`SourcePreservingForcedPair.lean`.  In particular,
`FinFourSourcePreservingSingletonFrame.nonempty_forcedPairPacket` preserves the
literal marked date and post-date spine and supplies the pure pair, zero
forced-owner defect, positive payer defect, mass floor, and exact payer-debt
subtraction.

### Correction

The sentence saying that punishment normality forces pair mode has the wrong
provenance.  The full-gap singleton join is supplied by the terminal
exploitability witness through
`exists_terminalGap_collision_at_singleton`; punishment normality is not the
step used by the checked constructor.

The question otherwise states the real obstruction correctly: changing the
payer's strategy can change the other three unrestricted caps, and the pure
pair screens its post-row tail.

### Recommended timeless replacement

```text
# Consume a source-attached concentrated singleton

Let a four-player quitting game have positive minimum terminal-semantic debt
D_* and a positive exploitability gap against every behavioral profile.
Suppose one fixed minimum source chronology contains an actual marked profile
with a singleton terminal coalition {j}, unconditional marked mass at least
lambda > 0, and a retained complete post-date tail.

The terminal gap permits a literal one-date normalization to a pure pair
{j,o}: o has zero marked defect, another player p has a fixed positive marked
defect, the marked mass and complete post-date tail are preserved, and changing
only p to its better endpoint subtracts exactly its gain from p's unrestricted
debt.

Construct terminal approximate Nash profiles with one limiting payoff, a
positive admissible payoff return, or a source-preserving finite-rank descent
with terminal consumers.  Alternatively prove that the supplied configuration
is incompatible with positive minimum debt, or give an explicit four-player
positive-gap table realizing all of it.

The local own-debt subtraction is not by itself total-debt or support descent:
the other players' unrestricted caps may change.  Replacing only the screened
post-pair tail is also ineffective.

This is an optional direct shortcut.  The existing source-preserving route
already sends the same data to the minimum-return/uniform-escape split.
```

## 2. `FIN4_DOUBLE_UNIQUE_CAP_CLOSURE.md`

### Status

**Current and open.**  The checked source interface is accurately described by
`FinFourPaidCapMaximalDoubleRegeneration.lean`.  It applies to the specialized
stationary singleton-base source and its literal owner repair.  Each is an
actual behavioral profile, but their cap vectors and terminal laws are
separate.  Uniqueness makes every selected exact prefix root all Continue and
the selected ports literally inert.

This does not duplicate the strict normalized inert point.  That point may be
an unattained carrier limit and carries a density passport.  The double port
consists of two attained actual profiles and carries same-law reset data on
each side.

No newer checked result consumes the paired state.  Fixed-law cap rigidity and
the all-Continue basin theorems explain the fixed face but do not eliminate it.

### Recommended timeless replacement

```text
# Consume paired unique all-Continue caps

In a four-player quitting game with positive minimum debt and a positive
all-behavior exploitability gap, suppose a stationary singleton-base
paid/reset source and its literal owner repair are both given.  Each actual
profile has a paid first-disagreement row, a zero-debt reset coordinate,
positive opponent incidence, and its complete terminal law.  At each profile's
unrestricted cap vector, all Continue is the unique exact product Nash root.

Prove that this paired configuration is impossible, construct terminal
approximate Nash profiles or a positive admissible payoff return, or regenerate
a source with a strict well-founded rank decrease.  An explicit four-player
positive-gap table realizing the complete paired configuration is an
acceptable negative answer.

The two caps and laws are not assumed equal.  Exact prefixing at either frozen
cap is the zero-absorption identity and therefore cannot be the required
operation.
```

## 3. `FIN4_FULLY_SCREENED_CUMULATIVE_CHARGE_CONSUMER.md`

### Status

**Open as a direct shortcut, but stale as an atlas component.**  The fully
screened Zeno branch is no longer a distinct producer residual.
`FinFourActualZenoDeletedSurvivalSource.nonempty_fullyScreenedClearingFamily`
and `FullyScreenedClearingPacketResult.consumerResult` clear at most four
players and send the source to the already maintained concentrated-packet
consumer.  This is recorded in
`FIN4_FULLY_SCREENED_FINITE_CLOCK_CLEARING_TO_CONCENTRATED_PACKET.md`.

The cumulative ledger in the question is not consumed by that clearing
theorem.  Its direct conversion to prescribed-payoff charge remains open.  It
is therefore reasonable to keep this as an explicitly optional stronger
question, but it should not be listed as an unresolved atlas SCC or required
roadmap edge.

The finite timing-Nash return-floor theorem is only a no-go for preserving
screening during Nashification; the all-Continue identity block prevents it
from consuming the ledger.

### Recommended timeless replacement

```text
# Directly consume fully screened cumulative cap defect

Let actual four-player profiles consist of finite source-attached prefix words
followed by one retained forced-pair source.  Suppose joint survival through
the words and every deleted-player survival probability tend to zero.  If
R_{n,t} is the total exact root defect against the complete cap of the actual
suffix at row t, assume the exact prefix account gives

  sum_t survival_{n,t} R_{n,t} >= D_*/2

eventually, where D_* > 0 is the global minimum debt.

Convert this order-one cumulative suffix-cap defect into terminal approximate
Nash profiles, a positive source-attached admissible payoff return, or a
renewable finite-rank source descent.  Alternatively prove that the supplied
fully screened positive-gap data are inconsistent, or realize them in an
explicit positive-gap table.

Selecting a positive cap-defect row is not enough: it must become an actual
prescribed-payoff edge with its continuation seam closed.  The known finite
clock-clearing theorem already maps this source to the general concentrated
packet problem, so this question asks only for a stronger direct use of the
cumulative ledger.
```

## 4. `FIN4_PAID_RESET_REGENERATION_RANK.md`

### Status

**Current and open.**  The exact one-step inputs are in
`PaidCapMaximalOneStepRegeneration.lean`,
`FinFourPaidResetDescentRegeneration.lean`, and
`FinFourPaidResetDoubleDescentRegeneration.lean`.  Positive maximal absorption
gives a literal lower-debt actual descendant with a fresh reset dispatch.
Both specialized double-port descent arms now have that provenance.

What remains absent is exactly the requested finite rank or return.  Repeated
canonical exact prefixes can have summable absorption while preserving
normalized debt support and noncollapsing paid/law data.  Those scalar
identities do not terminate the orbit.  Near the minimum fibre, all compatible
paid-cap ports can have arbitrarily small absorption and cap displacement, so
no uniform positive real debt step can be the rank.

The question is not resolved by the renewable support-cardinality rank in
`CanonicalPairRenewableSourceRank.lean`; that rank applies to minimum-fibre
full-replacement endpoints, not to arbitrary off-minimum paid/reset
regenerations.

### Recommended timeless replacement

```text
# Orient renewable paid/reset regeneration

Let an actual four-player paid/reset source carry a positive paid row, a
zero-debt reset player, positive opponent incidence, its complete terminal
law, and a fixed positive global minimum reference D_*.

Against its unrestricted cap, choose an exact product root of maximal
absorption.  If the root absorbs, prefixing it produces another actual
paid/reset source with strictly smaller total debt and a fresh reset dispatch.
If it does not absorb, all Continue is the unique exact root at that cap.

Iterate the absorbing alternative.  Prove that it admits a finite
source-preserving rank, compiles into a positive admissible payoff return, or
terminates in profiles yielding terminal approximate Nash play.  Alternatively
prove that an infinite regenerated orbit is impossible, or exhibit an
explicit positive-gap table carrying one.

Strict decrease of real debt, summable root absorption, and source
reconstruction alone are not well-founded.  Every recursive state and every
terminal consumer must retain the actual paid row, reset coordinate, incidence,
law, and common minimum reference.
```

## 5. `FIN4_RENEWAL_TERMINAL_EXIT_CONSUMERS.md`

### Status

**The three exits are current and open, but the stated paid-exit dependency is
wrong.**  `CanonicalPairRenewableSourceRank.lean` proves the finite renewable
descent and leaves exactly positive total slope, flat support entry, and an
off-minimum paid first disagreement.  `CanonicalPairMinimumEndpointRenewal.lean`
retains the same hard residual and gives a correct conditional composition.

For the first two exits, chronological shadowing is indeed a sufficient
consumer.

For the third exit, the checked actual-profile paid-cap theorem gives, on the
same literal paid profile,

```text
charged near-return | quantitative debt descent | inert stall.
```

The charged arm is consumed.  The quantitative descent and the **single-source
inert stall** remain open.  The specialized paired unique-cap theorem does not
come from this arbitrary terminal-exit profile.  It is an independent
same-reward-table construction.  Thus the present sentence identifying the
paid exit's two residuals with “paid/reset rank” and “paired unique caps” is
not source-faithful.

This exposes one missing maintained question: consume an arbitrary actual
paid-cap quantitative descent or inert stall on the same source.  The
paid/reset-rank question applies only after zero-debt reset and positive
incidence have also been produced.

### Recommended timeless replacement

```text
# Consume the terminal exits of renewable Fin4 support descent

A source-preserving finite descent through minimum-fibre full replacements
terminates at one of:

1. a positive total tangent slope;
2. flat tangent motion which creates debt outside the active support; or
3. an actual off-minimum full-replacement sequence carrying a fixed paid
   first-disagreement row.

For each alternative, construct terminal approximate Nash profiles with one
limiting payoff, a positive admissible payoff return, or a contradiction to
the positive exploitability gap.

For alternatives 1 and 2, it is enough to construct source-attached
chronological debt shadowing at every accuracy.  For alternative 3, apply any
consumer valid for the same actual paid profiles.  The exact paid-cap split
leaves a charged return, quantitative descent, or an inert stall; only the
charged return is already terminally consumed.  The remaining descent or
single-source stall must be consumed without replacing the terminal-exit
profile by an independently selected source.

The finite support descent itself is supplied.  Reconstructing it, or
producing another unconsumed paid row, is not an answer.
```

I recommend adding a separate focused question titled “Consume an arbitrary
actual paid-cap descent or inert stall” unless this obligation is kept
explicitly inside the terminal-exit capstone.

## 6. `FIN4_STRICT_NORMALIZED_INERT_IMPOSSIBILITY_OR_MODEL.md`

### Status

**Open, but the present statement stops before the strongest checked
dispatch.**  The one-density tent inequality is correct, but it is no longer
the sharp frontier object.

The current source chain is:

1. `nonempty_normalizedInertVanishingDensityBoundary` selects the
   density-to-zero boundary from the source-attached strict inert family;
2. its outcome is positive-density minimum return, zero limiting passport, or
   one carrier limit satisfying
   `root defect >= debt * root absorption` for every product root;
3. the zero-passport arm produces an actual Zeno source and then either a
   fixed positive-host endpoint or a same-source fully screened clearing
   family; and
4. the clearing family reaches the general concentrated-packet consumer.

The minimum-return arm goes back to the maintained minimum-return component.
The fixed host, concentrated collision output, and fixed-cap barrier remain
unconsumed.  A scalar Zeno model shows that the tent ledger alone does not
give a rank, but it is not a quitting-game counterexample.

Thus the current question is stale as a leaf description and overlaps both
the concentrated-singleton and fully screened questions.

### Recommended timeless replacement

```text
# Consume the strict normalized inert boundary

Let a source-attached four-player forced-pair family have normalized minimum
debt L > D_* and fixed positive paid-pair density.  Its density-to-zero
dispatch gives one of:

1. a positive-density return to the global minimum component;
2. an actual zero-density Zeno source which produces either a positive-host
   concentrated endpoint or a source-attached concentrated packet; or
3. a carrier point x such that, for every product root q,

      totalRootDefect(q; B(x)) >= D(x) * absorption(q).

In case 3 the barrier excludes every positive-absorption exact product root;
combined with finite root-Nash existence, it leaves all Continue as the only
exact root.  The point x need not be attained by one behavioral profile.

Consume cases 2 and 3 into terminal approximate Nash profiles, a positive
admissible payoff return, or a renewable finite-rank source descent, or prove
that they are incompatible with the positive minimum and hard residual.  An
explicit four-player table realizing the complete source-attached boundary
with a positive all-behavior exploitability gap is an acceptable negative
answer.

The unsigned root-defect barrier, another density split, and the scalar Zeno
ledger are not terminal consumers.
```

## 7. `FIN4_TWO_TIER_CHRONOLOGICAL_SHADOWING.md`

### Status

**Open and central, but its entrance data are misstated.**  The checked
`QuittingVanishingDebtAtomAccess` is not a positive terminal-law atom of one
fixed coalition.  It is a fixed mover, observer, and positive charge such that
eventually either:

- a terminal coordinate of the prescribed payoff difference carries a fixed
  signed contribution; or
- one common pure-time observer response creates a terminal response-square
  contribution while that response endpoint's observer debt tends to zero.

Every positive-minimum tangent frontier already has this alternative,
independently of the terminal exit tag.  Support entry additionally permits
choosing the zero-debt entrant as observer.

The two-tier producer boundary itself is correct.  The exact checked facts
are:

- the budget-stable iteration theorem consumes literal compatible packets
  with two fixed labels and an explicit coordinatewise small-debt seed;
- the bare packet-system type is universally inhabited for every reward table
  with at least two players, so inhabitance without source attachment is
  vacuous;
- a positive-minimum actual annotation cannot be the small-debt seed; and
- if an actual source of debt `D_*` is implemented by a seed with coordinate
  debts at most `eta`, with one-sided payoff and cap errors `alpha_i,beta_i`,
  then

  ```text
  D_* <= 4 eta + sum_i (alpha_i + beta_i).
  ```

Thus a vanishing implementation seam cannot hide the fixed positive debt.
The source/payoff-to-candidate implementation is a genuine second producer,
not bookkeeping.

The source-attached internal-debt telescope is another necessary account: a
compatible chain with divergent marked exposure and summable seams can force
divergent cumulative internal exact-Bellman debt drain.  No checked theorem
turns that drain into prescribed-payoff charge or an admissible return.

### Recommended timeless replacement

```text
# Construct source-attached two-tier chronological shadowing

Let a four-player positive-minimum tangent source carry fixed players p != o
and a fixed positive charge.  Along its actual source profiles, assume the
checked vanishing-debt atom alternative: eventually either a prescribed
payoff-difference atom retains the charge, or one common pure-time response by
o retains a response-square atom while o's debt at the response endpoint
tends to zero.

Tier I.  From this actual source data, construct reached ports with one
globally bounded semantic annotation family.  At every sufficiently small
scale, produce a literal finite Bellman block from the current port to a
successor port such that:

- the entrance and successor annotations are attached to the actual source;
- the complete payoff-and-cap seam and loss of future availability have an
  operationally sublinear total modulus;
- the same two fixed players p and o each accumulate a fixed positive fraction
  of the scale as Quit hazard; and
- the successor retains enough availability to repeat the construction.

Tier II.  For every requested accuracy eta, either implement a legal initial
port whose annotation has every debt coordinate at most eta, with all
unrestricted behavioral payoff and cap errors explicitly controlled, or
directly construct a uniform-equilibrium payoff.  The implementation errors
must pay the positive source-debt gap; semantic closeness without this account
does not suffice.

Compose the two tiers into one literal infinite chronology with summable
payoff/cap seams, divergent exposure of both fixed labels, vanishing joint and
deleted-player survival, and initial coordinate debts at most eta.  This must
control every unilateral behavioral deviation, including Never and
arbitrarily late stopping.

Inhabiting an abstract packet type without actual source anchoring, using a
positive-minimum source itself as the small-debt seed, or choosing fresh labels
at each block does not answer the question.
```

1. Correct the source attribution in the concentrated-singleton question.
2. Replace the terminal-exit paid branch by the generic same-source
   descent/inert obligation; do not route it directly to the paired double
   port.
3. Replace the strict-inert question by the density-boundary normal form.
4. Replace the shadowing entrance by the actual payoff-difference/response-
   square alternative and include the implementation inequality.
5. Mark the fully screened cumulative-charge question as an optional direct
   shortcut, not an atlas node.
6. Add a focused generic actual-profile paid-cap descent/inert question, or
   keep that obligation explicitly and self-contained in the renewable-exit
   capstone.
