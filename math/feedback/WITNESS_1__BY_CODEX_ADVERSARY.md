## Verdict

**FAIL as a Zeno Nash--Bellman witness and FAIL for export.**  The rational
matrix calculations, quadratic reward-table formulas, one-stage mixed-root
calculation, and uniqueness of all Continue at the limiting cap are correct.
The conditional stable-pencil reduction is also valid after its scope is
stated carefully.

The decisive defect is chronology orientation.  If

\[
 V(a)=s+b(a)\mathbf 1,
 \qquad b(a)=-\frac{a}{2(1-a/4)^3},
\]

and (a^+) is defined by (23), then the calculation proves

\[
 \boxed{V(a^+)=\operatorname{Prefix}_{q(a)}V(a)},
 \qquad a^+<a/2.                                      \tag{R1}
\]

This is an **outward prefix-state ray**.  In a forward behavioral profile the
current state must instead be the prefix of its next suffix state.  The roots
in (R1) are therefore ordered from right to left in every finite executable
word.  They do not form the consecutive dates (q(a_0),q(a_1),\ldots) of one
forward behavioral profile.  Reversing (R1) makes the parameters grow by more
than a factor two and leaves the stated interval after finitely many steps.

Thus the document has constructed an exact rational **cap-only outward-prefix
regression**, not an exact fully mixed behavioral Zeno orbit.  It has not
constructed a `QuittingForwardExactCapTail` either: that checked structure
also requires complete terminal-semantic pairs, carrier membership, and the
prescribed-payoff coordinate, none of which is supplied by the cap vectors
in the example.

The strongest honest result is worth retaining internally, but it is not a
counterexample model, source adapter, terminal consumer, or export packet.

## 1. Generic stable-pencil theorem: valid conditional algebra

Suppose a normalized omega chain satisfies

\[
 \tau_n=\rho_nc_n+(1-\rho_n)\tau_{n+1},
 \qquad
 M\tau_n+\rho_nCc_n=0,                         \tag{R2}
\]

with (ho_n\ge\eta>0).  Eliminating (c_n) gives exactly

\[
 (M+C)\tau_n=(1-\rho_n)C\tau_{n+1}.             \tag{R3}
\]

If (C) is invertible and (T=C^{-1}(M+C)), then

\[
 T\tau_n=(1-\rho_n)\tau_{n+1}.                  \tag{R4}
\]

Let (K) be the closed cone generated by the forward tail of the
(	au)-orbit.  Then (T(K)\subseteq K), and positivity gives

\[
 \|Ty\|_1\le(1-\eta)\|y\|_1\qquad(y\in K).      \tag{R5}
\]

If (Ty=0) for some nonzero (y\in K), normalize (y) and take
(lambda=0).  Otherwise the continuous map
(y\mapsto Ty/\|Ty\|_1) acts on the compact convex base
(K\cap\Delta_4), so Brouwer gives a fixed point.  In both cases there are
(x\in K\cap\Delta_4) and
(lambda\in[0,1-\eta]) with

\[
 Tx=\lambda x,
 \qquad
 \boxed{(M+(1-\lambda)C)x=0}.                   \tag{R6}
\]

This proof is sound.  The zero-image case should be mentioned explicitly,
because the normalized map is otherwise undefined there.

There are two scope corrections.

1. A proper-support (x) is an ambient boundary pencil certificate.  It does
   **not** mean that the actual source behavior has already descended to that
   face.  The eigenvector can be a limit of conic combinations of tail
   barycenters and need not be an omega-chain state, much less a regenerated
   source point.
2. The conclusion is normalized matrix algebra only.  It gives no absolute
   root scale, payoff/cap pair, terminal law, or behavioral chronology.

### Why the equality in (R2) is plausible and genuinely conditional

The checked omega chain currently exposes only `work_nonpos` and
`current_work_eq_zero`.  Nevertheless, under the stronger source hypothesis
used in the document--every finite current hazard coordinate is eventually
strictly positive--the unmultiplied equality is a valid short corollary.

`QuittingTailNormalizedCapFlow.subseq_scaledEndpointSlack_tendsto` identifies
the limit of renewal ratio times finite endpoint slack with minus the limiting
work coordinate.  Exact finite complementarity and strict positivity make
that finite slack exactly zero.  Applying this at each omega offset through
the common source subsequence gives work zero in every coordinate, even if an
omega-limit current coordinate itself vanishes.

This is not currently a named theorem in
`BallisticNormalizedOmegaChain.lean`, but it follows from the checked
ingredients.  It does not follow merely from positivity of the *limiting*
current vector.

## 3. Recurrence (23): exact orientation failure

The scalar function

\[
 f(u)=\frac{2u}{(1-u/4)^3}
\]

is strictly increasing on ((0,1)), with (f(u)>2u).  Hence every
(a_t\in(0,1)) has a unique (a_{t+1}\in(0,1)) satisfying

\[
 a_t=f(a_{t+1}),
 \qquad a_{t+1}<a_t/2.                            \tag{R13}
\]

Since (b(a_{t+1})=-a_t/4), (R12) becomes

\[
 \boxed{
 V(a_{t+1})=operatorname{PrefixCap}_{q(a_t)}V(a_t).}
 \tag{R14}
\]

For a genuine forward behavioral tail, however, the Bellman orientation is

\[
 B_t=operatorname{PrefixCap}_{x_t}B_{t+1},
 \quad
 x_t\text{ exact against }B_{t+1}.                \tag{R15}
\]

Equations (R14) and (R15) point in opposite directions.  A finite outward
chain through (a_0,\ldots,a_N) is executable only in the chronological order

\[
 q(a_{N-1}),q(a_{N-2}),\ldots,q(a_0),              \tag{R16}
\]

with (V(a_0)) at the far tail.  Increasing (N) prepends a new smaller
root; it does not append a next root to a fixed profile.

There is no infinite chronological reversal inside the advertised domain.
Following (R16) toward the future makes the (a)'s increase by more than a
factor two, so after finitely many steps they leave ((0,1)).  Conversely,
the coordinatewise limit of the growing finite outward words is all Continue:
for every fixed chronological date (k), its root is
(q(a_{N-1-k})\to0) as (N\to\infty).  The nonzero clock has escaped to
temporal infinity.

Consequently the bullets claiming an “exact fully mixed Zeno orbit,” positive
Quit probability “at every finite date,” a summable exact behavioral clock,
and a Nash--Bellman cap chronology all on one profile are not established.
The numerical sequence (sum_ta_t<\infty) is true, but it is indexed in
outward-prefix order, not in the chronological order of an infinite
executable word.

This is the same orientation boundary already documented for the checked
maximal-prefix ray: `QuittingForwardExactCapTail.forward` is

\[
 \operatorname{pair}(t+1)
 =\operatorname{Prefix}_{\operatorname{root}(t)}
    \operatorname{pair}(t),
\]

which is outward semantic prefixing despite the word “forward” in the type
name.  That type is actual semantic carrier data, not the suffix recursion of
one behavioral profile.

## 4. Limit-root uniqueness: PASS, with local scope only

At the cap (s), (R10) becomes (G(q,0)=Cq).  If (q) is an exact product
Nash root, every coordinate with (q_i>0) must have (G_i\ge0), so

\[
 q^TCq\ge0.                                       \tag{R17}
\]

If at least two coordinates are positive, the negative symmetric pair sums
give (q^TCq<0), contradiction.  If only (q_j>0), the displayed directed
collider has (q_k=0) and ((Cq)_k=q_j>0), contradicting optimality of sure
Continue for player (k).  Thus (q=0) is the unique exact product root at
the supplied cap (s).

This proves a local unique-all-Continue cap port.  It does not prove that
(s) is the unrestricted cap of an executable continuation, that it is a
positive-minimum semantic point, or that all Never is or is not a behavioral
equilibrium of any claimed source.  The document correctly concedes most of
this later, but “literal inert port” should retain the qualifier
**one-stage cap algebra**.
