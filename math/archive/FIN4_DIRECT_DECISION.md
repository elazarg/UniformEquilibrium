# Decide the four-player conjecture directly

## Mathematical data

Let \(r\) be a four-player quitting reward table. For every behavioral profile
\(\sigma\), let

\[
E_r(\sigma)=\max_i\bigl(B_i^r(\sigma)-U_i^r(\sigma)\bigr),
\]

where \(B_i^r\) is the supremum over all unilateral behavioral replacements,
including Never and arbitrarily late quitting.

## Question

Prove that

\[
\inf_\sigma E_r(\sigma)=0
\]

for every four-player reward table, and construct terminal approximate Nash
profiles with one limiting payoff.

An equally complete negative answer is one explicit reward table and one
\(\gamma>0\) with an exact proof that

\[
E_r(\sigma)\ge\gamma
\qquad\text{for every behavioral profile }\sigma.
\]

The proof may use any mathematical language and need not pass through the
source-preserving completion atlas.

## Nonanswers

- a positive gap for only stationary, periodic, bounded-memory, or
  bounded-horizon profiles;
- another necessary condition, atom, paid row, or compact limit with no
  terminal consumer;
- a supplied certificate whose hypotheses are not produced for every table;
  or
- a numerical candidate without an exact all-behavior proof.
