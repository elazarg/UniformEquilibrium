# Conference archive

This directory is a lossless maintenance lane, not a mathematical status
lane.  It contains only:

- non-mathematical artifacts removed from the live conference board; and
- dated lifecycle manifests explaining what was inspected and why research
  files were retained in their stable locations; and
- superseded question formulations retained for historical theorem and
  review links, outside the active question set; and
- processed external submissions removed from `gpt/`, after checking that
  their useful mathematics has a permanent home and retaining the relevant
  review. These are original intake records, not current theorem statements.

Research notebooks are not moved here merely because they are old,
superseded, or currently unreferenced.  Existing feedback and formalization
records often depend on their paths, and an old failed implication may still
contain the only exact regression or boundary calculation.  Such files remain
under `notes/` with their honest status.

`archive/artifacts/` is recoverable storage.  Nothing there is consumed by the
mathematical or formalization workflow.

[GPT packet retirement](GPT_PACKET_RETIREMENT.md) maps retired submissions
to their mathematical homes. Original submission bytes are retained; an
archived claim must be read with its review, not treated as a new result.
