# Prove or refute the corrected AGKRS Theorem 3.4 forward trichotomy

Archived as resolved: the direct chronological production theorem
`QuittingPayoffTable.stationary_or_instantPunishment_or_sequentiallyPerfectAbsorbing`
proves the positive answer from the literal approximate-equilibrium premise.
The residual-specific program below records an older alternative route and is
not the proof dependency used by the checked theorem.

## Question

For every finite quitting game having approximate equilibria at every
positive tolerance, prove the literal S.1/S.2/S.3 conclusion of AGKRS Theorem
3.4, with Simon's corrected stationarily-generated regime included.

Only the forward classification is asked for.  The printed reverse implication
uses a separate sequential-perfectness-to-equilibrium claim and is not part of
this question.

Alternatively, give one finite reward table satisfying the premise while all
three branches fail.

The branches are: the moving-root stationary alternative S.1, the sure-first-
stage punishment alternative S.2, and the absorbing sequentially
approximately perfect alternative S.3.  A solution must retain their stated
quantifiers over all required small tolerances and arbitrary unilateral
behavioral deviations.

## Conditional capstone

The checked reduction leaves consumers for two source classes.

### 1. Prioritized corrected-pointwise source

At one positive scale, a prioritized refined source retains failure of the
stationary, instant-punishment, well-supported
absorbing, and stationarily-generated pointwise branches.  Its checked normal
form is

```text
source-faithful all-Continue positive-rho residual
or positive-singleton defect.
```

Cofinal residuals retain scales tending to zero and one fixed ordered
owner/preemptor pair.  The missing theorem must turn this actual source
sequence into S.1, S.2, or S.3, or show that the residual family is
impossible.

A checked consumer turns a supplied rotation-uniform signed lasso into S.3.
Production of the lasso from the retained source-matched preemption seeds is
open.

### 2. Positive-joint summable exact-prefix source

The surviving source is an actual reached positive-joint punishment endpoint
which fails the exact sure-exit Nash-prefix test and whose canonical exact
semantic-prefix orbit has a
summable-charge all-Continue limit port.

Under failure of S.3, the checked positive-joint boundary leaves exactly:

```text
zero total charge and a constant all-Continue orbit;
or positive total charge with a fixed signed displaced terminal coalition.
```

A positive-charge port whose limit returns to the endpoint is consumed into
S.3.  The unresolved cases are the zero-charge all-Continue phantom and the
positive displaced port without return.

The stronger source-facing phantom package retains the originating residual,
reached endpoint, no-sure-exit proof, summable port, literal source suffixes,
fixed punished label, value identity, and full semantic convergence.  These
fields must be used to produce S.1/S.2/S.3 or a contradiction.

## Facts that do not close the trichotomy

- The endpoint and summable-port limit are diagonal points of the literal
  terminal-semantic carrier and are specific uniform-equilibrium payoff
  targets.
- Nonsummable positive-joint absorption yields well-supported S.3.
- Bounded exceptional horizons yield S.2.
- Divergent unique exceptional-owner sources yield S.2 or well-supported
  S.3.

The first fact is not an S.1/S.2/S.3 classification.  The other three remove
source classes and are not hypotheses to be reproved.

## Acceptable positive answer

Supply branch-valued consumers for both prioritized normal-form outputs and
for both surviving summable-port outputs, then compose them through the
conditional capstone.  An unconditional proof of the trichotomy is equally
acceptable.

## Acceptable negative answer

Give one explicit finite quitting table and prove all of:

- approximate-equilibrium existence at every positive tolerance;
- failure of S.1;
- failure of S.2; and
- failure of every S.3 absorbing sequentially approximately perfect branch.

## Nonanswers

- another residual, phantom, preemption edge, compact limit, or case split;
- a supplied signed-lasso verifier without a producer;
- a real-valued signed displacement treated as a well-founded rank;
- diagonal-carrier or uniform-payoff membership without S.1/S.2/S.3
  classification; or
- failure of one stationary root or one proposed sequential construction.
