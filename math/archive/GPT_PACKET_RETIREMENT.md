# Processed external submissions

Maintenance record: processed originals are removed from the live `gpt/`
inbox and retained here, byte-for-byte. Their former filenames and review
hashes identify the same submissions. Retirement does not mean every claim
was accepted, formalized, or conjecture-closing. The mathematical home and
review determine the scope, especially for rejected proposed consumers.

Frozen exports, formalized packets, and the active proof surfaces were not
edited. Rendered links to moved originals are redirected; historical input
names in hash records remain historical names. Nothing is permanently
deleted. Download metadata is kept separately in `artifacts/`.
