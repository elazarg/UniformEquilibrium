# Directly consume fully screened cumulative cap defect

## Mathematical data

Let actual four-player profiles consist of finite source-attached product-root
words followed by one retained forced-pair source. Suppose joint survival
through the words and every deleted-player survival probability tend to zero.

For row \(t\), let \(R_{n,t}\ge0\) be the total exact root defect against the
complete unrestricted cap of the actual suffix, and let \(c_{n,<t}\) be the
probability of reaching that row under the prescribed word. Assume the exact
prefix account gives, eventually,

\[
\sum_t c_{n,<t}R_{n,t}\ge \frac{D_*}{2},
\qquad D_*>0.
\]

## Question

Convert this order-one cumulative suffix-cap defect into terminal approximate
Nash profiles with one limiting payoff, a positive source-attached
admissible-payoff return, or a renewable finite-rank source descent.

Alternatively prove that the supplied fully screened positive-gap data are
inconsistent, or realize them in an explicit four-player table with a fixed
positive exploitability gap against every behavioral profile.

## Exact obstruction

The charge is computed against suffix caps and may diffuse over arbitrarily
many non-Nash rows. Selecting a row with positive root defect is insufficient:
it must become an actual prescribed-payoff edge and its continuation seam must
close for all behavioral deviations.

The supplied finite clock-clearing construction maps this source into the
general concentrated-packet problem. This question asks only for the stronger
direct use of the cumulative ledger; it is not a separate completion-atlas
component.

## Nonanswers

- another lower bound on aggregate cap defect;
- a positive terminal-law atom without chronological control;
- an invariant measure or carrier point without executable realization;
- a real-valued debt decrease without renewable rank; or
- control only over stationary, periodic, finite-horizon, or bounded
  deviations.
