# Consume the terminal exits of renewable Fin4 support descent

## Mathematical data

Let \(z\) be a positive-minimum terminal-semantic point of a four-player
quitting game, with an actual source chronology and tangent family. A
source-preserving finite descent through minimum-fibre full replacements
terminates at one of:

1. a positive total tangent slope;
2. flat tangent motion which creates debt outside the active support; or
3. an actual off-minimum full-replacement sequence carrying a fixed paid
   first-disagreement row.

## Question

For every alternative, construct terminal approximate Nash profiles with one
limiting payoff, a positive admissible-payoff return, or a contradiction to
the positive exploitability gap.

For alternatives 1 and 2, it is enough to construct source-attached
chronological debt shadowing at every positive accuracy.

For alternative 3, use a consumer valid for the same actual paid profiles.
The exact paid-cap dispatch on those profiles yields a charged return,
quantitative descent, or a single-source inert stall. The charged return has a
terminal consumer; the other two outputs are part of this question.

An explicit four-player positive-gap table realizing one terminal alternative
and defeating every displayed consumer is an acceptable negative answer.

## Source constraint

The specialized paired unique-all-Continue packet is constructed from a
different same-table double port. It is not automatically the inert output of
the arbitrary paid terminal-exit profile. A proof may use that independent
construction only through an explicit source bridge.

## Nonanswers

- reconstructing the supplied finite support descent;
- another tangent, atom, paid row, or compact limit;
- replacing the terminal-exit profile by an independently selected paid/reset
  source; or
- a conditional consumer whose additional producer is not derived from the
  stated terminal alternative.
