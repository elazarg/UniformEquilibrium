# Construct source-attached two-tier chronological shadowing

## Mathematical data

Let a four-player positive-minimum tangent source carry fixed distinct players
\(p,o\) and a fixed positive charge. Along its actual source profiles assume
the vanishing-debt atom alternative: eventually either

1. a coordinate of the prescribed payoff difference retains a fixed signed
   terminal contribution; or
2. one common pure-time response by \(o\) retains a response-square terminal
   contribution while \(o\)'s debt at the response endpoint tends to zero.

The response may be a finite quitting time or Never. The terminal contribution
is not assumed to be positive mass of one fixed coalition.

## Question

Construct both tiers below and compose them into terminal approximate Nash
profiles with one limiting payoff, hence a uniform-equilibrium payoff.

### Tier I: compatible actual ports

Construct reached ports with one globally bounded annotation family. At every
sufficiently small scale \(h\), produce a literal finite Bellman block from the
current port to a successor such that:

- the entrance and successor annotations are attached to the actual source;
- the complete payoff/cap seam and loss of future availability have an
  operationally sublinear total modulus;
- the same players \(p,o\) each accumulate at least \(\kappa h\) Quit hazard,
  for one fixed \(\kappa>0\); and
- the successor retains enough availability to repeat the construction.

The scales must be selectable with divergent total scale and summable total
seam and availability loss.

### Tier II: seed and implementation

For every \(\eta>0\), either implement a legal initial port whose annotation
has every debt coordinate at most \(\eta\), with all unrestricted behavioral
payoff and cap errors explicitly controlled, or directly construct a
uniform-equilibrium payoff.

If a source of debt \(D_*>0\) is implemented by a seed with coordinate debts
at most \(\eta\), and one-sided payoff and cap errors \(\alpha_i,\beta_i\), the
implementation must respect

\[
D_*\le 4\eta+\sum_i(\alpha_i+\beta_i).
\]

Thus the positive-minimum source itself cannot be silently relabelled as a
small-debt seed.

## Required composition

The two tiers must yield one literal chronology with summable payoff/cap
seams, divergent exposure of both fixed labels, vanishing joint and every
deleted-player survival probability, and initial debt below the requested
accuracy. It must control every unilateral behavioral deviation, including
Never and arbitrarily late quitting.

## Nonanswers

- inhabiting an abstract packet type without actual source anchoring;
- one persistent label or labels chosen afresh at every block;
- exact response transport through a new prefix over only one fixed suffix;
- a positive-minimum source used as the small-debt seed; or
- divergent internal debt drain without prescribed-payoff charge or a
  terminal consumer.
