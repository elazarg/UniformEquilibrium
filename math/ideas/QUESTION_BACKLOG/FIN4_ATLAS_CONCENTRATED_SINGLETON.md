# Consume the source-attached concentrated Fin4 packet

## Question

Let a four-player positive-minimum source carry one actual concentrated
singleton endpoint: a literal profile, marked date, singleton terminal
coalition, positive unconditional marked-mass floor, complete post-date tail,
and the original minimizing joint semantic/law point and chronology.  Prove
one of:

1. terminal approximate Nash profiles for every positive error;
2. a uniform-equilibrium payoff;
3. a positive cumulative admissible-payoff near-return;
4. a source-preserving finite-rank transition whose terminal outputs have
   consumers of type 1--3; or
5. a contradiction to the retained terminal exploitability witness.

An explicit all-behavior positive-gap table realizing the complete input is
an equally valid negative answer.

This is a shortcut question. The supplied forced-pair transition sends the
input into the minimum-return/uniform-escape roadmap, so a direct consumer is
not required for atlas coverage.

## Supplied data

The core retains:

- the same quantitative hard residual and positive global minimum \(D_*\);
- the selected joint semantic/law minimum source and causal chronology;
- one actual endpoint profile and marked date;
- one singleton terminal coalition with a fixed positive unconditional stage
  mass; and
- the complete literal post-date behavioral tail.

The one-date construction chooses a distinct player and its exact
best Boolean endpoint.  It preserves the marked mass and post-date tail and
makes that player's marked root defect exactly zero.  The source-indexed
construction yields a strong recurrent concentrated packet without asserting that
the modified whole profile is minimum or Nash.

The terminal exploitability witness can force pair mode: pureify the singleton
row and choose the supplied full-gap outsider whose Quit payoff exceeds its
Continue payoff. This gives one literal pure pair with fixed mass, an exact
zero defect for the forced outsider, and another fixed positive marked
defect/payoff gain. The payer's unrestricted debt decreases by exactly that
gain.

## Exact obstruction

The endpoint modification changes one player's strategy.  That player's own
best-response cap is unchanged, but other players' whole-profile caps may
rise.  Consequently:

- exact payer-debt subtraction does not imply total-debt descent;
- annihilating one debtor does not imply support descent;
- the modified whole profile need not lie near the minimum fibre; and
- source cap--Nash prefixes need not remain exact for the modified
  continuation.

The pure pair also screens every post-row tail from the whole terminal
semantic pair.  Replacing only the retained tail cannot repair the whole
profile.

## Available transition

For singleton minimum-law sources, the fixed-source forced-pair stream splits
exhaustively according to its literal post-row tail debt:

```text
uniform escape: D(tail_n) >= D_* + delta along a cofinal stream;
minimum return: D(tail_n) -> D_*.
```

The corresponding terminal components are uniform escape and minimum return.
The normalized minimum-return dispatch further gives a canonical renewable
support lane or a strict normalized inert point.

## Nonanswers

- constructing another generic concentrated packet;
- using zero marked defect as whole-root Nash exactness;
- selecting an unrelated minimum source or stationary handoff;
- a static reward inequality without executable chronology;
- a real-valued debt decrease without a renewable rank; or
- a finer residual list without consumers for every output.
