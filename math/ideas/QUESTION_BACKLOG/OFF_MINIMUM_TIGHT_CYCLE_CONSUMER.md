# Consume the tight cycle at an off-minimum unique-all-Continue cap

## Mathematical data

Let a four-player quitting game have positive minimum total terminal
debt \(D_*>0\) over the semantic carrier, rewards bounded by \(M\), and
the hard residual. Let \(y=(u,c)\) be a carrier point with
\(D(y)>D_*\) at whose cap all-Continue is the unique exact product
root. Write \(s_i=r_i(\{i\})\) and let

\[
Z=\{i: s_i=c_i\}
\]

be the tight set. The following are supplied as known:

1. \(Z\) is empty, or \(|Z|\ge2\) and every \(i\in Z\) has a joiner
   \(k\in Z\setminus\{i\}\) with \(r_k(\{i,k\})>r_k(\{i\})\), so \(Z\)
   carries a cycle of such edges;
2. if \(Z=\emptyset\), the exact-root correspondence is uniquely
   all-Continue on an open neighborhood of \(c\), so the point is
   robustly inert;
3. provenance: \(y\) is a limit of actual profiles \(\pi_n\) with
   marked dates \(m_n\) whose post-mark all-Continue-shift tails have
   total debt tending to \(D_*\); and
4. for every coordinate \(i\) tight in the limit and all
   \(\gamma<D_*\), \(\sigma>0\), eventually both the
   opponents-of-\(i\) pre-mark continue product and the Never mass of
   \(\pi_n\) are at most \((2M+\sigma)/(2M+\gamma)\); consequently the
   limiting terminal law of \(y\) puts mass at least
   \(D_*/(2M+D_*)\) on finite coalitions; and
5. with a uniform marked-mass floor \(\kappa\) at the marks (as the
   selection supplies), one \(\mathrm{moat}>0\) with, eventually: the
   marked live root pays total one-stage Nash defect at least
   \(\mathrm{moat}\) against the tail cap, and
   \(\kappa\,\mathrm{moat}+\mathrm{surv}_n\,D(\tau_n)\le D(\pi_n)\),
   where \(\tau_n\) is the post-mark all-Continue-shift tail of datum 3
   and \(\mathrm{surv}_n\) the joint live mass at the mark-plus-one date —
   the whole-minus-tail debt gap is uniformly charged at the mark.

## Question

In the cycle branch (\(Z\ne\emptyset\)), prove one of:

1. terminal approximate Nash profiles with one limiting payoff, or a
   uniform-equilibrium payoff;
2. a positive cumulative admissible-payoff near-return by an operation
   other than exact prefixing at the frozen cap;
3. a renewable well-founded rank which strictly decreases on complete
   sources; or
4. incompatibility of the cycle configuration with positive global
   minimality and the hard residual.

An explicit four-player table realizing the complete configuration,
with a certified positive exploitability gap against every behavioral
profile, is an acceptable negative answer.

## Constraints

The pre-mark absorption in datum 4 is absorption of the actual live
rows, not of exact roots; exact prefixing at the frozen cap is the
zero-absorption identity, so a consumer must either convert actual
absorption into charged exact chronology or bypass charge accounting.
Common all-Continue padding shifts every mark at zero cost, so any
consumer must be invariant under that time translation. A zero-minimum
table can realize a mutually eager tight pair with a third-player
blocker and the whole local cycle geometry; a proof must use the
positive minimum, the hard residual, or the provenance nonlocally.
Collision-free critical-face theorems for exact rows hold at exact
minimum tails and do not apply to the merely near-minimal tails here.

## Nonanswers

- the cycle or its collision signs alone, without the positive minimum;
- another compact limit, atom, or paid row without a consumer;
- a moat or defect bound at the frozen cap with no chronological use;
- a zero-minimum regression; or
- restating the inert branch (\(Z=\emptyset\)) as progress.
