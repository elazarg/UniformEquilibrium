# Consume the strict normalized inert boundary

## Mathematical data

Let a source-attached four-player forced-pair family have normalized minimum
debt \(L>D_*\) and fixed positive paid-pair density. Its density-boundary
dispatch gives one of:

1. a positive-density return to the global minimum component;
2. an actual zero-density Zeno source which produces either a positive-host
   concentrated endpoint or a source-attached concentrated packet; or
3. a carrier point \(x\) satisfying, for every product root \(q\),

   \[
   \operatorname{RootDefect}(q;B(x))
   \ge D(x)\operatorname{Absorption}(q).
   \]

In case 3, every positive-absorption exact root is excluded, so all-Continue is
the unique exact root. The carrier point need not be attained by one
behavioral profile.

## Question

Consume cases 2 and 3 into terminal approximate Nash profiles with one
limiting payoff, a positive admissible-payoff return, or a renewable finite
rank on complete sources. Alternatively prove that these cases are
incompatible with the positive minimum and hard residual.

An explicit four-player table realizing the complete source-attached boundary
with a fixed positive exploitability gap against every behavioral profile is
an acceptable negative answer.

## Constraints

The pure marked pair screens its post-row continuation from the whole terminal
semantic pair. The root-defect barrier is unsigned, and exact prefixing at the
unique all-Continue cap is the zero-absorption identity. A successful proof
must use the positive global minimum, terminal witness, or retained source
chronology nonlocally.

## Nonanswers

- another density, binding, flow, ballistic, parity, or complementarity split
  with an unconsumed output;
- the unsigned root-defect inequality alone;
- another compact cap limit;
- the scalar Zeno ledger without an executable source consumer; or
- a zero-minimum regression.
