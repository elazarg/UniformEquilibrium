# Current Recommendations for the Nonlocality Frontier

Date: 2026-08-26

## Maintenance note, 2026-08-28

The finite-roadmap problem has since been reduced to two source-preserving
terminal stream components.  The recommendations below remain useful as a
technique catalogue, but their current conjecture-facing destinations are now
sharper:

- renewal, flux, occupation, and chronological gluing methods should target
  the uniform-escape packet; and
- cocycle, support-rank, duality, and cap-leakage methods should target the
  minimum-return packet.

Intermediate normalized-inert, response-square, strict-ray, and support
objects are refinements of those packets rather than additional global atlas
leaves.  A technique counts as progress only when it consumes a whole packet
or gives a source-preserving transition already covered by the finite atlas.

This is a provisional research judgement, not a theorem-status document or an
assignment list. The stable technique identifiers refer to
[`NONLOCALITY_TECHNIQUE_CATALOGUE.md`](NONLOCALITY_TECHNIQUE_CATALOGUE.md).

## Present diagnosis

The central difficulty is no longer merely finding local gains, labels, atoms,
or reset rectangles. The difficult implication is to make several such facts
coexist on one literal behavioral source or one actually reached continuation,
while controlling best responses that may move arbitrarily far into the
future.

Three forms of nonlocality interact:

1. **Temporal nonlocality:** the behavioral cap ranges over complete stopping
   laws and can be witnessed arbitrarily late.
2. **Compactness nonlocality:** semantic pairs can converge while their clocks
   diffuse toward `Never`, leaving a cap defect not realized by any limiting
   profile.
3. **Provenance nonlocality:** separately selected minimizers, reset faces,
   paid rows, atoms, and exact roots need not form one executable chronology.

The recommendations below are ranked by their apparent ability to change a
named conjecture-facing deficit, not by elegance or generality.

## Evidence accumulated since the initial ranking

Several lenses have now been tested sharply enough to change how they should
be used.

- `NL-04` and `NL-23` produced a genuine algorithmic reduction: literal
  finite-clock semantic pairs are dense in the full carrier along an explicit
  cofinal support sequence.  Finite-clock search is therefore meaningful when
  it carries the compactified tail error; a fixed horizon by itself remains
  unsound.
- `NL-11` successfully removed finite label circularity at fixed scale: one
  small full-support prefix prepares every Fin4 coalition on the same literal
  source.  Exact examples show that this does not align the eventual signed
  causal date with the prepared stage.
- `NL-13` localized a positive terminal-law difference to an actual signed
  causal stage and a finite half-charge window.  The exact occupation identity
  then separates normalized root variation from survival-boundary variation.
  Neither term, using source-local window data alone, universally produces a
  Nash--Bellman edge.
- `NL-14` is consequently useful only when coupled to a global certificate,
  such as positive-minimum variational data or a dual separation theorem.  A
  richer source-local occupation flow is not by itself a producer.
- `NL-12` has an exact inert test case: arbitrarily deep literal all-Continue
  stacks preserve the full law, semantic pair, and a positive causal suffix
  atom while accumulating zero charge.  Depth without a suffix-changing
  operation is translation, not regeneration.

These results move the emphasis from discovering or aligning a marked event to
making its strategic sign executable under the global positive-minimum
constraint.

## Current highest-value synthesis target

The most useful control-theoretic formulation is a **closed-loop terminal
certificate**, not a generic controller metaphor.  Specify an augmented state
and an executable finite window such that every window ends in exactly one of:

- a checked compiler set;
- a strict natural-valued rank decrease with the full source packet
  regenerated; or
- another actual reached state satisfying the identical quantitative
  invariant.

Candidate state fields are the literal suffix law, semantic pair, a
first-disagreement or curvature witness, the causal signed window, and the
current positive-debt support.  The decisive test is renewal: if the invariant
cannot be re-applied at the endpoint, the construction is an open-loop
diagnostic rather than a producer.

## First priority

### R1 — Make the escape defect an explicit state variable

Relevant lenses: `NL-05`, `NL-06`, `NL-07`, `NL-08`.

The opponent-tight realization theorem shows that enough tight opponent clocks
eliminate semantic nonattainment. Its residual is sharply concentrated: on a
chosen limiting-law subsequence, at most one clock remains proper, and any cap
jump is attached to the escaping-clock boundary behavior.

The next useful question is not another abstract compactness lemma. It is
whether the remaining one-proper-clock defect can be consumed by:

- a strict minimum-fiber support loss;
- a source-matched paid or reset chronology;
- a contradiction with positive global minimum debt; or
- an explicit counterexample certificate.

A successful state should record the proper owner, mass at `Never`, cap jump,
singleton reward sign, and the actual approximating profiles. It must have a
transport or consumption theorem; otherwise it is only a classification.

Related checked record:
[`../formalized/OPPONENT_TIGHT_TERMINAL_SEMANTIC_REALIZATION.md`](../formalized/OPPONENT_TIGHT_TERMINAL_SEMANTIC_REALIZATION.md).

### R2 — Build the smallest provenance-complete Markov state

Relevant lenses: `NL-01`, `NL-03`, `NL-10`, `NL-11`.

The present semantic pair is too small: it forgets which actual stopping law,
cap witness, terminal atom, and continuation suffix generated its coordinates.
The full behavioral profile is too large: carrying it without a finite
transition interface merely restates the problem.

The target is a minimal dependent passport closed under literal prefixing and
resetting. Candidate fields are:

- the actual profile or named terminal suffix;
- its complete stopping law;
- the semantic pair;
- one or two pure-time witnesses;
- the paid first-disagreement row;
- a localized curvature square; and
- exact entrance and successor anchors for a consumer.

The test of usefulness is strict: can two successive transitions be composed
without selecting a semantically equivalent replacement source?

### R3 — Treat curvature as a transported cocycle, then consume its defect

Relevant lenses: `NL-10`, `NL-13`, `NL-17`.

Paid gain or signed port mass alone is not renewable. A full
source/mixed/receiving curvature square has a stronger exact transport law:
under a common cap prefix, either a controlled fraction of curvature survives
or a comparable immediate-Quit cap wall appears. A literal all-Continue prefix
preserves the square exactly.

The missing result is local and precise:

> Convert a positive localized curvature square, or its immediate-Quit wall,
> into a source-matched exact punishment-floor chronology, a finite support
> loss, or another curvature square with a nondegenerating passport.

Normalization alone is not enough: repeated non-inert transport may halve the
curvature indefinitely, and a cap-Nash root is not automatically an edge in
the prescribed-payoff relation.

[`../notes/CHATGPT_EXTERNAL__CURVATURE_PREFIX_RENEWAL.md`](../notes/CHATGPT_EXTERNAL__CURVATURE_PREFIX_RENEWAL.md).

### R4 — Formulate a genuine relaxation--recovery theorem at positive minimum

Relevant lenses: `NL-08`, `NL-09`, `NL-15`.

The compact terminal-semantic carrier is a relaxation of actual profile
semantics. The useful recovery question is:

> Which explicit defect conditions guarantee that a positive global minimum
> carrier point is realized by one actual profile, or by an actual
> source-matched tangent family retaining the cap-relevant data?

This should separate four outcomes rather than asserting generic closedness:

- exact realization;
- opponent-tight recovery;
- one-clock escape with an explicit cap defect; or
- a well-founded minimum-fiber change.

Prescribed-payoff convergence alone is insufficient, and positive debt alone
does not make the actual semantic image closed.

## Second priority

### R5 — Dualize source-matched chronology feasibility

Relevant lenses: `NL-14`, `NL-15`, `NL-16`.

Encode exact path candidates as occupation and transition flows with literal
source and tail constraints. If no admissible source-matched chronology exists,
seek a separating functional. A valuable dual certificate would have to become
one of:

- a contradiction with the positive terminal gap;
- a strict debt/support rank change;
- a terminal approximate equilibrium; or
- a checkable counterexample invariant.

This is more promising than dualizing only stationary roots, because the
present obstruction is chronological compatibility.

### R6 — Make source provenance into an explicit gluing problem

Relevant lenses: `NL-11`, `NL-04`.

Define the exact compatibility data among a rectangle source, fixed-law reset,
terminal atom, paid observer, reached continuation, and Bellman edge. Then ask
whether pairwise compatibility is sufficient, or exhibit the first higher
compatibility obstruction.

This language is useful only if it yields a finite obstruction or a gluing
theorem. Merely renaming alignment as descent data would not be progress.

### R7 — Regenerate only at actual reached tails

Relevant lenses: `NL-12`, `NL-13`, `NL-19`.

Renewal arguments should begin at a literal reached continuation with a
quantitative reach floor. A semantic all-Continue port is not enough. Block
charge may be accumulated over many small edges, but the renewed packet must
retain its source and labels.

The crucial invariant should be renewable—support rank, a nondecaying cocycle,
or a quantized defect—not merely a positive real gain that can shrink at each
restart.

### R8 — Revisit finite horizons only through projective compatibility

Relevant lenses: `NL-04`, `NL-23`.

Finite truncated timing equilibria exist, but they can hide the full-game gap
at the first time beyond the truncation. Any useful finite-horizon approach
must therefore produce compatible horizons plus a uniform tail estimate or an
explicit boundary defect. Separate Nash equilibria at every truncation are not
enough.

## Exploratory, lower-confidence lenses

### R9 — Generating functions for the infrared clock

Relevant lenses: `NL-20`, `NL-21`.

Represent stopping laws by generating functions and study the boundary near
the `Never` frequency. This may quantify cap jumps and separated stopping-time
scales. The obstacle is the supremum over deviations; transform methods must
interact with optimization, not merely prescribed laws.

### R10 — A compensated-compactness identity for caps

Relevant lens: `NL-09`.

Search for an exact pairing—possibly debt scaling, curvature, or Bellman
flux—that upgrades weak law convergence to convergence of cap-relevant
payoffs. This is speculative but directly addresses the mismatch between weak
terminal-law convergence and optimization over stopping times.

### R11 — Topological forcing after provenance is packaged

Relevant lens: `NL-18`.

Degree or parity might force a root or branch crossing in a finite-dimensional
passport space. It is unlikely to help before the state includes literal
source and continuation data, because a topologically forced semantic root can
still lack an executable chronology.

### R12 — Renormalize temporal scales, not merely edge charges

Relevant lenses: `NL-19`, `NL-21`.

Cumulative absorption already shows that many small charges can form one
proper block. A stronger blocking scheme might retain the dominant stopping
clock, cap defect, and terminal labels across scales. The main risk is losing
product behavior or exact source anchors during coarse-graining.

## Currently poor bets unless a new ingredient appears

The following moves are not forbidden, but existing results explain why they
are unlikely to change the frontier on their own:

- another finite-horizon cutoff without a uniform tail theorem;
- another static cycle or shared-label classification;
- a lone positive exact edge without return or regeneration;
- raw real-valued debt descent without a renewable passport;
- compactness of the relaxed semantic carrier without a recovery theorem;
- a cap-Nash chronology treated as a prescribed-payoff chronology;
- support rank without a no-new-debtor theorem;
- adding a spare player or cycle without controlling unrestricted deviations
  on the same actual profile; and
- independently selecting a minimizer, atom, paid row, and endpoint and then
  attempting to align them afterward.

## Current architectural bet

The most coherent combined route is:

1. compactify complete stopping laws and expose every boundary defect
   (`NL-05`--`NL-08`);
2. retain that defect inside a provenance-complete augmented state (`NL-01`,
   `NL-11`);
3. transport a curvature or flux passport exactly through prefixes (`NL-10`,
   `NL-13`); and
4. prove that either the passport regenerates at an actual reached tail or a
   finite support/defect rank strictly falls (`NL-12`, `NL-17`).

This is a recommendation, not a claim that such a finite passport or rank
exists. Its advantage is that every step directly addresses one of the three
identified forms of nonlocality.
