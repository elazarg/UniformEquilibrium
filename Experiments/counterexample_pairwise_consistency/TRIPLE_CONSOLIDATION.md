# Consolidation of the counterexample triple tests

## Decision

The local and selected-path conditions do not contradict one another.  The
four meaningful triple investigations instead isolate two constructive seams
that must be crossed on the data supplied by an actual terminal exploitability witness.

1. **Occupation/admissibility.**  Produce from the optimized tail a normalized
   singleton occupation which is simultaneously funded and above the actual
   punishment floor.
2. **Exact renewable realization.**  Lift that linked occupation or a blocked
   canonical window to exact floor-admissible Nash--Bellman progress, then
   renew the progress or close it by an exact return.  A suffix co-realizing an
   endpoint cap as both payoff and complete best-response envelope is an
   alternative closing datum.

The canonical capacity potential is the consumer of the second seam, not its
producer.  It can pay for finitely many positive edges, but it forbids a
positive charged return or indefinitely renewable progress.

## What is now ruled out as a proof strategy

- Pairwise compatibility, even on common reward data, cannot establish joint
  consistency or a counterexample.
- Summable absorption and debt conservation do not improve normalized window
  drift from `O(window absorption)` to `o(window absorption)`.
- A table-wide packet need not be the occupation packet of the selected tail.
- A zero-charge limit cap, a bounded selected cap ray, and exclusion of every
  pure coalition lock do not establish universal charge capacity.
- The same finite root word can be exact at an annotated cap and exploitable
  when behaviorally restarted, because the two continuation values differ.
- One positive exact edge does not contradict bounded capacity; it must be
  renewable or returned.

## Tight residual

Let the optimized/projective provenance be retained rather than treated as an
arbitrary exact tail.  The remaining high-value theorem has the following
form.

> From a stabilized canonical-window obstruction of the actual optimized tail,
> either obtain a compatible profile whose terminal exploitability is below
> the fixed gap, or construct a funded punishment-rational singleton
> occupation and lift its refusal/promise defect to renewable exact
> punishment-floor charge.

The first branch contradicts terminal instability.  The second contradicts
finite canonical capacity.  A proof may factor the second branch through:

```text
optimized provenance
  -> singleton concentration and active-owner pinning
  -> funding plus punishment floor
  -> exact charged lift with all collision deviations checked
  -> renewable return or cap-co-realizing suffix.
```

The funded packet is no longer algebraically opaque.  Its weighted surplus
and aggregate refusal gain equal the quadratic form of the singleton
solo-effect matrix; only reciprocal pair sums contribute.  Every counterexample
packet therefore supports a distinct pair with positive reciprocal solo
effect.  Tables with all reciprocal pair sums nonpositive are already solved.
The remaining charged lift must use that pair together with nonsingleton
collision rewards and continuation data.

Singleton concentration is formalized through the product-law pair-union
bound, weighted finite collision propagation, and conditional payoff
approximation.  Refusal survival-reweighting remains a separate interface.
None of the triple witnesses proves optimized-minimizer provenance.

## Next experiments

The next search should no longer enumerate arbitrary combinations of `T`,
`P`, `W`, and selected cap facts.  It should target one of the following.

1. Reconstruct optimized zero-boundary provenance for an exact rational tail,
   or prove a first-order identity forced specifically by that provenance.
2. Certify the tail-window singleton occupation, then test funding and the
   punishment floor on the same limiting object.
3. Search the complete mixed floor correspondence of the lock-clean candidate
   tables for a positive charged return or an explicit bounded potential.
4. Prove an exact collision-aware charged-lift theorem, with the collision-flat
   packet rotation as its positive regression.
5. Prove a whole-window return/co-realization alternative; local one-edge cap
   repair is known to be insufficient.

Every experiment must retain common reward data, exact endpoints, and the
distinction between annotation, realized payoff, and best-response envelope.
