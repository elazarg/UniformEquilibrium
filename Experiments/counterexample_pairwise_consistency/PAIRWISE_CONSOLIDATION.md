# Consolidation of the pairwise counterexample-consistency matrix

## Outcome

No pair of the four exposed condition clusters is inconsistent.

Three pairs have exact rational compatibility witnesses for their exposed
equations.  Two cap pairs realize all selected/local cap equations but fail
the universal capacity on explicit off-path charged cycles.  The final pair is
interface-disconnected: periodic behavioral replay is not exact annotated-path
replay.

This is substantive progress.  It prevents several local conditions from
being mistaken for a proof and identifies the additional data that a triple
must carry.

## The four interfaces hidden by the original clusters

### Optimized provenance `M`

An arbitrary exact positive-debt tail is weaker than the tail selected as a
projective limit of cutoff-wise optimized zero-boundary chains.  The exact
`T × P` and `T × W` witnesses do not establish this minimizer provenance.
Any later use of an optimization identity must retain it as a separate field.

### Occupation/admissibility bridge `O`

Late windows canonically produce normalized owner-hazard measures.  Exact
small-hazard estimates connect these measures to periodic singleton delivery
and refusal.  They do not identify them with the separately extracted packet.

For a tail-derived limiting mass, the stabilized phase branch can fail packet
funding, while the refusal branch recovers funding but may lack the punishment
floor or selected-owner alignment.  The needed object is a tail-derived packet
that is simultaneously funded and punishment-rational.

### Attachment/realization bridge `R`

The same finite product-action word may be:

- a behavioral word whose periodic payoff and complete deviation envelope are
  evaluated at its actual continuation; and
- an exact cap path whose roots are Nash at prescribed cap annotations.

These continuations can differ by order one.  Repetition yields a charged
exact cycle only if there is an exact endpoint return connector.  Equivalently,
one needs a suffix co-realizing the cap as prescribed payoff and full deviation
envelope.

### Global capacity `G`

A harmless zero-charge cap and a bounded selected path do not control all
exact punishment-floor roots.  Every cap-pair regression found an off-path
positive charged loop.  Full `C` requires a globally bounded budget potential

```text
B(F(x,u)) + q(x) <= B(u)
```

on the complete boxed punishment-floor exact-root correspondence, or an
equivalent universal argument.

The new singleton-lock screen is the first inexpensive consequence of `G`:
for every positive-solo owner `o`, some outsider `j` must satisfy

```text
r_j({o,j}) > r_j({o}).
```

The collision completion retained in `C172_CANDIDATE_REVIEW.md` is designed to
pass this screen, but its punishment floors and global capacity remain open.

## Triple stage

There are four meaningful triples.

### `T × P × W`

First determine whether one exact shared table/tail can realize all three
exposed clusters.  The likely witness has a table-wide packet away from the
tail, an unfunded tail occupation, and a phase-obstructed periodic family.
An exact witness would prove that `O`, not three-way local algebra, is
essential.  Optimized provenance remains a separately labelled strengthening.

### `T × P × C`

Combine the selected tail, packet support/refusal lasso, and global capacity.
The live question is whether the singleton-lock joining edges and packet
preference edges force a positive charged return, or whether the tail supplies
a funded punishment-rational occupation packet.  Both `O` and `G` are active.

### `T × W × C`

This is the exact attachment problem isolated by `W × C`.  Test whether the
common tail provenance and stabilized window branch force an exact endpoint
return or a co-realizing cap suffix.  This triple carries `R`, `G`, and, in its
strong form, `M`.

### `P × W × C`

Assume or derive an occupation link between the packet and stabilized refusal
windows, then ask whether the uniform refusal margin forces charged progress
in the complete floor correspondence.  This is the cleanest `O × G` problem.
The phase branch needs a separate funding repair before packet machinery
applies.

## First triple decision

The exposed `T × P × W` equations are jointly consistent on one rational
four-player table and one common tail/window family.  After taking a suffix
before defining the canonical windows, every window has the same player-2
last-phase obstruction, while the table-wide packet defect and the exact
phantom tail remain intact.  The tail-derived packet is punishment-rational
but underfunded, so it is not the funded packet supplied by `P`.

This result promotes `O` from a possible technical nuisance to a necessary
mathematical bridge.  It does not test `M`, and the witness explicitly fails
both global terminal instability and global capacity on a stationary charged
self-loop.  See `TPW_CYCLIC_FOUR_TRIPLE_REPORT.md` for the exact separation.

## Attachment triple decision

The full `T × W × C` triple remains open, but its direction is now precise.
If a canonical cap word is already an exact positive-charge floor path, the
canonical capacity potential strictly separates its endpoints.  Finite
capacity therefore forbids an exact endpoint return; it does not generate
one.  Producing that return from the tail/window obstruction would immediately
give the desired contradiction.  A suffix that co-realizes the endpoint cap
as both payoff and best-response envelope is an alternative sufficient seam.

An exact rational interface model shows that one can simultaneously have
zero diagonal seam, exact cap transport, bounded selected charge, blocked
canonical windows, and exclusion of all pure coalition locks.  Hence local
cap algebra and the coalition-lock screens do not supply the missing return.
The remaining statement genuinely combines optimized provenance, behavioral
window realization, and the complete global capacity geometry.

## Occupation--capacity triple decision

The full `P × W × C` triple remains open.  On an exact collision-flat
four-player table, a funded punishment-rational packet is literally the
singleton occupation of every canonical window, and one fixed refusal player
has a uniform gain.  The same packet-owner rotation lifts to an exact
punishment-floor cycle of charge `3/2`, so the natural linked construction
violates global capacity for the relevant reason rather than through an
unrelated loop.

This separates two missing general interfaces.  First, linked occupation data
must be lifted to exact floor edges while checking all collision deviations
and the actual punishment floor.  Second, the positive progress must be
renewable or closed by an exact return; a single positive edge may simply
consume finite potential.  The phase branch is already incompatible with
funding once target pinning is supplied, whereas the refusal branch reinforces
the packet and needs both of these charge interfaces.

## Tail--packet--capacity triple decision

The full `T × P × C` triple remains open, although all exposed selected-data
equations coexist exactly on the lock-clean four-player table.  That table has
a positive-debt phantom tail, a table-uniform refusal defect, exact bounded
transport along the selected augmented-cap ray, a zero-charge limit cap, and
no pure coalition lock.  Exact support enumeration rules out complementary
packets on every support, so compactness gives a continuum-wide packet margin
rather than a grid artifact.

The normalized occupation of the selected tail is nevertheless underfunded.
Thus the funded packet is still a separate object, and strict joining edges
that destroy pure locks do not automatically generate replacement mixed
roots or returns.  The residual is the same structural combination: `O` must
make the tail occupation admissible, `G` must hold on every exact floor root,
and `M` may still restrict which exact tails can arise from the optimizer.
