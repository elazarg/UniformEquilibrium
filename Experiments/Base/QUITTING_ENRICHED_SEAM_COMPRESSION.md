# Enriched exact-seam compression

## Result

The nonconstructive finite-iteration reduction survives a substantial
enrichment of boundary holonomy.  It also survives exact seam compatibility,
provided a composable pair and its composition are compressed jointly rather
than choosing representatives independently.

The Lean experiment
`QuittingEnrichedSeamCompression.lean`
works with the production compact metric semantic target for finite marked
absorption paths.  That target records, in one point:

- the completed coalition absorption law, including `Never`;
- all five boundary-holonomy coordinates per player;
- exact-D entry and exit anchors;
- the marked terminal packet;
- entry debt; and
- the entire compact marked-stage graph, including roots, full and deleted
  clocks, absorption coordinates, local Bellman data, prefix/suffix affine
  summaries, obstacles, and pure-quit values.

For every positive semantic resolution, Lean proves that every finite coherent
cylinder is close to a genuinely realized representative of uniformly bounded
finite complexity.

## 1. The compact realized-range principle

The abstract lemma is simple but useful.  If

```text
encode : Source -> Target
```

lands in a compact metric space, then for every `delta > 0` there is a finite
set of actual sources whose encoded values form a `delta`-net of the entire
range.  Closedness of the range is unnecessary: cover its compact closure and
recenter the finite cover in the original range.

For any natural-valued source cost, taking the maximum over the finite source
codebook gives

```text
exists K, for every source x,
  exists representative r,
    cost(r) <= K
    and dist(encode(x), encode(r)) < delta.
```

This is nonconstructive and gives no formula for `K`.

## 2. Semantic stage count versus literal row count

The source-free cylinder stores a finite set of marked semantic stages.  Its
cardinality is a useful complexity, but exact duplicate stages collapse in a
set.  Consequently a bound on marked-stage cardinality is not automatically a
bound on the number of game dates in a generating word.

The experiment keeps the distinction explicit.  It proves both:

1. a uniform bound on the number of distinct marked stages; and
2. a uniform bound on a literal chronological generation cost.

The second uses an inductive witness mirroring production provenance:

```text
realized block cost = its literal row length,
exact splice cost   = outer cost + inner cost.
```

Every semantically coherent finite cylinder has such a witness because its
chronology is generated by realized blocks and exact-anchor splices.  The
finite codebook is then selected from cylinders carrying these witnesses.

Thus the literal bound is genuine; it is not inferred from a source-forgetting
stage set.

## 3. Compressing exact seams as single objects

Let an exact seam be a pair `(outer,inner)` with literal equality

```text
outer.exitAnchor = inner.entryAnchor.
```

Independently approximating `outer` and `inner` would generally destroy that
equality.  The experiment instead encodes the triple

```text
(
  semanticTarget(outer),
  semanticTarget(inner),
  semanticTarget(outer.compose(inner))
).
```

This triple lives in a compact metric product.  Its finite net is recentered
in the range of **actual exact seams**.  Lean therefore proves that, for every
`delta > 0`, there is a uniform literal row bound and every exact seam has a
representative exact seam such that:

```text
total literal input-row cost <= K,
outer semantics   are delta-close,
inner semantics   are delta-close,
composed semantics are delta-close,
representative.outer.exitAnchor
  = representative.inner.entryAnchor exactly.
```

Recording the composition is important.  It prevents the compression from
quietly assuming that composition is uniformly continuous on arbitrary
completion fibres or that two close independently chosen blocks compose to
the desired result.

## 4. Concrete exact-D consequence

The Urysohn metric on the joint target is not given by a useful formula, but
the entry and exit anchor projections are continuous.  Compactness upgrades
them to uniform continuity.

Lean derives the following coordinate-facing statement.  For every
`epsilon > 0`, there is a uniform literal finite representative family such
that every exact seam has a representative exact seam with

```text
dist(outer entry anchors) < epsilon,
dist(outer exit anchors)  < epsilon,
dist(inner entry anchors) < epsilon,
dist(inner exit anchors)  < epsilon,
```

and the representative's outer exit and inner entry remain exactly equal.

Since a `QuittingDebtPoint` contains the Nash--Bellman payoff/root point and
the playerwise dynamic-debt vector, this is simultaneous control of the full
exact-D seam state, not merely payoff closeness.

## 5. What this closes

The following potential obstructions are now ruled out at the existential
finite-accuracy level:

- ordered diffuse singleton flow is not intrinsically infinite-dimensional;
- requiring actual common-root realizations does not destroy finite
  approximation;
- retaining law, holonomy, packet, debt, anchors, and the marked obstacle
  graph does not destroy it;
- retaining an actual chronological row-cost witness does not destroy it; and
- exact internal seam compatibility does not destroy it when the seam is
  compressed jointly with its composition.

Thus compactness already gives a very broad, non-effective finite reduction.

## 6. What remains open

### Effective modulus and bound

The production metric is obtained by Urysohn metrization.  The proof supplies
neither an explicit semantic radius for a desired coordinate tolerance nor a
computable bound

```text
K(n, rewardBound, epsilon).
```

An effective theorem needs an explicit product metric or direct coordinate
moduli, including a quantitative metric for the marked-stage hyperspace.

### Replacement between fixed external neighbours

The theorem replaces an entire exact seam by another exact seam.  It does not
hold the original prefix and suffix fixed.  A short representative whose
entry and exit anchors are merely close need not splice exactly between those
fixed neighbours.

The remaining compiler-facing alternatives are:

1. an exact endpoint-conditioned finite codebook;
2. a quantitative seam-repair lemma turning close anchors into an exact seam;
3. a loop/return argument selecting two occurrences with compatible anchors;
   or
4. a proof that the UE inequalities tolerate approximate seams with an
   explicit repair budget.

Stopping-law-mixture work now gives a precise partial mechanism for items 2
and 4. Convex mixtures of complete stopping laws are behaviorally realizable;
the entire terminal outcome law and every fixed-deviation payoff are affine.
Other players' best-response values and debts are convex, not globally
affine. Their convexity defect is bounded exactly by the weighted endpoint
regrets of any common deviation. Hence a common optimal witness gives an
affine stratum, and a common approximate witness gives an explicit
approximate-affinity bound.

There is an important zero-slack improvement: if both endpoints have zero
semantic debt, the whole mixture segment has zero semantic debt. On that
face the terminal payoff/envelope pair is genuinely affine. Conversely, a
formal two-player example has best-response value
`max (1-lambda, lambda)`, so witness switching creates a strict kink and rules
out a global affine chart.

More generally, if the source globally minimizes total debt and the endpoint
lies on the same minimum-total-debt fiber, finite-player summation forces
every coordinatewise convex debt inequality to be equality. Thus
minimum-fiber stopping-law directions are exact affine debt strata even when
no common best-response witness is attained.

What is not yet known is whether endpoint-conditioned minimum-fiber mixture
directions—especially directions inside the zero-debt face—have enough rank
to solve the original fixed entry/exit equations, particularly for
conditional roots and marked chronology. See
[`QUITTING_STOPPING_LAW_MIXTURE_WITNESS_STRATA.md`](QUITTING_STOPPING_LAW_MIXTURE_WITNESS_STRATA.md).

The infinite best-response family is no longer an additional obstruction at
fixed accuracy. Along any chosen mixture segment, a finite pure-time passport
of size at most `floor(4M/delta)+2` approximates one observer's full
behavioral envelope within `epsilon+delta`; all nonmoving observers admit
simultaneous passports with the corresponding linear total-size bound. Every
retained deviation-gain chart is exactly affine. Thus, once an
endpoint-conditioned segment has been found, its terminal debt geometry has
a finite affine atlas with an effective complexity bound. What remains
non-finite or non-effective is the construction of the segment and the exact
external-anchor equations themselves. See
[`QUITTING_STOPPING_LAW_MIXTURE_FINITE_WITNESS_PASSPORT.md`](QUITTING_STOPPING_LAW_MIXTURE_FINITE_WITNESS_PASSPORT.md).

There is also now one exact zero-displacement criterion. If two endpoint
profiles share their time-zero root, terminal payoff vector, and terminal
semantic-debt vector, and the source is a global total-debt minimum, their
whole stopping-law segment preserves that terminal-semantic port literally;
the zero-debt face needs no separate minimum hypothesis. This moves the
problem from generic local seam repair to finding nontrivial same-port fibers.
The later exact bridge identifies that semantic debt with the finite exact-D
costate at every boundary of an all-Continue-completed production chain, and
even identifies the full raw payoff/root/debt port before the cutoff. It still
does not preserve a prescribed exit anchor and marked chronology, nor show
that stopping-law mixtures remain inside the exact local-chain carrier. See
[`QUITTING_STOPPING_LAW_MIXTURE_FIXED_PORT.md`](QUITTING_STOPPING_LAW_MIXTURE_FIXED_PORT.md).
See also
[`QUITTING_DYNAMIC_DEBT_TERMINAL_SEMANTIC_BRIDGE.md`](QUITTING_DYNAMIC_DEBT_TERMINAL_SEMANTIC_BRIDGE.md).

There is now also a genuine finite-iteration reduction for one stochastic
button. Capping it at one finite date simultaneously approximates every
player's payoff, full behavioral envelope, and semantic debt whenever the
button has no Never atom or the maximal pair-deleted survival clock dies.
That clock is now factored exactly: its limit is a product of the Never masses
outside the mover/observer pair. Hence the universal one-button cap criterion
holds iff the moved button has zero Never mass or two distinct other buttons
do. Globally, two zero-Never seeds suffice to cap every player—even when all
remaining buttons have positive Never mass—within one common semantic error
budget. The resulting deviation problems share one finite profile-dependent
horizon. All cap dates may be placed after an arbitrary common prefix, so the
entire entry live-root word is preserved exactly through that prefix. The
construction still does not preserve an exit anchor or project the result
back into the exact Nash--Bellman chain class. See
[`QUITTING_STOPPING_LAW_FINITE_CAP_CONVERGENCE.md`](QUITTING_STOPPING_LAW_FINITE_CAP_CONVERGENCE.md).

Without two seeds, literal finite-horizon capping is false in general, but
the weaker operation actually needed for terminal semantic evaluation is now
unconditional. One finite global root prefix plus one of the finitely many
elementary cemetery-aware boundaries (`Never`, `sureJoint`, or
`sureSolo(owner)`) approximates payoff, full envelope, and debt, and its exact
semantic pair is computed by a finite backward recursion. Thus the remaining
infinite issue is not evaluation; it is exact seam and local-chain repair. See
[`QUITTING_ELEMENTARY_TAIL_SEMANTIC_REDUCTION.md`](QUITTING_ELEMENTARY_TAIL_SEMANTIC_REDUCTION.md).

For a localized chronological atom there is a sharper marked-date form.
Choosing the cutoff strictly after the atom keeps every earlier root and the
atom's unconditional terminal-coalition mass exactly fixed, while changing
the continuation payoff/envelope/debt pair at the marked date arbitrarily
little. Hence a strict signed estimate with quantitative margin survives and
an exact zero debt becomes quantitatively small. This can simplify an
atom-to-strategy compiler. It neither creates the missing sign nor supplies
Nash--Bellman compatibility of the retained prefix; exact-chain repair remains
the missing operation.

### Knife-edge constraints

Positive-margin inequalities are stable under sufficiently close semantic
replacement.  Exact equalities and zero-slack Nash faces are not.  They must
be retained as intrinsic eligibility conditions, represented by exact
coordinates, or repaired after replacement.

### Multiplicity

The marked-stage graph is a set and forgets multiplicity of identical stage
coordinates.  Literal generation cost is therefore carried separately.  If a
future decoder needs the multiplicity distribution itself, that distribution
must be added as another compact coordinate.

## 7. Revised next question

The main question is no longer whether a finite approximation exists.  It is:

```text
Can the compact enriched exact-seam reduction be made effective and converted
into a replacement between fixed surrounding blocks, with an explicit Nash/
debt/punishment error budget?
```

This is a substantially narrower target than compressing arbitrary quitting
chronology.  Fourier truncation still offers no obvious advantage: the landed
compact semantic coordinates already retain the nonlinear obstacle and seam
data directly.
