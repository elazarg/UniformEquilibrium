# Holonomy equivalence after stochastic-button compression

## Answer in one paragraph

There is useful geometry, but it is not naturally Fourier geometry.  A finite
quitting block acts on terminal continuation data through a playerwise
affine/max-affine semigroup.  No matter how long the block is, its complete
prescribed and pure-stopping boundary semantics are exactly five real numbers
per player.  Equality of those `5n` coordinates gives a nontrivial exact
equivalence relation.  There are generally infinitely many exact classes, but
at every positive accuracy the realized subset has a finite codebook of
**genuinely realized blocks**.  Consequently there is a uniform finite row
bound, independent of the source horizon, with a Lean-checked uniform
incentive-error estimate.  The result is non-effective and boundary-semantic:
what remains open is preservation of source-relative seam/provenance data and
an explicit useful bound, not the bare existence of bounded-length holonomy
representatives.

## 1. Why unordered singleton occupation fails

The companion Lean experiment
`QuittingSingletonOccupationOrderObstruction.lean`
uses three players: an owner and two opponents `left,right`.  It compares the
two opponent words

```text
left fair coin;  then right quits surely
right fair coin; then left quits surely.
```

Both words have exactly

```text
singleton occupation(left)  = 1/2,
singleton occupation(right) = 1/2,
singleton occupation(owner) = 0,
collision mass              = 0,
terminal survival           = 0.
```

The reward table makes owner quitting with `left` worth `1` and owner quitting
with `right` worth `-1`.  The owner's two-row Bellman stopping values are then

```text
left then right:  1,
right then left:  1/2.
```

Lean proves both values and concludes

```text
same unordered singleton occupation
  does not imply
same finite boundary holonomy.
```

Thus no decoder of the unordered singleton occupation vector alone can
recover stopping incentives.  Adding finitely many ordinary time moments is
not automatically safe either: the relevant operation is chronological
composition through a `max`, rather than integration against a fixed linear
basis.

## 2. The exact finite-dimensional quotient

For each player `i`, a finite root block has the five coefficients

```text
(B_i, P_i; A_i, T_i, chi_i).
```

They act on prescribed and unilateral terminal values by

```text
Prescribed_i(v) = B_i + P_i v,
BestResponse_i(w) = max(A_i, T_i + chi_i w).
```

Here:

- `B_i` is the prescribed value with zero terminal payoff;
- `P_i` is the prescribed full-survival coefficient;
- `A_i` is the best pure stopping value attained before the terminal edge;
- `T_i` is the value of continuing through the block before the terminal
  unilateral payoff is attached; and
- `chi_i` is opponent-only survival through the block.

Chronological concatenation is closed in these coordinates:

```text
(B,P) o (B',P')
  = (B + P B', P P'),

(A,T,chi) o (A',T',chi')
  = (max(A, T + chi A'), T + chi T', chi chi').
```

This is the geometry generated by varying player buttons: a
finite-dimensional, piecewise-polynomial semigroup with a max-affine obstacle
face.  A long sequence does not generate an ever-growing list of affine
pieces; associativity collapses it back to one early scalar and one tail
affine branch.

The Lean file
`QuittingHolonomyEquivalenceCompression.lean`
defines finite blocks to be equivalent when these bundled holonomies agree
and proves:

1. this is an equivalence relation;
2. equality of the raw `5n` coordinates is equivalent to equality of bundled
   holonomy;
3. equivalent blocks have identical prescribed finite values for every
   terminal value;
4. equivalent blocks have identical finite Bellman best responses for every
   terminal unilateral value; and
5. consequently they have identical boundary gaps for every terminal debt
   and terminal payoff.

So the quotient is exact for the boundary semantics that the UE argument
actually consumes.

## 3. Why the exact quotient does not have finitely many classes

The quotient has finite dimension, not finite cardinality.  Even a one-stage
one-player word can vary its own continuation probability continuously, which
varies the prescribed survival coefficient `P`.  Hence an exact finite list
of holonomy classes cannot cover unrestricted mixed buttons.

This is the right place to weaken “finite” to “finite at resolution
`delta`.”  That weakening is quantitative, not heuristic.

Every actual finite block for a fixed reward table lies in the coefficient
box

```text
B_i, A_i, T_i in [-R,R],
P_i, chi_i       in [0,1],
```

where `R` is the reward bound.  This `5n`-dimensional box is compact.  The new
Lean experiment applies finite ball-cover compactness and proves that, for
every `delta > 0`, there is a finite set of coordinate codes such that every
finite block—regardless of its length—is within `delta` of one code.

More strongly, the centers can be chosen from the set of actually realized
finite holonomies.  Closedness of that realized set is unnecessary: its
closure is a closed subset of the compact coefficient box, and a finite ball
cover can be recentered in the original subset.  Choosing one source block for
each center gives a finite set of genuinely realized representatives.  If `K`
is the maximum of their lengths, Lean proves

```text
for every finite block b,
  there is a finite block r with
    length(r) <= K
    and dist(holonomy(b), holonomy(r)) < delta.
```

The bound `K` may depend on the fixed reward table and `delta`, but not on the
length of `b`.  No computable formula or reasonable growth rate for `K` is
obtained.

If all attached prescribed and unilateral continuation values have absolute
value at most `M`, the same file proves the uniform gain estimate

```text
|gain(block; v,w) - gain(code; v,w)|
  < (3 + 2 M) delta
```

for every player.  The constant comes from the three intercept-like
coordinates and the two survival coordinates weighted by the continuation
bound.  It is sufficient, not claimed sharp.

This gives a finite number of **approximate semantic classes** at every fixed
accuracy and a finite-iteration representative for each class.

## 4. Combination with stochastic buttons

The E68--E69 stochastic-button results split a player-deleted chronology at
threshold `eta` into

```text
finitely many large opponent packets
+ diffuse rows with total strategic collision O(eta)
+ an exponentially small opponent-survival tail.
```

The present result supplies the missing finite coordinate for the ordered
diffuse singleton part:

```text
full chronology
  -> large nonlinear packets
     + one diffuse-block holonomy in R^(5n)
     + collision error O(eta)
     + tail error O(exp(-L)).
```

At an additional coordinate resolution `delta`, the holonomy is within
`delta` of a genuinely realized block of uniformly bounded length and changes
bounded boundary gains by at most `(3+2M)delta`.

Thus, at the level of prescribed payoff, stopping cap, and boundary regret,
the diffuse part really can be reduced to a uniformly finite number of
iterations at every fixed accuracy.  The proof is existential rather than an
algorithm for finding the representative.

## 5. The exact fence: realized word versus spliceable replacement

The first compact-box cover may use abstract affine/max-affine centers, but
the strengthened theorem chooses centers generated by actual common
product-root words.  It even works inside any prescribed set `eligible` of
finite blocks: representatives can be selected from `eligible` itself.
Therefore any property intrinsic to one source block can be retained—for
example, membership in a fixed class of Nash-certified paths—provided that
class is what is covered.

What raw holonomy still forgets includes:

- entry and exit dynamic-debt provenance;
- marked terminal packets and tight punishment-floor witnesses; and
- source-relative seam compatibility with the particular prefix and suffix
  being replaced.

The selected representative is a real short quitting word, but holonomy
closeness alone does not say it can be substituted into the original full
strategy at the same seam.  Exact equality is also not asserted: the reduction
is accuracy-by-accuracy.

This distinction is load-bearing.  The bare bounded-length realization
question is closed nonconstructively; the compiler-facing substitution
question requires an enriched interface and stability at the seams.

The follow-up Lean experiment
`QuittingEnrichedSeamCompression.lean`
now closes much of this fence existentially.  It uses the production compact
semantic target containing law, holonomy, exact-D entry/exit anchors, packet,
entry debt, and the entire marked-stage graph.  By compressing an exact seam
together with its composition, it obtains a uniformly bounded literal
chronological representative which is itself an exact seam and simultaneously
approximates the outer, inner, and composed enriched semantics.

What remains is effectiveness and substitution between fixed external
neighbours.  See
[`QUITTING_ENRICHED_SEAM_COMPRESSION.md`](QUITTING_ENRICHED_SEAM_COMPRESSION.md).

## 6. Standalone questions now exposed

The work leaves several clean questions which no longer need to be phrased as
questions about quitting games as a whole.

A parallel landed result supplies an additional discrete reduction axis.  At
the negative singleton gate, `TerminalSemanticPlayerDeletion` proves:

```text
strict owner-side coalition toggle
or
exact deletion to a nonempty game with one fewer player,
with the same positive exploitability floor.
```

Thus the deletion branch can be handled inductively in player number.  In a
cardinality-minimal counterexample it is impossible, so every player at that
gate is toggle-essential.  For fixed `n`, the possible toggle-witness supports
form a finite combinatorial atlas (owners and nonempty opponent coalitions),
although the payoff magnitudes and holonomy coordinates remain continuous.
This cleanly removes deletion-inert players before the effective and
seam-compatible realization questions are attacked.

### Q1. Effective bounded-length realization

The existence theorem gives some finite

```text
K(reward table, delta),
```

but no formula.  Can one prove an explicit bound depending only on coarse
data such as

```text
K(n, rewardBound, delta),
```

and construct the representative?  This is now the main quantitative
finite-iteration question.

### Q2. Enriched seam realization

The arbitrary-`eligible` theorem preserves any property of a block considered
in isolation.  Which additional finite coordinates must be appended to
holonomy so that closeness also preserves the relations needed by the UE
compiler: entry and exit debt anchors, marked owner/action data, and the
one-sided punishment floors at the actual prefix/suffix seams?

The existing production target already supplies the enrichment, and E72 shows
that its realized image has bounded-cost finite nets, including nets of exact
seam triples.  The remaining question is quantitative: replace the
noncanonical compact metric by explicit coordinate moduli and an effective
cost bound.

### Q3. Realized-image closedness

Is the closure of arbitrary-length realized holonomies itself realized by
finite words, or only by an added infinite-word boundary?  Existing
compactness results deliberately retain the full path at a fixed cutoff;
they do not prove closedness after projecting away length and provenance.
This question matters for exact limiting realization, but the finite-accuracy
short-word theorem does not require it.

### Q4. Seam admissibility and margins

If consecutive diffuse intervals are replaced by nearby short
representatives, which finite interface data and quantitative slack ensure
that the replacements can be spliced without losing the Nash/debt inequalities
at their common seam?  Knife-edge equalities need either exact retention or a
separate repair step; compactness alone supplies neither.

E72 retains exact equality inside the representative seam.  The unresolved
case is replacement between the **fixed original** outer and inner neighbours,
where close new endpoints are not literally the required old endpoints.

### Q5. Restricted positive strata

The landed minimum-state classification says exact Nash roots at a positive
minimum are all-Continue or solo debt-gate roots.  On that restricted atlas,
after recursively taking every exact player-deletion branch, is the remaining
toggle-essential realized holonomy semigroup much smaller?  Does it admit an
effective and seam-compatible short-word theorem with substantially better
bounds than unrestricted product roots?

This is probably the best next target: it uses the strongest new structural
information, the new cardinality descent, and asks for constructive
realization only on the nondeletable strata actually visited by the
construction.

## 7. Fourier verdict

Fourier analysis is not nonsense here, but it attacks the wrong object unless
additional periodic or smooth time structure is available.  The obstacle
operator is max-affine and the constraints are chronological and one-sided.
Boundary holonomy already gives an exact finite sufficient statistic for a
finite block, whereas Fourier truncation would introduce approximation before
addressing realizability.

Fourier modes may still be useful for studying special periodic families or
the spectrum of repeated holonomy composition.  They are not presently the
missing compression theorem.  Bare bounded-length approximation already
follows from compactness; the missing theorem is effective, enriched,
seam-compatible realization in the holonomy geometry.
