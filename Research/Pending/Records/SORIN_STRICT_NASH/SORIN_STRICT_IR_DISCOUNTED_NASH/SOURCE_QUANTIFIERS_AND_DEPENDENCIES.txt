Boundaries and nonclaims
-----------------------
All finite player counts, including zero, are permitted. Empty-player metric
norm is handled by the positive-error finite-Pi norm theorem. Positive cycle
length, positive uniform strict margin and scalar bounds are internally chosen.
Payoff/security levels may be negative; no positivity assumption on them.
Rates are strictly positive below a threshold <=1, hence strictly below1.
No equilibrium assertion at lambda=0 or1 is added. Owned calendar delivery
continues to cover every valid paper rate0<lambda<=1 in the source producer.
The Nash theorem covers full unilateral behavioral replacements, not pure,
stationary, calendar-only, or first-stage-only deviations.
Only actual supported off-calendar histories require punishment cap witnesses;
the public source producer derives those witnesses, not the caller.

Not proved: the whole property_4_discounted/literal Lemma2, one threshold
uniform over ALL individually rational targets, full-dimensional closure via
strict targets and compact covering, or the corrected two-player flat-security
coordinate boundary. Those remain separate source/dependency tasks. No theorem
claim is made for arbitrary N-player degenerate security faces.
