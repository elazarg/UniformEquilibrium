Exact source scope and actual-profile quantifiers
-------------------------------------------------
Game: asymmetricGeneralizedDilemma alpha beta x y, exactly the original table:
CC = (beta-y,beta-y), CD = (alpha-x,beta), DC = (beta,alpha-x), DD=(alpha,alpha).
Rate: asymmetricCriticalDiscount alpha beta x y, exactly the original max
quotient. Original hypotheses: alpha < beta-y, 0<x, 0<y.
No positivity of alpha or beta. All new profile producers and payoff consumers
use actual stochastic behavioral profiles, not a prescribed utility oracle.

Stationary branch:
  y <= x, including x=y. There is NO premise x < beta-alpha.
  Actual calendar CC forever; actual nonculprit punishment defects after the
  first observed mismatch on deviation support. Its root payoff is the printed
  pair (beta-y) (beta-y). The delivery theorem itself needs no ordering premise:
  the constant calendar delivers that value at any internally valid critical
  rate; the actual deviation cap, Nash and payoff membership retain y<=x.
  For EVERY player who and EVERY full BehaviorStrategy who replacement,
  the actual discounted payoff of Function.update is at most the source root
  value. There is no pure/stationary/one-shot deviation restriction.

Alternating branch:
  x<y. Actual calendar DC,CD,DC,..., starting with (beta,alpha-x).
  Root delivery is exactly alternatingDiscountedPayoff rate
    (pair beta (alpha-x)) (pair (alpha-x) beta),
  not an alternative rate or silently swapped starting phase.
  The same unrestricted full behavioral replacement quantifier is capped.
  The low phase may have zero equilibrium surplus and a sharp incentive
  equality; no positive punishment margin is assumed.

Boundary discounts:
  Both rates are internally shown strictly between 0 and 1 from the original
  hypotheses. There is no extra discount endpoint exclusion at final input.
  No new claim is made at rate zero or one outside those source hypotheses.
  The reusable factored delivery/cap primitives include lam=1 under their
  explicitly stated generic action bounds, reusing the owned telescope and
  support-Bellman endpoint coverage. Positive-depth reachability is not assumed;
  the history-support adapter covers depth zero as well.

Nonclaims:
  No above-critical uniqueness, equilibrium-preservation under parameter
  decreases, whole concluding_remark_4 proof, unsupported/off-path child Nash,
  subgame perfection, finite-horizon Nash, uniform equilibrium, or universal
  punishment-culprit identity at unreachable histories.
