Literal open claim read:
  property_4_discounted (G : FiniteStageGame)
    (hregular : FullDimensional G.individuallyRationalPayoffs OR
      Fintype.card G.Player = 2) :
    HausdorffConvergesAtZero G.discountedEquilibriumPayoffs
      G.individuallyRationalPayoffs

HausdorffConvergesAtZero is the fixed-set contract:
  for every positive epsilon, there is one positive threshold, such that for
  every positive rate below it both payoff sets are epsilon-close.
It is NOT a pointwise threshold depending on each target. This packet does
not substitute pointwise production for that uniform claim.

Primary known source
--------------------
Sorin (1986), On Repeated Games with Complete Information,
DOI 10.1287/moor.11.1.147, printed page 149, Lemma 2 proof; added-in-proof
correction on printed page 160. The paper first uses finite rational/periodic
pure paths approximating a fixed strictly individually rational target. Their
discounted tails stay strictly above minmax levels for small enough rate;
public deviations trigger opponent minmax punishments. Full dimensionality
and the two-player degenerate branch are treated separately at the end.
