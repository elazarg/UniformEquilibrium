## Semantic scope and nonclaims

Eta throughout is the actual infimum of MAX terminal regret over all full
behavioral profiles, with response caps over every full behavioral strategy.
Unbounded stopping dates and Never are retained by the existing robustness
owner; neither finite-menu optimization nor prescribed-payoff compression
replaces them. Live and Never rewards remain model-defined zero; the table
encoding changes only the sixty terminal recipient rewards. No affine row
shift, sign restriction, membership-stretch ancestry, single-pivot slice or
rational-maximizer claim is added.

The single scale is chosen by 74 via 66's canonical finite reward bound.
There is no second scale in the screening perturbation. The source predicate
receives the produced literal free block and the SAME polynomial; it never
uses a favorable genericity certificate as a final input. Initial positivity,
not nonvanishing or a positive screened floor alone, proves the no-UE claim.

68–73 were inspected to establish the boundary, but are NOT imports of this
unit. Their existing fixed/fiber-uniform MAX-minimum singleton collars can be
attached separately to this same-b output. No collar, finite clock, silence,
tester weights, pressure, source profile, low-regret construction or solution
of the quitting conjecture is asserted here.
