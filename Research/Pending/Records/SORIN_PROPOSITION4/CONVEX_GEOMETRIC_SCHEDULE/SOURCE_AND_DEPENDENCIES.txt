SOURCE AND MATHEMATICAL STEP
Sorin (1986), Proposition 4, printed p.151, gives the connected-Caratheodory
representation followed by the geometric schedule: a largest coefficient is
at least 1/N, remove lambda from it, divide the residual coefficients by
1-lambda, and repeat. All stages and residuals here are internally selected.
The connected representation prerequisite follows the classical proof
reproduced by Bernard Teissier (2004), Proposition 4.2, pp.220-221:
https://library.slmath.org/books/Book51/files/07teissier.pdf

version of the reflected-simplex argument and full preconnected scope.

EXACT QUANTIFIERS AND BOUNDARIES
The generic affine-step theorem takes any finite-dimensional real normed space,
any preconnected source, any actual convex-hull point, and 0 <= weight < 1
with weight * max(1, finrank) <= 1. It internally selects an actual source point
and actual convex-hull residual. No finite/compact/path source premise or
source nonemptiness, maximizer, coefficient or source-point certificate.
The generic schedule accepts an actual bounded one-step relation in an
arbitrary real-coordinate function space; from the initial region membership
it selects a complete stage sequence with exact geometric delivered values.
Neither coordinate index nor stage type needs finiteness/nonemptiness supplied.
Its discount domain is 0 < weight <= 1, including weight one. No delivered
value, orbit, limit, summability or stage schedule is supplied as a hypothesis.
Proposition 4 retains literally 0 < lambda < 1/playerCount. Positive player
count, max-dimension identity and lambda < 1 are derived internally. For zero
players its stated hypotheses are contradictory. No lambda=0, lambda=1 or
threshold equality is added to that paper theorem. Dimension zero remains
valid in the unrestricted generic representation and peeling APIs.
The actual feasible profile is behavioral on all realized-action histories;
its mixed stage profile depends only on calendar time. This is feasibility,
not an equilibrium or an assertion about unilateral deviation caps.
Proposition 6's assumptions and conclusion are unchanged. The private scalar
interface used by the pending P15 vertex unit is unchanged. P15's held final
equality patch and all frozen P15 artifacts are unmodified.
