## Fixed-target exact full behavioral Nash

`exists_discountedNash_allSmallRates_of_strictIR` and
`exists_discountedNash_close_allSmallRates_of_strictIR`, both in
`Literature/Sorin1986.lean`, have the correct stronger fixed-profile quantifiers:
given ONE actual feasible strict-IR target and positive error, internally
produce a positive threshold at most one and ONE actual behavioral profile,
then for EVERY positive rate below that threshold obtain EXACT discounted
Nash against EVERY player's unrestricted full behavioral replacement, and
delivery close to that SAME target. The profile does not depend on the later
rate, deviator or history. No calendar, punishment, cap, Nash profile or payoff
certificate is supplied by the caller.

001 merely exposes, through a private stronger version of the existing
periodic producer, its already-proved exact calendar delivery for all valid
paper rates. The old public theorem delegates and retains its exact output
scope. This is proof reuse, not a second periodic or minmax construction.

The private `FiniteStageGame.discounted_deviation_le_calendar_of_supported_cap`
is a truthful generic adapter with conditional cap/security/patience inputs.
003 supplies each input internally from the actual predecessor producer:
cap equals actual minmax plus margin; every calendar phase is at least cap
plus margin; a common finite stage bound controls all players; the canonical
one-step patience lemma supplies rate*(2*bound) <= (1-rate)*margin.

The adapter's potential is the actual discounted calendar continuation while
the realized action history agrees with the calendar, and the selected cap
after its first mismatch. On a matching next action, the actual calendar
head-tail identity is exact. On a mismatch, the one-period gain is at most
2*bound, dominated by the discounted continuation margin. The proof averages
over the actual independently mixed current joint action. Off-calendar status
persists for every successor; its supported stage cap and constant successor
potential imply the Bellman inequality.

Crucially, the off-calendar cap is required ONLY on support of the ACTUAL
unilaterally updated history law. The predecessor's first-mismatch attribution
provides exactly that condition. No bound on the selected culprit's unused
fallback behavior at unreachable histories, and no global subgame-perfection
claim, is introduced. The local on-calendar comparison happens to hold for
all pure joint actions, a valid stronger scalar inequality not a restriction
of actual deviations. The adapter need not separately assume pure prescribed
play: the public producer supplies exact prescribed calendar delivery, which
is the only such fact needed to turn the universal deviation cap into Nash.

The potential is uniformly bounded by max(bound,|cap|). Upper phase bounds
come from the existing normalized discounted series bound; lower bounds come
from cap+margin. Hence the existing history-dependent support-only Bellman
telescope applies with a genuine bounded geometric residual. No unbounded
sum interchange or new stopping-time argument is silently introduced.

## Full-dimensional weak-IR targets and uniform threshold

`FiniteStageGame.individuallyRationalPayoffs_isCompact`, in the same paper
file, identifies the actual set as its finite pure-payoff convex hull
intersected with closed coordinate security constraints. It delegates compact
convex hulls to the canonical finite-dimensional owner.

`FiniteStageGame.exists_strictIR_of_fullDimensional` uses precisely the existing
ambient-ball definition of FullDimensional, not relative interior or an
affine-dimension substitute. Lowering every center coordinate by radius/2
stays in the ball in the finite-Pi supremum norm. The security inequalities at
that lowered point prove strict security at the original center. No strict
point is assumed as an extra witness.

The private weak-to-strict target approximation chooses a positive mixing
coefficient clipped at one half. Convexity supplies actual correlated
feasibility and coordinatewise security becomes strict. Its norm estimate
has the correct multiplication orientation for the inspected
`exists_pos_mul_lt` API. It uses arbitrary signed rewards/security levels.

`exists_discountedNash_close_allSmallRates_of_mem_IR_of_fullDimensional` then
chooses the nearby strict target once and calls the actual strict-IR Nash
producer. Its SAME profile precedes all smaller positive rates; two errors
strictly below half the requested tolerance give delivery to the original
weakly IR target. The strategy is not changed with the rate.

`property_4_discounted_of_fullDimensional` uses the actual compact IR set's
finite cover by balls centered IN that set. It selects a threshold and fixed
behavioral profile for every center, then obtains one positive common lower
bound on the finitely many thresholds by the owned finite-margin theorem.
The final threshold is clipped at one and is selected BEFORE every rate and
EVERY weakly IR target. At a target, a covering center selects one already
constructed actual profile, giving exact full behavioral Nash and the two
half-error triangle estimate. Neither the finite cover nor a profile family
is an input. This genuinely proves the requested uniform target quantifier,
not merely pointwise convergence with a target-dependent final threshold.

For the reverse Hausdorff direction it uses the original
`lemma_1_Elambda_subset_Delta` on the actual discounted equilibrium set.
This theorem constructs security deviations and uses actual discounted
feasibility; it is not the open Property 4. The source insertion occurs after
that owner, avoiding a forward reference or duplicated security proof. The
two distance orientations match the literal `HausdorffClose` definition.

## Endpoints, foundations and open source boundary

- Empty finite player types are retained: strict coordinate predicates are
  vacuous, finite-Pi norm closeness uses positive accuracy, pure joint actions
  still have their canonical inhabitant, and the finite threshold selector
  does not demand a nonempty index type. No hidden positive player count.
- Rewards and security levels can be negative. The proof uses absolute stage
  bounds, not nonnegative payoff assumptions.
- The paper current-stage rate is lambda; every calculation translates it
  to repository continuation 1-lambda. The recurrence starts at actual date
  zero and advances one stage after the current payoff. There is no dropped
  first-stage term or off-by-one change of calendar phase.
- The private adapter accepts every valid paper rate, including lambda=1,
  when its explicit patience premise holds; the canonical telescope includes
  continuation zero. Public small-rate producers assert only 0<lambda<threshold
  with threshold<=1, so neither lambda=0 nor lambda=1 is silently added.
- All infinite behavioral deviations, including those that never depart the
  prescribed calendar, are included in the actual update/telescope theorem.
  No stationary, pure, finite-support or first-period-only deviation class
  is substituted.
- Static inspection found no call to an unfinished Literature declaration in
  the new proof paths. The literal `property_4_discounted` and `lemma_2` remain
  unchanged, with FullDimensional OR exactly two players as hypotheses and
  their open proof status. Neither is imported as a theorem dependency of the
  new full-dimensional branch. P15 and critical constructors remain unchanged.
  Root's requested transitive axiom harnesses are still essential confirmation.

The source's separate flat-security two-player boundary is NOT supplied by
strict-IR target approximation. Nothing here claims it for arbitrary
degenerate N-player IR sets. Do not retire the whole discounted folk clause
or erase its two-player alternative on the strength of this narrower branch.
